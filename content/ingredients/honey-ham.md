## The short version

Sweet, juicy, mild deli ham. It isn't fancy, and that's the point: it's the
meat picky eaters trust and the one that makes a brunch or holiday board feel
familiar. Pair it with Swiss and spicy brown mustard and you've built the
best bite on the board.

## What it is

Honey ham is a wet-cured, fully cooked pork ham. The leg is injected or
soaked in a brine of salt, sugar, honey and curing salt, then smoked or
cooked and chilled. Deli honey ham is sliced from a boneless log; spiral-cut
holiday hams are the bone-in version with a honey glaze.

**Taste:** sweet, mild and savory with a gentle smoke
**Texture:** moist, tender and smooth; slices fold without tearing
**Look:** rosy pink slices, sometimes with a darker glazed edge

The pink comes from the cure. Sodium nitrite reacts with myoglobin in the
meat to form a stable pink pigment that survives cooking, which is why ham
stays pink and roast pork turns gray. The brine also holds water in the
muscle, so wet-cured ham stays far juicier and milder than a dry-cured ham
like prosciutto.

For a drier, smoky German-style ham, try [Black Forest ham](/ingredients/black-forest-ham/).

## What it pairs with — and why

| Pair it with | Why it works |
|---|---|
| **Baby Swiss or Gouda** | Nutty, buttery cheese and sweet ham. The sandwich classic. |
| **Spicy brown mustard** | Sharp heat cuts the sweetness. |
| **Pineapple** | Bright acid echoes the holiday glaze. |
| **Pepper jelly** | Sweet heat on salty-sweet meat. |
| **Black Forest ham** | Serve both and taste wet-cured against smoked dry-cured. |
| **Riesling, rosé or a light lager** | Fruity or crisp, nothing too tannic. |

**Skip it with:** dessert items like chocolate and caramel in the same
corner. Honey ham is already sweet; it needs salt, acid and mustard around
it, not more sugar.

## How to prep it for the board

1. **Ask the deli for slices** a little thicker than sandwich-thin.
2. **Fold slices into quarters** or ribbon them loosely in a pile.
3. **Roll ham around a stick of Swiss** and a smear of mustard for roll-ups.
4. **Serve about 1.5 oz per person.**
5. **Keep it cold until guests arrive,** and refill in small batches.
6. **Fix:** if slices look wet, blot them with a paper towel before
   folding. Wet ham makes crackers soggy.

## Quick FAQ

**What is honey ham?**
A wet-cured, fully cooked ham with honey added to the cure or brushed on as
a glaze. It's sweeter and juicier than dry-cured hams like prosciutto.

**Is honey ham good on a charcuterie board?**
Yes, especially for kids, brunch and holiday boards. Fold or roll it, and
pair it with Swiss and mustard.

**What's the difference between honey ham and prosciutto?**
Prosciutto is dry-cured for a year or more and eaten raw, so it's silky and
salty. Honey ham is brined and cooked, so it's moist, sweet and mild.

**How long can ham sit out on a board?**
Up to 2 hours at room temperature. Refill from the fridge rather than
leaving a big pile out.

**What cheese goes with honey ham?**
Baby Swiss, Gouda, Gruyère and sharp cheddar. Nutty and buttery cheeses love
sweet ham.
