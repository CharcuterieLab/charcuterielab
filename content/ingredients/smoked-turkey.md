## The short version

Thin-sliced smoked turkey breast from the deli. It's lean, mild and a little
smoky, it costs far less than prosciutto and it's the meat to add when some
guests don't eat pork. Rolled up next to cranberry sauce and Swiss, it turns
any board into a Thanksgiving sandwich.

## What it is

Deli smoked turkey is whole or formed turkey breast that's brined, smoked
and fully cooked, then chilled and sliced. Unlike salami or prosciutto, it
isn't dry-cured, so it's moist and perishable. It's one of the best-selling
deli meats in the country.

**Taste:** mild, savory and lightly smoky, sometimes peppered or
honey-glazed
**Texture:** tender, moist and smooth; thin slices fold easily
**Look:** pale ivory-pink slices with a tan or peppered edge

The pink edge is a smoke ring. Smoke contains nitrogen dioxide, which
dissolves in the moist surface of the meat and reacts with its myoglobin,
fixing a pink color just under the skin. Brine adds salt and water before
cooking, which is why deli turkey stays juicy rather than drying out like a
roast.

## What it pairs with — and why

| Pair it with | Why it works |
|---|---|
| **Cranberry sauce** | Tart fruit lifts a mild, lean meat. The holiday pairing. |
| **Baby Swiss or havarti** | Soft, buttery cheese adds the fat turkey lacks. |
| **Honey mustard** | Sweet heat on a mild bite. |
| **Apples** | Crisp, juicy and tart. |
| **Pepper jelly** | Sweet heat for a party twist. |
| **Pinot noir, Beaujolais or a dry cider** | Light, fruity drinks for light meat. |

**Skip it with:** strong cured meats like 'nduja or spicy soppressata on
the same stack. They overpower turkey's gentle flavor. Give it its own row.

## How to prep it for the board

1. **Ask the deli for slices** slightly thicker than sandwich-thin so they
   hold a fold.
2. **Fold each slice into quarters** and line them up like fans, or roll
   them into tubes.
3. **Wrap slices around cheese sticks** or a strip of havarti for roll-ups.
4. **Serve about 1.5 oz per person.**
5. **Keep it cold until the last minute,** and put out half at a time.
6. **Fix:** if slices tear, they're too thin or too cold. Let them sit 5
   minutes, then fold.

## Quick FAQ

**Can you put turkey on a charcuterie board?**
Yes. Deli smoked turkey is a lean, mild option and the go-to meat when some
guests don't eat pork.

**How do you serve deli turkey on a board?**
Fold slices into quarters, roll them into tubes, or roll them around a stick
of cheese. Ask for slices a little thicker than sandwich-thin.

**How long can deli turkey sit out?**
No more than 2 hours at room temperature, or 1 hour above 90°F. Put out half
and refill from the fridge.

**What goes with smoked turkey on a board?**
Cranberry sauce, baby Swiss, havarti, honey mustard and apples. Think
Thanksgiving sandwich.

**Is smoked turkey gluten-free?**
Most plain deli turkey is, but some brands use wheat-based binders or
glazes. Read the label.
