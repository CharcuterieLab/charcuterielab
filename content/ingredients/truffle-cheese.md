## The short version

Cheese studded with black truffle flecks and an aroma nobody mistakes for
anything else. It's the most reliably impressive thing you can put on a board —
and most of what's sold is flavored with a synthetic compound rather than with
truffle.

## What it is

Truffle cheeses are usually a semi-firm sheep's or cow's-milk base — pecorino,
gouda or a soft-ripened cheese — with truffle pieces folded into the curd. The
aroma is dominated by 2,4-dithiapentane, a sulphur compound that occurs
naturally in truffles and is also manufactured cheaply for use in truffle oil
and flavoring.

**Taste:** earthy, mushroomy, garlicky, savory, intense
**Texture:** depends on the base — semi-firm and sliceable, or soft and
spreadable
**Look:** pale paste flecked with visible black specks

The synthetic aroma question is the mechanism worth understanding. Real truffle
loses most of its aroma within days and costs a fortune; 2,4-dithiapentane is
stable, cheap and smells overwhelmingly of truffle. Almost all affordable
truffle cheese uses it, often alongside a small quantity of real truffle for
the visible flecks. That doesn't make the cheese bad — but it explains why a
$20 wedge smells as strong as a $200 tuber.

## What it pairs with — and why

| Pair it with | Why it works |
|---|---|
| **Crostini and plain bread** | Nothing should compete with an aroma this dominant. |
| **Honey** | Sweetness against earthy sulphur. Truffle honey doubles down; plain honey balances. |
| **Prosciutto** | Salt and cure with mushroom and earth. |
| **Fresh pear** | Cool and sweet, cutting the intensity. |
| **Walnuts** | Tannin and earth, both of which the truffle aroma already gestures at. |
| **Champagne or Nebbiolo** | Acid or structure. Truffle and Barolo is a classic Piedmontese pairing. |
| **Scrambled eggs, elsewhere in the meal** | Worth knowing: truffle cheese melted into eggs is better than most truffle dishes. |

**Skip it with:** blue cheese and smoked cheese — three assertive aromas in one
place and none of them win. Also skip serving two truffled items on one board.

## How to prep it for the board

1. **Serve about 1 oz per person.** It's intense and expensive.
2. **Cut into thin slices or small wedges** so the black flecks show in
   cross-section.
3. **Temper 30 minutes** — the aroma is the product and cold suppresses it.
4. **Put it at one end of the board with its own knife.** Truffle aroma
   transfers to everything and lingers on a blade.
5. **Serve it early in a tasting order.** Nothing tastes quite the same
   afterwards.
6. **Keep it double-wrapped in the fridge** or the whole shelf will smell of
   it.
7. **Fix:** if the aroma has faded, the cheese is old. Unlike most cheeses,
   truffle cheese gets less interesting with time.

## What to buy

| Product | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Sottocenere al Tartufo** | Cheese counters, Whole Foods | Italian, ash-rubbed, truffled. The best-known and reliably good. |
| **Moliterno al Tartufo** | Cheese counters, Whole Foods | Sardinian sheep's milk with truffle veins. Richer and more savory. |
| **Truffle Tremor (Cypress Grove)** | Whole Foods, cheese counters | American goat cheese with truffle. Soft, excellent, distinctive. |
| **Truffle gouda** | Grocery cheese case, Trader Joe's | Widely available and cheap. Usually heavily flavored. |
| **Pecorino al tartufo** | Italian delis, cheese counters | Sheep's-milk base. Salt and truffle work well together. |
| **Fresh truffle, shaved over plain cheese** | Specialty, seasonal | The real thing, if you're spending. |

**Label check:** read the ingredient list. Real truffle appears as "black
truffle" with a percentage; synthetic aroma appears as "truffle flavoring" or
"aroma." Many cheeses have both.

**Skip:** buying truffle cheese for a board that already has a truffle honey or
truffle salami. One truffled item per board.

## Quick FAQ

**Is truffle cheese made with real truffles?**
Usually a small amount, for the visible flecks — but the aroma almost always
comes from 2,4-dithiapentane, a synthetic compound. Check the ingredient list.

**Does that make it bad?**
No. It's a legitimate, widely-liked cheese. It just isn't the same thing as
fresh truffle, and it shouldn't cost as if it were.

**Which truffle cheese should I buy first?**
Sottocenere al Tartufo, or Truffle Tremor if you prefer goat cheese.

**How much per person?**
About 1 oz. It's strong.

**Why does the aroma fade?**
The aromatic compounds are volatile. Truffle cheese is one of the few cheeses
that's best young.
