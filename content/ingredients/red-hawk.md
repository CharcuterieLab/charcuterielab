## The short version

A washed-rind triple-crème from Cowgirl Creamery in Point Reyes, California.
Orange-pink rind, dense buttery paste, and a beefy savory funk. It's the
American washed-rind cheese that proved the country could make one, and it won
Best of Show at the American Cheese Society.

## What it is

Red Hawk is made in Point Reyes Station, north of San Francisco, from organic
cow's milk with cream added — putting it in triple-crème territory. The rind is
washed in brine during a six-week aging, which develops *Brevibacterium linens*
and the characteristic orange-pink surface. Cowgirl Creamery credits the local
fog and coastal air for the particular bacterial culture that develops.

**Taste:** rich, beefy, savory, tangy, moderately funky
**Texture:** dense and buttery, softening toward the rind
**Look:** small round, sticky orange-pink rind, pale dense paste

Fat plus washed rind is the mechanism, and the combination is unusual. Most
washed-rind cheeses are made from standard milk, so the funk sits in the
foreground. Red Hawk's added cream coats the palate and pulls the savory notes
back — you get a beefy, umami-rich cheese rather than an aggressive one.

## What it pairs with — and why

| Pair it with | Why it works |
|---|---|
| **Baguette** | Plain bread under a rich, aromatic cheese. |
| **Dry cider or sparkling wine** | Acid and bubbles against a triple-crème. |
| **Cornichons and pickled onion** | Sharp vinegar cutting fat and funk. |
| **Walnuts and almonds** | Tannin and crunch against a dense paste. |
| **Honey** | Sweetness rounding off the savory edge. |
| **Apple and pear** | Fresh acid, cold and clean. |
| **Zinfandel, Pinot Noir, Belgian ale** | Californian wines or a malty beer. |

**Skip it with:** delicate fresh cheeses and aged hard cheeses. It's rich and
aromatic and it dominates its corner.

## How to prep it for the board

1. **Temper 45 minutes.** It should soften noticeably near the rind.
2. **Cut wedges from the center out**, keeping rind on each piece.
3. **Serve about 1 oz per person.** One small round serves four to six.
4. **Give it its own knife and corner.** The rind transfers aroma to anything
   the blade touches next.
5. **Use it as the American answer to Époisses** on a domestic board —
   alongside Humboldt Fog, Maytag Blue and a Vermont cheddar it makes a
   genuinely strong all-American selection.
6. **The rind is edible** and carries most of the funk.
7. **Fix:** an ammonia smell means it's over-ripe. Buy it from a counter that
   turns stock quickly.

**Label check:** buy it from a counter with turnover. Red Hawk is a small round
with a short window, and a tired one smells of ammonia.

**Skip:** buying it outside its distribution area without checking freshness.
It travels less well than a hard cheese.

## Quick FAQ

**What is Red Hawk?**
A washed-rind triple-crème cheese from Cowgirl Creamery in Point Reyes,
California. Rich, beefy and moderately pungent.

**How strong is it?**
Between Taleggio and Époisses. Aromatic, but the added cream softens the funk
considerably.

**Can you eat the rind?**
Yes, and it carries most of the flavor.

**What else should be on an American cheese board?**
Humboldt Fog, Maytag Blue or Rogue Creamery blue, Cabot Clothbound cheddar, and
Vella Dry Jack.

**How much per person?**
About 1 oz. One small round serves four to six.
