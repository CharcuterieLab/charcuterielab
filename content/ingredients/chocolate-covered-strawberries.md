## The short version

Juicy strawberries dipped in chocolate and left to set. They're the most
romantic thing you can put on a board and one of the easiest homemade
showpieces: 30 minutes of work for a dozen guests. The only rules are dry
berries and good chocolate.

## What it is

Chocolate-covered strawberries are fresh strawberries, washed, dried and
dipped in melted chocolate, then set on parchment. Lorraine Lorusso of
Chicago is often credited with popularizing them in the 1960s. They're now a
Valentine's Day staple and a common gift from florists and gift shops.

**Taste:** juicy, tart-sweet berry against rich, slightly bitter chocolate
**Texture:** a thin snap of chocolate, then soft, juicy fruit
**Look:** glossy dark or white chocolate shells with green tops

The snap depends on the chocolate. Cocoa butter can set in several crystal
forms, and only one (Form V) is glossy and snappy at room temperature.
Tempering, or using compound melting wafers with other fats, gives you that
finish. Water is the enemy: a few drops make melted chocolate seize into a
grainy paste, which is why berries must be bone-dry.

## What it pairs with — and why

| Pair it with | Why it works |
|---|---|
| **Fresh berries** | Undipped berries make the dipped ones look special. |
| **Brie or mascarpone** | Creamy cheese with chocolate and fruit. Dessert in one bite. |
| **Shortbread** | Buttery crumble beside a juicy, chocolatey bite. |
| **Dark chocolate squares** | Build a chocolate corner. |
| **Chocolate-covered pretzels** | Sweet-salty next to sweet-tart. |
| **Champagne, prosecco or a ruby port** | Bubbles or berry wine with berries. |

**Skip it with:** cured meats and blue cheese in the same corner. Chocolate
and salami fat taste greasy together; keep the berries on the sweet side.

## How to prep it for the board

1. **Wash and dry the berries completely,** at least an hour on a towel.
2. **Melt chocolate** in 20-second bursts in the microwave, stirring each
   time, until just smooth.
3. **Dip each berry by the leaves,** let the excess drip and set it on
   parchment.
4. **Drizzle white chocolate on top** for the classic look.
5. **Set in the fridge 20 to 30 minutes,** then hold somewhere cool.
6. **Fix:** if chocolate seizes, stir in a teaspoon of neutral oil or
   shortening until it loosens.

## What to buy

| Product | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Large fresh strawberries with green tops** | Produce section | Pick firm, fully red berries. |
| **Ghirardelli Melting Wafers** | Baking aisle | Set glossy without tempering. |
| **Baker's or Ghirardelli bittersweet bars** | Baking aisle | Richer flavor; needs care to set shiny. |
| **Ready-made dipped strawberries** | Grocery bakery, Costco seasonally | Easy, but buy the same day. |
| **Dark chocolate** | Candy aisle | Squares for the board. See its own page. |

**Label check:** "melting wafers" or "candy melts" set shiny with no
tempering. Chocolate chips contain stabilizers and set dull and soft.

**Skip:** berries with white shoulders. They're underripe and sour, and
chocolate won't fix that.

## Quick FAQ

**How far ahead can I make chocolate-covered strawberries?**
Up to about 8 hours ahead. After a day, the berries start to weep juice and
the chocolate softens.

**Should chocolate-covered strawberries go in the fridge?**
Briefly, to set them. For long storage, keep them cool but out of the
fridge if you can, because condensation makes them sweat when they come out.

**Why won't the chocolate stick to my strawberries?**
The berries are wet. Wash them, then let them dry completely, an hour on a
towel. Any water makes chocolate slide off or seize.

**What chocolate is best for dipping strawberries?**
Melting wafers or a good bar of semisweet or bittersweet chocolate. Melting
wafers set shiny without tempering.

**How many chocolate-covered strawberries per person?**
About 2 per person on a mixed board, 3 or 4 on a dessert board.
