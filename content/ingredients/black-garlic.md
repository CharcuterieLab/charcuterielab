## The short version

Whole heads of garlic held in warm, humid heat for weeks until the cloves
turn jet-black, soft as a date and sweet. The harsh garlic bite is gone.
What's left tastes like balsamic, molasses and tamarind. Set out a few
peeled cloves beside goat cheese and watch people's faces.

## What it is

Black garlic became popular in Korea and Japan, and is now made worldwide.
Whole bulbs are kept at roughly 140 to 170°F with high humidity for three to
six weeks, then dried. The cloves come out black and sticky, with a texture
between a soft date and a gummy candy.

**Taste:** sweet and savory, like balsamic, molasses, tamarind and dried plum
**Texture:** soft, chewy and spreadable
**Look:** glossy jet-black cloves, inside a papery tan skin

It's often called fermented, but it isn't. The change is the Maillard
reaction happening very slowly: sugars and amino acids in the garlic react
over weeks at low heat and turn brown, then black, building sweet and
savory compounds. At the same time, the sharp sulfur compound allicin breaks
down, which is why the garlic bite disappears.

## What it pairs with — and why

| Pair it with | Why it works |
|---|---|
| **Fresh chèvre** | Tangy cream against sweet, dark garlic. The best match. |
| **Burrata** | Mild milk lets the balsamic notes shine. |
| **Aged gouda** | Caramel on caramel, plus a savory edge. |
| **Toasted sourdough** | A plain, chewy base to spread it on. |
| **Olive oil** | Mash the cloves with oil for an instant dip. |
| **Pinot Noir, an amber ale or a dry sherry** | Earthy, savory drinks to match. |

**Skip it with:** dessert items. It's sweet, but savory-sweet, and next to
chocolate it reads as odd rather than clever.

## How to prep it for the board

1. **Peel the cloves.** The papery skin slips off easily.
2. **Serve about one clove per person** whole, or mashed.
3. **Mash 6 to 8 cloves with 2 tablespoons of olive oil or soft butter** and
   a pinch of salt for a quick spread.
4. **Place whole cloves in a small dish** so their black color stands out.
5. **Put it next to the goat cheese** so guests try them together.
6. **Fix:** if the cloves have dried and hardened, warm them in a little
   olive oil for 2 minutes.

## Quick FAQ

**What is black garlic?**
Regular garlic held at warm temperature and high humidity for several weeks.
The cloves turn black, soft and sweet as sugars and amino acids react.

**Is black garlic fermented?**
It's often called fermented, but no microbes are involved. The change comes
from slow browning reactions, the same chemistry as toast or caramel.

**What does black garlic taste like?**
Sweet and savory, like balsamic vinegar, molasses, tamarind and dried plum,
with only a faint garlic note.

**Will it give guests garlic breath?**
Much less than raw garlic. The sharp sulfur compounds break down during
aging.

**How do you serve it?**
Whole peeled cloves in a small dish, or mashed with olive oil or butter into
a spread.
