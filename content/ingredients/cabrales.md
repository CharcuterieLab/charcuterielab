## The short version

A cave-aged blue from the Picos de Europa in Asturias, made from a blend of
cow's, sheep's and goat's milk. It is stronger than Roquefort — genuinely, not
as a boast. Serve half an ounce, put honey and cider beside it, and warn
people.

## What it is

Cabrales DOP is made in a handful of Asturian villages and aged two to four
months in natural limestone caves in the mountains, where humidity approaches
saturation. No mold is added — the *Penicillium* comes from the cave air. In
summer, milk from all three animals is blended; in winter it's cow's milk only.
Traditionally wrapped in sycamore or maple leaves.

**Taste:** extremely sharp, salty, spicy, acidic, long finish
**Texture:** moist, crumbly, sticky, sometimes almost spreadable
**Look:** ivory to grey paste, heavily veined blue-green, no rind

Wild mold in a saturated cave is the mechanism. Commercial blues are inoculated
with a selected *P. roqueforti* strain and aged in controlled conditions.
Cabrales gets whatever the cave has and a humidity level that pushes mold growth
hard — which produces a much more aggressive, less predictable cheese.

## What it pairs with — and why

| Pair it with | Why it works |
|---|---|
| **Asturian cider (*sidra*)** | The regional pairing. Sharp, dry, acidic cider is the only drink that stands up. |
| **Honey** | Essential rather than optional. Cabrales without sweetness is punishing. |
| **Walnut raisin bread** | Sweet, tannic and substantial enough to carry it. |
| **Membrillo (quince paste)** | Dense floral sweetness against extreme salt. |
| **Fresh pear** | Cool sweet water as relief. |
| **Walnuts** | Tannin meeting blue bitterness. |
| **Sweet sherry — PX, Oloroso** | Spanish, sweet and fortified. Sugar against salt. |

**Skip it with:** everything mild, everything smoked, and anything you want
guests to taste afterwards. Cabrales flattens the palate for several minutes.

## How to prep it for the board

1. **Serve half an ounce per person.** Less than any other cheese in this
   section.
2. **Warn people.** "This is the strongest thing on the board" is a service,
   not a spoiler, and it stops someone taking a Stilton-sized piece.
3. **Temper 45 minutes.**
4. **Give it its own small plate**, off the main board if possible. The aroma
   travels.
5. **Put honey and membrillo directly beside it** with their own spoons.
6. **Serve it last** in any tasting order.
7. **Fix:** wrap in foil and double-bag it in the fridge. Cabrales will scent
   everything around it within a day.

## What to buy

| Product | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Cabrales DOP** | Cheese counters, specialty shops, Spanish grocers | The real thing. Look for the DOP label and the leaf wrapping or its imprint. |
| **Valdeón (Queso de Valdeón)** | Cheese counters, Whole Foods | Wrapped in sycamore leaves, from León. Gentler and easier to find. |
| **Picón Bejes-Tresviso** | Specialty shops | Another Picos de Europa blue. Similar intensity. |
| **Roquefort** | Whole Foods, cheese counters | The step down in intensity — see its own page. |
| **Bleu d'Auvergne** | Cheese counters | Much gentler cow's-milk blue — see its own page. |
| **Spanish specialty grocer** | Local, or online | Best chance of finding genuine DOP Cabrales. |

**Label check:** the DOP mark. Much of what's sold as Cabrales outside Spain is
Valdeón, which is a good cheese but a milder one.

**Skip:** serving it to a general crowd. This is a cheese for a board where at
least some guests are enthusiasts.

## Quick FAQ

**Is Cabrales stronger than Roquefort?**
Yes. Wild cave mold, near-saturated humidity and a three-milk blend make it
noticeably more aggressive.

**What are the leaves for?**
Traditionally sycamore or maple leaves wrapped the cheese during aging. Modern
DOP cheese is usually wrapped in foil printed with a leaf pattern.

**What do I serve with it?**
Honey and Asturian cider, at minimum. It's punishing without sweetness.

**How much per person?**
Half an ounce. Genuinely.

**Is Valdeón the same thing?**
No — Valdeón is a related leaf-wrapped Spanish blue from León. Similar look,
gentler cheese, easier to find.
