## The short version

Pretzel twists in a chocolate coat. Salt makes the chocolate taste sweeter,
the pretzel adds crunch, and they cost very little. On a dessert board
they're the gap filler that ties everything together, and they're easy to
dress up for any holiday with a drizzle or colored sprinkles.

## What it is

Chocolate-covered pretzels are hard pretzels, twists, rods or crisps, dipped
or enrobed in milk, dark, white or yogurt-style coating. They're sold as
candy in most grocery stores and are one of the simplest homemade holiday
treats: a bag of pretzels, a bag of chocolate and 20 minutes.

**Taste:** sweet chocolate, salty pretzel, a malty toasted finish
**Texture:** snappy chocolate shell over a hard, crunchy pretzel
**Look:** glossy brown or white twists, often drizzled or sprinkled

Salt is a flavor amplifier for sweetness. Small amounts of salt suppress
bitterness, so chocolate's bitter notes recede and its sweetness and cocoa
aroma come forward. The pretzel's alkaline-browned crust also adds a malty,
slightly bitter note that keeps the bite from tasting like pure candy.

## What it pairs with — and why

| Pair it with | Why it works |
|---|---|
| **Caramel dip** | Dip a chocolate pretzel in caramel for a turtle-style bite. |
| **Chocolate-covered strawberries** | Salty crunch beside sweet, juicy fruit. |
| **Sharp aged cheddar** | Salty, crystalline cheese and salty-sweet chocolate. |
| **Dark chocolate and sea salt caramels** | Build a sweet-salty corner. |
| **Plain mini pretzels** | Mix both so the bowl isn't all sweet. |
| **Stout, porter, coffee or a tawny port** | Roasty drinks for chocolate. |

**Skip it with:** cured meats and blue cheese right beside them. Chocolate
next to salami fat tastes greasy, and blue cheese clashes with milk
chocolate.

## How to prep it for the board

1. **Pour them into a small bowl** or tuck them into gaps on the sweet side.
2. **Keep them away from heat and wet foods** so the chocolate doesn't melt
   or bloom.
3. **Serve 2 to 3 pieces per person.**
4. **For homemade,** dip mini twists, drip off the excess and set them on
   parchment. Add sprinkles before the chocolate sets.
5. **Mix dark and white chocolate** for a contrast that looks good in
   photos.
6. **Fix:** if they have a white haze (bloom), they're still fine to eat.
   Use them in a bowl rather than front and center.

**Label check:** "chocolate" means real cocoa butter. "Chocolatey" or
"confectionery coating" means other fats, which melt less but taste waxier.

**Skip:** yogurt-covered pretzels on a warm day. The coating softens fast.

## Quick FAQ

**Are chocolate-covered pretzels good on a charcuterie board?**
Yes, on dessert boards and on the sweet corner of a mixed board. They add
salty crunch beside fruit and chocolate.

**How do you make chocolate-covered pretzels?**
Dip mini twists or rods in melted chocolate, let the excess drip, set them
on parchment and chill 15 minutes. Add sprinkles before they set.

**How long do homemade chocolate pretzels last?**
About 2 weeks in an airtight container somewhere cool. Humidity softens the
pretzels.

**What cheese goes with chocolate-covered pretzels?**
Sharp aged cheddar and aged Gouda. Salty, crunchy cheese and salty, sweet
chocolate are a surprising match.

**Are there gluten-free chocolate pretzels?**
Yes. Some brands make them, or dip gluten-free pretzels yourself.
