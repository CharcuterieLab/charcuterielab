## The short version

Quail eggs are about the size of a large olive, with speckled shells and
rich yolks. Peeled, boiled and pickled in a spiced vinegar brine, they become
one perfect bite: creamy yolk, firm white, tangy finish. They're adorable,
filling and one of the best keto options for a board.

## What it is

Pickled quail eggs are hard-boiled, peeled quail eggs stored in a vinegar
brine with salt and spices. Common flavors are plain, jalapeño, Cajun and
beet (which turns them pink). They're sold in jars in Southern gas stations
and grocery stores, and fresh quail eggs for pickling at home are sold in
Asian and Latin markets.

**Taste:** rich yolk, tangy vinegar, spice or chili from the brine
**Texture:** firm white, creamy-dense yolk
**Look:** small smooth white or pale gold eggs, pink if beet-pickled

Size is the mechanism. A quail egg has a much higher ratio of yolk to white
than a chicken egg, so each bite is richer. Because they're small, the brine
reaches the yolk in days rather than weeks, so the whole egg tastes seasoned
instead of just the outer white. Acid from the vinegar also firms the white
and keeps the eggs safe in the fridge.

**Skip it with:** dessert items. They're savory and tangy, and next to
sweets they confuse guests.

## How to prep it for the board

1. **Drain the eggs well** and pat them dry so they don't leave puddles.
2. **Halve a few lengthwise** to show the yolks.
3. **Serve one or two eggs per person.**
4. **Sprinkle the cut halves with flaky salt or everything seasoning.**
5. **Place them in a small dish** with toothpicks, in the briny corner.
6. **Fix:** if they taste too sharp, roll them in a teaspoon of olive oil
   with salt and pepper. Fat softens the vinegar.

## Quick FAQ

**What do pickled quail eggs taste like?**
Like a deviled egg in one bite: creamy yolk, firm white and a tangy, spiced
brine. Jalapeño versions add gentle heat.

**Are quail eggs different from chicken eggs?**
They're about a fifth the size and have a higher ratio of yolk to white, so
they taste richer.

**Where can I buy them?**
Buc-ee's, Walmart online and many Asian or Latin markets sell jars of pickled quail
eggs. Fresh quail eggs are at Asian markets if you want to pickle your own.

**How long do homemade pickled quail eggs keep?**
Up to about two weeks in the fridge. Never store homemade pickled eggs at
room temperature.

**Are they keto?**
Yes. Eggs and vinegar brine have almost no carbs. Check jars for added sugar.
