## The short version

Normandy's bloomy-rind cheese: same white mold as brie, smaller wheel, much
more flavor. Earthy, mushroomy, sometimes faintly barnyardy. If brie has ever
struck you as bland, this is the cheese you actually wanted.

## What it is

Camembert de Normandie has been made since the late 1700s and comes in a small
round wooden box — not decoration, but a functional container that lets the
cheese breathe while holding its shape as it softens. The wheels are about
250g, far smaller than a brie, which means more rind surface relative to paste.

**Taste:** mushroomy, earthy, savory, faintly sulphurous when ripe
**Texture:** soft to runny, more elastic than brie
**Look:** small ivory round, white rind flecked with rust and grey

Surface-to-volume ratio is the mechanism. The rind mold does the ripening, and
a Camembert wheel has much more rind per ounce of cheese than a wide flat brie.
More rind means more enzymatic breakdown, which means more flavor — that's the
entire difference between the two cheeses, and it's geometry rather than
recipe.

## What it pairs with — and why

| Pair it with | Why it works |
|---|---|
| **Baguette** | Plain wheat under an assertive cheese. The French pairing, unchanged. |
| **Tart apple slices** | Normandy grows apples for a reason — acid and sweetness against earthy fat. |
| **Dry cider** | The regional drink and the correct one. Acid and light sweetness. |
| **Walnuts** | Tannic bitterness meets mushroomy funk. Both get more interesting. |
| **Cornichons** | Sharp vinegar resets the palate after a rich, ripe bite. |
| **Honey** | Sweetness rounds off the sulphur notes in a very ripe wheel. |
| **Calvados or Normandy cider brandy** | Regional, and strong enough to stand up. |

**Skip it with:** everything crackers and other loud bases, and delicate
toppings like smoked salmon, which lose. A ripe Camembert is a much bigger
flavor than people expect from a white-rinded cheese.

## How to prep it for the board

1. **Temper for 45–60 minutes.** Same rule as brie, same reason, and even more
   payoff — the aromatics here are worth releasing.
2. **Cut wedges from the center out.** Small wheels mean small wedges; six to
   eight per wheel is right.
3. **Serve 1–2 oz per person.** One 250g wheel covers four to six as part of a
   board.
4. **Eat the rind.** More so than with brie — the rind is where the mushroom
   note lives.
5. **Bake it in the box.** Remove any plastic, score the top, bake at 350°F for
   15 minutes and serve molten with bread. It's the best thing you can do with
   a supermarket Camembert.
6. **Keep it away from the fresh cheeses.** It's pungent enough to affect
   anything mild sitting next to it.
7. **Fix:** if it smells strongly of ammonia, it's over-ripe. A faint whiff on
   opening that fades in ten minutes is normal; a persistent sharp smell is
   not.

## What to buy

| Product | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Président Camembert** | Most grocery stores | The widely available default. Mild for the category. |
| **Ile de France Camembert** | Grocery cheese case | A little more character. |
| **Camembert de Normandie AOP** | Cheese counters, Whole Foods, Murray's | The protected original. Much stronger and much better. |
| **Le Châtelain Camembert** | Grocery, cheese counter | Widely imported and consistently good. |
| **Trader Joe's Camembert** | Trader Joe's | Cheap and reliable, especially for baking. |
| **Any wheel in a wooden box** | Cheese counter | The box is a good sign — it means the producer expects it to ripen. |

**Label check:** press gently. It should give evenly across the whole surface.
A wheel that's firm in the middle needs more time; one that's collapsing and
smells of ammonia is past it.

**Skip:** pre-cut Camembert wedges. Small wheels are cheap enough to buy whole,
and a cut face dries fast.

## Quick FAQ

**Is Camembert just stronger brie?**
Effectively, yes — same mold, smaller wheel, more rind per ounce, so more
flavor. Earthier and more mushroomy.

**Can you eat the rind?**
Yes, and here you especially should. The rind is where the mushroom character
comes from.

**Why does it come in a wooden box?**
The box lets the cheese breathe while holding its shape as it softens. It's
also oven-safe, which is why baked Camembert is served in it.

**How do I know if it's gone off?**
A strong, persistent ammonia smell. A faint whiff on opening that fades is
normal ripeness.

**How much per person?**
1–2 oz. One small wheel serves four to six on a mixed board.
