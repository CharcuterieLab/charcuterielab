## The short version

Variety is the whole decision. **Ataulfo** (also sold as honey or champagne
mango) is small, yellow, flat, and has no fibre — it slices into smooth gold
ribbons. The big red-and-green Tommy Atkins mangoes in most supermarkets are
stringy and will leave floss between your teeth. Same fruit name, different
ingredient.

## What it is

A tropical stone fruit with a large flat pit that runs the length of the fruit.
Ripeness is judged by give and smell, not colour — a red mango can be rock hard,
and a green Keitt can be perfectly ripe.

**Taste:** very sweet, low acid, resinous-floral
**Texture:** dense and smooth in Ataulfo, fibrous in the common red types
**Look:** deep gold slices or ribbons

The mechanism is sugar with almost no acid, which is unusual on a board — most
fruit brings both. That makes mango behave like a condiment rather than a palate
cleanser: it works where you'd otherwise reach for honey or jam, adding sweetness
to salt without adding sharpness.

## What it pairs with — and why

| Pair it with | Why it works |
|---|---|
| **Prosciutto** | Salt against pure sugar, no acid interfering. Wrap a ribbon of mango in a slice — it's the tropical version of melon and ham. |
| **Fresh goat cheese** | The cheese supplies all the acid; the mango supplies all the sugar. A complete pairing from two incomplete halves. |
| **Feta** | Salt and brine against heavy sweetness. This is a salad, on a board. |
| **Manchego** | Nutty sheep milk and resinous fruit — the aromas overlap more than you'd expect. |
| **Coppa and spicy soppressata** | Chilli heat and mango sugar; the standard tropical hot-sweet pairing. |
| **Chilli-lime salt (Tajín)** | Turns mango from fruit into an actual snack. |

**Skip it with:** blue cheese, unless there's chilli in the mix. Low-acid
sweetness makes blue taste soapy.

## How to prep it for the board

1. **Buy Ataulfo** — small, kidney-shaped, yellow. Not the big red ones.
2. **Judge ripeness by squeeze and smell**, never colour. It should give like a
   ripe avocado and smell sweet at the stem.
3. **Slice down either side of the flat pit**, then score the flesh in a grid
   and turn the skin inside out — or peel and cut ribbons off.
4. **Cut ribbons or thin slices, not cubes.** Cubes read as fruit salad;
   ribbons read as a board.
5. **Blot lightly.** Ripe mango is wetter than it looks.
6. **Add lime and chilli salt** if it's going anywhere near cured meat.
7. **Fix:** stringy? You bought Tommy Atkins. Purée it, or slice it thinner
   across the grain.

## What to buy

| Product | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Ataulfo / honey / champagne mango** | Grocery, Latin markets, Mar–Jul | The board mango. Fibreless, dense, gold. |
| **Alphonso mango** | Indian grocers, spring | The best mango in the world and priced accordingly. Very short season. |
| **Keitt mango** | Grocery, late summer | Stays green when ripe. Large, low-fibre, good. |
| **Tommy Atkins** | Any grocery, year-round | The big red one. Fibrous and mild. Usable but not ideal. |
| **Frozen mango chunks** | Freezer aisle | Fine for purée, wrong texture for a board once thawed. |
| **Dried mango** | Trader Joe's, bulk bins | Excellent. Buy unsweetened and unsulphured. |

**Label check:** squeeze it. Give at the shoulders means ripe; a hard mango
needs three days on the counter and will never be as good.

**Skip:** mangoes with black sap marks around the stem and any that smell
fermented.

## Quick FAQ

**Which mango is best for a charcuterie board?**
Ataulfo — small, yellow, kidney-shaped, sold as honey or champagne mango. No
fibre, so it slices cleanly.

**How do I tell if a mango is ripe?**
By squeeze and smell, not colour. It should give slightly and smell sweet at the
stem.

**How do I cut a mango?**
Slice down either side of the flat central pit, then either score and invert the
skin, or peel and cut ribbons.

**What cheese goes with mango?**
Fresh goat cheese, feta, and manchego. Skip blue cheese unless there's chilli
involved.

**Why is my mango stringy?**
Tommy Atkins and similar large red varieties are fibrous. Buy Ataulfo instead.
