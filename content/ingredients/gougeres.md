## The short version

Small savory choux puffs with cheese in the batter, from Burgundy. They're
hollow, warm, and gone in one bite. Putting a bowl of warm gougères on a board
changes the whole event — it's the difference between guests grazing and guests
gathering.

## What it is

Gougères are choux pastry — the same dough as a profiterole — with grated
Gruyère or Comté beaten in. The dough is cooked on the stove first, then
enriched with eggs, then piped and baked. Steam inflates the puff from inside,
leaving a hollow shell.

**Taste:** nutty, cheesy, eggy, savory
**Texture:** crisp shell, hollow airy interior, slightly custardy at the base
**Look:** golden domes, irregular, about an inch across

Steam expansion is the mechanism, and it's why gougères are hollow rather than
dense. High moisture in the dough turns to steam in a hot oven and pushes the
shell outward before the crust sets. That hollow is what makes them feel
weightless despite being made of butter, eggs and cheese.

## What it pairs with — and why

| Pair it with | Why it works |
|---|---|
| **Champagne and crémant** | The traditional Burgundian pairing, and the one to lead with. Bubbles cut butter and eggs. |
| **Chablis and white Burgundy** | Regional and correct. High-acid white against a rich puff. |
| **Nothing at all** | Worth stating: gougères are complete. They need no topping and no accompaniment. |
| **A soft ripened cheese alongside** | Warm cheese puff and cool creamy cheese is a good sequence, not a stacked bite. |
| **Cornichons and pickled things** | Acid to reset between rich bites. |
| **Charcuterie generally** | They belong beside the board rather than on it — a warm counterpoint to cold meat. |
| **Red Burgundy** | Lighter reds work; big tannic reds don't. |

**Skip it with:** anything stacked on top. These are hollow and delicate and
they aren't a base — trying to use one as a cracker crushes it. Aged hard
cheese alongside is also redundant; the cheese is already inside.

## How to prep it for the board

1. **Serve warm, from the oven, in a bowl or napkin-lined basket** beside the
   board — not on it. They lose their crispness against damp cheese.
2. **Serve 2–3 per person.**
3. **Make the dough ahead and pipe onto a sheet, then freeze.** Bake straight
   from frozen, adding about five minutes. This is the trick that makes
   gougères practical for a party.
4. **Bake at 400°F for about 20–25 minutes** until deeply golden. Underbaked
   gougères collapse — take them further than feels right.
5. **Don't open the oven for the first 15 minutes.** The steam is doing the
   work.
6. **Bring them out during the party, not before.** The smell alone is the
   point.
7. **Fix:** softened gougères re-crisp at 350°F for 4 minutes and are nearly as
   good.

## What to buy

| Product | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Gruyère or Comté** | Cheese counter, most grocery | The cheese that belongs in the batter. Buy a wedge and grate it. |
| **Frozen gougères** | Trader Joe's (seasonal), specialty and gourmet freezers, Costco | Sold under various names as cheese puffs. Convenient and decent. |
| **Trader Joe's frozen cheese puffs** | Trader Joe's | Appear around the holidays. Genuinely good for the price. |
| **A French bakery or traiteur** | Local | Some make them fresh to order. Worth asking. |
| **Pão de queijo** | Grocery freezer, Latin markets | The Brazilian relative — different dough, same warm-cheese-puff role, and easier to buy. See that page. |

**Label check:** for frozen versions, look for real cheese named on the
ingredient list — Gruyère, Emmental or Comté — rather than "cheese flavoring."

**Skip:** sweet profiteroles or cream puffs as a stand-in. Same pastry,
completely wrong direction.

## Quick FAQ

**What are gougères?**
Savory choux pastry puffs with cheese in the dough, from Burgundy. Served warm.

**Can I make them ahead?**
Yes — pipe them onto a sheet and freeze raw, then bake from frozen with five
extra minutes. That's the standard method for entertaining.

**Which cheese should I use?**
Gruyère or Comté. Both are nutty and melt cleanly. Aged cheddar works but is
less traditional.

**How many per person?**
2–3. They're one bite each and people take more than they mean to.

**Why did mine collapse?**
Underbaked, or the oven was opened too early. Take them to a deep gold and
leave the door shut for the first fifteen minutes.
