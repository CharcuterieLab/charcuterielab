## The short version

Tiny hard pretzel twists from the snack aisle. They're salty, crunchy and
sturdy enough to drag through any dip, and they cost almost nothing. On a
board they fill every gap, carry the mustard, and turn caramel and chocolate
into salted dessert.

## What it is

Hard pretzels grew out of the soft German pretzel. Pennsylvania, settled by
German immigrants, became the center of American pretzel baking, and the
town of Hanover still is. Mini pretzels are small twists or rods of plain
dough, dipped in an alkaline bath, salted and baked until fully dry and
crisp.

**Taste:** salty, toasty and slightly bitter-malty
**Texture:** hard, crunchy and dry
**Look:** small glossy brown twists with flecks of coarse salt

The flavor is chemistry. The alkaline dip, lye or baking soda, raises the
pH of the dough's surface, which speeds up the Maillard browning reaction in
the oven. That's what makes the crust deep brown, glossy and tasting of
pretzel instead of bread. Baking low and long then dries the whole pretzel
so it shatters instead of chews.

## What it pairs with — and why

| Pair it with | Why it works |
|---|---|
| **Spicy brown or honey mustard** | Salt, toast and heat: the ballpark combination. |
| **Cheese curds or cheddar** | Salty crunch next to mild, milky cheese. |
| **Ranch or beer cheese dip** | Sturdy enough to scoop thick dips without breaking. |
| **Caramel dip and chocolate** | Salt makes sweet taste sweeter. |
| **Pretzel crisps** | Flat, cracker-style pretzels for spreading. |
| **Lager, Märzen or a hefeweizen** | Bavarian beer for a Bavarian bread. |

**Skip it with:** delicate fresh cheeses. Salt and the pretzel's bitter
crust overwhelm a soft burrata or fresh chevre.

## How to prep it for the board

1. **Pour them straight into gaps** between items, or into a small bowl.
2. **Keep them away from wet foods** like pickles, olives and jam.
3. **Serve about an ounce per person.**
4. **Put them out last** so they stay crisp.
5. **For a dessert board,** dip half of each pretzel in melted chocolate and
   let it set.
6. **Fix:** if they've gone soft, bake them at 300°F for 5 minutes, then cool.

## Quick FAQ

**Are pretzels good on a charcuterie board?**
Yes. Mini pretzels add salt and crunch, scoop dips and fill gaps for very
little money.

**Why are pretzels brown and shiny?**
They're dipped in an alkaline bath, traditionally lye or baking soda,
before baking. The high pH speeds browning and gives the glossy crust and
distinctive flavor.

**What dips go with pretzels?**
Spicy brown mustard, honey mustard, beer cheese, ranch, caramel and
chocolate.

**Are pretzels vegan?**
Most hard pretzels are: flour, water, yeast, salt and oil. Some brands add
butter or milk, so check the label.

**Are there gluten-free mini pretzels?**
Yes. Snyder's of Hanover and Glutino both sell gluten-free pretzels.
