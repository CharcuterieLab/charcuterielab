## The short version

The provolone at the deli counter: smooth, mild, a little salty and often
lightly smoky. It isn't the sharp aged provolone in the specialty case. It's
the sub-shop cheese, and on a board it does one job perfectly: rolling
around salami.

## What it is

Provolone is a pasta filata, or stretched-curd, cheese. The curd is heated
and kneaded like mozzarella, shaped, brined and aged. Mild provolone, called
provolone dolce in Italy, is aged two to three months and is what most US
delis slice. The sharp, long-aged piccante style is a different experience.

**Taste:** mild, milky and salty, sometimes with a gentle smoke
**Texture:** smooth, firm and pliable; slices bend without tearing
**Look:** pale ivory with a waxy or thin tan rind on the log

The stretching is why it rolls so well. Kneading hot curd lines the proteins
up into long parallel strands, like taffy. That makes deli provolone
flexible and elastic, so a thin slice wraps around a salami without
cracking. Young provolone is also moist and low in acid, which keeps its
flavor mild.

## What it pairs with — and why

| Pair it with | Why it works |
|---|---|
| **Hard salami or Genoa salami** | Salty, garlicky meat and mild cheese. The Italian sub pairing. |
| **Pepperoni** | Spice and fat against a smooth, cool cheese. |
| **Banana peppers or pepperoncini** | Tangy heat cuts the richness. |
| **Roasted red peppers** | Sweet and soft next to salty. |
| **Aged provolone** | Serve both and let guests taste what aging does. |
| **Chianti, a light red or an Italian lager** | Easy-drinking and acidic. |

**Skip it with:** delicate fresh cheeses like burrata on the same plate. Two
pale, mild cheeses blur together. Let provolone be the mild one.

## How to prep it for the board

1. **Ask for slices about 1/16 inch thick** at the deli, a little thicker
   than sandwich-thin.
2. **Stack a slice of provolone and a slice of salami,** roll them tight and
   cut in half. Pin with a toothpick if needed.
3. **Or fold slices into quarters** and fan them in a row.
4. **Serve about 1 oz per person.**
5. **Assemble roll-ups up to 4 hours ahead,** covered in the fridge.
6. **Fix:** if roll-ups unroll, chill them 15 minutes. Cold fat holds the
   shape.

## Quick FAQ

**What is provolone cheese?**
A stretched-curd Italian-style cheese, made like mozzarella and then aged.
Deli provolone in the US is the mild, young version, often lightly smoked.

**What's the difference between provolone and aged provolone?**
Deli provolone (provolone dolce) is aged a few months and tastes mild and
milky. Aged provolone (piccante) is aged a year or more and tastes sharp and
peppery.

**How do you serve provolone on a charcuterie board?**
Roll deli slices around salami, or fold them into quarters. Cubes from a
chunk work too.

**Is provolone smoked?**
Many deli brands are lightly smoked or smoke flavored. Check the label if you
want plain.

**What meat goes with provolone?**
Hard salami, Genoa salami, pepperoni and capicola. Add banana peppers and you
have an Italian sub on a board.
