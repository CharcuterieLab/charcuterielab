## The short version

Dry-cured, cold-smoked ham from the Black Forest, with a near-black exterior
from fir and pine smoke. Firmer and smokier than prosciutto. Be aware that most
American "Black Forest ham" is a wet-cured, cooked deli ham with nothing in
common with it.

## What it is

Schwarzwälder Schinken PGI is boned pork leg, dry-salted with garlic,
coriander, juniper and pepper, cured for weeks, then cold-smoked over fir
brush for several more, then aged. The smoke and the seasoning darken the
exterior to near-black — the name describes the ham's colour and its home.

**Taste:** smoky, juniper-scented, savoury, firm salt
**Texture:** firm and dense, drier than prosciutto, chewy at thicker cuts
**Look:** deep red muscle, white fat, a distinctly dark to black rind

Cold smoke over weeks is the mechanism. Unlike hot-smoked deli ham, which is
cooked, Schwarzwälder Schinken is smoked at low temperature over a long period —
it stays raw and continues to cure. That's why the real thing is sliced thin
like prosciutto rather than layered like sandwich ham.

## What it pairs with — and why

| Pair it with | Why it works |
|---|---|
| **Rye crispbread and cocktail rye** | Sour dark bread against smoke and salt. The German plate. |
| **Emmental or Gruyère** | Mild alpine cheese under a smoky ham. |
| **Cornichons and pickled onion** | Sharp acid cutting smoked fat. Essential. |
| **Grainy mustard and horseradish** | Heat and sharpness, both traditional. |
| **Melon and pear** | Sweet cold fruit, as with any dry-cured ham. |
| **Butter on dark bread** | The simplest German service and a very good one. |
| **Dark beer, dry Riesling, cider** | Malt or acid against smoke. |

**Skip it with:** smoked gouda or speck — one smoked item per board — and
delicate fresh cheeses, which the smoke buries.

## How to prep it for the board

1. **Buy the dry-cured PGI version if you can.** The American deli slice is a
   different food, and if that's what you have, treat it as sandwich ham.
2. **Slice thin.** Real Black Forest ham is firm; a thick slice is chewy.
3. **Fold into loose quarters.** It holds a fold better than prosciutto.
4. **Temper 20 minutes.**
5. **Serve 1–2 oz per person.**
6. **Acid beside it** — cornichons, pickled onion, mustard.
7. **Fix:** if it tastes flat and wet rather than smoky and firm, it's the
   cooked deli version. Nothing to fix; buy the dry-cured one next time.

**Label check:** *Schwarzwälder Schinken* and a PGI mark for the real thing.
"Black Forest style" on an American deli ham means a cooked, wet-cured product.

**Skip:** assuming the deli-counter version is the same food. It isn't, and the
difference is the whole page.

## Quick FAQ

**Is American Black Forest ham the real thing?**
Usually not. Most US deli Black Forest ham is wet-cured and cooked. The German
PGI product is dry-cured and cold-smoked, and eaten like prosciutto.

**Why is the outside black?**
Weeks of cold smoking over fir and pine brush, plus the spice rub.

**Is it raw?**
The dry-cured German version is uncooked but fully cured, like prosciutto. The
American deli version is cooked.

**How much per person?**
1–2 oz as one of several meats.

**What's the closest alternative?**
Speck — Italian, juniper-spiced and cold-smoked. Very similar in role.
