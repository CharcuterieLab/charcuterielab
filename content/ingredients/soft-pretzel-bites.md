## The short version

Little nuggets of soft pretzel: dark, glossy crust, chewy middle, coarse
salt on top. Bake a box from the freezer right before the party, pile them
warm at the edge of the board with mustard and a cheese dip, and they'll be
the first thing gone.

## What it is

Soft pretzels came to America with German immigrants, especially to
Pennsylvania and Philadelphia, where they're still a street-corner staple.
Pretzel bites are the same dough cut into small pieces. Most people buy them
frozen and bake them in minutes; bakeries and mall pretzel shops sell them
fresh.

**Taste:** malty, toasty and salty, with a faint mineral tang
**Texture:** thin, chewy crust over a soft, dense middle
**Look:** small, deep mahogany nuggets with white coarse salt

The crust gets its color and flavor from an alkaline dip. A lye or baking
soda bath raises the pH of the dough's surface, which speeds up the Maillard
reaction in the oven so the crust goes dark brown and glossy in minutes. It
also gelatinizes the outer starch, giving that thin, chewy skin that plain
bread doesn't have.

## What it pairs with — and why

| Pair it with | Why it works |
|---|---|
| **Spicy brown or whole grain mustard** | Heat and vinegar against salty, malty bread. |
| **Honey mustard** | A sweeter dip for kids. |
| **Beer cheese or pimento cheese** | Warm bread dragged through creamy cheese. |
| **Cheese curds** | A Wisconsin beer-hall plate. |
| **Hard salami or bratwurst coins** | Salty meat with chewy bread. |
| **Oktoberfest, lager or a hefeweizen** | Malty beer for a malty bread. |

**Skip it with:** delicate fresh cheeses. Pretzel salt and crust overwhelm
them. Give the bites mustard and bold dips instead.

## How to prep it for the board

1. **Bake them last,** 5 to 8 minutes at 375 to 400°F, or follow the box.
2. **Brush with water and sprinkle coarse salt** before baking so it sticks.
3. **Serve 3 to 4 bites per person.**
4. **Pile them warm** in a cloth-lined bowl at the edge of the board, so
   they don't steam the crackers.
5. **Set the mustard and cheese dip right beside them.**
6. **Fix:** if they've gone cold and dense, rewarm at 350°F for 3 to 4
   minutes. Avoid the microwave, which makes them tough.

## Quick FAQ

**Are soft pretzel bites good on a charcuterie board?**
Yes. Served warm with mustard and a cheese dip, they make a board feel like
a beer-hall spread.

**How do you heat frozen pretzel bites?**
Bake them at 375 to 400°F for about 5 to 8 minutes, or follow the box.
Brush with a little water and add the salt just before baking.

**Can you make soft pretzel bites ahead?**
Bake them ahead, then rewarm at 350°F for 3 to 4 minutes right before
serving. Cold pretzel bites turn dense.

**What dip goes with soft pretzels?**
Spicy brown mustard, whole grain mustard, honey mustard, beer cheese and
pimento cheese.

**Why do soft pretzels taste different from bread?**
They're dipped in an alkaline bath before baking, which speeds browning and
gives the dark, glossy crust its pretzel flavor.
