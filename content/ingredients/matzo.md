## The short version

Unleavened flour-and-water bread, rolled flat, docked and baked fast. It's a
clean, neutral, extremely cheap cracker that happens to be associated with one
holiday — and there's no reason at all not to use it on a board in October.

## What it is

Matzo is made under a time constraint: from the moment water touches flour,
the dough must be mixed, rolled, docked and baked in **under eighteen
minutes**, before fermentation can begin. That's a religious requirement, and
it also produces a very particular cracker — no yeast, no rise, no flavor
development beyond the bake itself.

**Taste:** clean, plain, faintly toasted
**Texture:** dry, brittle, snaps into irregular shards
**Look:** large pale squares, blistered and docked with rows of holes

Speed is the mechanism, and its side effect is neutrality. With no fermentation
there are no sour or yeasty notes, and with no fat there's nothing to coat the
palate. What's left is closer to a water cracker than anything else in this
tier — a genuine blank, at a fraction of the price of a specialty box.

## What it pairs with — and why

| Pair it with | Why it works |
|---|---|
| **Chopped liver and pâté** | The traditional pairing, and the same logic as melba toast: rich and soft on dry and plain. |
| **Cream cheese and smoked salmon** | Neutral base, so the fish leads. Works as well as any bagel chip. |
| **Whitefish salad** | The deli plate, and matzo is what it belongs on. |
| **Brie and Camembert** | Plain and dry against soft and rich. It behaves like a large water cracker. |
| **Charoset, or any fruit-and-nut paste** | Sweet and textured on a completely plain base. |
| **Butter and flaky salt** | Warm matzo with good butter is genuinely excellent and costs nothing. |
| **Sparkling wine, dry white** | The cracker adds nothing, so pair to the topping. |

**Skip it with:** thick dips — it's large, flat and brittle and it snaps in the
middle. Break it small first if dips are the plan.

## How to prep it for the board

1. **Break each sheet into rough quarters or sixths.** A full sheet is far too
   big, and torn edges look far better than cut ones.
2. **Serve about one sheet per person**, broken — so four to six pieces each.
3. **Warm it 3 minutes at 300°F.** Matzo out of the box is fine; warm matzo is
   noticeably better, and almost nobody does this.
4. **Try it brushed with olive oil and salt** before warming. It becomes a
   different and much better cracker.
5. **Stand a few large shards upright.** It's rigid enough to hold, and the
   pale blistered surface looks good in profile.
6. **Use it year-round.** The only reason it isn't a standard board cracker is
   habit.
7. **Fix:** it's bone dry already. Humidity softens it and a low oven fixes it
   completely.

## What to buy

| Product | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Manischewitz Matzo** | Nearly every grocery store, kosher aisle | The most widely available. Plain and reliable. |
| **Streit's Matzo** | Grocery kosher aisle, Amazon | The other major American brand. Very similar. |
| **Yehuda Matzo** | Grocery, Amazon | Widely stocked, often cheapest. |
| **Egg matzo** | Same aisle | Richer and slightly sweet. Not kosher for Passover, but good on a board. |
| **Whole wheat matzo** | Same aisle | Nuttier and more substantial. Good with aged cheese. |
| **Handmade shmura matzo** | Kosher markets, online | Round, irregular, hand-rolled. Beautiful on a board and expensive. |

**Label check:** flour and water. That's it for plain matzo. Egg matzo and
flavored versions add to that list.

**Skip:** matzo crackers seasoned with garlic or onion if you want the
neutrality. The plain sheets are the useful product.

## Quick FAQ

**Can I use matzo on a board outside Passover?**
Yes. It's sold year-round in most supermarkets and it's a good, cheap neutral
cracker.

**Why does matzo have to be baked in 18 minutes?**
Religious law: after 18 minutes, dough is considered to have begun leavening.
The constraint is what defines the product.

**Is matzo gluten-free?**
No — it's wheat flour and water. Oat and gluten-free matzo exist but are
specialty items.

**How much per person?**
About one sheet, broken into four to six pieces.

**Should I do anything to it?**
Brush with olive oil, salt it, and warm it for three minutes. It's a large
improvement for very little effort.
