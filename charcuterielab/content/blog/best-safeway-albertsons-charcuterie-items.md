# The Best Safeway and Albertsons Charcuterie Board Items, Ranked

> **Quick Answer:** The best cheese at Safeway and Albertsons for a charcuterie board is Laura Chenel Original Fresh Goat Cheese (4.7 out of 5). Primo Taglio, the store's deli brand, is the one to trust: its fresh mozzarella, provolone and pepperoni all rank well. Best value: Lucerne Pepper Jack. The same picks apply at Vons, Jewel-Osco, Acme, Shaw's, Tom Thumb and other Albertsons-owned stores.

## Building a board from these

For 8 to 10 guests, pick:

- **Cheeses:** Laura Chenel goat cheese, Primo Taglio provolone and Tillamook or Lucerne pepper jack. Add a sharp cheddar from the cheese case.
- **Meats:** Primo Taglio pepperoni plus salami and prosciutto from the deli counter.
- **Extras:** crackers, olives, grapes, a jam and Primo Taglio mozzarella balls with cherry tomatoes and basil.

Plan on about 3 oz of cheese and 3 oz of meat per guest when the board is the party food, or 2 oz each before a meal.

## What to skip

[The Takeout](https://www.thetakeout.com/2249755/every-major-grocery-store-ranked-cheese-selection/) ranked Safeway 12th of 13 chains for cheese: reviewers found Lucerne mozzarella and cheddar weak on flavor and complained about inconsistent deli slicing. Buy cheddar from a better brand in the cheese case, and ask the deli to slice meat thin.
