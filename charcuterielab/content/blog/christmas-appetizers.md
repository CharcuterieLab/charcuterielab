# 40 Christmas Appetizers You Can Make Ahead (Easy + Festive)

> **Quick Answer:** Mix four kinds: **no-cook bites** (caprese wreath, stuffed dates), **warm bites** (cranberry brie bites, meatballs), **a dip** and **one showstopper board**. Plan **4–6 pieces per guest before dinner**, **12–15 if appetizers are the meal**. Almost everything here can be prepped a day ahead.

<h2 id="how-many">How many appetizers to make</h2>
<p>The number depends on one question: <strong>is dinner coming after?</strong></p>
:::

| Your party | Pieces per guest | How many kinds | Include |
|---|---|---|---|
| Appetizers, then a sit-down dinner | 4 to 6 | 3 or 4 | Light bites. Skip the sliders. |
| Open house or drop-in (2 hours) | 8 to 10 | 5 or 6 | One warm, one dip, one board |
| Appetizers are the whole party (3 hours) | 12 to 15 | 6 to 8 | Two filling ones, like sliders and meatballs |

**Quick math:** 12 guests at a party where appetizers are the meal × 12 pieces = about 144 pieces. Six kinds means about 24 of each. A board counts as several "pieces" per guest. For the cheese and meat on a board, use our standard of **2 oz each per guest before dinner, 3 oz as the party food** ([full guide](/blog/how-much-charcuterie-per-person/)), or let the [Party Planner](/party-planner/) do the math for your guest count.

From 20 guests, add about 10% so the late arrivals still find food.

<h2 id="no-cook">No-cook Christmas appetizers (1–10)</h2>
<p>No oven needed, so these are the ones to lean on when the oven is full of ham or turkey.</p>
:::

### 1. Caprese Wreath

Arrange cherry tomatoes, small mozzarella balls and basil leaves in a ring on a round platter, with a small bowl of balsamic glaze in the middle. Sprinkle with flaky salt.

- **Make ahead:** Build it up to 4 hours ahead, cover and chill. Add the basil and glaze just before serving so the basil doesn't darken.
- **Tip:** A [good balsamic glaze](/blog/best-balsamic-glaze/) is thick enough to stay in the bowl rather than running across the platter.

![A caprese wreath of cherry tomatoes, mozzarella balls and basil leaves arranged in a ring around a small bowl of balsamic glaze](/images/christmas-appetizers-caprese-wreath.jpg)

### 2. Prosciutto-Wrapped Pears

Slice a firm Bosc pear into wedges, toss them in lemon juice, add a few arugula leaves and a small piece of blue cheese, and wrap each one in half a slice of prosciutto.

- **Make ahead:** Up to 4 hours, covered in the fridge. The lemon keeps the pears from browning.

### 3. Salami Cream Cheese Roll-Ups

Spread softened cream cheese on slices of Genoa salami, add a strip of pepperoncini or a green onion, roll them up tightly and slice in half. Stand them up on a plate with a toothpick through each.

- **Make ahead:** The day before. They hold their shape better after a night in the fridge.

### 4. Cucumber Smoked Salmon Bites

Top thick slices of English cucumber with herbed cream cheese, a curl of [smoked salmon](/blog/best-smoked-salmon/) and a sprig of dill.

- **Make ahead:** Slice the cucumber and mix the cream cheese the day before. Assemble within 2 hours of serving, or the cucumber weeps. Pregnant guests should skip cold smoked salmon (see [keeping it safe](#safety)).

### 5. Antipasto Skewers

Thread a tortellini or mozzarella ball, a folded slice of salami, an olive, a basil leaf and a cherry tomato onto a short skewer. Drizzle with Italian dressing.

- **Make ahead:** The day before, covered. Full method and 10 combinations in our [charcuterie skewers guide](/blog/charcuterie-skewers/).

### 6. Goat Cheese Snowballs

Roll 8 oz of cold goat cheese into 1-inch balls, then roll each one in finely chopped dried cranberries, pecans and parsley. Serve each one on a pretzel stick.

- **Make ahead:** Up to 2 days ahead without the coating. Roll them in the coating on the day.

### 7. Stuffed Dates

Slit [Medjool dates](/blog/best-medjool-dates/) lengthwise, pull out the pit, and fill each with goat cheese or blue cheese and half a pecan. Drizzle with honey and a pinch of flaky salt.

- **Make ahead:** The day before. Add the honey just before serving.

### 8. Shrimp Cocktail Ring

Buy cooked, peeled shrimp. Thaw them overnight in the fridge, pat them dry and lay them in a ring around a bowl of cocktail sauce, with lemon wedges and rosemary sprigs tucked in like a wreath.

- **Make ahead:** Thaw and arrange in the morning. Serve over a tray of ice.

### 9. Marinated Olives and Feta

Warm olive oil with strips of orange zest, rosemary, a smashed garlic clove and chili flakes, then pour it over olives and cubes of feta.

- **Make ahead:** Up to 3 days in the fridge. Homemade garlic-in-oil must stay refrigerated and be used within 4 days. Bring it to room temperature 30 minutes before serving so the oil turns liquid again. Which olives to buy: [best olives for charcuterie](/blog/best-olives-for-charcuterie/).

### 10. Santa Hat Fruit Skewers

On a toothpick, stack a green grape, a slice of banana, a strawberry (pointed end up) and a mini marshmallow on top. The kids' table favorite.

- **Make ahead:** Wash the fruit the day before. Assemble within an hour of serving, because banana browns.

:::html
<h2 id="warm">Warm Christmas appetizers (11–20)</h2>
<p>The ones people go back for. Most can be frozen unbaked and baked straight from the freezer on the day.</p>
:::

### 11. Cranberry Brie Bites

Cut a sheet of thawed puff pastry into 24 squares and press them into a mini muffin tin. Add a cube of brie and a teaspoon of cranberry sauce to each. Bake at 375°F for 15 to 20 minutes, until puffed and golden. Top with a pecan and a rosemary needle.

- **Make ahead:** Assemble, freeze in the tin, then bag them. Bake from frozen, adding about 5 minutes.
- **Which brie?** A mild, everyday brie melts best here. Save the good one for the board. See [best brie brands](/blog/best-brie-brands/) and [best cranberry sauce](/blog/best-cranberry-sauce/).

![Golden cranberry brie bites in puff pastry cups, each topped with cranberry sauce and a pecan, on a wooden board with rosemary](/images/christmas-appetizers-cranberry-brie-bites.jpg)

### 12. Baked Brie

A whole wheel of brie, topped with jam or cranberries and nuts, baked until molten. Serve it with crackers and apple slices.

- **Make ahead:** Top it and wrap it the day before, then bake it as guests arrive. Times, toppings and the puff-pastry version: [baked brie recipe](/blog/baked-brie-recipe/).

### 13. Pigs-in-a-Blanket Wreath

Wrap cocktail sausages in strips of crescent dough and arrange them in a ring on a parchment-lined sheet pan, touching each other. Brush with egg and sprinkle with everything seasoning. Bake at 375°F for 12 to 15 minutes. Put a bowl of mustard in the middle and add rosemary "holly".

- **Make ahead:** Freeze the wrapped, unbaked sausages, then bake from frozen, adding a few minutes. Our pick of [mustards for dipping](/blog/best-mustard-for-charcuterie/).

![A golden ring of pigs in a blanket arranged like a Christmas wreath, with a bowl of mustard in the center and rosemary sprigs and cherry tomatoes tucked in](/images/christmas-appetizers-pigs-in-blanket-wreath.jpg)

### 14. Bacon-Wrapped Dates

Stuff pitted dates with a sliver of Parmesan or an almond, wrap each one in a third of a slice of bacon, and secure it with a toothpick. Bake at 400°F for 15 to 20 minutes, turning once, until the bacon is crisp.

- **Make ahead:** Wrap them the day before and chill them on the sheet pan. Bake just before serving.

### 15. Sausage-Stuffed Mushrooms

Mix browned Italian sausage with cream cheese and Parmesan, pile it into cremini mushroom caps and bake at 375°F for about 20 minutes.

- **Make ahead:** Stuff them the day before and bake from the fridge, adding 5 minutes.

### 16. Slow-Cooker Cocktail Meatballs

Frozen cooked meatballs in a sauce of grape jelly and chili sauce (or cranberry sauce and barbecue sauce for a Christmas twist). Cook on low for about 4 hours, until they reach 165°F, then switch the cooker to "warm".

- **Make ahead:** No prep needed. Start them 4 hours before guests arrive. Put a cup of toothpicks beside them.

### 17. Ham and Swiss Sliders

Split a pack of sweet dinner rolls in one sheet, fill it with ham and Swiss, and brush the tops with melted butter mixed with Dijon and poppy seeds. Bake covered at 350°F for 15 minutes, then uncovered for 5 more.

- **Make ahead:** Assemble the night before, cover and chill. Brush with the butter just before baking. This is the filling one that turns appetizers into dinner. A great use for [leftover Christmas ham](/blog/leftover-charcuterie/).

### 18. Puff Pastry Christmas Tree

Spread pesto on one sheet of puff pastry and cover it with a second sheet. Cut it into a tree shape, slice branches into each side and twist each one. Brush with egg, sprinkle with Parmesan and bake at 400°F for about 15 to 18 minutes.

- **Make ahead:** Shape it, cover it and chill it the morning of the party. Bake it right before guests arrive. Use the scraps as a star on top.

![A golden puff pastry Christmas tree with twisted branches filled with pesto, dusted with Parmesan, on a dark baking sheet](/images/christmas-appetizers-puff-pastry-tree.jpg)

### 19. Sausage Balls

A Southern Christmas classic: 1 lb of breakfast sausage, 2 cups of shredded sharp cheddar and about 1½ to 2 cups of baking mix, rolled into 1-inch balls. Bake at 400°F for 18 to 20 minutes, until they're browned and the sausage is cooked through (160°F).

- **Make ahead:** Freeze them raw on a tray, then bag them. Bake straight from frozen, adding a few minutes.

### 20. Whipped Ricotta Crostini with Hot Honey

Toast thin slices of baguette at 400°F for 8 to 10 minutes. Top them with ricotta whipped with lemon zest and salt, then drizzle with [hot honey](/blog/best-hot-honey/) and add a few pomegranate seeds.

- **Make ahead:** Toast the crostini up to 3 days ahead and store them airtight. Whip the ricotta the day before. Top them just before serving.

<h2 id="dips">Christmas dips and spreads (21–30)</h2>
<p>A dip is the cheapest way to feed a lot of people, and it holds the table together. Put out one cold and one warm.</p>
:::

### 21. Cranberry Salsa over Cream Cheese

In a food processor, pulse 12 oz of fresh cranberries with about ⅓ cup of sugar, a seeded jalapeño, a handful of cilantro, 2 green onions and the juice of a lime. Chill it, then spoon it over a block of cream cheese. Serve with crackers.

- **Make ahead:** Make the salsa a day ahead (it needs at least 4 hours for the sugar to tame the cranberries). Spoon it on just before serving.

![Bright red cranberry salsa with jalapeño and cilantro spooned over a block of cream cheese, surrounded by crackers](/images/christmas-appetizers-cranberry-salsa.jpg)

### 22. Pepper Jelly over Cream Cheese

The 2-minute appetizer: a jar of red or green pepper jelly poured over a block of cream cheese. Serve with butter crackers.

- **Make ahead:** No prep needed. Our picks: [best pepper jelly](/blog/best-pepper-jelly/).

### 23. Hot Spinach Artichoke Dip

Cream cheese, sour cream, Parmesan, mozzarella, thawed chopped spinach and chopped artichoke hearts, baked at 350°F for 20 to 25 minutes until bubbling.

- **Make ahead:** Mix it 2 days ahead and bake on the day. Short on time? Here are the [best store-bought spinach dips](/blog/best-spinach-dip/).

### 24. Hot Crab Dip

Cream cheese, mayonnaise, lump crab, Old Bay-style seasoning, lemon and cheddar, baked at 375°F for 20 to 25 minutes.

- **Make ahead:** Mix it the day before, then bake it as guests arrive.

### 25. Whipped Feta with Pomegranate

Blend 8 oz of feta with 4 oz of cream cheese and a splash of olive oil until smooth. Spread it in a shallow bowl and top with pomegranate seeds, chopped pistachios and a drizzle of honey. Red and green on white.

- **Make ahead:** Whip it 2 days ahead. Add the toppings on the day.

### 26. French Onion Dip

Slowly caramelized onions stirred into sour cream with a little garlic and Worcestershire. Serve with kettle chips.

- **Make ahead:** It's better after a day in the fridge. Store-bought picks: [best French onion dip](/blog/best-french-onion-dip/).

### 27. Beer Cheese

Melt sharp cheddar, cream cheese and a splash of lager with Dijon and garlic powder. Serve it warm with soft pretzel bites.

- **Make ahead:** Make it 2 days ahead and rewarm it gently. Keep it warm in a small slow cooker.

### 28. Pimento Cheese

Sharp cheddar, mayonnaise, diced pimentos and a pinch of cayenne. Serve it with celery, crackers or on the board.

- **Make ahead:** Up to 3 days. Or buy one of our [favorite pimento cheeses](/blog/best-pimento-cheese/).

### 29. Pinecone Cheese Ball

Shape your cheese ball mixture into an oval, then press whole almonds into it in overlapping rows so it looks like a pinecone. Add a rosemary sprig at the top.

- **Make ahead:** Make the cheese ball up to 3 days ahead, and add the almonds on the day. Our [cheese ball recipe](/blog/cheese-ball-recipe/) has the base mixture. No time? See the [best store-bought cheese balls](/blog/best-store-bought-cheese-balls/).

### 30. Christmas Butter Board

Softened butter swirled across a board and topped with honey, cranberries, rosemary, orange zest and flaky salt. Serve it with warm bread.

- **Make ahead:** Bring the butter to room temperature 1 hour ahead and build the board just before serving. The full method: [butter board](/blog/butter-board/).

<h2 id="safety">Keeping Christmas appetizers safe</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks">
<li><strong>2 hours, then refill.</strong> Perishable food shouldn't sit out more than 2 hours (<a href="https://ask.fsis.usda.gov/article/What-is-the-2-Hour-Rule-with-leaving-food-out" target="_blank" rel="noopener">USDA</a>). Put out half and refill from the fridge.</li>
<li><strong>Hot stays hot.</strong> Keep warm dips and meatballs at 140°F or warmer in a slow cooker on "warm" or a 200°F oven. Reheated food should reach 165°F (<a href="https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety" target="_blank" rel="noopener">USDA</a>).</li>
<li><strong>Cold stays cold.</strong> Set the shrimp and creamy dips over a tray of ice.</li>
<li><strong>Pregnant guests.</strong> The CDC advises against cold deli meat and refrigerated smoked salmon unless they're heated until steaming, and recommends pasteurized cheeses (<a href="https://www.cdc.gov/listeria/prevention/index.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">CDC</a>). The warm bites (#11–19) are good options. More: <a href="/blog/pregnancy-safe-charcuterie-board/">pregnancy-safe charcuterie board</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Label the nuts.</strong> The goat cheese snowballs, stuffed dates, pinecone cheese ball and brie bites all contain nuts. A small card helps guests with allergies.</li>
</ul>
:::

<h2 id="faq">FAQ</h2>
:::

**What are the best Christmas appetizers to make ahead?**

Cheese balls, cranberry salsa, pimento cheese, marinated olives and most dips can be made 2 to 3 days ahead. Sausage balls, pigs in a blanket and meatballs freeze for up to a month. Skewers, roll-ups and stuffed dates can be assembled the day before and kept covered in the fridge.

**How many appetizers do I need per person?**

Before a sit-down dinner, plan 4 to 6 pieces per guest from 3 or 4 different appetizers. When appetizers are the whole party, plan 12 to 15 pieces per guest over about 3 hours, from 6 to 8 different appetizers, including at least two that are filling, like sliders or meatballs.

**Which Christmas appetizers can you freeze?**

Unbaked sausage balls, unbaked pigs in a blanket, cooked meatballs, unbaked cranberry brie bites and cheese balls without the nut coating all freeze well for up to a month. Bake sausage balls and pigs in a blanket straight from frozen, adding a few minutes. Dips with sour cream or mayonnaise, cucumber and fresh fruit don't freeze well.

**What are some easy last-minute Christmas appetizers?**

Pepper jelly or cranberry sauce poured over a block of cream cheese with crackers takes 2 minutes. Stuffed dates, salami roll-ups, a caprese wreath and a store-bought shrimp ring all take under 20 minutes with no cooking.

**How long can Christmas appetizers sit out?**

No more than 2 hours at room temperature, according to the USDA. Keep hot appetizers at 140°F or warmer in a slow cooker or warm oven, set cold dips and shrimp over ice, and put out smaller amounts that you refill rather than everything at once.
