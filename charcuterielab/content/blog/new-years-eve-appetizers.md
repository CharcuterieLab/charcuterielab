# 40 New Year's Eve Appetizers (Easy, Fancy + Make-Ahead)

> **Quick Answer:** Mix five kinds: **cold bites** (caviar chips, stuffed Peppadews), **warm bites** you bake from the freezer (Brie bites, gougères), **a dip**, **one showstopper board** and **midnight snacks** held back for 11:30. If appetizers are dinner, plan **15–20 pieces per guest** over the night and serve them in **three waves**.

<h2 id="how-many">How many appetizers to make</h2>
<p class="sk-lede">New Year's Eve is a long night, so it depends on one question: <strong>are appetizers the dinner?</strong></p>
:::

| Your party | Pieces per guest | How many kinds | Include |
|---|---|---|---|
| Drinks and bites before going out (2 hours) | 6 to 8 | 4 or 5 | Cold bites and one dip |
| Appetizers, then a sit-down dinner | 4 to 6 | 3 or 4 | Light, elegant bites |
| Party until midnight, appetizers are dinner | 15 to 20 | 8 to 10 | Two filling ones (sliders, meatballs) and midnight snacks |

**Quick math:** 12 guests × 16 pieces = about 190 pieces. With 8 kinds, that's about 24 of each. Serve them in waves: about **half when guests arrive**, a **second round around 10 pm** and the **midnight snacks at 11:30**. For the cheese and meat on a board, plan **about 3 oz of each per guest** when it has to last until midnight, or let the [Party Planner](/party-planner/) do the math for your guest count.

<p class="sk-lede">No oven needed. Make these while the oven is busy with the warm round.</p>
:::

:::html
<div class="ny-items">
<div class="ny-item" id="ny-1">
<h3><span class="ny-num">1</span> Smoked Salmon Cucumber Bites</h3>
<p class="ny-pills"><span class="ny-tag ny-t-nocook">No-cook</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-gf">Gluten-free</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-fancy">Fancy</span></p>
<p>Top thick slices of English cucumber with herbed cream cheese, a curl of <a href="/blog/best-smoked-salmon/">smoked salmon</a>, a few capers and a sprig of dill.</p>
<p class="ny-ahead"><strong>Make ahead:</strong> Mix the cream cheese the day before. Assemble within 2 hours of serving, or the cucumber weeps.</p>

</div>
<div class="ny-item" id="ny-2">
<h3><span class="ny-num">2</span> Caviar Potato Chip Bites</h3>
<p class="ny-pills"><span class="ny-tag ny-t-nocook">No-cook</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-fancy">Fancy</span></p>
<p>Spoon a dab of crème fraîche onto sturdy <a href="/blog/best-kettle-chips/">kettle chips</a>, add a little caviar or trout roe and snip chives on top. It's the most famous high-low pairing with Champagne.</p>
<p class="ny-ahead"><strong>Make ahead:</strong> Set out the parts and assemble as guests arrive. Chips go soft in about 20 minutes.</p>
<p class="ny-more">Budget to splurge: <a href="/blog/best-caviar/">the best caviar to buy</a>.</p>
</div>
<div class="ny-item" id="ny-3">
<h3><span class="ny-num">3</span> Prosciutto-Wrapped Breadsticks</h3>
<p class="ny-pills"><span class="ny-tag ny-t-nocook">No-cook</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-budget">Budget</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-kid">Kid-friendly</span></p>
<p>Wrap a thin slice of <a href="/ingredients/prosciutto-di-parma/">prosciutto</a> in a spiral around the top half of each <a href="/ingredients/grissini/">grissini</a>. Stand them up in a glass.</p>
<p class="ny-ahead"><strong>Make ahead:</strong> Up to 2 hours ahead. Longer and the breadsticks soften.</p>

</div>
<div class="ny-item" id="ny-4">
<h3><span class="ny-num">4</span> Goat Cheese Stuffed Peppadews</h3>
<p class="ny-pills"><span class="ny-tag ny-t-nocook">No-cook</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-ahead">Make-ahead</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-veg">Vegetarian</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-gf">Gluten-free</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-budget">Budget</span></p>
<p>Pipe or spoon soft <a href="/ingredients/fresh-chevre/">goat cheese</a> into sweet-hot <a href="/ingredients/peppadew/">Peppadew peppers</a>. Finish with chopped chives.</p>
<p class="ny-ahead"><strong>Make ahead:</strong> The day before. Keep covered in the fridge.</p>

</div>
<div class="ny-item" id="ny-5">
<h3><span class="ny-num">5</span> Cheese and Grape Skewers</h3>
<p class="ny-pills"><span class="ny-tag ny-t-nocook">No-cook</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-ahead">Make-ahead</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-gf">Gluten-free</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-kid">Kid-friendly</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-budget">Budget</span></p>
<p>Thread a cube of <a href="/ingredients/manchego/">Manchego</a> or cheddar, a green grape and a folded slice of salami onto a short pick.</p>
<p class="ny-ahead"><strong>Make ahead:</strong> The day before, covered.</p>

</div>
<div class="ny-item" id="ny-6">
<h3><span class="ny-num">6</span> Shrimp Cocktail Cups</h3>
<p class="ny-pills"><span class="ny-tag ny-t-nocook">No-cook</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-gf">Gluten-free</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-fancy">Fancy</span></p>
<p>Spoon cocktail sauce into small clear cups and hang 2 or 3 cooked, chilled shrimp over each rim with a lemon wedge. Easier to eat than a shared ring.</p>
<p class="ny-ahead"><strong>Make ahead:</strong> Make the sauce up to 2 days ahead. Fill the cups within 2 hours and keep them on ice.</p>

</div>
<div class="ny-item" id="ny-7">
<h3><span class="ny-num">7</span> Endive Boats with Blue Cheese and Pear</h3>
<p class="ny-pills"><span class="ny-tag ny-t-nocook">No-cook</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-veg">Vegetarian</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-gf">Gluten-free</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-fancy">Fancy</span></p>
<p>Fill endive leaves with crumbled blue cheese, finely diced pear and toasted walnuts. Drizzle with honey.</p>
<p class="ny-ahead"><strong>Make ahead:</strong> Dice the pear in lemon juice and toast the nuts the day before. Fill within 2 hours.</p>

</div>
<div class="ny-item" id="ny-8">
<h3><span class="ny-num">8</span> Deviled Eggs, Three Ways</h3>
<p class="ny-pills"><span class="ny-tag ny-t-ahead">Make-ahead</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-veg">Vegetarian</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-gf">Gluten-free</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-budget">Budget</span></p>
<p>Make one batch of filling and top a third each with paprika, everything seasoning and a little salmon roe.</p>
<p class="ny-ahead"><strong>Make ahead:</strong> Boil the eggs and make the filling the day before. Pipe the filling on the day.</p>

</div>
<div class="ny-item" id="ny-9">
<h3><span class="ny-num">9</span> Smoked Salmon Blini</h3>
<p class="ny-pills"><span class="ny-tag ny-t-nocook">No-cook</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-fancy">Fancy</span></p>
<p>Top store-bought <a href="/ingredients/blini/">blini</a> with crème fraîche, a fold of smoked salmon and dill. Warm the blini for 5 minutes first if you have room in the oven.</p>
<p class="ny-ahead"><strong>Make ahead:</strong> Assemble within 1 hour of serving.</p>
<p class="ny-more">Want a whole station? See the <a href="/printables/new-years-eve-blini-bar/">New Year's Eve blini bar plan</a>.</p>
</div>
<div class="ny-item" id="ny-10">
<h3><span class="ny-num">10</span> Marinated Feta and Olive Cups</h3>
<p class="ny-pills"><span class="ny-tag ny-t-nocook">No-cook</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-ahead">Make-ahead</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-veg">Vegetarian</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-gf">Gluten-free</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-budget">Budget</span></p>
<p>Toss cubes of <a href="/ingredients/feta/">feta</a> and mixed olives with olive oil, lemon zest, chili flakes and oregano. Serve in small cups with picks.</p>
<p class="ny-ahead"><strong>Make ahead:</strong> Up to 3 days ahead. It gets better as it sits.</p>

</div>

<h2 id="warm">Warm bites (11–20)</h2>
<p class="sk-lede">Most of these freeze, so you can bake them straight from the freezer on the night.</p>
:::

:::html
<div class="ny-items">
<div class="ny-item" id="ny-11">
<h3><span class="ny-num">11</span> Mini Crab Cakes</h3>
<p class="ny-pills"><span class="ny-tag ny-t-freezes">Freezes</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-fancy">Fancy</span></p>
<p>Bind lump crab with a little mayo, mustard, egg and panko, shape into 1½-inch cakes and bake at 425°F for about 12 minutes. Top with a dot of rémoulade.</p>
<p class="ny-ahead"><strong>Make ahead:</strong> Shape and freeze up to a month ahead. Bake from frozen, adding a few minutes.</p>

</div>
<div class="ny-item" id="ny-12">
<h3><span class="ny-num">12</span> Bacon-Wrapped Dates</h3>
<p class="ny-pills"><span class="ny-tag ny-t-ahead">Make-ahead</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-gf">Gluten-free</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-budget">Budget</span></p>
<p>Stuff <a href="/ingredients/medjool-dates/">Medjool dates</a> with a little blue cheese or an almond, wrap in half a slice of bacon and bake at 400°F for 15 to 20 minutes, until crisp.</p>
<p class="ny-ahead"><strong>Make ahead:</strong> Stuff and wrap the day before. Bake on the night.</p>

</div>
<div class="ny-item" id="ny-13">
<h3><span class="ny-num">13</span> Brie Bites with Fig Jam</h3>
<p class="ny-pills"><span class="ny-tag ny-t-freezes">Freezes</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-veg">Vegetarian</span></p>
<p>Press squares of puff pastry into a mini muffin tin, add a cube of Brie and a little <a href="/ingredients/fig-jam/">fig jam</a>, and bake at 375°F for 15 minutes.</p>
<p class="ny-ahead"><strong>Make ahead:</strong> Freeze unbaked in the tin, then pop them out into a bag. Bake from frozen.</p>

</div>
<div class="ny-item" id="ny-14">
<h3><span class="ny-num">14</span> Gougères (Cheese Puffs)</h3>
<p class="ny-pills"><span class="ny-tag ny-t-freezes">Freezes</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-veg">Vegetarian</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-fancy">Fancy</span></p>
<p>Little French choux puffs made with Gruyère. They're the classic Champagne bite in Burgundy, where they started.</p>
<p class="ny-ahead"><strong>Make ahead:</strong> Pipe and freeze unbaked for up to a month, or bake and reheat for 5 minutes.</p>
<p class="ny-more">Or buy them frozen: see <a href="/ingredients/gougeres/">gougères</a>.</p>
</div>
<div class="ny-item" id="ny-15">
<h3><span class="ny-num">15</span> Broiled Oysters with Garlic-Parmesan Butter</h3>
<p class="ny-pills"><span class="ny-tag ny-t-gf">Gluten-free</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-fancy">Fancy</span></p>
<p>Top oysters on the half shell with garlic butter and Parmesan, and broil about 3 inches from the heat until the edges curl and the butter bubbles, at least 3 minutes. Cooking them is the CDC's advice for oysters.</p>
<p class="ny-ahead"><strong>Make ahead:</strong> Make the butter up to 3 days ahead. Have the oysters shucked the day you buy them and keep them on ice.</p>

</div>
<div class="ny-item" id="ny-16">
<h3><span class="ny-num">16</span> Sausage-Stuffed Mushrooms</h3>
<p class="ny-pills"><span class="ny-tag ny-t-ahead">Make-ahead</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-gf">Gluten-free</span></p>
<p>Fill cremini caps with cooked Italian sausage, cream cheese and Parmesan. Bake at 375°F for 20 minutes.</p>
<p class="ny-ahead"><strong>Make ahead:</strong> Fill the day before and refrigerate. Bake on the night.</p>

</div>
<div class="ny-item" id="ny-17">
<h3><span class="ny-num">17</span> Cocktail Meatballs</h3>
<p class="ny-pills"><span class="ny-tag ny-t-freezes">Freezes</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-budget">Budget</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-kid">Kid-friendly</span></p>
<p>Simmer frozen meatballs in grape jelly and chili sauce, or a Swedish-style cream gravy, in a slow cooker on low. Serve with picks.</p>
<p class="ny-ahead"><strong>Make ahead:</strong> Cooked meatballs freeze for a month. Keep them warm on low all night.</p>

</div>
<div class="ny-item" id="ny-18">
<h3><span class="ny-num">18</span> Everything Pigs in a Blanket</h3>
<p class="ny-pills"><span class="ny-tag ny-t-freezes">Freezes</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-budget">Budget</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-kid">Kid-friendly</span></p>
<p>Wrap cocktail sausages in strips of crescent dough, brush with egg and sprinkle with everything bagel seasoning. Bake at 375°F for 12 to 15 minutes. Serve with mustard.</p>
<p class="ny-ahead"><strong>Make ahead:</strong> Freeze unbaked on a tray, then bag them. Bake from frozen.</p>

</div>
<div class="ny-item" id="ny-19">
<h3><span class="ny-num">19</span> Spinach and Gruyère Mini Quiches</h3>
<p class="ny-pills"><span class="ny-tag ny-t-freezes">Freezes</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-veg">Vegetarian</span></p>
<p>Fill mini tart shells with egg, cream, wilted spinach and Gruyère. Bake at 350°F for about 20 minutes.</p>
<p class="ny-ahead"><strong>Make ahead:</strong> Bake, cool and freeze. Reheat at 350°F for 8 to 10 minutes.</p>

</div>
<div class="ny-item" id="ny-20">
<h3><span class="ny-num">20</span> Beef Tenderloin Crostini</h3>
<p class="ny-pills"><span class="ny-tag ny-t-ahead">Make-ahead</span><span class="ny-tag ny-t-fancy">Fancy</span></p>
<p>Toast baguette slices, spread with horseradish cream and top with thin slices of rare roast beef tenderloin and arugula.</p>
<p class="ny-ahead"><strong>Make ahead:</strong> Roast the beef and toast the crostini the day before. Slice the beef cold and assemble within 2 hours.</p>

</div>
</div>

<h2 id="safety">Keeping it safe on a long night</h2>
:::

- **The 2-hour rule:** perishable food shouldn't sit out longer than 2 hours, according to the USDA. A party until midnight is longer than that, so put out smaller platters and replace them rather than refilling the same one.
- **Hot food at 140°F or above:** use a slow cooker on low or a warm oven for meatballs and dips.
- **Cold food over ice:** shrimp, crab, smoked salmon and creamy dips go on a tray of crushed ice.
- **Oysters:** the CDC advises against eating raw or undercooked oysters, so this list cooks them (#15).
- **Pregnant guests:** skip cold-smoked salmon and anything with raw fish or unpasteurized soft cheese. See [which cheeses are safe during pregnancy](/blog/cheese-safe-during-pregnancy/).
- **Driving home:** keep sparkling cider and a zero-proof option on the table all night. See [zero-proof pairings](/pairings/zero-proof/).

<h2 id="faq">FAQ</h2>
:::

**How many appetizers do I need for a New Year's Eve party?**

If appetizers are dinner and the party runs to midnight, plan 15 to 20 pieces per guest over the night, from 8 to 10 different appetizers, including at least two filling ones like sliders or meatballs. For drinks before going out, 6 to 8 pieces per guest is enough. Serve them in waves rather than all at once.

**What are the best make-ahead New Year's Eve appetizers?**

Dips like French onion, whipped feta and black-eyed pea dip can be made 1 to 2 days ahead. Brie bites, gougères, pigs in a blanket, crab cakes, mini quiches and meatballs freeze for up to a month and bake from frozen. Skewers, stuffed Peppadews and deviled egg filling can be made the day before.

**What appetizers go best with Champagne?**

Salty, fatty and fried foods. The acidity and bubbles cut through richness, so potato chips with crème fraîche and caviar, gougères, Parmigiano-Reggiano, prosciutto, fried bites and buttery cheeses all taste better with Champagne. Very sweet desserts make dry Champagne taste sour.

**What foods are lucky to eat on New Year's Eve?**

Twelve grapes at midnight in Spain, black-eyed peas and greens in the American South, lentils with sausage in Italy, pickled herring in Germany, Poland and Scandinavia, and pork in many places because pigs root forward. All of them are easy to turn into appetizers.

**What can I serve at midnight on New Year's Eve?**

Something warm and salty, like ham and Swiss sliders, mini grilled cheese with tomato soup or sheet-pan nachos, plus a sweet bite like chocolate-dipped strawberries. Put them out around 11:30 so they're ready for the countdown.

**How long can appetizers sit out at a New Year's Eve party?**

No more than 2 hours at room temperature, according to the USDA. A party that runs until midnight is longer than that, so put out smaller platters and replace them, keep hot food at 140°F or above, and keep seafood and dips over ice.
