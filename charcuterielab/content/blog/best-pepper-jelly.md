## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** Real peppers near the top of the list and a clear heat level. Some are mostly apple juice and sugar with little pepper flavor.

**Skip:** Pepper jellies that taste like plain fruit jelly. If you can't find the pepper, it isn't doing its job.

## Pepper jelly questions

**What is the best pepper jelly?**

Braswell's Jalapeño Pepper Jelly, Daily Meal's top pick, which is also inexpensive and widely stocked.

**What do you serve pepper jelly with?**

Spoon it over a block of cream cheese, or pair it with goat cheese, sharp cheddar and crackers.

**Is pepper jelly spicy?**

Usually mild to medium. Habanero versions are much hotter.
