<h2 id="what-to-look-for">What to look for</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Look for:</strong> A block that looks moist and pale yellow, with small irregular holes. &quot;Cream Havarti&quot; or &quot;creamy Havarti&quot; is softer and milder than plain.</li><li class="sk-x"><strong>Skip:</strong> Pre-sliced packs for the board itself. Thin slices dry at the edges within an hour. Buy a block, cut it into thick slices or cubes, and keep the deli slices for sandwiches.</li></ul>
:::

- **How much to buy:** about 1 oz of Havarti per guest when it's one of three cheeses.
- **How to serve it:** cut it cold, since it's soft, then let it sit out 20 to 30 minutes. It pairs well with apples, grapes, rye crackers, ham and a dry white wine.

## How to choose

- **For a classic board:** plain Havarti (Buholzer Brothers or Castello) as the mild cheese, next to an aged cheddar and a blue or a brie.
- **For a second cheese with flavor:** dill Havarti. Caraway and jalapeño versions are common too.
- **For a slider or sandwich board:** sliced Havarti from the deli counter.

Havarti is aged about three months, so it stays mild. An aged Havarti (sometimes labeled "aged" or "extra aged") is firmer and nuttier, with a hint of hazelnut ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Havarti)).

<h2 id="faq">Havarti questions</h2>
:::

**What is the best Havarti cheese brand?**

Buholzer Brothers Havarti from Klondike Cheese Co. took first and second place in the Havarti class at the 2025 US Championship Cheese Contest. Castello Creamy Havarti is the easiest to find.

**Is Havarti good on a charcuterie board?**

Yes. It's mild and buttery, so it balances strong cheeses like aged cheddar and blue, and almost every guest will eat it.

**What is the difference between Havarti and creamy Havarti?**

Creamy Havarti is made so more whey stays in the curd, which makes it softer and milder. Plain Havarti is a little firmer and more flavorful.

**Is Havarti Danish?**

It was first made in Denmark, but most Havarti sold in the US is made in Wisconsin.
