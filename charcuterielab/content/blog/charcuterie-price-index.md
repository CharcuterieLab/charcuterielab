<h2 id="price-index-questions">Price index questions</h2>
:::

**Which store has the cheapest charcuterie board?**

Aldi. The same 9-item board for 10 guests cost $33.90 at Aldi, $42.52 at Walmart, $44.29 at Kroger and $50.20 at Trader Joe's in October 2026, at regular prices.

**How much does a charcuterie board for 10 cost?**

About $33.90 to $50.20 for a simple board before a meal with two cheeses, two meats, crackers, grapes, almonds, olives and fig spread. That's $3.39 to $5.02 a guest. If the board is the meal, plan about 1½ times that.

**Is it worth shopping at two stores?**

Usually not. Buying every item wherever it was cheapest came to $30.58, only $3.32 less than doing it all at Aldi.

**Is Trader Joe's expensive for a charcuterie board?**

It was the most expensive of the four for this basket, mostly because of meat ($10.98 for salami), a $7.99 cheddar and $6.99 grapes. Its fig butter, kalamata olives and crackers were among the cheapest.
