<h2 id="price-index-questions">Price index questions</h2>
:::

**Which store has the cheapest charcuterie board?**

Aldi, at $33.90 for a board for 10 guests. Walmart was $42.52, Kroger $44.29 and Trader Joe's $50.20, at regular prices in October 2026.

**How much does a charcuterie board for 10 cost?**

About $33.90 to $50.20 for a simple board before a meal, or $3.39 to $5.02 a guest. If the board is the meal, plan about 1½ times that.

**Is it worth shopping at two stores?**

Usually not. Buying every item wherever it was cheapest came to $30.58, only $3.32 less than doing it all at Aldi.

**Is Trader Joe's expensive for a charcuterie board?**

It was the most expensive of the four, mostly because of the meat ($10.98 for salami), a $7.99 cheddar and $6.99 grapes. Its fig butter, olives and crackers were among the cheapest.
