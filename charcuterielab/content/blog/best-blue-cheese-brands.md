<h2 id="faq">Blue cheese questions</h2>
:::

**What is the best blue cheese for a charcuterie board?**

Point Reyes Original Blue or Kingston Creamery Breezy Blue. For beginners, BelGioioso Gorgonzola is gentler.

**What goes with blue cheese?**

Honey, pears, figs, walnuts and a sweet wine like port.

**Should I serve more than one blue?**

Usually one is enough. Put it at the edge of the board with its own knife.
