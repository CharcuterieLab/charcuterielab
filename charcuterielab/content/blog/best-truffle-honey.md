<h2 id="how-to-choose">Truffle honey on a cheese board</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Drizzle it on salty, aged cheese:</strong> Parmigiano-Reggiano, aged pecorino, aged Gouda and Manchego.</li><li><strong>Or on soft, mild cheese:</strong> warm brie, burrata, ricotta and goat cheese, where the truffle aroma has room to show.</li><li><strong>Use a little:</strong> a thin drizzle per wedge. ## Truffle honey questions

**What is the best truffle honey?**

Sabatino for a jar you can find at the supermarket, and Urbani for a gift from an Italian truffle house.

**Is truffle honey made with real truffles?**

Usually with some real truffle plus truffle aroma or flavor. Check the label; TruffleHunter lists 1% truffle shavings.

**What cheese goes with truffle honey?**

Aged Parmigiano-Reggiano, pecorino, aged Gouda and Manchego, or soft brie, burrata, ricotta and goat cheese.

**How do you serve truffle honey?**

In a small bowl with a honey dipper, drizzled thinly over each piece of cheese.

**Does truffle honey go bad?**

Honey keeps for a long time, but the truffle aroma fades. Use it within a few months of opening and keep the lid tight.
