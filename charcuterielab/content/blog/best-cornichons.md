<h2 id="how-to-choose">How to choose</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Want the classic French bite?</strong> Look for vinegar, onions and tarragon or mustard seed, and no sugar, in the ingredients.</li><li><strong>Check "Product of".</strong> Several French-style jars are packed in India or Turkey. That isn't bad, just know what you're buying.</li><li><strong>Skip "piquant" or sweet jars</strong> if you want sour. Sharp and sour is what cuts through pâté and rich cheese.</li><li><strong>How many?</strong> About 3 to 4 per guest on a board. A 12 to 14 oz jar covers 10 to 12 people.</li></ul><p class="sk-note">On the board: put them in a small bowl next to the pâté, salami or a creamy cheese, and pull them out of the brine with a fork. <a href="/ingredients/cornichons/">More on cornichons</a></p>
:::

## Cornichon questions

**Are cornichons the same as gherkins?**

Mostly. Every cornichon is a gherkin, but not every gherkin is a cornichon. A cornichon is picked when it's tiny and is usually tart and seasoned with tarragon and pepper. American gherkins tend to be sweeter.

**Where are cornichons in the grocery store?**

Usually next to the other pickles or in the international aisle. If your store doesn't have them, try the deli section, a specialty grocer or Amazon.

**Do cornichons need to be refrigerated after opening?**

Yes. Keep the jar in the fridge with the cornichons under the brine and the lid tight. They keep for one to three months. Use a fork, not fingers, and throw them out if the brine looks cloudy or moldy.

**Do cornichons have added sugar?**

It depends on the brand. Maille lists no sugar, and the 365 jar shows 0 g. Hengstenberg lists sugar, and Trader Joe's tastes sweet. Read the ingredients if you want the classic sour style.

**How many cornichons per person on a charcuterie board?**

Plan on 3 to 4 per guest. A 12 to 14 oz jar is enough for a board for 10 to 12 people.
