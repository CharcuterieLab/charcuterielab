### 1. Heluva Good! French Onion Dip

**★★★★½ 4.7 out of 5** · Best overall · Best for: The classic chip dip · $ budget

The most consistent winner. [Chowhound](https://www.chowhound.com/1991772/store-bought-french-onion-dips-ranked-worst-best) ranked it first of seven for its punchy onion flavor and "Goldilocks" onion pieces. [Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/best-french-onion-dip/) scored it 9.5 out of 10 as the best thick dip, and [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2203091/store-bought-french-onion-dips/) placed it 2nd of six.

- **Why we like it:** Bold onion flavor, everywhere, cheap.
- **Keep in mind:** Contains gelatin and MSG, and it's saltier than most.
- **Where to buy:** Nearly every grocery store.

### 2. Daisy Sour Cream French Onion Dip

[Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/best-french-onion-dip/) liked its short ingredient list but missed a cooked-onion taste.

## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** Sour cream or cultured cream should be the first ingredient. Gelatin, modified starch and oil further down are what make a dip jiggle or taste greasy.

**Skip:** Great Value French Onion Dip. [Chowhound](https://www.chowhound.com/1991772/store-bought-french-onion-dips-ranked-worst-best) called it runny, with an "oddly sour and fake-tasting aftertaste."

**Serving it:** dairy dips should stay cold until guests arrive and come off the table after 2 hours. Put the dip in a bowl, not the tub, with ridged chips, pretzels, carrots and celery around it. For a quick upgrade, stir a spoonful of caramelized onions into any store-bought tub.
