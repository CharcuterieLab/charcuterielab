<h2 id="what-to-look-for">What to look for</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Look for:</strong> Sour cream or cultured cream should be the first ingredient. Gelatin, modified starch and oil further down are what make a dip jiggle or taste greasy.</li><li class="sk-x"><strong>Skip:</strong> Great Value French Onion Dip. <a href="https://www.chowhound.com/1991772/store-bought-french-onion-dips-ranked-worst-best" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Chowhound</a> called it runny, with an &quot;oddly sour and fake-tasting aftertaste.&quot;</li></ul>
:::

**Serving it:** dairy dips should stay cold until guests arrive and come off the table after 2 hours. Put the dip in a bowl, not the tub, with ridged chips, pretzels, carrots and celery around it. For a quick upgrade, stir a spoonful of caramelized onions into any store-bought tub.

Heluva Good! It ranked first at Chowhound, 9.5 out of 10 at Sporked and 2nd at Tasting Table. Daisy won Tasting Table's test.

Daisy Sour Cream French Onion Dip, made with cultured cream and a short ingredient list.

**How long can French onion dip sit out?**
