## How we rank

Every product gets a score from 1 to 5 on five things, weighted into one overall score out of 5:

| What we score | Weight | What earns a 5 |
|---|---|---|
| How it works on a board | 30% | Sturdy, the right size and texture, and it lets the rest of the board shine |
| Published taste tests and awards | 25% | Ranked at or near the top by reputable taste tests, or wins major food awards |
| Ingredients | 15% | A short list led by the real ingredient, with no fillers |
| Value | 15% | A low price for what you get |
| Easy to find | 15% | Sold in most grocery stores |

**Researched, not taste-tested (yet).** These rankings come from published taste tests (from outlets like Tasting Table, Sporked, Daily Meal and The Kitchn), award results, ingredient labels and prices. We have not tasted every product side by side. When we taste-test a category ourselves, that guide will say Tested. Each guide lists its sources.

**Updated regularly.** Products change recipes, prices move and stores change what they stock. Each guide shows when it was last checked.

**Independent.** No brand paid to be included or for its position.
