<h2 id="what-to-look-for">What to look for</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Look for:</strong> An age statement in months. Anything over 12 months will have crystals and a butterscotch flavor. Wax coatings are not eaten.</li><li class="sk-x"><strong>Skip:</strong> Thin pre-sliced Gouda for a board. It turns plasticky. Cut an aged Gouda into shards instead.</li></ul>

<h2 id="faq">Gouda questions</h2>
:::

**What is the best Gouda for a charcuterie board?**

An aged Gouda with crystals. Rembrandt 12 Month topped Tasting Table's test; Trader Joe's 1000 Day is the best value.

**What are the crunchy bits in aged Gouda?**

Protein crystals that form as the cheese ages. They are a good sign.

**What goes with aged Gouda?**

Apples, pears, dark chocolate, walnuts and a malty beer or a Merlot.
