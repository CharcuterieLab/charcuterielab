# The Best Gouda Brands for a Charcuterie Board, Ranked

> **Quick Answer:** Our top pick for Gouda on a charcuterie board is Trader Joe's 1000 Day Gouda (4.7 out of 5). Best value: Trader Joe's Goat Milk Gouda. Splurge: Beemster XO. For a board, aged Gouda is the prize: deep caramel flavor and crunchy crystals. Young Gouda is milder and softer. These brands cover both.

### 1. Trader Joe's 1000 Day Gouda

**★★★★½ 4.7 out of 5** · Best overall · Best for: Very aged, low price · $ budget

Aged about 2.7 years. [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/1763522/store-bought-gouda-cheese-ranked/) ranked it second for its nutty, mushroomy notes, subtle sweetness and crystals.

## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** An age statement in months. Anything over 12 months will have crystals and a butterscotch flavor. Wax coatings are not eaten.

**Skip:** Thin pre-sliced Gouda for a board. It turns plasticky. Cut an aged Gouda into shards instead.

## Gouda questions

**What is the best Gouda for a charcuterie board?**

An aged Gouda with crystals. Rembrandt 12 Month topped Tasting Table's test; Trader Joe's 1000 Day is the best value.

**What are the crunchy bits in aged Gouda?**

Protein crystals that form as the cheese ages. They are a good sign.

**What goes with aged Gouda?**

Apples, pears, dark chocolate, walnuts and a malty beer or a Merlot.
