# The Best Gruyère Brands for a Charcuterie Board, Ranked

Gruyère is the nutty, crystal-flecked alpine cheese that makes a board taste expensive. The catch is the label: in the US, "gruyere" can mean a 12-month Swiss wheel or a 4-month domestic block. These are the ones that come out best in published tastings, ranked for a cheese board.

### 1. Emmi Kaltbach Le Gruyère AOP

**★★★★½ 4.4 out of 5** · Best overall · Best for: The showpiece cheese · $$$ premium

Aged more than 12 months in sandstone caves. Tasters in [America's Test Kitchen](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/cookscountry/taste_tests/1791-gruyere-cheese)'s Gruyère test called it funky, fruity and nutty, and one said it "would be a showstopper on a cheese plate."

- **Why we like it:** Deep, aged flavor with crunchy crystals. Made for eating, not just melting.
- **Keep in mind:** The priciest here, and the brown cave rind is not for eating.
- **Where to buy:** Whole Foods, Kroger and other grocery cheese cases, cheese counters.

### 2. Emmi Le Gruyère AOP

**★★★★½ 4.3 out of 5** · Best value · Best for: An easy, mild Gruyère · $$ mid-range

A young Swiss import aged 5 to 6 months that [America's Test Kitchen](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/cookscountry/taste_tests/1791-gruyere-cheese) recommended and one taster called "Gruyère for beginners": buttery and nutty, softer than the older wheels. Walmart shoppers rate it 4.6 out of 5 across more than 1,700 ratings. (Source: [Walmart](https://www.walmart.com/reviews/product/37560425).)

- **Why we like it:** Real AOP Gruyère in most supermarkets, in a 6 oz piece.
- **Keep in mind:** Milder and softer than an aged Gruyère, with fewer crystals.
- **Where to buy:** Walmart, Kroger, Target and most grocery cheese cases.

### 3. 1655 Le Gruyère AOP

**★★★★☆ 4.2 out of 5** · Best for: The taste-test winner · $$ mid-range

The winner of [America's Test Kitchen](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/cookscountry/taste_tests/1791-gruyere-cheese)'s Gruyère test. Aged 12 to 14 months, it had the most fat and one of the lowest moisture levels, with an "excellent crystalline structure" and a "deeply aged, caramel-like" flavor. [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/1907808/grocery-store-cheese-expensive) also points to it as the Gruyère to buy.

- **Why we like it:** The top score in the only big published Gruyère test.
- **Keep in mind:** Harder to find than the Emmi cheeses, and the test is from 2017.
- **Where to buy:** Cheese counters and specialty grocers.

### 4. Mifroma Le Gruyère Cavern AOP

**★★★★☆ 4.0 out of 5** · Best for: Nutty and complex · $$ mid-range

Cave-aged 9 to 11 months. [America's Test Kitchen](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/cookscountry/taste_tests/1791-gruyere-cheese)'s tasters found it nutty and complex, with a fruity tang and a savory onion note. One counted "five visible tyrosine crystals in a single piece."

- **Why we like it:** Plenty of crystals and a layered flavor.
- **Keep in mind:** Less widely stocked than Emmi.
- **Where to buy:** Cheese counters and some grocery cheese cases.

### 5. Emmi Roth Grand Cru Surchoix

**★★★★☆ 4.0 out of 5** · Best pasteurized · Best for: A pasteurized Gruyère · $$ mid-range

A Wisconsin alpine-style cheese made with pasteurized milk and aged at least 9 months. [America's Test Kitchen](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/cookscountry/taste_tests/1791-gruyere-cheese)'s tasters noted caramelized sweetness, mushroom and onion notes, and a buttery texture with crunchy crystals.

- **Why we like it:** The best choice when a guest needs pasteurized cheese.
- **Keep in mind:** Not Swiss AOP Gruyère, and a little less complex.
- **Where to buy:** Most grocery cheese cases and deli counters.

### 6. Boar's Head Blanc Grue Gruyère

**★★★☆☆ 3.2 out of 5** · Best for: Sandwiches more than boards · $$ mid-range

A domestic, pasteurized Gruyère-style cheese with no set aging time. [America's Test Kitchen](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/cookscountry/taste_tests/1791-gruyere-cheese) recommended it with reservations ("not offensive but no sparkle"), and [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/1899821/boars-head-cheeses-ranked-best-worst/) ranked it 8th of 11 Boar's Head cheeses, between a good Swiss and provolone.

- **Why we like it:** Sliced to order at the deli counter.
- **Keep in mind:** Mild and drier than the Swiss cheeses. Not AOP.
- **Where to buy:** Boar's Head deli counters.

## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** Look for "Le Gruyère AOP" and Swiss origin. A 2023 US court ruling lets American cheesemakers call any cheese "gruyere", so the AOP mark is how you know you're getting the real Swiss cheese. (Source: [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/1564501/best-affordable-cheese-tastes-expensive).) An age statement of 10 months or more means more flavor and more crystals.

**Skip:** Pre-shredded Gruyère for a board. It's coated in anti-caking starch and dries out fast. Buy a wedge.

**Serving it:** take Gruyère out of the fridge 30 to 45 minutes before serving. Cut it into thin batons or shards rather than cubes, and plan on about 1 oz per person when it's one of three cheeses.

## Gruyère questions

**What is the best Gruyère to buy at the grocery store?**

Emmi Kaltbach Le Gruyère AOP for a board, for its long cave aging and deep flavor. 1655 Le Gruyère AOP won America's Test Kitchen's taste test, and Emmi Le Gruyère AOP is the easiest real Swiss Gruyère to find.

**Is American gruyere the same as Swiss Gruyère?**

No. Swiss Le Gruyère AOP has strict rules on where and how it's made and aged. American cheese labeled gruyere does not have to follow them, so look for the AOP mark.

**Is Gruyère pasteurized?**

Swiss Le Gruyère AOP is made from raw milk. If a guest needs pasteurized cheese, Emmi Roth Grand Cru Surchoix is made with pasteurized milk.

**How long before serving should Gruyère come out of the fridge?**

30 to 45 minutes.
