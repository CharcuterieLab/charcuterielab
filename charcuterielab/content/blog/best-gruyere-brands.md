<h2 id="what-to-look-for">What to look for</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Look for:</strong> Look for &quot;Le Gruyère AOP&quot; and Swiss origin. A 2023 US court ruling lets American cheesemakers call any cheese &quot;gruyere&quot;, so the AOP mark is how you know you&#x27;re getting the real Swiss cheese. (Source: <a href="https://www.tastingtable.com/1564501/best-affordable-cheese-tastes-expensive" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Tasting Table</a>.) An age statement of 10 months or more means more flavor and more crystals.</li><li class="sk-x"><strong>Skip:</strong> Pre-shredded Gruyère for a board. It&#x27;s coated in anti-caking starch and dries out fast. Buy a wedge.</li></ul>
:::

**Serving it:** take Gruyère out of the fridge 30 to 45 minutes before serving. Cut it into thin batons or shards rather than cubes, and plan on about 1 oz per person when it's one of three cheeses.

<h2 id="faq">Gruyère questions</h2>
:::

**What is the best Gruyère to buy at the grocery store?**

Emmi Kaltbach Le Gruyère AOP for a board, for its long cave aging and deep flavor. 1655 Le Gruyère AOP won America's Test Kitchen's taste test, and Emmi Le Gruyère AOP is the easiest real Swiss Gruyère to find.

**Is American gruyere the same as Swiss Gruyère?**

No. Swiss Le Gruyère AOP has strict rules on where and how it's made and aged. American cheese labeled gruyere does not have to follow them, so look for the AOP mark.

**Is Gruyère pasteurized?**

Swiss Le Gruyère AOP is made from raw milk. If a guest needs pasteurized cheese, Emmi Roth Grand Cru Surchoix is made with pasteurized milk.

**How long before serving should Gruyère come out of the fridge?**

30 to 45 minutes.
