## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** Cheese and milk near the top of the list, and visible peppers. Jarred quesos are shelf-stable and thicker. Refrigerated deli quesos usually taste fresher.

**Skip:** Leaving queso out to cool on the board. It thickens and skins over. Keep it warm in a mini slow cooker on low and stir it now and then.

**Serving it:** heat queso until it's steaming, then keep it warm in a small slow cooker or a bowl you refill from the stove. Hot food should stay hot (140°F or above), or come off the table after 2 hours. Serve it with tortilla chips, pretzels and roasted peppers.

## Queso questions

**What is the best store-bought queso?**

Marketside Queso Blanco from Walmart's deli, which Sporked scored 10 out of 10. For a jar, On the Border Salsa Con Queso.

**What is the best jarred queso?**

On the Border Salsa Con Queso, a longtime Sporked favorite. Aldi's Casa Mamita tastes almost the same for less.

**How do you keep queso warm on a board?**

Use a mini slow cooker on low, or heat small batches and refill the bowl.

**How long can queso sit out?**

Keep it hot, at 140°F or above, or take it off the table after 2 hours.
