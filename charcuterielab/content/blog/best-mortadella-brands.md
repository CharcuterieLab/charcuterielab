### 1. Columbus Mortadella

**★★★★½ 4.5 out of 5** · Best overall · Best for: The classic deli mortadella · $ budget

[Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/best-mortadella/) named it the best budget mortadella with 9 out of 10: heavier on nutmeg and baking spices, with squares of fat, a "pure, classic deli meat". Columbus is also on [Tasting Table](https://tastingtable.com/2137403/deli-meat-brands-buy-avoid)'s short list of deli meat brands to buy.

- **Why we like it:** Great flavor at a low price, and you can have it sliced thin to order.
- **Keep in mind:** The texture isn't completely smooth.
- **Where to buy:** Deli counters and Costco (sold by the pound).

### 2. Fra' Mani Mortadella

**★★★★☆ 4.2 out of 5** · Best pre-sliced · Best for: A pre-sliced pack · $$ mid-range

Made in the San Francisco Bay Area from pork shoulder, leg and belly, slow-roasted and finished with a little fruitwood smoke. It was a Good Food Awards finalist. (Source: [Zingerman's Deli](https://www.zingermansdeli.com/2024/01/mortadella/).) Costco has carried a 16 oz sliced pack. (Source: [Warehouse Runner](https://app.warehouserunner.com/costco/1697729-fra-mani-sliced-mortadella-16-ounces).)

- **Why we like it:** Craft mortadella that's already sliced.
- **Keep in mind:** Sold sliced at Costco and specialty shops more than at regular grocers.
- **Where to buy:** Costco and specialty grocers.

### 3. Mastro San Daniele Style Mortadella

**★★★★☆ 4.1 out of 5** · Best for: Big, peppery slices · $$ mid-range

[Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/best-mortadella/)'s best of the best, with 10 out of 10: very large slices with whole peppercorns, each one "savory, meaty, piquant, and fatty."

- **Why we like it:** The top score in Sporked's test.
- **Keep in mind:** Easiest to find in Canada. In the US, look in Italian delis.
- **Where to buy:** Italian delis and specialty stores.

### 4. Veroni Italian Mortadella with Pistachio

**★★★½☆ 3.6 out of 5** · Best for: Grab-and-go pistachio mortadella · $$ mid-range

An imported Italian mortadella with pistachios in a 4 oz pre-sliced pack. [Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/best-mortadella/) tried Veroni but didn't score it. (Source: [Kroger](https://www.kroger.com/p/veroni-italian-mortadella-with-pistachio-nuts/0085408600720).)

- **Why we like it:** Imported, with pistachios, and easy to find in the packaged deli case.
- **Keep in mind:** Pricey per ounce, and 4 oz only covers a small board.
- **Where to buy:** Kroger, Stop & Shop, ShopRite and other grocers.

### 5. Ferrarini Truffle Mortadella

**★★★½☆ 3.5 out of 5** · Best splurge · Best for: A special-occasion board · $$$ premium

Studded with slivers of black truffle. [Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/best-mortadella/) gave it 9 out of 10, called it "special occasion mortadella" and said the truffle complements the fatty meat without overwhelming it.

- **Why we like it:** A real showpiece for a luxury board.
- **Keep in mind:** Hard to find and expensive.
- **Where to buy:** Italian delis and specialty shops.

### 6. Olympia Provisions Mortadella

**★★★½☆ 3.5 out of 5** · Best for: An American craft mortadella · $$$ premium

From the Portland salumeria that [Tasting Table](https://tastingtable.com/2137403/deli-meat-brands-buy-avoid) lists as a deli meat brand to buy, with a range "from salami cotto to mortadella."

- **Why we like it:** Careful craft production. A pistachio version is available.
- **Keep in mind:** Mostly sold whole or online, at a premium.
- **Where to buy:** Olympia Provisions online, Murray's and specialty shops.

## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** Look for "Mortadella Bologna IGP" on imported mortadella, and visible white cubes of fat in a fine pink paste. The list should start with pork. Pistachios are traditional but optional.

**Skip:** Bologna as a stand-in. It comes from the same family of sausages, but it's finely blended with no fat cubes, and it reads as lunch meat on a board.

**Serving it:** have it sliced very thin, then fold each slice into quarters or ruffle it into a loose rose. Take it out of the fridge about 20 minutes before serving. Meat in total: 2 oz per guest before a meal, 3 oz when the board is the party food, 4 oz when it's dinner. For pregnant guests, the CDC advises heating deli meats until steaming hot, so set some aside for them.

## Mortadella questions

**What is the best mortadella brand?**

Columbus for most boards: Sporked gave it 9 out of 10, and it's sold at deli counters and Costco. Mastro scored a perfect 10 but is hard to find in the US.

**Is mortadella the same as bologna?**

No. Bologna descends from mortadella, but mortadella has visible cubes of fat and often pistachios, and a silkier texture.

**How do you put mortadella on a charcuterie board?**

Have it sliced very thin, then fold slices into quarters or ruffle them into roses. Thick slices are chewy.

**How much mortadella per person?**

Meat in total: 2 oz per guest before a meal, 3 oz when the board is the party food, 4 oz when it's dinner. Mortadella is usually one of two or three meats in that total.
