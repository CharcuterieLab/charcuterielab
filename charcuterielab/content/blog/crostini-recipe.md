# How to Make Crostini: Crisp Baguette Toasts in 10 Minutes

> **Quick Answer:** Slice a baguette **1/4 to 1/2 inch thick**, brush with **olive oil**, sprinkle with salt and bake at **400°F for 8 to 10 minutes** until golden. One baguette makes **30 to 40** crostini. They keep **3 days** in an airtight bag.

Crostini are the sturdiest thing you can put on a board: they hold soft cheese, whipped dips and heavy toppings without snapping. Want to know more about what they are? See [crostini](/ingredients/crostini/) in the ingredient library.

## Easy oven crostini

**Makes:** 30 to 40 · **Serves:** 10 to 12 on a board · **Prep:** 5 minutes · **Bake:** 8 to 10 minutes

**Ingredients**
- 1 [baguette](/ingredients/baguette/) (about 20 to 24 inches)
- 1/4 to 1/3 cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 1/2 teaspoon fine salt or flaky sea salt
- 1 garlic clove, halved (optional)

**Steps**
1. Heat the oven to 400°F and set a rack in the middle.
2. Slice the baguette 1/4 to 1/2 inch thick on a slight angle.
3. Lay the slices on a baking sheet in one layer, brush both sides lightly with olive oil and sprinkle with salt.
4. Bake for 8 to 10 minutes, until golden at the edges and crisp, flipping halfway if you want both sides browned.
5. For garlic crostini, rub the cut side of a garlic clove over the warm toasts. Cool completely before storing.

**Three tips that make them better**
- **Use a day-old baguette.** Slightly stale bread slices cleanly and crisps faster. A serrated bread knife is a must.
- **Brush, don't drizzle.** Drizzled oil pools and leaves soggy spots. A pastry brush gives an even, light coat.
- **Watch the last 2 minutes.** Thin slices go from golden to burnt quickly. Pull them when the edges color.

## Variations

| Version | How |
|---|---|
| **Garlic** | Rub warm toasts with a halved raw garlic clove |
| **Herb and parmesan** | Sprinkle with grated Parmigiano and dried Italian herbs before baking |
| **Everything bagel** | Brush with oil and sprinkle with everything bagel seasoning |
| **Sourdough or ciabatta** | Same method; cut larger slices in half for bite size |
| **Air fryer** | 360°F for 4 to 6 minutes in one layer, shaking once |

2. Brie and fig jam
3. Goat cheese, honey and pistachios
4. Prosciutto and a sliver of pear
5. Burrata, cherry tomatoes and basil
6. Smoked salmon, cream cheese and capers
7. Blue cheese, walnuts and honey
8. Manchego and quince paste
9. Tapenade and a shaving of Parmigiano
10. [Marinated olives](/blog/marinated-olives-recipe/), chopped, with a little of their oil

On a big board, set out plain crostini and let guests build their own. For a party, top 2 or 3 kinds just before serving so they stay crisp.

## How many to make

Plan **3 to 4 crostini per guest** next to crackers on a charcuterie board, or **5 to 6 per guest** if they're the only bread. One baguette covers about 10 people.

## Make-ahead and storage

- **Up to 3 days ahead:** cool completely, then store in an airtight container or zip-top bag at room temperature.
- **Re-crisp:** 3 to 4 minutes at 350°F if they soften.
- **Freezer:** freeze plain baked crostini for up to 1 month; re-crisp from frozen for 5 minutes.
- **Toppings:** add wet toppings no more than 30 minutes before serving.

## FAQ

**What temperature do you bake crostini?**

400°F for 8 to 10 minutes, until golden at the edges and crisp. Thicker slices or a crowded pan may need 2 to 3 minutes more. Flip them halfway if you want both sides golden.

**How thick should crostini be?**

About 1/4 to 1/2 inch. Thinner slices turn crunchy all the way through, like a cracker. Thicker ones stay a little soft in the middle, which is better for wet toppings like ricotta or tomatoes.

**How far ahead can you make crostini?**

Up to 3 days. Cool them completely and store in an airtight container or zip-top bag at room temperature. If they soften, bake at 350°F for 3 to 4 minutes to re-crisp. Add toppings just before serving.

**What is the difference between crostini and bruschetta?**

Crostini are small, thin slices of baguette baked until crisp. Bruschetta is usually a larger, thicker slice of country bread, grilled or toasted, rubbed with garlic and topped, most famously with tomatoes.

**How many crostini do you get from one baguette?**

A standard 20 to 24 inch baguette makes about 30 to 40 slices cut 1/2 inch thick on a slight angle. Plan 3 to 4 crostini per guest on a charcuterie board.
