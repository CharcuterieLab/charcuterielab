## Cocktail cherry questions

**What are the best cocktail cherries?**

Luxardo Original Maraschino Cherries, Drinkhacker's winner of 21 brands. For bourbon cherries, Jack Rudy and Blanton's both earned an A-.

**What's the difference between Luxardo and Amarena cherries?**

Both are small, dark Italian sour cherries in syrup. Luxardo uses Marasca cherries and is firmer and tarter; Amarena Fabbri is sweeter and softer.

**Do cocktail cherries need to be refrigerated?**

Luxardo and Albero D'Oro say not to. Check each jar, since some bourbon cherries recommend refrigerating after opening.

**Are cocktail cherries alcoholic?**

Luxardo and Amarena are in syrup. Bourbon cherries from Jack Rudy, Blanton's and Yes Cocktail Co. contain bourbon, so keep them away from kids.

**How do you use cocktail cherries on a board?**

Spoon them over ricotta, mascarpone or goat cheese, or set them on a dessert board with dark chocolate and biscotti.
