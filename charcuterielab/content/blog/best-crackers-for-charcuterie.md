<h2 id="what-to-look-for">What to look for</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Look for:</strong> For a base cracker, flour and salt should lead the list. For a character cracker, you want to see the seeds, fruit or herbs rather than read them ninth on the list.</li><li class="sk-x"><strong>Skip:</strong> Heavily seasoned snack crackers (ranch, cheese dust, everything-bagel heavy) as the only cracker. They fight the cheese.</li></ul>

<h2 id="faq">Crackers questions</h2>
:::

**What crackers are best for a charcuterie board?**

Use two: a plain base like Carr's Table Water Crackers and one with character, such as La Panzanella Croccantini or Raincoast Crisps.

**What is the best budget cracker for a board?**

Milton's Craft Bakers Original Multi-Grain. It is sturdy, flavorful and inexpensive for the size.

**How many kinds of crackers should a board have?**

Two or three. More than that crowds the board without adding much.
