## Building a board from these

For a board for 20, Manchego, the Coastal English cheddar and Boursin make a strong cheese trio, with Columbus Salame and the Kirkland reduced-sodium salame for meat. Add both crackers, figs and grapes.

Plan on about 3 oz of cheese and 3 oz of meat per guest when the board is the party food, plus 10% from 20 guests up. Our [Costco charcuterie board for 20](/blog/costco-charcuterie-board/) has the full cart with ounces, prices and a plan for leftovers.

## What to skip

[Daily Meal](https://www.thedailymeal.com/2028097/best-worst-costco-kirkland-cheese-items/) ranked the Kirkland Sartori BellaVitano Cabernet last of nine Kirkland cheeses and found the Kirkland sharp cheddar flat. [The Takeout](https://www.thetakeout.com/2188869/best-worst-costco-deli-meat/) found the Citterio prosciutto chewy, which is why it's last here.

Costco packs are big. Our guide to [storing a charcuterie board](/blog/how-to-store-charcuterie-board/) covers how long each leftover keeps. See every ranked guide on our [charcuterie buying guides](/blog/best-charcuterie-brands/) page.

<h2 id="faq">Costco charcuterie questions</h2>
:::

**What is the best cheese at Costco for a charcuterie board?**

Kirkland Signature Manchego, a six-month Spanish DOP Manchego at a bulk price. The Kirkland Coastal English cheddar was the top Kirkland cheese in Daily Meal's test.

**Is Costco cheaper for a charcuterie board?**

For 15 or more guests, usually yes, because cheese and salami cost less per ounce. For a small board, the big packs leave a lot left over.

**How much should I buy at Costco for a board?**

About 3 oz of cheese and 3 oz of meat per guest when the board is the party food, or 2 oz each before a meal, plus 6 to 12 crackers per guest.
