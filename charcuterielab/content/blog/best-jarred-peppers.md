<h2 id="how-to-choose">How to serve jarred peppers</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Stuff Peppadews in 10 minutes:</strong> pipe in goat cheese or herbed cream cheese with a zip-top bag with the corner cut off.</li><li><strong>Wrap cherry peppers:</strong> fill with a cube of provolone, then wrap in a slice of salami or prosciutto.</li><li><strong>Dress roasted peppers:</strong> drain, slice, and toss with olive oil, garlic, salt and parsley.</li><li><strong>Contain the brine:</strong> serve wet peppers in a small bowl, not loose on the board.</li><li><strong>How much:</strong> about 2 to 3 peppers per guest.</li></ul>

## Jarred pepper questions

**What are the best jarred peppers for a charcuterie board?**

Peppadew Mild Whole Piquanté Peppers for stuffing, and Divina Organic roasted red peppers, Milk Street's top pick.

**Are Peppadews the same as cherry peppers?**

No. They look alike but are different peppers; Peppadews are sweeter, which is why America's Test Kitchen's tasters preferred them.

**What do you stuff Peppadew peppers with?**

Goat cheese, herbed cream cheese, feta or mozzarella pearls.

**What's the best brand of roasted red peppers?**

Divina Organic won Milk Street's test; DeLallo was second and costs about half as much.

**How long do opened jarred peppers last?**

Several weeks in the fridge if kept under the brine. Stuffed peppers last only a few days.
