<h2 id="how-to-choose">Peppermint bark on a dessert board</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Break it into 1 to 2 inch shards</strong> so guests can grab one piece without snapping a slab on the board.</li><li><strong>Keep it off warm things.</strong> White chocolate softens fast, so set it away from baked brie or a hot dip.</li><li><strong>Pair it with</strong> dark chocolate, shortbread, Christmas cookies, salted pretzels, clementines and a mild cheese like mascarpone or brie.</li><li><strong>Put it next to the mugs.</strong> It&#x27;s the easiest stir-in for a hot cocoa bar.</li><li><strong>How much:</strong> about 1 oz per person on a dessert board, so a 1 lb tin covers about 16 people.</li></ul>

## Peppermint bark questions

**What is the best store-bought peppermint bark?**

Williams Sonoma's, which five food pros picked for Simply Recipes. For a party on a budget, Costco's Kirkland bark is crunchier and much cheaper per ounce.

**Is Costco peppermint bark as good as Williams Sonoma?**

Delish found Costco's crunchier with milder mint, and Williams Sonoma's more minty and better for gifting. Costco's cost $9.99 for 21 oz in 2023 against $29.95 a pound for Williams Sonoma's.

**When is peppermint bark in stores?**

It's seasonal. Costco's returned in late October in 2024, and most brands are on shelves from about November through December.

**How do you break peppermint bark into pieces?**

Leave it at room temperature for a few minutes, then press a large knife straight down through it, or tap the slab in its bag on the counter.

**How much peppermint bark per person?**

About 1 oz per person on a dessert board alongside other sweets.
