<h2 id="how-to-choose">How to serve pickle dip</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Best dippers:</strong> kettle chips, ridged potato chips, pretzels, pork rinds, celery and bell pepper strips. Salty and crunchy is the point.</li><li><strong>On a board:</strong> next to salami, summer sausage and sharp cheddar. The brine cuts through the fat the same way pickles do.</li><li><strong>Make a store tub better:</strong> stir in a spoonful of chopped dill pickles and fresh dill, and top with a little pickle juice and black pepper.</li><li><strong>Keep it cold</strong> and out no more than 2 hours. It's a sour cream or cream cheese dip.</li></ul>

## Pickle dip questions

**What is the best store-bought pickle dip?**

Taste of the South Fried Pickle & Ranch Dip scored 10/10 at Sporked. For the chunkiest, most pickle-forward dip, Plucky.

**Where can I buy pickle dip?**

Taste of the South is at Costco, Sam's Club, H-E-B and Walmart; Plucky at Target; Park Street Deli at Aldi; Heluva Good Grillo's at Northeast grocers.

**What do you eat with pickle dip?**

Kettle chips, ridged potato chips, pretzels, pork rinds, celery and bell peppers. It's also good on burgers.

**Is pickle dip gluten-free?**

Plucky is labeled gluten-free. Taste of the South Fried Pickle & Ranch contains bread crumbs (wheat).

**Did Trader Joe's make a pickle dip?**

Trader Joe's has sold a seasonal Dill Pickle Hummus, which Food Network liked, but it comes and goes.
