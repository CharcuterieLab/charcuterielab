<h2 id="how-to-choose">Gift chocolates on a dessert board</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Take them out 30 minutes early.</strong> Filled chocolates taste best at cool room temperature, not fridge-cold.</li><li><strong>Cut a few in half</strong> so guests can see the fillings and share the adventurous flavors.</li><li><strong>Pair them with</strong> aged Gouda, blue cheese, fresh berries, dried figs, Marcona almonds and a glass of port or sparkling wine.</li><li><strong>Keep them away from warm dishes</strong> and sunny windows; bonbons soften fast.</li><li><strong>How much:</strong> plan 2 to 3 pieces per person alongside other sweets, so a 16-piece box covers 6 to 8 people.</li></ul>

## Chocolate gift box questions

**What is the best box of chocolates to give?**

Recchiuti's Black Box was Wirecutter's pick, L.A. Burdick topped Tasting Table's test of 12, and Consumer Reports' favorite was Dandelion's Single-Origin Truffle Collection.

**Is Godiva a good gift box?**

Testers were lukewarm. Tasting Table called the Goldmark box poor value, and Consumer Reports' testers found Godiva's truffles chewy and waxy.

**How long do boxed chocolates keep?**

Fresh bonbons are best within a few weeks; check the box. Store them cool and dry, around 60 to 68°F, not in the fridge.

**How many chocolates per person on a dessert board?**

About 2 to 3 pieces per person alongside other sweets.

**What goes with chocolates on a board?**

Aged Gouda, blue cheese, berries, dried figs, Marcona almonds and port or sparkling wine.
