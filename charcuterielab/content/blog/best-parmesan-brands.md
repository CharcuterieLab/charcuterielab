# The Best Parmesan for a Charcuterie Board, Ranked

> **Quick Answer:** Our top pick for Parmesan on a charcuterie board is Boar's Head Parmigiano-Reggiano (4.5 out of 5). Best value: Kirkland Signature Parmigiano-Reggiano. We ranked 6 widely sold options on how they work on a board, published taste tests and awards, ingredients, value and how easy they are to find.

For a board you want a wedge you can break into craggy chunks, not a tub of shreds. Real Parmigiano-Reggiano usually wins, but a couple of American Parmesans hold their own.

### 1. Boar's Head Parmigiano-Reggiano

**★★★★½ 4.5 out of 5** · Best overall · Best for: Classic 24-month Parmigiano · $$ mid-range

[America's Test Kitchen](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/taste_tests/1695-supermarket-parmesan)'s winner: aged 24 months, intensely flavorful and nutty, with a crystalline texture.

- **Why we like it:** Test-kitchen winner. Sold at many deli counters.
- **Keep in mind:** Price varies by store.
- **Where to buy:** Deli counters.

### 2. Sartori SarVecchio Parmesan

**★★★★☆ 4.2 out of 5** · Best for: Best American Parmesan · $$ mid-range

A Wisconsin Parmesan aged 20 months. [America's Test Kitchen](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/taste_tests/1695-supermarket-parmesan) named it the top domestic option, and [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2178190/store-bought-parmesan-cheese-brands-ranked/) ranked Sartori's shaved Parmesan first of ten store-bought Parmesans.

- **Why we like it:** Top domestic pick. Easy to find.
- **Keep in mind:** Not true Parmigiano-Reggiano.
- **Where to buy:** Most grocery stores.

### 3. Kirkland Signature Parmigiano-Reggiano

**★★★★☆ 4.2 out of 5** · Best value · Best for: Big boards on a budget · $ budget

Real 24-month Parmigiano-Reggiano at a low price per pound. [Eat This, Not That](https://www.eatthis.com/costco-cheeses-taste-test/) called it a household staple and ranked Costco's 36-month Stravecchio even higher, though [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/1995140/costco-kirkland-cheese-ranked-worst-best/) found the 24-month version middling.

- **Why we like it:** Real DOP, very good price. 36-month version available.
- **Keep in mind:** Mixed reviews. Large wedge.
- **Where to buy:** Costco.

### 4. Il Villaggio Parmigiano-Reggiano 18 Month

## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** For Parmigiano-Reggiano, the dotted name stamped into the rind and a DOP mark. An age of 24 months is the classic; 36 months is drier and more crystalline.

**Skip:** Pre-shredded or grated Parmesan for a board. Buy a wedge and break it with a small knife.

## Parmesan questions

**What is the best Parmesan for a cheese board?**

A wedge of real Parmigiano-Reggiano aged 24 months. Boar's Head won America's Test Kitchen's tasting; Kirkland is the best value.

**Is Parmesan the same as Parmigiano-Reggiano?**

In the US, no. Parmigiano-Reggiano is a protected Italian cheese; Parmesan can be made anywhere.

**How do I serve Parmesan on a board?**

Break it into rough chunks with a short, pointed knife instead of slicing. Take it out 45 to 60 minutes before serving.
