## Building a board from these

For 12 people with the board as the party food, plan about 2½ pounds of cheese and 2¼ pounds of meat. A strong Publix board is the Vintage Gouda, French Brie and Honey Goat Cheese, with Boar's Head Parmigiano in chunks. For meat, the Herb-Coated Salami and Publix Prosciutto, sliced at the deli. Add Palmetto Cheese with crackers, grapes and nuts.

Short on time? Publix sells [Boar's Head charcuterie boxes and platters to order ahead](https://www.publix.com/mc/order-ahead/order-charcuterie-boxes). Use our [Party Planner](/party-planner/) for exact amounts for your guest count.

## What to skip

- **Publix-brand cheddar for a board.** [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/1590585/worst-brand-cheese-publix/) ranked it lowest in its cheese brand test for its lack of flavor. Use an aged cheddar from the specialty case instead.
- **Cheese without checking the date.** [The Takeout](https://www.thetakeout.com/2249755/every-major-grocery-store-ranked-cheese-selection/) cited complaints about expired cheese at Publix. Check the sell-by date on cut wedges.

Watch for BOGO deals on specialty cheese in the weekly ad, a tip from [Parade](https://parade.com/food/best-things-to-buy-at-publix-deli)'s Publix superfan. See every ranked guide on our [charcuterie buying guides](/blog/best-charcuterie-brands/) page.

<h2 id="faq">Publix charcuterie questions</h2>
:::

**What is the best Publix cheese for a charcuterie board?**

Boar's Head Parmigiano Reggiano, sold at the Publix Deli. From Publix's own specialty case, the Imported Vintage Gouda and the Honey Goat Cheese are the best picks.

**Can you order a charcuterie board from Publix?**

Yes. Publix sells Boar's Head charcuterie boxes and platters to order ahead online or at the deli.

**What meats does Publix sell for charcuterie?**

Publix-brand Herb-Coated Salami, Pepper-Coated Salami and Prosciutto at the deli, plus Boar's Head meats sliced to order.

**How much should I buy at Publix for a board?**

About 3 oz of cheese and 3 oz of meat per guest when the board is the party food, or 2 oz each before a meal, plus 10 to 12 crackers per guest.
