<h2 id="how-to-choose">Tawny vs ruby, and what cheese goes with each</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Ruby</strong> is young, red and fruity: pour it with Stilton, Gorgonzola and dark chocolate.</li><li><strong>Tawny</strong> is aged in wood, amber and nutty: pour it with aged cheddar, aged Gouda, Manchego, nuts and dried fruit.</li><li><strong>Serve it</strong> slightly cool, about 55 to 60°F, in small 2 to 3 oz pours.</li><li><strong>Once open:</strong> ruby keeps about 2 weeks and tawny 1 to 3 months, corked in the fridge.</li></ul>

## Port questions

**What is the best port wine for the money?**

Kirkland Signature 10 Year Tawny at Costco, about $17 and made by Fonseca.

**Tawny or ruby port with cheese?**

Ruby for blue cheese like Stilton; tawny for aged cheddar, Gouda and nutty cheeses.

**Should port be chilled?**

Slightly. Serve it around 55 to 60°F, a little cooler than room temperature.

**How long does port last once opened?**

Ruby about 2 weeks, tawny 1 to 3 months, corked and refrigerated.

**How much port per person?**

A 2 to 3 oz pour. A 750 ml bottle serves 8 to 12 people.
