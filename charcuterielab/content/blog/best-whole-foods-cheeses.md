# The Best Whole Foods Cheeses for a Charcuterie Board, Ranked

> **Quick Answer:** Our top Whole Foods cheese for a charcuterie board is Isigny Sainte-Mère Double Cream Brie (4.3 out of 5), tied with Vermont Creamery Bijou. Best value: Sartori BellaVitano. Splurge: Uplands Pleasant Ridge Reserve, a three-time best-in-show American cheese.

Whole Foods has one of the best grocery cheese counters in the country, with specialty cheeses you'd otherwise find only at a cheese shop. These are the ones that work best on a board, plus two ways to pay less for them.

*How we ranked these: these are researched picks, not a side-by-side tasting. We scored each product on published taste tests, awards and shopper ratings, its ingredients, price and how easy it is to find, plus how it behaves on a board. Store stock changes, especially for seasonal items, so check your local store. ### 1. Isigny Sainte-Mère Double Cream Brie

**★★★★½ 4.3 out of 5** · Best overall · Best for: The soft cheese · $$ mid-range

A French double cream brie from Normandy. [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2053323/whole-foods-cheeses-charcuterie-board/) picked it for being even richer and creamier than a standard brie, with a subtle sweetness.

- **Why we like it:** Richer than most grocery brie, at a fair price.
- **Keep in mind:** Take it out 20 to 30 minutes before serving so it softens.
- **Where to find it:** Whole Foods cheese counter.

### 2. Vermont Creamery Bijou

**★★★★½ 4.3 out of 5** · Best for: A small, pretty goat cheese · $$ mid-range

Small aged goat's milk buttons with a wrinkled rind. [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2053323/whole-foods-cheeses-charcuterie-board/) called them rich, creamy and elegant, with a striking look on a board.

- **Why we like it:** Looks impressive with no effort.
- **Keep in mind:** Small, so buy one pack per 4 to 6 guests.
- **Where to find it:** Whole Foods cheese case.

### 3. Sartori BellaVitano

**★★★★☆ 4.2 out of 5** · Best value · Best for: A crowd-pleaser · $$ mid-range

A Wisconsin cheese between a cheddar and a Parmesan, sold plain or rubbed with flavors such as Merlot or tomato basil. [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2053323/whole-foods-cheeses-charcuterie-board/) picked the tomato basil version as an award-winning, easy savory cheese.

- **Why we like it:** Liked by almost everyone, and easy to find.
- **Keep in mind:** The flavored versions are strong, so pick one.
- **Where to find it:** Whole Foods cheese case.

### 4. Uplands Pleasant Ridge Reserve

**★★★★☆ 4.1 out of 5** · Splurge · Best for: A cheese-lover's board · $$$ premium

A Wisconsin Alpine-style cheese from grass-fed cows, with caramel and grassy notes. It has won Best of Show at the American Cheese Society three times, according to [The Cheese Atlas](https://thecheeseatlas.com/cheese-profiles/pleasant-ridge-reserve/).

- **Why we like it:** One of the most awarded American cheeses.
- **Keep in mind:** Expensive, and not every store has it.
- **Where to find it:** Whole Foods cheese counter. Ask if it's not out.

### 5. Jasper Hill Harbison

**★★★★☆ 4.1 out of 5** · Best for: A spoonable centerpiece · $$$ premium

A soft Vermont cheese wrapped in spruce bark, rich enough to eat with a spoon. It was named Best of Show at the American Cheese Society awards, reported [Gourmet News](https://www.gourmetnews.com/cellars-jasper-hills-harbison-named-best-show-acs-awards/).

- **Why we like it:** Peel back the top rind and it becomes the star of the board.
- **Keep in mind:** Pricey, and ripe wheels run. Serve it on a plate.
- **Where to find it:** Whole Foods cheese counter.

### 6. Cypress Grove Humboldt Fog

**★★★★☆ 4.0 out of 5** · Best for: A goat cheese with a wow factor · $$$ premium

A California goat cheese with a line of vegetable ash through the middle, tangy and creamy near the rind. It's one of the top picks in our [goat cheese ranking](/blog/best-goat-cheese-brands/).

- **Why we like it:** Looks and tastes special.
- **Keep in mind:** Expensive per ounce.
- **Where to find it:** Whole Foods cheese counter.

### 7. El Trigal Manchego, aged 6 months

**★★★★☆ 4.0 out of 5** · Best for: A Spanish board · $$ mid-range

A semi-firm Spanish sheep's milk cheese that [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2053323/whole-foods-cheeses-charcuterie-board/) described as tangy, nutty, fresh and creamy, and easy to slice.

- **Why we like it:** A good everyday Manchego.
- **Keep in mind:** Milder than older Manchego.
- **Where to find it:** Whole Foods cheese counter.

### 8. Somerdale Wensleydale with Cranberries

**★★★★☆ 3.8 out of 5** · Best for: A sweet, crumbly cheese · $$ mid-range

A crumbly British cheese studded with cranberries. [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2053323/whole-foods-cheeses-charcuterie-board/) liked its fresh, slightly sweet and acidic flavor next to salty meats.

- **Why we like it:** Festive and kid-friendly.
- **Keep in mind:** Sweet, so it suits holiday and dessert boards best.
- **Where to find it:** Whole Foods cheese case.

### 9. Roth 3 Chile Pepper Gouda

**★★★★☆ 3.8 out of 5** · Best for: A little heat · $$ mid-range

A Wisconsin Gouda with jalapeño, chipotle and habanero. [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2053323/whole-foods-cheeses-charcuterie-board/) said it adds smoky heat without being too spicy.

- **Why we like it:** Keeps a board from being boring.
- **Keep in mind:** Not for guests who avoid spice.
- **Where to find it:** Whole Foods cheese case.

### 10. Klare Melk Raw Gouda with Truffle

**★★★½☆ 3.5 out of 5** · Best for: A truffle cheese · $$ mid-range

A Dutch raw-milk Gouda with black truffle, sold only at Whole Foods according to [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2053323/whole-foods-cheeses-charcuterie-board/).

- **Why we like it:** Earthy truffle flavor that suits a fall or winter board.
- **Keep in mind:** Truffle divides people, and it's made from raw milk.
- **Where to find it:** Whole Foods cheese case.

## How to pay less at Whole Foods

- **Check the cut-ends bin.** Many stores sell small, discounted pieces of their big wheels in a basket at the end of the cheese case. [Apartment Therapy](https://www.apartmenttherapy.com/whole-foods-charcuterie-board-tip-37247873) roughly halved the cost of a three-cheese board this way.
- **Watch for 12 Days of Cheese.** In recent Decembers Whole Foods has run a sale with one specialty cheese half off each day, starting around December 12, according to [Ain't Too Proud To Meg](https://ainttooproudtomeg.com/whole-foods-12-days-of-cheese-recommendations/).
- **Ask for a taste.** The counter staff can cut you a sample before you buy.

[The Takeout](https://www.thetakeout.com/2249755/every-major-grocery-store-ranked-cheese-selection/) ranked Whole Foods second of 13 grocery chains for its cheese selection, noting its trained cheese staff.

## Building a board from these

Pick one soft cheese (Isigny brie or Harbison), one firm (Pleasant Ridge Reserve, El Trigal Manchego or BellaVitano) and one with a twist (Bijou, Humboldt Fog or the Wensleydale). Plan on about 3 oz of cheese per guest in total when the board is the party food.

For pregnant guests, the CDC recommends pasteurized cheeses, so skip the raw-milk truffle Gouda. See every ranked guide on our [charcuterie buying guides](/blog/best-charcuterie-brands/) page.

## Whole Foods cheese questions

**What is the best Whole Foods cheese for a charcuterie board?**

Isigny Sainte-Mère Double Cream Brie for a soft cheese, and Uplands Pleasant Ridge Reserve if you want a special firm cheese. Sartori BellaVitano is the best value.

**How can I save on cheese at Whole Foods?**

Look for the bin of discounted cut ends at the end of the cheese case, and watch for the 12 Days of Cheese sale in December.

**Does Whole Foods have cheeses you can't get elsewhere?**

Yes. Some cheeses, such as Klare Melk Raw Gouda with Truffle, are sold only at Whole Foods, and the counter carries small-farm American cheeses most supermarkets don't.
