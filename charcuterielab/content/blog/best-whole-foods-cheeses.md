## How to pay less at Whole Foods

- **Check the cut-ends bin.** Many stores sell small, discounted pieces of their big wheels in a basket at the end of the cheese case. [Apartment Therapy](https://www.apartmenttherapy.com/whole-foods-charcuterie-board-tip-37247873) roughly halved the cost of a three-cheese board this way.
- **Watch for 12 Days of Cheese.** In recent Decembers Whole Foods has run a sale with one specialty cheese half off each day, starting around December 12, according to [Ain't Too Proud To Meg](https://ainttooproudtomeg.com/whole-foods-12-days-of-cheese-recommendations/).
- **Ask for a taste.** The counter staff can cut you a sample before you buy.

[The Takeout](https://www.thetakeout.com/2249755/every-major-grocery-store-ranked-cheese-selection/) ranked Whole Foods second of 13 grocery chains for its cheese selection, noting its trained cheese staff.

## Building a board from these

Pick one soft cheese (Isigny brie or Harbison), one firm (Pleasant Ridge Reserve, El Trigal Manchego or BellaVitano) and one with a twist (Bijou, Humboldt Fog or the Wensleydale). Plan on about 3 oz of cheese per guest in total when the board is the party food.

For pregnant guests, the CDC recommends pasteurized cheeses, so skip the raw-milk truffle Gouda. See every ranked guide on our [charcuterie buying guides](/blog/best-charcuterie-brands/) page.

<h2 id="faq">Whole Foods cheese questions</h2>
:::

**What is the best Whole Foods cheese for a charcuterie board?**

Isigny Sainte-Mère Double Cream Brie for a soft cheese, and Uplands Pleasant Ridge Reserve if you want a special firm cheese. Sartori BellaVitano is the best value.

**How can I save on cheese at Whole Foods?**

Look for the bin of discounted cut ends at the end of the cheese case, and watch for the 12 Days of Cheese sale in December.

**Does Whole Foods have cheeses you can't get elsewhere?**

Yes. Some cheeses, such as Klare Melk Raw Gouda with Truffle, are sold only at Whole Foods, and the counter carries small-farm American cheeses most supermarkets don't.
