# Which Cheeses Are Safe During Pregnancy? A List for Your Cheese Board

> **Quick Answer:** In the US, **pasteurized cheese is generally safe**: cheddar, Parmesan, Swiss, mozzarella, feta and cream cheese. **Avoid raw-milk cheese and queso fresco** unless heated until steaming. Check labels and ask your doctor if unsure.

*This is general food-safety information from US and UK health agencies, not medical advice. Your doctor or midwife knows your situation.*

## Why cheese matters in pregnancy: Listeria

The concern is *Listeria*, a bacterium that's rare but more dangerous in pregnancy. Pasteurizing milk kills it, which is why the rules center on pasteurized versus raw milk. ([FDA](https://www.fda.gov/food/health-educators/listeria-food-safety-moms-be))

## What US health agencies say

- **Choose:** cheese made with pasteurized milk, including hard cheeses like Asiago, cheddar, Parmesan and Swiss, and soft cheeses like cottage cheese, cream cheese, string cheese, feta and mozzarella. ([CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/listeria/prevention/index.html))
- **Avoid:** any cheese made with raw milk, and queso fresco, queso blanco and requesón, even when made with pasteurized milk, unless heated until steaming hot. ([FDA](https://www.fda.gov/food/health-educators/listeria-food-safety-moms-be), [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/listeria/prevention/index.html))

## What UK advice says (stricter on soft cheese)

The NHS agrees that hard cheeses are fine, even unpasteurized ones, and that pasteurized soft cheeses like mozzarella, feta, cream cheese, ricotta, halloumi and goat's cheese without a white rind are fine. But it says **mould-ripened soft cheeses with a white rind** (brie, Camembert, rinded goat's cheese) and **soft blue cheeses** (gorgonzola, Danish blue, Roquefort) should be eaten **only when cooked until steaming hot**, pasteurized or not. ([NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/pregnancy/keeping-well/foods-to-avoid/))

If you want the most cautious approach, follow the UK advice for brie and blue cheese.

## How to read a cheese label

- Look for **"made with pasteurized milk"** on the label or ask at the cheese counter.
- **"Raw milk"** or **"lait cru"** means unpasteurized.
- Imported specialty cheeses are more likely to be raw milk, so check them carefully.

## FAQ

**What cheeses are safe during pregnancy?**

In the US, cheese made with pasteurized milk is generally considered safe, including cheddar, Parmesan, Swiss, Gouda, mozzarella, feta, cream cheese, cottage cheese and string cheese. The exception is queso fresco-type cheese, which should be heated until steaming hot.

**Can I eat brie while pregnant?**

US agencies treat pasteurized brie like other pasteurized cheese. UK advice is stricter: eat brie, Camembert and other white-rind cheeses only cooked until steaming hot, like baked brie. Ask your doctor which advice to follow.

**Is blue cheese safe during pregnancy?**

Pasteurized blue cheese falls under the US pasteurized-cheese guidance. The UK says soft blue cheeses such as gorgonzola and Danish blue should be cooked until steaming hot.

**Is Parmesan safe during pregnancy?**

Yes. Hard cheeses like Parmesan are considered safe. The CDC lists pasteurized hard cheeses as a safer choice, and the UK's NHS says hard cheese is fine even when made from unpasteurized milk.

**Why is queso fresco a problem even when pasteurized?**

Both the FDA and CDC say to avoid unheated queso fresco-type cheeses even when they're made with pasteurized milk. These fresh, moist cheeses can pick up Listeria during cheesemaking after the milk is pasteurized, so heat them until steaming hot.
