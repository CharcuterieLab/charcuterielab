## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** How it ships. Boxes with soft cheese or sliced meat need cold packs and fast shipping, so they suit someone who will be home to take delivery. Shelf-stable boxes (whole summer sausage, aged cheese in wax or vacuum packs, crackers) can sit on a porch.

**Skip:** Boxes where most of the weight is "cheese blends", spreads or processed cheese. Read the contents list: real cheese names (cheddar, Gouda, Manchego) and whole cured salami are the sign of a better box.

**Order by:** for Christmas, most companies want orders in by mid-December for standard shipping, and perishable boxes often don't ship on weekends. Order in the first week of December to be safe.
**How much a box feeds:** plan on about 3 oz of meat and 3 oz of cheese per person when the box is the main snack. A 3 lb box serves about 8.

## Charcuterie gift box questions

**What is the best meat and cheese gift box?**

Harry & David's Ultimate Meat and Cheese Gift Box: about 5.5 lb of sausage, salami, cheese and crackers for about $110. For a board that arrives already arranged, Boarderie is the best choice.

**What is a good cheap charcuterie gift?**

The Swiss Colony Sausage and Cheese Bars, at about $25 to $45. Or make your own: our DIY gift basket guide has a $25 version.

**Do meat and cheese gift boxes need to be refrigerated?**

Boxes with soft cheese or sliced meat do, and arrive with cold packs. Whole summer sausage and waxed or vacuum-packed aged cheese are shelf-stable until opened.

**When should I order a charcuterie gift box for Christmas?**

In the first week of December. Holiday cut-off dates come early, and perishable boxes often only ship on weekdays.
