<h2 id="how-to-choose">How to pair non-alcoholic wine with a board</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Go crisp and sparkling.</strong> Alcohol-free wines are lighter, so acidity and bubbles matter more than body. Sauvignon Blanc, Riesling and sparkling pair best.</li><li><strong>Pick lighter cheeses:</strong> goat cheese, feta, brie and young Gouda, rather than big blues and old cheddars.</li><li><strong>Salty and fatty meats</strong> like prosciutto and salami love the acid in Riesling and sparkling.</li><li><strong>Serve them colder</strong> than you would the real thing; it tightens the flavors.</li><li><strong>Know the label:</strong> "non-alcoholic" can mean up to 0.5% alcohol, while "alcohol-free" means 0%.</li></ul>

## Non-alcoholic wine questions

**What is the best non-alcoholic wine?**

Giesen 0% Sauvignon Blanc, 12x75's best all-around pick at about $15. For bubbles, Mionetto Alcohol-Removed Sparkling.

**What is the best non-alcoholic red wine?**

Ariel Cabernet Sauvignon is the affordable, easy-to-find pick (about $11). Zeronimo Leonis Blend is the premium choice.

**Does non-alcoholic wine have any alcohol?**

Products labeled non-alcoholic can contain up to 0.5% alcohol. Alcohol-free products contain none.

**What cheese goes with non-alcoholic wine?**

Lighter cheeses: goat cheese, feta, brie and young Gouda. Crisp whites and sparkling styles pair best.

**Is Töst a wine?**

No. It's a tea-based sparkling drink with white tea, ginger, elderberry and cranberry, but it pours and pairs like a dry sparkling wine.
