<h2 id="what-to-look-for">What to look for</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Look for:</strong> Look for &quot;low-moisture mozzarella&quot; (whole milk or part-skim) and a short ingredient list: milk, salt, cultures and enzymes. Whole-milk sticks taste richer. Cheddar and colby jack &quot;sticks&quot; won&#x27;t string.</li><li class="sk-x"><strong>Skip:</strong> Leaving string cheese out for the whole party. It&#x27;s a cheese like any other: keep it cold until the board goes out and take it off the table after 2 hours.</li></ul>
:::

**Serving it:** cut sticks into coins, peel them into strings for little kids, or wrap a slice of salami around each one. Two sticks per child is plenty with other food on the board. See our [kids' charcuterie board](/blog/charcuterie-board-for-kids/) for the rest of the spread.

<h2 id="faq">String cheese questions</h2>
:::

**What is the best string cheese?**

It depends on the test. Organic Valley won at Sporked, Kerrygold Cheddar Sticks won at Daily Meal, and Frigo placed well in all three, which is why it's our top pick.

**Is string cheese the same as mozzarella?**

Yes. It's low-moisture mozzarella stretched so the proteins line up, which is why it peels into strings.

**Is string cheese good on a kids' charcuterie board?**

Yes. Cut it into coins or shapes, or wrap it with salami.

**How long can string cheese sit out?**

No more than 2 hours, like the rest of the board.
