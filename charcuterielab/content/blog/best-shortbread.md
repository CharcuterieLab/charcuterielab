# The Best Store-Bought Shortbread for a Dessert Board, Ranked

> **Quick Answer:** Our top pick for shortbread on a charcuterie board is Walkers Pure Butter Shortbread (4.5 out of 5). Value: Lorna Doone Shortbread Cookies. Butter flavor: Trader Joe's French Galettes. We ranked 6 widely sold options on how they work on a board, published taste tests and awards, ingredients, value and how easy they are to find.

Shortbread is the cracker of the dessert board: buttery, not too sweet and sturdy enough for jam, cheese or a square of dark chocolate. It's also a natural on Christmas cheese boards. Two big taste tests ranked the grocery options, and the classic tartan box came out near the top of both.

### 1. Walkers Pure Butter Shortbread

**★★★★½ 4.5 out of 5** · Best overall · Best for: Every dessert board · $$ mid-range

[Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2197875/store-bought-butter-cookies-shortbread-ranked/)'s winner of 12: firm but very crumbly, and less sweet than Royal Dansk. [Daily Meal](https://www.thedailymeal.com/1607985/store-bought-shortbread-cookies-ranked-worst-best/) ranked it 2nd of 10 for its iconic sandy texture.

- **Why we like it:** Real butter, and a size that holds jam or cheese.
- **Keep in mind:** Breaks easily, and pricier than mass-market brands.
- **Where to buy:** Nearly every grocery store, Costco and Target.

### 2. Trader Joe's French Galettes

**★★★★½ 4.3 out of 5** · Best butter flavor · Best for: Butter-forward flavor · $ budget

[Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2197875/store-bought-butter-cookies-shortbread-ranked/) ranked them 3rd and called them the most butter-forward cookies it tasted, with a short ingredient list. Compact and dense, though a little dry.

- **Why we like it:** Tastes the most like butter.
- **Keep in mind:** Only at Trader Joe's.
- **Where to buy:** Trader Joe's.

### 3. Royal Dansk Danish Butter Cookies

**★★★★☆ 4.2 out of 5** · Best for: Holiday boards · $ budget

[Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2197875/store-bought-butter-cookies-shortbread-ranked/)'s runner-up: rich and buttery with a touch of sweetness, in a variety of shapes.

- **Why we like it:** The classic blue tin, with shapes that look festive on a board.
- **Keep in mind:** Sweeter than shortbread.
- **Where to buy:** Nearly every grocery store, especially at the holidays.

### 4. Lorna Doone Shortbread Cookies

**★★★★☆ 4.0 out of 5** · Best value · Best for: Budget boards · $ budget

[Daily Meal](https://www.thedailymeal.com/1607985/store-bought-shortbread-cookies-ranked-worst-best/)'s winner of 10: crumbly, with a sandy bite, "the tried and true shortbread cookie on the shelves." [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2197875/store-bought-butter-cookies-shortbread-ranked/) ranked it 5th and noted it uses oils and artificial flavor instead of butter.

- **Why we like it:** Cheap and easy to find.
- **Keep in mind:** No real butter.
- **Where to buy:** Nearly every grocery store.

### 5. Keebler Sandies Shortbread Classic

**★★★★☆ 3.9 out of 5** · Best for: A cheap, classic sleeve · $ budget

6th in both [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2197875/store-bought-butter-cookies-shortbread-ranked/) and [Daily Meal](https://www.thedailymeal.com/1607985/store-bought-shortbread-cookies-ranked-worst-best/): crumbly, with butter in the ingredients and a vanilla note, though the butter flavor is mild.

- **Why we like it:** Easy-open sleeve, low price.
- **Keep in mind:** Mild flavor.
- **Where to buy:** Nearly every grocery store.

### 6. 365 Butter Shortbread

**★★★★☆ 3.8 out of 5** · Best for: A real-butter store brand · $ budget

[Daily Meal](https://www.thedailymeal.com/1607985/store-bought-shortbread-cookies-ranked-worst-best/) ranked it 5th for its buttery flavor and sweet-savory balance, but found the texture too moist.

- **Why we like it:** Real butter at a store-brand price.
- **Keep in mind:** Softer than classic shortbread.
- **Where to buy:** Whole Foods.

## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** Butter should be in the first three ingredients. Several cheaper "shortbread" cookies use vegetable oil and butter flavoring instead, which is why they taste flat.

**Skip:** Sugar-free shortbread. In [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2197875/store-bought-butter-cookies-shortbread-ranked/)'s test, the sugar-free cookies were dry, mealy or left an aftertaste.

**Serving it:** lay shortbread next to brie, aged cheddar, blue cheese or mascarpone, with jam, honey or dark chocolate. Plan two or three pieces per guest on a dessert board. See our [dessert board guide](/blog/dessert-board/) for the full build.

## Shortbread questions

**What is the best store-bought shortbread?**

Walkers Pure Butter Shortbread. It won Tasting Table's test and came 2nd at Daily Meal.

**What cheese goes with shortbread?**

Aged cheddar, brie, blue cheese, mascarpone and Wensleydale with cranberries.

**Is Lorna Doone real shortbread?**

It's a shortbread-style cookie, but it's made with oils and artificial flavor rather than butter.

**How much shortbread for a dessert board?**

Two or three pieces per guest alongside other sweets.
