## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** The oil they're roasted in. Some use peanut oil, which matters for a guest with a peanut allergy.

**Skip:** Raw Marcona almonds for a board. The oil and salt are most of the appeal.

**What are the best Marcona almonds?**

**Are Marcona almonds safe for peanut allergies?**
