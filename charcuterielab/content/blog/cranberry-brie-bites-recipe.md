# Cranberry Brie Bites: The Easy Puff Pastry Holiday Appetizer

> **Quick Answer:** Cut **1 sheet of puff pastry** into **24 squares** and press into a mini muffin tin. Add a **1/2-inch cube of brie**, **1 teaspoon cranberry sauce** and chopped pecans. Bake at **375°F for 15 to 18 minutes**. Serve warm with rosemary and a drizzle of honey.

Baked brie in one bite, with no knife needed at the party. They're the hot thing next to a cold [charcuterie board](/blog/christmas-charcuterie-board/), and they use up Thanksgiving's leftover cranberry sauce.

## Cranberry brie bites

**Makes:** 24 · **Prep:** 15 minutes · **Bake:** 15 to 18 minutes

**Ingredients**
- 1 sheet frozen [puff pastry](/blog/best-puff-pastry/) (half of a 17.3 oz box), thawed in the fridge
- Flour, for dusting
- Butter or nonstick spray, for the pan
- 8 oz [brie](/ingredients/brie/), cut into 24 cubes about 1/2 inch
- 1/2 cup whole-berry [cranberry sauce](/ingredients/cranberry-sauce/)
- 1/4 cup chopped pecans
- 1 tablespoon finely chopped fresh rosemary or thyme
- Honey, for drizzling (optional)

**Steps**
1. Heat the oven to 375°F and grease a 24-cup mini muffin tin.
2. Unfold the puff pastry on a lightly floured surface and roll it gently into about a 10 by 12 inch rectangle. Cut it into 24 squares, about 2 inches each.
3. Press each square into a muffin cup, letting the corners stand up.
4. Put a cube of brie in each cup, then top with about 1 teaspoon cranberry sauce and a few chopped pecans.
5. Bake for 15 to 18 minutes, until the pastry is puffed and golden and the brie has melted.
6. Cool in the pan for 5 minutes, then lift out, sprinkle with rosemary and drizzle with honey if you like. Serve warm.

**Three tips that make them better**
- **Keep the pastry cold.** Thaw it in the fridge, not on the counter, and work quickly. Warm pastry gets sticky and doesn't puff.
- **Cut the brie cold.** Straight from the fridge it cubes cleanly. Leave the rind on; it helps the cubes hold their shape.
- **Don't overfill.** About a teaspoon of sauce per cup. More bubbles over and burns on the pan.

## 4 variations

| Version | Swap |
|---|---|
| **Fig and prosciutto** | [Fig jam](/ingredients/fig-jam/) instead of cranberry, with a small piece of prosciutto on top after baking |
| **Hot honey and pecan** | Skip the cranberry; add candied pecans and a drizzle of [hot honey](/ingredients/hot-honey/) after baking |
| **Apple and walnut** | A spoon of apple butter, chopped walnuts and a pinch of cinnamon |
| **Pepper jelly** | Red pepper jelly instead of cranberry, for sweet heat |

For the pecans, try our [candied pecans](/blog/candied-pecans-recipe/), chopped. They add crunch and an extra layer of sweetness.

## Make-ahead and freezing

- **1 day ahead:** assemble in the tin, cover and refrigerate. Bake straight from the fridge, adding 1 to 2 minutes.
- **Freeze unbaked:** freeze the filled tin until firm, then move the bites to a freezer bag for up to 1 month. Bake from frozen at 375°F, adding 3 to 5 minutes.
- **Reheat baked bites:** 5 minutes at 350°F. The microwave makes the pastry soggy.
- **Serving:** they're best warm, within 30 minutes of baking, and shouldn't sit out more than 2 hours.

## How many to make

Plan **2 to 3 bites per guest** as one of several appetizers, or **4 to 5** if they're the main hot appetizer. One batch of 24 serves 8 to 12 people; double it for a crowd with two tins or two batches.

## Serving them with a board

Set the bites on a separate plate or tray next to the board, so the warm pastry doesn't soften crackers. They match a holiday board with [brie](/blog/brie-charcuterie-board/), sugared cranberries, rosemary sprigs and sparkling wine. ## FAQ

**How do you make cranberry brie bites?**

Cut a thawed sheet of puff pastry into 24 squares and press them into a greased mini muffin tin. Add a 1/2-inch cube of brie and a teaspoon of cranberry sauce to each, top with chopped pecans, and bake at 375°F for 15 to 18 minutes until puffed and golden.

**Do you remove the rind from brie for brie bites?**

No. The rind is edible and helps the cubes hold their shape as they melt. If you prefer, trim the top rind only. Cut the brie cold, straight from the fridge, for the cleanest cubes.

**Can you make cranberry brie bites ahead of time?**

Yes. Assemble them in the tin, cover and refrigerate up to 1 day, then bake. Or freeze them unbaked in the tin, pop them into a freezer bag once firm, and bake from frozen, adding 3 to 5 minutes.

**Can I use canned cranberry sauce?**

Yes. Whole-berry canned cranberry sauce works well and looks better than jellied. Leftover homemade cranberry sauce from Thanksgiving is even better.

**Can you make brie bites without a muffin tin?**

Yes. Cut the pastry into squares, lay them on a parchment-lined sheet, add the brie and cranberry in the center and fold up the corners, or use pre-baked phyllo cups for a crisper bite.
