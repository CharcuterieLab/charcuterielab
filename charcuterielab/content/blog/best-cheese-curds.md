<h2 id="how-to-choose">How to keep curds squeaky and serve them on a board</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Fresh is everything:</strong> curds squeak best the day they're made and lose it after a day or two in the fridge.</li><li><strong>Bring back the squeak</strong> by letting them sit at room temperature, or microwaving a bowl for 5 to 10 seconds.</li><li><strong>Serve them loose</strong> in a bowl; no knife needed. About 1 to 2 oz per person.</li><li><strong>Wisconsin board:</strong> curds, summer sausage, kielbasa, sharp cheddar, dill pickles, pretzels and a local beer.</li><li><strong>Store</strong> in a closed container in the fridge and eat within about a week.</li></ul>

## Cheese curd questions

**What are the best cheese curds to buy?**

At the supermarket, Aldi's Happy Farms White Cheddar Curds. From a creamery, Decatur Dairy and Ellsworth Cooperative Creamery, both 2022 World Championship winners.

**Why don't my cheese curds squeak?**

Squeak fades as curds age. Let them warm to room temperature or microwave them for a few seconds.

**Are cheese curds good on a charcuterie board?**

Yes. They're mild and salty, so they go with summer sausage, kielbasa, pickles and beer.

**Where can I buy cheese curds outside Wisconsin?**

Aldi, Kroger stores, Whole Foods and online from Wisconsin creameries.

**How long do cheese curds last?**

About a week in the fridge once opened, though the squeak goes after a day or two.
