# The Best Prosecco for a Charcuterie Board, Ranked

> **Quick Answer:** Our top pick for prosecco on a charcuterie board is Mionetto Prosecco Brut (4.6 out of 5). Value: Ruffino Prosecco. Splurge: Santa Margherita Prosecco Superiore Brut. We ranked 7 widely sold options on how they work on a board, published taste tests and awards, ingredients, value and how easy they are to find.

Bubbles are the easiest drink to pair with a charcuterie board: the acidity and fizz cut through salty cured meat and rich cheese, and prosecco does it for less than Champagne. Most good bottles cost under $20. These rankings combine Food & Wine's 2025 tasting, Cheapism's blind tasting of 10 bottles and VinePair's tasting of dozens of proseccos under $20.

*How we ranked these: these are researched picks, not a side-by-side tasting. ## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** "Prosecco DOC" is the everyday level. "Prosecco Superiore DOCG" (often from Valdobbiadene or Conegliano) is the higher tier. Sweetness runs the opposite of what the words suggest: Brut is driest, Extra Dry is a little sweet, and Dry is sweeter still.

**Skip:** Pouring it warm. Chill prosecco to about 40 to 45°F, and open it just before toasting. Keep an opened bottle in the fridge with a sparkling-wine stopper.

**How much to buy:** a 750 ml bottle pours about five 5-oz glasses. For a toast, one bottle covers 6 to 8 people. For a party, plan on about half a bottle per guest who drinks.
**What it pairs with:** prosciutto, salami, Parmigiano-Reggiano, brie, salted nuts, olives and anything fried. Extra dry is the better match for fruit and dessert boards.

## Prosecco questions

**What is the best prosecco under $20?**

Mionetto Prosecco Brut, a classic, dry bottle sold almost everywhere. Ruffino is the value pick, and Torresella won Cheapism's blind tasting.

**Which is drier, Brut or Extra Dry prosecco?**

Brut. Extra Dry is slightly sweeter, despite the name.

**Is prosecco good with charcuterie?**

Yes. Its acidity and bubbles cut through salty cured meat and rich cheese. Brut suits savory boards; Extra Dry suits fruit and dessert boards.

**How much prosecco do I need for a party?**

About half a bottle per guest who drinks. A 750 ml bottle pours about five glasses.
