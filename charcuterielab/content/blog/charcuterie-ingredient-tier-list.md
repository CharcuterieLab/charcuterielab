# The Charcuterie Tier List: 32 Board Ingredients Ranked from S to F

> **Quick Answer:** S tier: aged cheddar, brie, prosciutto, Genoa salami, fig jam and Marcona almonds. A tier: Manchego, soppressata, honeycomb, grapes, cornichons and dark chocolate. B tier sits in the middle, C tier needs the right partner, D tier is situational, and F tier is pre-cut cheese cubes, unlemoned apple slices and wet fruit on crackers.

Tier lists start arguments, and that is the point. Every ingredient here was ranked on three things: **how much it pairs with**, **how many guests will eat it**, and **whether it still looks good after two hours** on the board. Read it, disagree in the comments, then make your own.

## The full tier list

| Tier | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| **S** | Aged cheddar, brie, prosciutto, Genoa salami, fig jam, Marcona almonds |
| **A** | Manchego, soppressata, honeycomb, grapes, cornichons, dark chocolate |
| **B** | Gouda, goat cheese, castelvetrano olives, dried apricots, water crackers, baguette |
| **C** | Blue cheese, mortadella, pepper jelly, pistachios, pickled onions, mustard |
| **D** | Pepperoni, strawberries, carrot sticks, flavored crackers, candied nuts |
| **F** | Pre-cut cheese cubes, sliced apples without lemon, wet fruit on crackers |

## S tier: the board is not a board without them

**Aged cheddar.** Sharp, crumbly, with those crunchy crystals. It pairs with fruit, jam, beer, wine and nearly every meat. Read the [aged cheddar guide](/ingredients/aged-cheddar/).

![S tier charcuterie board with aged cheddar, brie, prosciutto, Genoa salami, fig jam and Marcona almonds](/images/charcuterie-ingredient-tier-list-s-tier.webp)

**Brie.** The cheese people walk to first. Soft, mild and buttery, and it turns anything sweet into a dessert.

**Prosciutto.** Salty, silky and it drapes. It makes a plain board look expensive.

**Genoa salami.** Mild, garlicky, loved by kids and adults. The easiest meat to fold into a river.

**Fig jam.** One spoonful connects the salty side of the board to the creamy side.

**Marcona almonds.** Softer and richer than regular almonds, and the crunch every board needs.

## A tier: almost perfect

**Manchego**, nutty and firm. **Soppressata**, a bit more bite than Genoa. **Honeycomb**, which looks stunning and tastes even better on blue cheese. **Grapes**, the classic palate cleanser. **Cornichons**, the acid that resets your mouth. **Dark chocolate**, which pairs with aged cheese better than most people expect.

## B tier: reliable, not exciting

Gouda, goat cheese, castelvetrano olives, dried apricots, water crackers and baguette. They all do their job. None of them are why people crowd the board.

## C tier: great with the right partner

**Blue cheese** splits a room, but put it next to honey or pear and the skeptics come around. **Mortadella** is soft and mild and gets overlooked. **Pepper jelly**, **pistachios**, **pickled onions** and **mustard** are fantastic in small amounts and easy to overdo.

## D tier: situational

**Pepperoni** belongs on a game-day board, not a wine night. (Yes, it is a kind of salami; see [salami vs pepperoni](/blog/salami-vs-pepperoni/).) **Strawberries** are beautiful but wet and short-lived. **Carrot sticks** feel like a lunchbox. **Flavored crackers** fight with the cheese. **Candied nuts** are good but can turn a board into dessert.

## F tier: we need to talk

**Pre-cut cheese cubes** dry out and all taste the same. Cut your own from a block. **Sliced apples without lemon** turn brown in minutes; toss them in lemon juice first. **Wet fruit placed on crackers** makes everything soggy. Put it in a bowl.

Every F tier item has a fix, which is why it is F tier and not banned. See the [10 board mistakes](/blog/charcuterie-board-mistakes/) for more.
