## Cheese club questions

**How much does a cheese of the month club cost?**

From about $46 a month plus shipping (Gourmet Cheese of the Month) to about $70 (Murray's, free shipping) and $99 (Cheese Grotto, express shipping included). Zingerman's is $250 for a prepaid three months.

**How is the cheese kept cold in shipping?**

Clubs ship overnight or express in insulated boxes with ice packs. The packs may thaw by arrival; refrigerate the cheese as soon as it lands.

**How long does shipped cheese last?**

At least two weeks wrapped in the fridge, per Jasper Hill. Cheese Grotto says its boxes are best eaten within two to three weeks.

**Can you give a one-month cheese club?**

Not everywhere. Murray's dropped its one-month plan. Zingerman's and Cheese Grotto sell prepaid three-month gifts, and Di Bruno offers recurring monthly plans you can cancel.

**When should I order a cheese club for Christmas?**

By early December. Murray's ships the first box the month after purchase, Cheese Grotto ships only on Tuesdays, and Zingerman's lets you pick the delivery date.
