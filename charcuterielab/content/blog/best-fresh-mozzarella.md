# The Best Fresh Mozzarella for a Charcuterie Board, Ranked

> **Quick Answer:** Our top pick for fresh mozzarella on a charcuterie board is Primo Taglio Fresh Mozzarella (4.5 out of 5). Best value: Trader Joe's Fresh Mozzarella. We ranked 4 widely sold options on how they work on a board, published taste tests and awards, ingredients, value and how easy they are to find.

Fresh mozzarella should be milky, bouncy and a little sweet. On a board it cools down spicy salami and pairs with tomatoes and basil. These are the brands that taste freshest.

## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** Check the date and buy the freshest pack. Mozzarella packed in water keeps its texture best.

**Skip:** Low-moisture block mozzarella on a board. It's for pizza, not for eating fresh.

## Fresh mozzarella questions

**What is the best fresh mozzarella for a board?**

Primo Taglio if you shop at Safeway or Albertsons; Polly-O is the best widely available choice.

**How do I serve fresh mozzarella on a board?**

Drain it, pat it dry, tear or slice it, then season with olive oil, salt and pepper just before serving.

**How long can fresh mozzarella sit out?**

No more than 2 hours, or 1 hour above 90°F.
