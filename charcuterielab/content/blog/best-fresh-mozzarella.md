<h2 id="faq">Fresh mozzarella questions</h2>
:::

**What is the best fresh mozzarella for a board?**

Primo Taglio if you shop at Safeway or Albertsons; Polly-O is the best widely available choice.

**How do I serve fresh mozzarella on a board?**

Drain it, pat it dry, tear or slice it, then season with olive oil, salt and pepper just before serving.

**How long can fresh mozzarella sit out?**

No more than 2 hours, or 1 hour above 90°F.
