Beer cheese is Kentucky's party spread: sharp cheddar blended with beer, garlic and cayenne, served cool with crackers and celery. It was born in Winchester, Kentucky, and the best tubs still come from there. No national outlet has taste-tested store-bought beer cheese, so these picks come from the Winchester Beer Cheese Festival, cheese contest results, ingredient lists and where you can actually buy each one.

<h2 id="how-to-choose">How to serve beer cheese on a game-day board</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Serve it cool, not cold.</strong> Take it out about 30 minutes early so it softens and the flavor comes forward, and put it back within 2 hours.</li><li><strong>Kentucky style:</strong> a bowl of beer cheese with celery, carrots, saltines and pretzel rods.</li><li><strong>Game-day board:</strong> add soft pretzel bites, seared kielbasa coins, summer sausage, dill pickles and grainy mustard.</li><li><strong>Want it warm?</strong> Spoon it onto a burger or stir it into mac and cheese. Melted down it turns into the beer cheese dip Midwest bars serve with pretzels.</li><li><strong>How much:</strong> an 8 oz tub serves about 6 to 8 people as a dip alongside other food.</li></ul>

## Beer cheese questions

**What is the best store-bought beer cheese?**

Hall's Original Snappy, from Winchester, Kentucky, which won Best Beer Cheese at the town's Beer Cheese Festival. For heat, Hall's Hot-n-Snappy, the 2022 winner.

**Where did beer cheese come from?**

Winchester, Kentucky. It's credited to Joe and Johnny Allman, whose recipe the Hall family took over in 1965. Allman's beer cheese is still made from the family recipe.

**Is beer cheese served hot or cold?**

Kentucky beer cheese is a cool spread, served from the fridge after softening for about 30 minutes. The warm, melted beer cheese dip served with pretzels at bars is a different, queso-like dish.

**Does beer cheese have alcohol in it?**

It's made with beer and not cooked, so a small amount can remain. Check the label for anyone avoiding alcohol.

**What do you eat with beer cheese?**

Celery, carrots, saltines and pretzels, Kentucky style. On a game-day board, add soft pretzels, kielbasa, summer sausage and pickles.

**How long can beer cheese sit out?**

Up to 2 hours at room temperature, then back in the fridge.
