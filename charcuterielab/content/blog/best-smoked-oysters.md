<h2 id="how-to-choose">How to serve smoked oysters on a board</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Drain them</strong> and blot lightly on a paper towel, or serve them in a small dish with a little of their oil.</li><li><strong>Put out toothpicks</strong> and something plain and crisp: saltines, water crackers or thin crostini.</li><li><strong>The classic party bite:</strong> a cracker, a smear of cream cheese, one smoked oyster and a drop of hot sauce or lemon.</li><li><strong>Pair them with</strong> sharp cheddar, pickled onions, cornichons, buttered rye and a glass of dry sparkling wine or a crisp lager.</li><li><strong>How much:</strong> a 3 oz tin is enough for 4 to 6 people to try a couple each.</li></ul>

## Smoked oyster questions

**What are the best canned smoked oysters?**

Geisha Fancy Smoked Oysters in Sunflower Oil, which scored 9.5/10 in Sporked's test of nine. Polar Fancy Whole (8/10) are the biggest.

**Are canned smoked oysters cooked?**

Yes. Canned smoked oysters are fully cooked and shelf-stable, so you can eat them straight from the tin.

**What do you eat smoked oysters with?**

Saltines or water crackers, cream cheese, a dash of hot sauce or lemon, sharp cheddar and pickles.

**Which smoked oysters should you skip?**

In Chowhound's 2025 ranking, Great Value smoked oysters left a bitter, overpowering smoke aftertaste, and Pampa's came with an intensely hot chili.

**How do you store leftover smoked oysters?**

Move them out of the tin into a covered container in the fridge and eat them within a couple of days.
