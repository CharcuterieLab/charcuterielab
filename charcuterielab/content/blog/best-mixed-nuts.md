## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** "Fancy", "deluxe" or "select" on the label usually means few or no peanuts. Look for nuts, oil and salt, and nothing else for a plain mix.

**Skip:** Honey-roasted or heavily seasoned mixes next to delicate cheese. They overpower it. Save them for a game-day board.

**Serving it:** serve nuts in a small bowl or use them to fill gaps between the cheese and meat. Toast them in a dry pan for 3 to 4 minutes to freshen a bag that's been open a while.
**Nut allergies:** keep nuts in their own bowl with a separate spoon, and label them. See our [nut-free board guide](/dietary/nut-free/) if a guest is allergic.

## Mixed nut questions

**What are the best mixed nuts for a charcuterie board?**

A peanut-free fancy mix. Trader Joe's Fancy Mixed Nuts scored 10 out of 10 at Sporked, and Kirkland Extra Fancy Mixed Nuts is the best value for a big party.

**What does "fancy" mixed nuts mean?**

It usually means a mix with more premium nuts like cashews, pecans and Brazil nuts, and few or no peanuts.

**How many nuts do I need for a board?**

About 1 ounce per person, or a small bowl for every 6 to 8 guests.

**How do I keep nuts away from a guest with a nut allergy?**

Serve them in a separate bowl with their own spoon, keep them away from the cheese and crackers, and label them.
