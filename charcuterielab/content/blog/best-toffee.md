> **Quick Answer:** To splurge, **Enstrom's Almond Toffee** from Colorado ($32.99 a pound), which The Nibble called possibly the best toffee in America. For a gift box, **See's Toffee-ettes**. For a classic that's easy to find, **Almond Roca** ($7.99 for a 10 oz canister). To break into a board on a budget, **Heath** bars.

## Toffee questions

**What is the best toffee to buy?**

For a splurge, Enstrom's Almond Toffee from Colorado. For a classic that's easy to find and shelf-stable, Almond Roca.

**What's the difference between toffee and buttercrunch?**

Both are butter and sugar cooked until crunchy. Buttercrunch, like Almond Roca, is usually lighter and coated in chocolate and nuts.

**Does toffee need to be refrigerated?**

Fresh butter toffee like Enstrom's does. Packaged toffee like Almond Roca and Heath is shelf-stable; keep it cool and dry.

**What cheese goes with toffee?**

Aged cheddar, aged Gouda and Parmesan. Their salty, crunchy crystals match the buttercrunch.

**How much toffee per person?**

About 1 oz per person on a dessert board.
