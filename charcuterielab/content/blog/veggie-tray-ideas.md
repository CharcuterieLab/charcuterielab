# Veggie Tray Ideas: What to Include, How Much to Buy and How to Arrange It

> **Quick Answer:** A great veggie tray needs crunchy vegetables in at least five colors, one or two dips in the middle, and a few extras like olives or pickles. Plan on about 3 to 4 ounces of vegetables per person when it's one of several appetizers, or 5 to 6 ounces if it's the main snack. Cut everything the day before and assemble it within an hour of serving.

## What to put on a veggie tray

Pick six to eight vegetables across these groups so the tray looks full and tastes varied.

| Job on the tray | Best picks | Prep |
|---|---|---|
| Sweet and crunchy | Carrots, bell peppers (red, yellow, orange), snap peas | Sticks, strips, whole peas |
| Cool and fresh | Cucumbers, celery | Spears or thick coins; celery in 4-inch sticks |
| Peppery | Radishes | Halved, or whole with a bit of stem |
| Juicy | Cherry or grape tomatoes | Whole |
| Hearty | Broccoli and cauliflower | Bite-size florets |
| Extras | Olives, cornichons, marinated artichokes, hummus | In small bowls |

Baby carrots and pre-cut florets save time. Whole carrots cut into sticks taste sweeter and look better, so use them if you have 10 extra minutes.

## The dips

Two dips cover most crowds: one creamy, one lighter.

- **Creamy:** ranch, French onion or spinach dip. See our rankings of the [best ranch dip](/blog/best-ranch-dip/), [best French onion dip](/blog/best-french-onion-dip/) and [best spinach dip](/blog/best-spinach-dip/).
- **Lighter:** hummus or tzatziki. See the [best store-bought hummus](/blog/best-hummus/).

Plan on about 2 tablespoons of dip per person, and put the dips in the center or one corner so every vegetable is within reach.

## How much to buy

| Guests | Vegetables (side appetizer) | Vegetables (main snack) | Dip |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 1½ to 2 lb | 2½ to 3 lb | about 1 cup |
| 12 | 2¼ to 3 lb | 3¾ to 4½ lb | about 1½ cups |
| 20 | 3¾ to 5 lb | 6¼ to 7½ lb | about 2½ cups |
| 30 | 5½ to 7½ lb | 9½ to 11¼ lb | about 3¾ cups |

These are weights after trimming. Buy about a quarter more of whole vegetables like peppers and broccoli to allow for stems and cores.

## How to arrange it

1. **Place the dip bowls first.** They anchor the tray.
2. **Add the big groups** (carrots, cucumbers, peppers) in long rows or wedges, with contrasting colors side by side: orange next to green, red next to white.
3. **Fill the gaps** with tomatoes, radishes and snap peas.
4. **Finish** with small bowls of olives or pickles and a few herb sprigs.

Rows of color read clearly from across the room and make the tray easy to refill.

## Make it ahead

- **The day before:** cut carrots, celery, peppers, broccoli and cauliflower. Store them in airtight containers lined with a damp paper towel. Keep carrots and celery in a little cold water to stay crisp.
- **The day of:** cut cucumbers and radishes, which go limp or bleed if cut too early.
- **Within an hour of serving:** assemble the tray and spoon out the dips.

Like any party food, don't leave cut vegetables and dairy dips out for more than 2 hours. Set out half and refill from the fridge.

## FAQ

**What vegetables go on a veggie tray?**

Carrots, celery, bell peppers, cucumbers, cherry tomatoes, broccoli, cauliflower, snap peas and radishes. Pick six to eight across different colors.

**How much veggie tray do I need per person?**

About 3 to 4 ounces of vegetables per person when it's one of several appetizers, or 5 to 6 ounces if it's the main snack.

**Can you make a veggie tray the night before?**

Yes. Cut sturdy vegetables the day before and store them in airtight containers with a damp paper towel. Cut cucumbers and radishes on the day, and assemble the tray within an hour of serving.

**What dip goes on a veggie tray?**

Ranch is the classic. Add a second, lighter dip such as hummus or tzatziki for variety.

**How long can a veggie tray sit out?**

No more than 2 hours, especially with dairy dips. Set out half and refill from the fridge.
