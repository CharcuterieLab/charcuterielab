<h2 id="how-to-choose">How to pick one on Amazon</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Check who ships it.</strong> "Ships from Amazon" usually means Prime speed. Harry &amp; David and Swiss Colony boxes often ship from the seller and take longer, so order early in December.</li><li><strong>Read the net weight, not the photo.</strong> The most common complaint across these listings is a box that looks smaller than pictured. Weight tells you how much food is really inside.</li><li><strong>Shelf-stable vs. cold-packed.</strong> Summer sausage and wax-sealed cheese boxes (Hickory Farms, Wisconsin, Swiss Colony) are fine in transit. Imported soft cheeses (igourmet) ship with ice packs and should go straight into the fridge.</li><li><strong>Crackers or not.</strong> Wisconsin Cheese Company's boxes skip crackers. Add a box of water crackers if you're gifting it as a ready-to-serve board.</li><li><strong>Send it to arrive on a weekday</strong> so it doesn't sit on a porch, and add a gift note in the order.</li></ul>

## Gift basket questions

**What is the best charcuterie gift basket on Amazon?**

Hickory Farms Signature Beef is the most-rated (4.6 stars from 2,300+ ratings) at about $45. For the most food, Harry & David's Ultimate box is over 6 pounds. For looks, the California Delicious crate.

**Do meat and cheese gift baskets need to be refrigerated?**

Summer sausage and wax-sealed cheese boxes are shelf-stable until opened, so they can sit under the tree. Refrigerate after opening. Boxes with soft imported cheeses ship cold and go straight into the fridge.

**How far ahead should I order for Christmas?**

Two weeks ahead is safe. Boxes that ship from the seller rather than Amazon can take 5 to 10 days in December.

**How much should I spend on a meat and cheese gift?**

$25 to $45 covers a thank-you gift, $45 to $90 a family gift, and $100 or more a box that feeds a party.

**What's the difference between a gift basket and a charcuterie board kit?**

A basket is sealed food to open later. A board kit, or a box that arrives arranged, is meant to be set out right away. Our gift boxes guide covers the arranged ones.
