# The Best Mustard for a Charcuterie Board, Ranked

> **Quick Answer:** Our top pick for mustard on a charcuterie board is Maille Old Style Whole Grain Mustard (4.8 out of 5). Best value: Grey Poupon Harvest Coarse Ground. Splurge: Pommery Moutarde de Meaux. We ranked 5 widely sold options on how they work on a board, published taste tests and awards, ingredients, value and how easy they are to find.

A small bowl of mustard makes cured meat, pâté and sharp cheese taste better. Whole-grain mustard looks best on a board; a smooth Dijon is the sharper option.

### 1. Maille Old Style Whole Grain Mustard

**★★★★★ 4.8 out of 5** · Best overall · Best for: Whole-grain crunch · $$ mid-range

[America's Test Kitchen](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/taste_tests/2409-whole-grain-mustard)'s best whole-seed mustard, with a nice crunch and pop and bright acidity.

- **Why we like it:** Test-kitchen winner. Everywhere.
- **Keep in mind:** Milder than some.

### 2. Maille Dijon Originale

**★★★★½ 4.6 out of 5** · Best for: Best Dijon · $$ mid-range

[Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/1704481/store-bought-dijon-mustard-brands-ranked/)'s number one Dijon, balancing sharpness with a note of fruitiness.

- **Why we like it:** Top-ranked Dijon.
- **Keep in mind:** Smooth, so less visual texture.

### 3. Grey Poupon Harvest Coarse Ground

**★★★★½ 4.5 out of 5** · Best value · Best for: Budget coarse mustard · $ budget

[America's Test Kitchen](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/taste_tests/2409-whole-grain-mustard)'s best coarse-ground mustard: very cohesive, gently crunchy and mildly spicy.

**★★★★☆ 3.8 out of 5** · Best splurge · Best for: The gift-worthy pot · $$$ premium

The classic French whole-grain mustard in a wax-sealed crock. [America's Test Kitchen](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/taste_tests/2409-whole-grain-mustard) recommended it, noting softer seeds and a salty edge.

## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** Mustard seed, vinegar or wine, and salt. Whole-grain means you should see intact seeds.

**Skip:** Yellow ballpark mustard on a nice board. It's too sweet and bright for cured meat.

## Mustard questions

**What is the best mustard for a charcuterie board?**

Maille Old Style Whole Grain, America's Test Kitchen's top whole-seed mustard.

**Dijon or whole-grain for a board?**

Whole-grain looks better and has texture; Dijon is sharper. Serve one of each on a large board.

**What goes with mustard on a board?**

Salami, summer sausage, pâté, aged cheddar and pretzels.
