<h2 id="what-to-look-for">What to look for</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Look for:</strong> &quot;Sparkling cider&quot; in a grocery store is non-alcoholic, carbonated apple juice. Hard cider is a different, alcoholic drink, usually sold with beer. Look for 100% juice on the label.</li><li class="sk-x"><strong>Skip:</strong> Ciders that go flat. <a href="https://www.thedailymeal.com/1692709/store-bought-apple-ciders-ranked-worst-best/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Daily Meal</a> found Aldi&#x27;s Nature&#x27;s Nectar sparkling cider lacked bubbles and apple flavor, and its Louisburg sparkling cider had lost its fizz. Chill bottles well, and open them just before toasting.</li></ul>
:::

<h2 id="faq">Sparkling cider questions</h2>
:::

**What is the best non-alcoholic sparkling cider?**

Martinelli's Sparkling Cider, which scored a perfect 10 in Sporked's taste test. Welch's Sparkling Apple Cider is the drier pick.

**Is sparkling cider alcoholic?**

No. Grocery-store sparkling cider like Martinelli's is carbonated apple juice. Hard cider is the alcoholic version.

**Does sparkling cider go with charcuterie?**

Yes. Its bubbles and acidity cut through salty meat and rich cheese, and the apple flavor pairs especially well with cheddar and brie.

**How much sparkling cider do I need for a party?**

A 25.4 oz bottle pours about five glasses. For a toast, one bottle covers 6 to 8 people.
