# The Best Sparkling Cider for a Charcuterie Board, Ranked (Non-Alcoholic)

> **Quick Answer:** Our top pick for sparkling cider on a charcuterie board is Martinelli's Sparkling Cider (4.8 out of 5). Driest: Welch's Sparkling Apple Cider. For the holidays: R.W. Knudsen Sparkling Cider & Spice. Fruit flavor: Martinelli's Sparkling Apple-Cranberry. We ranked 8 widely sold options on how they work on a board, published taste tests and awards, ingredients, value and how easy they are to find.

Sparkling cider is the toast for everyone who isn't drinking: kids, drivers and anyone skipping alcohol. It works on a board the same way prosecco does, cutting through salty meat and rich cheese, with apple sweetness that suits sharp cheddar and brie. These rankings combine Sporked's sparkling cider taste test, Rhett & Link's Martinelli's tasting and the sparkling picks in Mashed's and Daily Meal's apple cider rankings.

## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** "Sparkling cider" in a grocery store is non-alcoholic, carbonated apple juice. Hard cider is a different, alcoholic drink, usually sold with beer. Look for 100% juice on the label.

**Skip:** Ciders that go flat. [Daily Meal](https://www.thedailymeal.com/1692709/store-bought-apple-ciders-ranked-worst-best/) found Aldi's Nature's Nectar sparkling cider lacked bubbles and apple flavor, and its Louisburg sparkling cider had lost its fizz. Chill bottles well, and open them just before toasting.

**How much to buy:** a 25.4 oz bottle pours about five 5-oz glasses. For a toast, one bottle covers 6 to 8 people.
**What it pairs with:** sharp cheddar, brie, Gouda, prosciutto, apples, pears and salted nuts. The drier Welch's suits salty meat best. See our [zero-proof pairings](/pairings/zero-proof/) for more drinks.

## Sparkling cider questions

**What is the best non-alcoholic sparkling cider?**

Martinelli's Sparkling Cider, which scored a perfect 10 in Sporked's taste test. Welch's Sparkling Apple Cider is the drier pick.

**Is sparkling cider alcoholic?**

No. Grocery-store sparkling cider like Martinelli's is carbonated apple juice. Hard cider is the alcoholic version.

**Does sparkling cider go with charcuterie?**

Yes. Its bubbles and acidity cut through salty meat and rich cheese, and the apple flavor pairs especially well with cheddar and brie.

**How much sparkling cider do I need for a party?**

A 25.4 oz bottle pours about five glasses. For a toast, one bottle covers 6 to 8 people.
