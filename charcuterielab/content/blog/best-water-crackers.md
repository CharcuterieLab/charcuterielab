### 1. Carr's Table Water Crackers, Original

**★★★★½ 4.5 out of 5** · Best overall · Best for: Every board · $$ mid-range

The default for good reason. The label is short (wheat flour, palm oil, salt, plus added vitamins), the cracker is thin and crisp, and it shows up on nearly every published list of the best crackers for cheese. (Source: [Carr's Table Water Crackers ingredient label (Kellogg SmartLabel)](https://smartlabel.kelloggs.com/Product/Index/10059290573302), [Cheapism](https://www.aol.com/board-basics-15-best-crackers-203000992.html), [Striped Spatula](https://stripedspatula.com/best-crackers-for-cheese/).)

- **Why we like it:** Neutral, lightly toasty flavor. Thin but holds a slice of hard cheese. Sold almost everywhere.
- **Keep in mind:** Small box for the price. Breaks under very firm spreads.
- **Where to buy:** Most grocery stores, cracker or cheese aisle.

### 2. Carr's Table Water Crackers, Cracked Pepper

**★★★½☆ 3.6 out of 5** · Best for: Soft and goat cheese · $$ mid-range

The same cracker with black pepper baked in. It's the one flavored water cracker that still works on a mixed board, because pepper suits almost every cheese.

- **Why we like it:** Adds a little bite to mild cheese. Same sturdiness as the original.
- **Keep in mind:** Not neutral, so pair it rather than use it for everything.
- **Where to buy:** Most grocery stores.

### 3. 365 by Whole Foods Market Organic Water Crackers

**★★★½☆ 3.5 out of 5** · Best value · Best for: Organic on a budget · $ budget

An organic water cracker at a store-brand price. It comes in original and cracked pepper and does the same job as the name brand.

- **Why we like it:** Organic. Low price per box.
- **Keep in mind:** Only at Whole Foods and Amazon. Few published reviews.
- **Where to buy:** Whole Foods, Amazon.

### 4. Good & Gather Water Crackers

**★★★½☆ 3.5 out of 5** · Best for: Cheapest close match · $ budget

Target's store brand makes a plain water cracker that fills the same role for less. Buy two boxes for a party.

- **Why we like it:** Low price. Easy to find at Target.
- **Keep in mind:** Few published reviews. Plainer toast flavor.
- **Where to buy:** Target.

### 5. Trader Joe's Cracker Assortment

**★★★½☆ 3.3 out of 5** · Best for: Variety on a budget · $ budget

Trader Joe's water crackers come in a four-cracker assortment box. [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2040326/trader-joes-crackers-ranked-worst-best/) called them very plain, which it noted makes them a good crunchy base for pretty much anything.

- **Why we like it:** Four crackers in one box. Low price.
- **Keep in mind:** Only part of the box is water crackers.
- **Where to buy:** Trader Joe's.

### 6. Olina's Bakehouse Wafer Crackers

**★★★☆☆ 3.0 out of 5** · Best splurge · Best for: Boards you'll photograph · $$$ premium

Thinner and more artisan-looking than a standard water cracker, in natural, cracked pepper and seeded versions. It's the pick when the board is going on camera.

- **Why we like it:** Very thin and elegant. Several flavors.
- **Keep in mind:** More fragile. Highest price per ounce here.
- **Where to buy:** Whole Foods, Walmart, specialty grocers, Amazon.

## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** The ingredients should start with flour, water or oil, and salt. If butter is near the top, it's a butter cracker with a water-cracker name, and it will mute whatever you put on it.

**Skip:** Flavored water crackers like roasted garlic or sesame for a general board. They stop being neutral, which is the whole reason to buy a water cracker.

## Water crackers questions

**What is the best water cracker for a charcuterie board?**

Carr's Table Water Crackers, Original. They are thin, neutral, sturdy enough for hard cheese and sold almost everywhere.

**Are store-brand water crackers as good as Carr's?**

Close. Store brands from Target and Whole Foods do the same job for less, though they have far fewer published reviews.

**How many water crackers per person?**

Plan 6 to 8 crackers per guest before a meal, 10 to 12 when the board is the party food and 12 to 15 when it is dinner.
