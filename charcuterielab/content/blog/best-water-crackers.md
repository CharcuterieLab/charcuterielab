<h2 id="what-to-look-for">What to look for</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Look for:</strong> The ingredients should start with flour, water or oil, and salt. If butter is near the top, it&#x27;s a butter cracker with a water-cracker name, and it will mute whatever you put on it.</li><li class="sk-x"><strong>Skip:</strong> Flavored water crackers like roasted garlic or sesame for a general board. They stop being neutral, which is the whole reason to buy a water cracker.</li></ul>
:::

<h2 id="faq">Water crackers questions</h2>
:::

**What is the best water cracker for a charcuterie board?**

Carr's Table Water Crackers, Original. They are thin, neutral, sturdy enough for hard cheese and sold almost everywhere.

**Are store-brand water crackers as good as Carr's?**

Close. Store brands from Target and Whole Foods do the same job for less, though they have far fewer published reviews.

**How many water crackers per person?**

Plan 6 to 8 crackers per guest before a meal, 10 to 12 when the board is the party food and 12 to 15 when it is dinner.
