<h2 id="how-to-choose">How to heat and serve mulled wine</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Warm, never boil.</strong> Heat it in a pot or slow cooker until steaming. Boiling cooks off the alcohol and makes the spices bitter.</li><li><strong>Microwave a single mug</strong> for about a minute if you're not making a batch.</li><li><strong>How much:</strong> a 1-liter bottle fills about six 6-ounce mugs.</li><li><strong>Garnish</strong> with an orange slice and a cinnamon stick.</li><li><strong>Next to it,</strong> set out a winter board: aged cheddar, Stilton, gingerbread, spiced nuts, clementines and dark chocolate.</li><li><strong>Label it</strong> so kids and non-drinkers know which pot is which.</li></ul>

## Mulled wine questions

**What is the best mulled wine to buy?**

Gerstacker Nürnberger Glühwein, a German Christmas-market style, about $11 a liter. For value, Aldi's Winterliebe Glühwein is about $6.

**What's the difference between mulled wine and Glühwein?**

Glühwein is the German and Austrian name for mulled wine: red wine warmed with sugar, cinnamon, cloves and citrus.

**How do you heat bottled mulled wine?**

Warm it in a pot or slow cooker until steaming, but don't let it boil. A single mug takes about a minute in the microwave.

**Does Trader Joe's sell mulled wine?**

Yes, Trader Joe's sells a seasonal Hark & Holly Glühwein in stores that carry wine.

**What goes with mulled wine?**

Aged cheddar, Stilton, gingerbread, spiced nuts, clementines and dark chocolate.
