> **Quick Answer:** The original is **Skillet Bacon Jam** from Seattle, the spread that started the trend. The easiest to find is **TBJ Gourmet**, made with uncured bacon (about $10 for 9 oz). For a cheese board, **Stonewall Kitchen Bourbon Bacon Jam**, sweetened with Maine maple syrup. On a budget, **Target's Good & Gather** for $4.99.

<h2 id="how-to-choose">Bacon jam on a charcuterie board</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Serve it at room temperature</strong> in a small bowl with a spoon. Cold bacon jam is stiff; 20 minutes out of the fridge loosens it.</li><li><strong>Spoon it over a soft cheese:</strong> warm brie, goat cheese or a block of cream cheese with crackers is the fastest crowd-pleaser.</li><li><strong>Pair it with</strong> sharp cheddar, aged Gouda, Manchego, crostini, apple slices and pickles to cut the richness.</li><li><strong>On a burger or slider bar</strong>, it&#x27;s the special sauce bacon jam was invented to be.</li><li><strong>Refrigerate after opening</strong> and use within the time on the label.</li></ul>

## Bacon jam questions

**What is the best bacon jam?**

Skillet, the Seattle original. For a jar you can find at the supermarket, TBJ Gourmet; for a cheese board, Stonewall Kitchen Bourbon Bacon Jam.

**What is bacon jam made of?**

Cooked bacon slow-simmered with onions, vinegar and brown sugar until it's thick and spreadable.

**Does bacon jam need to be refrigerated?**

Unopened jars like Skillet's are shelf-stable. Refrigerate after opening.

**What cheese goes with bacon jam?**

Brie, goat cheese and cream cheese for spreading; sharp cheddar, aged Gouda and Manchego for slicing.

**Is bacon jam like onion jam?**

It's close: both are slow-cooked onion jams, and bacon jam adds bacon for smoke and salt.
