## Small bowl questions

**What size bowls go on a charcuterie board?**

Mostly 2 to 6 ounces: pinch bowls for jam, honey and mustard, ramekins for olives, nuts and dips.

**How many small bowls do I need for a board?**

Three for a small board and five for a large one.

**What is a pinch bowl?**

A very small bowl, usually about 2 ounces, made for staging ingredients and serving condiments.

**Are ramekins good for charcuterie?**

Yes. A 4 to 6 ounce ramekin is the right size for olives, nuts and dips.

**Should bowls go on the board or next to it?**

On the board. Place them first, then arrange meat and cheese around them.
