# The Best Provolone for a Charcuterie Board, Ranked

> **Quick Answer:** Our top pick for provolone on a charcuterie board is Boar's Head Picante Provolone (4.7 out of 5). Smoky pick: Red Apple Smoked Provolone. Value: Kroger Smoke Flavored Provolone. We ranked 8 widely sold options on how they work on a board, published taste tests and awards, ingredients, value and how easy they are to find.

Provolone is the Italian-American deli cheese: mild and milky when young, sharp and tangy when aged. Sharp (picante) provolone is the one for a board, cut into chunks next to salami and olives. Mild sliced provolone works for roll-ups and slider boards. These rankings combine Sporked's provolone taste test of 16 brands, its overall cheese rankings and two 2026 sliced-cheese rankings from The Takeout and Tasting Table.

*How we ranked these: these are researched picks, not a side-by-side tasting. We scored each product on published taste tests and awards, its ingredient list, price and where you can buy it, plus how that style of product behaves on a board. Sources are listed at the end, and the full method is on our [buying guides hub](/blog/best-charcuterie-brands/#how-we-rank). Prices and stock last checked October 2026.*

### 1. Boar's Head Picante Provolone

**★★★★½ 4.7 out of 5** · Best overall · Best for: Sharp provolone for the board · $$ mid-range

[Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/best-provolone-cheese/)'s #1 provolone, 10 out of 10: the bite on the finish smacks you in the mouth in the most wonderful way. It's also the provolone pick in Sporked's [best cheese](https://sporked.com/article/best-cheese/) rankings.

- **Why we like it:** Sharp, funky and the best-reviewed provolone you can buy.
- **Keep in mind:** Strong for kids and cautious eaters. Pair it with a mild cheese.
- **Where to buy:** Boar's Head deli counters.

## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** "Picante" or "sharp" means aged longer, with more bite: the best choice for a board. "Dolce" or mild provolone is younger and softer. Smoked provolone adds a smoky note; "smoke flavored" means added flavoring rather than real smoke.

**Skip:** Bland store-brand slices for the board itself. [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2169853/sliced-cheese-brands-ranked-worst-best/) found 365's provolone especially flavorless. Save the cheap slices for sandwiches.

**How much to buy:** about 1 oz per guest when it's one of three cheeses.
**How to serve it:** cut sharp provolone into rough chunks or thick sticks; roll mild slices around salami or roasted peppers. It pairs well with salami, soppressata, olives, pepperoncini, figs and a Chianti.

## Provolone questions

**What is the best provolone cheese brand?**

Boar's Head Picante Provolone: Sporked's top provolone with a perfect 10. Primo Taglio, the Albertsons store brand, scored 9.5.

**Is provolone good on a charcuterie board?**

Yes, especially sharp (picante) provolone cut into chunks. It goes with every Italian cured meat.

**What is the difference between mild and sharp provolone?**

Sharp (picante) provolone is aged longer and has a tangy bite. Mild (dolce) provolone is younger, softer and milkier.

**Is smoked provolone really smoked?**

Some is, like Red Apple. Others, labeled smoke flavored, use added flavoring.
