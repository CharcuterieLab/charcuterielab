<h2 id="different">What makes these charcuterie books different</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks">
<li><strong>A shopping list for every board:</strong> the exact items and amounts, so you buy enough and nothing goes to waste. Most board books skip this.</li>
<li><strong>The order to build it:</strong> numbered steps for where each item goes, so it looks like the picture.</li>
<li><strong>The why:</strong> the pairing rule behind each board (salt with sweet, fat with acid), so you can riff once you get it.</li>
<li><strong>Swaps:</strong> what to use when the store is out of something, or a guest can't eat it.</li>
<li><strong>One big idea per book:</strong> shapes, countries, two people, keto, vegan or ingredients. Pick the one that fits the person.</li>
</ul>

**What makes a charcuterie book unique?**

Most charcuterie board books are photo books: they show a finished board but don't tell you what to buy or how much. A useful one gives every board a shopping list with amounts, the order to build it and swaps. Ours also each do one thing other board books don't, like shaped showpiece boards, full keto macros or boards sized for exactly two.

**Is there a charcuterie book with shaped or themed boards?**

Yes. Impossible Boards has 25 boards built into shapes, like a salami dragon, a giant octopus and a fondue volcano, each with the one trick that makes the shape work and a shopping list for 12 to 15 guests.

**Is there a keto or vegan charcuterie book?**

Yes. Keto & Low-Carb Boards has 20 boards at 4 to 9 g net carbs per serving with full macros. 15 Show-Stopping Plant-Based Boards has 15 boards with no meat and no dairy.

**Which charcuterie book is best for beginners?**
