# The Best Feta Brands for a Charcuterie Board, Ranked

> **Quick Answer:** Our top pick for feta on a charcuterie board is Mt Vikos Traditional Feta (4.5 out of 5). Value: Athenos Feta Chunk. Splurge: Roussas Barrel-Aged Feta. We ranked 8 widely sold options on how they work on a board, published taste tests and awards, ingredients, value and how easy they are to find.

Feta at the grocery store runs from creamy Greek sheep's milk blocks in brine to dry, salty crumbles. For a board you want a block you can cut or break into chunks, so these rankings favor blocks over crumbles, then lean on two published taste tests.

*How we ranked these: these are researched picks, not a side-by-side tasting. We scored each product on published taste tests and awards, its ingredient list, price and where you can buy it, plus how that style of product behaves on a board. ### 1. Mt Vikos Traditional Feta

**★★★★½ 4.5 out of 5** · Best overall · Best for: The real Greek feta · $$ mid-range

Made in Greece from sheep's and goat's milk. [Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/best-feta/) named it the best of the best with 10 out of 10: creamy, moist without brine, and more flavor than just tang. [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2130882/store-bought-feta-cheese-ranked) placed it 6th of 9, finding it very salty with mild acidity.

- **Why we like it:** Creamy, traditional and easy to find.
- **Keep in mind:** Saltier than most. Pair it with fruit or honey.
- **Where to buy:** Whole Foods and many grocery cheese cases.

### 2. Athenos Feta Chunk

**★★★★½ 4.3 out of 5** · Best value · Best for: A firm block that cuts cleanly · $ budget

[Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/best-feta/) gave the chunk 9 out of 10 for its springy, fresh-curd texture that cuts cleanly. [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2130882/store-bought-feta-cheese-ranked) ranked the Athenos crumbles 3rd of 9 for their aged flavor and tang.

- **Why we like it:** Cheap, everywhere, and it holds a neat cube.
- **Keep in mind:** Drier than a brined Greek feta, and made from cow's milk.
- **Where to buy:** Nearly every grocery store.

### 3. Odyssey Feta Chunk in Brine

**★★★★☆ 4.2 out of 5** · Best for: A creamy brined block · $ budget

Made in Wisconsin and sold swimming in brine. [Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/best-feta/) called it very creamy and gave it 8.5 out of 10, with a flavor like olive brine.

- **Why we like it:** Creamy and moist, good with olives and cured meat.
- **Keep in mind:** Brined cow's milk feta, so milder than Greek.
- **Where to buy:** Grocery cheese cases.

### 4. Trader Joe's Authentic Greek Feta in Brine

**★★★★☆ 4.2 out of 5** · Best for: Budget Greek feta · $ budget

Packed in brine, which keeps it moist and adds salt. [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2130882/store-bought-feta-cheese-ranked) ranked it 5th of 9 and found it fresher and younger-tasting than some.

- **Why we like it:** Greek feta in brine at a low price.
- **Keep in mind:** Mild next to Mt Vikos or Roussas.
- **Where to buy:** Trader Joe's.

### 5. Roussas Barrel-Aged Feta

**★★★★☆ 4.1 out of 5** · Best splurge · Best for: A feta guests notice · $$$ premium

Aged in wooden barrels. [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2130882/store-bought-feta-cheese-ranked) ranked it 2nd of 9 for its complexity and slight nuttiness, with a creamy texture that still crumbles.

- **Why we like it:** The most complex feta here.
- **Keep in mind:** Pricier and harder to find.
- **Where to buy:** Specialty grocers, Greek markets and some cheese counters.

### 6. Primo Taglio Feta Cheese Crumbles

**★★★★☆ 4.0 out of 5** · Best for: Topping, not slicing · $ budget

[Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2130882/store-bought-feta-cheese-ranked)'s top feta: complex, with "a yeastiness and nuttiness", well-balanced acidity and plenty of salt.

- **Why we like it:** The best-tasting crumbles in a published test.
- **Keep in mind:** Crumbles don't hold a shape on a board. Use them on whipped feta or a salad.
- **Where to buy:** Safeway, Albertsons and Vons.

### 7. Valbreso French Sheep's Milk Feta

**★★★★☆ 3.9 out of 5** · Best for: A funkier feta · $$ mid-range

A French sheep's milk cheese aged in brine. [Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/best-feta/) gave it 9 out of 10 and called it very funky. It doesn't say "feta" on every label, because in Europe only Greek cheese can use the name.

- **Why we like it:** Creamy and tangy, with more depth than cow's milk feta.
- **Keep in mind:** Strong for cautious guests.
- **Where to buy:** Cheese counters and specialty grocers.

### 8. Président Feta Chunk

**★★★★☆ 3.8 out of 5** · Best for: A safe, mild block · $ budget

[Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2130882/store-bought-feta-cheese-ranked) ranked it 7th of 9: solid, affordable and inoffensive, with a clean dairy flavor and decent salt and tang.

- **Why we like it:** Mild enough for everyone.
- **Keep in mind:** Not much complexity.
- **Where to buy:** Most grocery stores.

## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** Sheep's milk, or sheep's and goat's milk, and packed in brine. "Greek" and the PDO mark mean traditional feta. Cow's milk feta is milder and firmer.

**Skip:** Pre-crumbled feta for the board itself. It's dry and won't hold a shape. Keep crumbles for salads and whipped feta.

**Serving it:** feta is a fresh cheese, so take it out of the fridge 15 to 20 minutes before serving. Pat the block dry, cut it into cubes or break it into rough chunks, and finish with olive oil, oregano or honey.

## Feta questions

**What is the best feta cheese brand?**

Mt Vikos Traditional Feta, which scored 10 out of 10 in Sporked's test. Roussas Barrel-Aged ranked 2nd in Tasting Table's test, and Athenos Feta Chunk is the best value.

**Is block or crumbled feta better?**

Block feta, ideally in brine. It's creamier and holds a shape on a board. Crumbles are drier and better for salads.

**Is feta safe during pregnancy?**

Pasteurized feta is generally considered safe. Check the label, and skip raw-milk feta.

**How long before serving should feta come out of the fridge?**

15 to 20 minutes.
