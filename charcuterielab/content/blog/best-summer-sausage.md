# The Best Summer Sausage for a Charcuterie Board, Ranked

> **Quick Answer:** Our top pick for summer sausage on a charcuterie board is Cloverdale Original Tangy Summer Sausage (4.4 out of 5). Best value: Trader Joe's Natural Turkey Summer Sausage. ## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** The meat listed first, and a mild tang from fermentation. Many are beef, some are beef and pork, and a few are game meats.

**Skip:** Cutting summer sausage into thin slices. It's better thick-cut into coins or half-moons.

## Summer sausage questions

**What is the best summer sausage for a charcuterie board?**

Old Wisconsin for thick slicing and easy availability, or Cloverdale for the classic tangy flavor that topped Sporked's test.

**Does summer sausage need to be refrigerated?**

Unopened, many are shelf-stable; once opened, refrigerate it. Follow the label.

**How do I cut summer sausage for a board?**

Cut it into coins about 1/4 inch thick, or halve the coins into half-moons.
