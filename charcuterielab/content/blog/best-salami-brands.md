<h2 id="what-to-look-for">What to look for</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Look for:</strong> Pork (and maybe veal), salt, wine and spices. Long ingredient lists with fillers or corn syrup are worth avoiding.</li><li class="sk-x"><strong>Skip:</strong> Pre-sliced salami that has been sitting in a tray. The edges dry and curl. Buy a chub and slice it, or buy it at the counter.</li></ul>

<h2 id="faq">Salami questions</h2>
:::

**What is the best salami brand for a charcuterie board?**

Olli Salumeria, for its well-reviewed variety pack, with Volpi Genoa as the classic single choice.

**Is Genoa or hard salami better for a board?**

Genoa is softer and more garlicky; hard salami is drier and firmer. Genoa is easier to fold and more versatile.

**How much salami per person?**

Meat in total: 2 oz per guest before a meal, 3 oz when the board is the party food, 4 oz when it is dinner. Salami is usually part of that.
