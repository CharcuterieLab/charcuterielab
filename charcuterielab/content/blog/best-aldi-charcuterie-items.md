# The Best Aldi Charcuterie Board Items, Ranked

> **Quick Answer:** The best Aldi cheese for a charcuterie board is Emporium Selection Not Your Average Cheddar (4.7 out of 5). Best meat: Appleton Farms Prosciutto. Best soft cheese: Emporium Selection Double Crème Brie. A full board for 6 to 8 from these picks costs well under $40.

Aldi is the cheapest place to build a good-looking board. Its Emporium Selection cheeses and Appleton Farms meats do better in taste tests than their prices suggest. These are the picks that work best, with the ones to skip at the end.

*How we ranked these: these are researched picks, not a side-by-side tasting. We scored each product on published taste tests, awards and shopper ratings, its ingredients, price and how easy it is to find, plus how it behaves on a board. Store stock changes, especially for seasonal items, so check your local store. Sources are listed at the end, and the full method is on our [buying guides hub](/blog/best-charcuterie-brands/#how-we-rank). Prices and stock last checked October 2026.*

### 1. Emporium Selection Not Your Average Cheddar

**★★★★½ 4.7 out of 5** · Best overall · Best for: Everyone at the table · $ budget

A cheddar blended with Parmesan flavor. [Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/aldi-cheese-ranking/) put it first of 15 Aldi cheeses, 9 out of 10, for its tangy cheddar balanced by sweet, nutty Parmesan notes.

- **Why we like it:** The top Aldi cheese in Sporked's test.
- **Keep in mind:** Aldi only.
- **Where to find it:** Aldi cheese case.

### 2. Specially Selected Traditional Burrata

**★★★★½ 4.5 out of 5** · Best for: A showpiece center · $ budget

Fresh mozzarella filled with cream, our best-value pick in the [burrata ranking](/blog/best-burrata/).

- **Why we like it:** Makes a cheap board look expensive.
- **Keep in mind:** Serve it within a day or two of buying, and cut it open at the table.
- **Where to find it:** Aldi cheese case.

### 3. Emporium Selection Le Gruyère

**★★★★½ 4.5 out of 5** · Best for: A nutty, firm cheese · $ budget

A Swiss-style Gruyère that [Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/aldi-cheese-ranking/) rated 8 out of 10, calling it premium quality at a budget price.

- **Why we like it:** Cheese-counter flavor for a fraction of the price.
- **Keep in mind:** Firm, so slice it thin or cut it into sticks.
- **Where to find it:** Aldi cheese case.

### 4. Emporium Selection Double Crème Brie

**★★★★½ 4.3 out of 5** · Best soft cheese · Best for: The soft cheese · $ budget

A small, rich brie wheel for about $4. Both [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/1936495/aldi-products-charcuterie-board/) and [Chowhound](https://www.chowhound.com/2106125/aldi-affordable-gourmet-charcuterie-board-picks/) put it on their Aldi board lists for its buttery, earthy flavor.

- **Why we like it:** Every board needs a soft cheese, and this one is cheap.
- **Keep in mind:** Take it out 20 to 30 minutes before serving.
- **Where to find it:** Aldi cheese case, about $3.95.

### 5. Appleton Farms Prosciutto

**★★★★☆ 4.2 out of 5** · Best meat · Best for: The main meat · $ budget

Our top pick in the [prosciutto ranking](/blog/best-prosciutto-brands/), for its strong reviews and low price. [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/1936495/aldi-products-charcuterie-board/) liked its fatty, salty richness on a board.

- **Why we like it:** The best-reviewed cheap prosciutto we found.
- **Keep in mind:** 4 oz packs go fast. Buy one per 4 to 5 guests.
- **Where to find it:** Aldi deli case.

### 6. Emporium Selection Vintage Irish Cheddar

**★★★★☆ 4.2 out of 5** · Best for: A sharp cheddar · $ budget

An aged Irish cheddar that [Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/aldi-cheese-ranking/) rated 7.5 out of 10: toasty, a little smoky, salty and creamy.

- **Why we like it:** An aged, crumbly cheddar for very little money.
- **Keep in mind:** Crumbly, so cut it into rough chunks.
- **Where to find it:** Aldi cheese case.

### 7. Emporium Selection Dill Havarti

**★★★★☆ 4.0 out of 5** · Best for: A mild, herby cheese · $ budget

Creamy Havarti with dill. [Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/aldi-cheese-ranking/) gave it 8.5 out of 10 and said it tastes like pickle cheese, and [Chowhound](https://www.chowhound.com/2106125/aldi-affordable-gourmet-charcuterie-board-picks/) liked the bright contrast it adds.

- **Why we like it:** Soft, mild and easy for kids.
- **Keep in mind:** The dill is strong if you don't like pickles.
- **Where to find it:** Aldi cheese case, about $3.29.

### 8. Emporium Selection Borgonzola

**★★★★☆ 4.0 out of 5** · Best for: A gentle blue · $ budget

A triple-cream blue with a soft rind. [Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/aldi-cheese-ranking/) rated it 8.5 out of 10: ultra creamy, salty and savory.

- **Why we like it:** A blue cheese for people who think they don't like blue cheese.
- **Keep in mind:** Soft and rich, so a small piece goes a long way.
- **Where to find it:** Aldi cheese case.

### 9. Emporium Selection Manchego DOP

**★★★★☆ 3.9 out of 5** · Best for: A Spanish board · $ budget

A protected-origin Spanish Manchego. [Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/aldi-cheese-ranking/) called it a versatile, salty and mild board cheese, 7 out of 10.

- **Why we like it:** Real DOP Manchego for very little.
- **Keep in mind:** Milder than an older Manchego.
- **Where to find it:** Aldi cheese case.

### 10. Savoritz Six Cracker Assortment

**★★★½☆ 3.6 out of 5** · Best for: Variety on a budget · $ budget

Six cracker shapes in one box. [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/1936495/aldi-products-charcuterie-board/) and [Chowhound](https://www.chowhound.com/2106125/aldi-affordable-gourmet-charcuterie-board-picks/) both liked the mix of shapes for the look of the board.

- **Why we like it:** One box gives the board variety.
- **Keep in mind:** Some of the crackers are buttery and break easily.
- **Where to find it:** Aldi cracker aisle, about $4.19.

### 11. Appleton Farms Salami Collection

**★★★½☆ 3.6 out of 5** · Best for: A second meat · $ budget

Three salami flavors in one 12 oz pack, picked by [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/1936495/aldi-products-charcuterie-board/) for convenience.

- **Why we like it:** Three meats for the price of one pack.
- **Keep in mind:** Pre-sliced and on the thin side.
- **Where to find it:** Aldi deli case.

### 12. Specially Selected Cheese Pairing Spread Set

**★★★½☆ 3.4 out of 5** · Best for: The sweet element · $$ mid-range

Four small jars, including fig honey and cherry rosemary. [Chowhound](https://www.chowhound.com/2106125/aldi-affordable-gourmet-charcuterie-board-picks/) liked the sweet contrast they add, at about $7.69.

- **Why we like it:** Four spreads in one box.
- **Keep in mind:** An Aldi Find, so it's in stores for a limited time, often around the holidays.
- **Where to find it:** Aldi Finds aisle.

## Building a board from these

For 6 to 8 people, buy Not Your Average Cheddar, the Double Crème Brie and Le Gruyère, plus the Appleton Farms prosciutto and salami collection, the cracker assortment, grapes and dried apricots. It comes to well under $40.

Plan on about 3 oz of cheese and 3 oz of meat per guest when the board is the party food, or 2 oz each before a meal. For more cheap boards, see our [budget charcuterie board](/blog/budget-charcuterie-board/) and the [$20 charcuterie board challenge](/blog/20-dollar-charcuterie-board-challenge/).

## What to skip

In [Daily Meal](https://www.thedailymeal.com/2062703/aldi-emporium-selection-cheese-ranked/)'s test of Emporium Selection cheeses, the garlic herb goat cheese tasted mostly of herbs and the Parmesan was too hard and sour. The Applewood smoked Gruyère is very smoky, so save it for a barbecue board.
