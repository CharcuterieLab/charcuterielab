## Make your own cheese advent calendar

Cheaper than most boxes ($40 to $60), and it works anywhere.

1. **Buy 6 cheeses and 6 jams.** About 6 oz each. Mix soft, blue, firm, aged and one fun one (smoked or cranberry).
2. **Cut each cheese in two.** That's 12 pieces.
3. **Wrap and number them 1 to 12.** Soft and blue go first, since they keep the shortest.
4. **Each night,** take one out 30 to 60 minutes early and serve it with a jam and crackers.

Each piece is about 3 oz, enough for two. For 24 nights, buy 12 cheeses. Need help picking? See our [best brand guides](/blog/best-charcuterie-brands/).
