# Cheese Advent Calendars 2026: Every One Announced So Far

> **Quick Answer:** As of October 20, 2026, three cheese-board advent calendars are confirmed for this season in the US: the **12 Days of Murray's** (nine cheeses plus jams, honey and crackers), the **Williams Sonoma 12 Days of Cheese** crate ($129.95), and **Bonne Maman's 24 mini jam jars** ($55.99), which pair well with a cheese board. Aldi, Tillamook and others haven't announced their 2026 calendars yet. Aldi's are expected in early November. Outside the US, the UK has the most choice, from The Crafty Herd at £14 to cheesemonger calendars at £135 to £224. Germany, Sweden and Australia each have at least one. We'll update this page as more launch.

*Last checked October 20, 2026. Advent calendars sell out fast and prices change, so check the retailer before you plan around one.*

### 12 Days of Murray's Cheese

[Sporked previewed the 2026 box](https://sporked.com/?p=79041) on October 14 and called it a new design. The cheeses include Manchego PDO, truffle cheddar, Merlot BellaVitano, smoked Gouda, Wensleydale with cranberries and Murray's English cheddar, plus Divina fig, sour cherry and Calabrian chili-orange spreads, Greek honey, and Effie's cocoa cakes. When we checked, [Murray's page](https://murrayscheese.com/dp/12-days-of-murray-s-cheese) listed it at $65 but showed it as sold out, with a sign-up for restock alerts.

**Best for:** a cheese lover who'd use it as a 12-night tasting. The portions are big enough to build a small board each night.

### Williams Sonoma 12 Days of Cheese

Listed at $129.95 in [Good Morning America's 2026 advent calendar roundup](https://goodmorningamerica.com/shop/story/unique-advent-calendars-2026-136718827). In past years the crate has held cheeses from Beehive Cheese of Utah. It's the most giftable box here and the most expensive per ounce.

**Best for:** a gift that arrives looking like a gift.

### Bonne Maman Advent Calendar

Not cheese, but the most board-friendly calendar under $60: 24 mini jars of jam, also in [GMA's 2026 roundup](https://goodmorningamerica.com/shop/story/unique-advent-calendars-2026-136718827). Pair each day's jar with one cheese and you have a two-ingredient board every night. Fig, apricot and cherry go with almost any cheese.

**Best for:** pairing with a block of cheddar, brie or Manchego from your own store.

## Make your own cheese advent calendar

If the boxes sell out, cost more than you want to spend or don't ship to you, make one. It works anywhere you can buy cheese.

1. **Buy six cheeses** (about 6 oz or 170 g each) and **six jams or spreads.** Mix the textures: one soft, one blue, two firm, one aged, one fun (smoked, cranberry or truffle).
2. **Cut each cheese in two** so every cheese shows up twice, with a different spread each time.
3. **Wrap each portion** in parchment, then foil or a small bag, and **number them 1 to 12.** Keep the soft and blue cheeses for the first week.
4. **Each night:** take out one portion 20 to 30 minutes before eating for soft cheese, or 45 to 60 minutes for firm and aged cheese, and serve it with its spread and a few crackers.

That's 3 oz a night, enough for two people to share. For 24 nights, use 12 cheeses. Sign up in the box near the top of this page and we'll send a free one-page DIY shopping list.
