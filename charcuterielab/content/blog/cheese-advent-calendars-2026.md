### 12 Days of Tillamook

The 2026 box is twice the cheese of last year's: 12 full-size blocks instead of six cheeses and six surprises. [Tillamook's shop page](https://shop.tillamook.com/products/12-days-of-tillamook) lists four Maker's Reserve extra sharp cheddars aged 3, 7, 10 and 14 years (the 14-year is a creamery exclusive), four Farmers' Collection cheeses (English-style sweet cheddar, truffle white cheddar, smoked black pepper white cheddar and extra sharp white cheddar), and medium cheddar, sharp cheddar, pepper jack and Colby jack blocks. That's about 92 oz of cheese for $115, or roughly $1.25 an ounce, and orders over $100 ship free. [Sporked covered the launch](https://sporked.com/article/your-favorite-cheese-just-released-an-exclusive-advent-calendar/) on October 19.

It goes on sale **November 9 at 9 a.m. Pacific** and is a limited run, so be on the page at drop time. It ships cold, Monday to Wednesday.

**Best for:** cheddar lovers, and the most cheese per dollar of any 2026 calendar. Taste the aged cheddars in order, 3-year to 14-year, to see what aging does. Our [aged cheddar guide](/ingredients/aged-cheddar/) explains the crunchy crystals you'll find in the older ones.

### Cheese Brothers 12 Days of Cheesemas

Wisconsin's Cheese Brothers pack [12 full-size blocks and bags](https://www.cheesebros.com/products/12-days-of-cheesemas-advent-calendar) (5 to 8 oz each) from their own range, which can include cheese curds and extra-aged cheddars. It's $119.95 with free shipping in the US. It's a pre-order that ships between November 18 and December 7, and anything else in the same order waits for it, so buy other cheese separately.

**Best for:** the safest order-it-today pick. It's on sale now, it's all full-size cheese, and it ships in time.

## Make your own cheese advent calendar

If the boxes sell out, cost more than you want to spend or don't ship to you, make one. It works anywhere you can buy cheese, and six good wedges and six small jars run $40 to $60 at most US stores.

1. **Buy six cheeses** (about 6 oz or 170 g each) and **six jams or spreads.** Mix the textures: one soft, one blue, two firm, one aged, one fun (smoked, cranberry or truffle).
2. **Cut each cheese in two** so every cheese shows up twice, with a different spread each time.
3. **Wrap each portion** in parchment, then foil or a small bag, and **number them 1 to 12.** Put the soft and blue cheeses in the first week, since they keep the shortest.
4. **Each night:** take out one portion 20 to 30 minutes before eating for soft cheese, or 45 to 60 minutes for firm and aged cheese, and serve it with its spread and a few crackers.

That's 3 oz a night, enough for two people to share. For 24 nights, use 12 cheeses. Want the shopping list done for you?
