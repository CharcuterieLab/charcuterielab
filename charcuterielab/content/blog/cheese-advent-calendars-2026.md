## Too late for December 1?

You still have three good options:

- **Switch to a 12-day calendar.** A 12-day countdown can start as late as December 13 and still finish on Christmas Eve. Cheese Brothers' 12 Days of Cheesemas was still shipping in late November.
- **Do the 12 days of Christmas instead.** Start on December 25 and run to January 5, so a late order still works.
- **Make your own in one shopping trip.** It takes about 20 minutes to wrap, and it can start any night you like (below).

## Make your own cheese advent calendar

Cheaper than most boxes ($40 to $60), and it works anywhere.

1. **Buy 6 cheeses and 6 jams.** About 6 oz each. Mix soft, blue, firm, aged and one fun one (smoked or cranberry).
2. **Cut each cheese in two.** That's 12 pieces.
3. **Wrap and number them 1 to 12.** Soft and blue go first, since they keep the shortest.
4. **Each night,** take one out 30 to 60 minutes early and serve it with a jam and crackers.

Each piece is about 3 oz, enough for two. For 24 nights, buy 12 cheeses. Need help picking? See our [best brand guides](/blog/best-charcuterie-brands/).

**What cheese advent calendars are available in 2026?**

In the US the Cheese Brothers 12 Days of Cheesemas ($109.95, down from $119.95) is the one you can still order, and it offers a ship-now option as well as the Nov 18 to Dec 7 pre-order window. **Is the Tillamook cheese advent calendar sold out in 2026?**

Yes. It went on sale Monday, November 9 at 9 a.m. Pacific and sold out; Tillamook's own page now says that is a wrap until next year.
