## Make your own cheese advent calendar

Cheaper than most boxes ($40 to $60), and it works anywhere.

1. **Buy 6 cheeses and 6 jams.** About 6 oz each. Mix soft, blue, firm, aged and one fun one (smoked or cranberry).
2. **Cut each cheese in two.** That's 12 pieces.
3. **Wrap and number them 1 to 12.** Soft and blue go first, since they keep the shortest.
4. **Each night,** take one out 30 to 60 minutes early and serve it with a jam and crackers.

Each piece is about 3 oz, enough for two. For 24 nights, buy 12 cheeses. Need help picking? See our [best brand guides](/blog/best-charcuterie-brands/).

## Cheese advent calendar questions

**What cheese advent calendars are available in 2026?**

In the US you can order the Cheese Brothers 12 Days of Cheesemas ($119.95), Redhead Creamery 12 Days of Cheesemas (about $159) and the Williams Sonoma crate ($129.95) now. The 12 Days of Tillamook ($115) goes on sale November 9. Aldi's are expected in stores around November 4. Murray's ($65) is sold out.

**When does the Tillamook cheese advent calendar come out in 2026?**

November 9, 2026 at 9 a.m. Pacific, on Tillamook's online shop. It's $115 for 12 full-size cheddars and jacks, twice the cheese of last year.

**Does Aldi have a cheese advent calendar in 2026?**

Not announced yet. Aldi's calendars usually reach US stores on the first Wednesday of November, which is November 4 this year. Last year it sold a 24-day calendar for $16.99 and a 12-day one for $24.99, in stores only.

**How much is the Murray's cheese advent calendar?**

$65 for a 2.54 lb box, but it's sold out for the season. The $90 price in some roundups was last year's.

**Does Costco have a cheese advent calendar in 2026?**

Not that we've seen yet. In past years some warehouses sold a 12 Days of Festive Cheese box, about 3 lb of European cheese for $49.99. Check the cheese case in November.

**Is there a cheese advent calendar in the UK in 2026?**

Yes. The cheapest is The Crafty Herd (£14 at Ocado). Cheesemonger calendars from The Courtyard Dairy, George & Joseph, Paxton & Whitfield and The Welsh Cheese Company cost £135 to £224.

**Can you buy a cheese advent calendar in Australia?**

Yes. Woolworths sells the Thomas Dux 12 Days of Cheese calendar. It was A$28 last time, so check the current price.

**Can I make my own cheese advent calendar?**

Yes. Buy six cheeses and six spreads, cut each cheese in two, and wrap and number the 12 pieces. Use 12 cheeses for 24 nights.
