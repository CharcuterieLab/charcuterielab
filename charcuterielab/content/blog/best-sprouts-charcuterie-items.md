# The Best Sprouts Charcuterie Board Items: What to Buy

Tasting Table ranked Sprouts 7th of 12 chains for cheese, and The Takeout ranked it 6th of 13, so it's a solid mid-range stop with a strong snack aisle.

## Building a board from these

- **Cheese:** pick up a block of sharp cheddar and a brie from the cheese case, plus the store-brand sliced cheeses for roll-ups.
- **Crackers:** the sea salt pita crackers, plus the grain-free crackers for gluten-free guests.
- **Fruit and nuts:** Sprouts' produce and bulk bins are its strength. Grapes, apples, dried apricots, almonds and walnuts by the scoop.
- **Meat:** salami and prosciutto from the deli counter.

Plan on about 3 oz of cheese and 3 oz of meat per guest when the board is the party food, or 2 oz each before a meal.

**What should I buy at Sprouts for a charcuterie board?**

The store-brand sliced pepper jack and provolone, sea salt baked pita crackers, grain-free crackers for gluten-free guests, fruit and nuts from the bulk bins, and salami from the deli counter.

**Is Sprouts good for cheese?**

It's mid-range. Tasting Table ranked it 7th of 12 chains for cheese and The Takeout 6th of 13. The store-brand cheeses are well rated by shoppers.

**Does Sprouts have gluten-free crackers?**

Yes. Its grain-free vegan cheddar crackers are a Tasting Table must-buy.
