<h2 id="what-to-look-for">What to look for</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Look for:</strong> Buttermilk, sour cream or yogurt near the top, and visible herbs. For dipping, choose a product labeled &quot;dip&quot; or &quot;dressing and dip.&quot; Plain dressings are often too thin.</li><li class="sk-x"><strong>Skip:</strong> Pouring a thin bottled dressing into the bowl. It pools and won&#x27;t cling. If it&#x27;s all you have, stir in a spoonful of sour cream or Greek yogurt to thicken it.</li></ul>
:::

**Serving it:** ranch is dairy, so keep it cold. Set the bowl in a larger bowl of ice, and don't leave it out more than 2 hours, or 1 hour above 90°F. Plan on about 2 tablespoons per guest with a veggie tray.

<h2 id="faq">Ranch dip questions</h2>
:::

**What is the best store-bought ranch dip?**

Marie's Creamy Ranch Dressing & Dip, Chowhound's top ranch and a top-4 pick at Tasting Table. For a thick dip tub, Dean's, which scored 10 out of 10 at Sporked.

**What is the best ranch for a veggie tray?**

A thick refrigerated ranch like Marie's or Marzetti Ranch Veggie Dip, which cling to vegetables better than bottled dressing.

**How do you thicken ranch dressing for dipping?**

Stir in sour cream or plain Greek yogurt, a spoonful at a time.

**How long can ranch dip sit out?**

No more than 2 hours, or 1 hour above 90°F. Keep the bowl over ice.
