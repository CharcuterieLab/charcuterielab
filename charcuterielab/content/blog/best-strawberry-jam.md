<h2 id="what-to-look-for">What to look for</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Look for:</strong> Strawberries should be the first ingredient, sweetened with sugar or fruit juice. High fructose corn syrup and &quot;seedless&quot; usually mean a smoother, sweeter jam with less fruit flavor.</li><li class="sk-x"><strong>Skip:</strong> Seedless strawberry jam. <a href="https://www.tastingtable.com/1870685/strawberry-jam-brands-ranked/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Tasting Table</a> ranked Smucker&#x27;s Seedless last of eight for having almost no texture.</li></ul>
:::

**Serving it:** spoon the jam into a small bowl with a spoon, not straight onto the board. It goes with brie, goat cheese, ricotta, fresh mozzarella, shortbread and dark chocolate. Swirl a spoonful into whipped ricotta for an easy dip.

<h2 id="faq">Strawberry jam questions</h2>
:::

**What is the best strawberry jam?**

Bonne Maman Strawberry Preserves. It won Chowhound's 12-jar test, and Bonne Maman's strawberry spread came 2nd at Tasting Table. Crofter's Organic won Tasting Table's test.

**What cheese goes with strawberry jam?**

Brie, goat cheese, ricotta, fresh mozzarella and mild white cheddar.

**What's the difference between jam, preserves and fruit spread?**

Preserves have the biggest fruit pieces, jam is smoother, and fruit spread is usually sweetened with fruit juice or less sugar.

**Is strawberry jam good on a charcuterie board?**

Yes, especially on dessert boards and boards for kids. It's milder than fig jam.
