<h2 id="faq">Pepperoni questions</h2>
:::

**What is the best pepperoni for a charcuterie board?**

Boar's Head, which topped Tasting Table's test. Buy it sliced at the deli or as a whole stick.

**Is pepperoni a salami?**

Yes. It's an American salami, usually pork and beef seasoned with paprika and chili.

**How do I serve pepperoni on a board?**

Fan large slices or cut a stick into coins. Fold them so they don't lie flat and dry out.
