# The Best Pepperoni for a Charcuterie Board, Ranked

> **Quick Answer:** Our top pick for pepperoni on a charcuterie board is Boar's Head Pepperoni (4.5 out of 5). Best value: Trader Joe's Ciao Pizza Prima Pepperoni. Splurge: Vincenza's Crafted Charcuterie Pepperoni. We ranked 5 widely sold options on how they work on a board, published taste tests and awards, ingredients, value and how easy they are to find.

Pepperoni on a board should have real flavor, not just grease. Larger, sandwich-style slices and whole sticks work better than tiny pizza rounds.

## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** Pork and beef, salt, paprika and spices. A long list with corn syrup or lots of additives is a sign of a lesser product.

**Skip:** Tiny pizza pepperoni for a board. It's oily and gets lost next to the cheese.

## Pepperoni questions

**What is the best pepperoni for a charcuterie board?**

Boar's Head, which topped Tasting Table's test. Buy it sliced at the deli or as a whole stick.

**Is pepperoni a salami?**

Yes. It's an American salami, usually pork and beef seasoned with paprika and chili.

**How do I serve pepperoni on a board?**

Fan large slices or cut a stick into coins. Fold them so they don't lie flat and dry out.
