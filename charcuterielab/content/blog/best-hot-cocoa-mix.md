## Hot cocoa questions

**Milk or water for hot cocoa mix?**

Follow the package. Water-only mixes like Land O Lakes already contain dry milk. Real-chocolate mixes like Williams Sonoma's are meant for hot milk. Most testers preferred milk when a mix allowed it.

**How many packets per person for a hot cocoa board?**

One packet makes one 8 oz mug. Plan on one to two mugs per guest over a party, so a 50-count box covers 25 to 35 guests.

**What goes on a hot cocoa board?**

Two or three mixes, marshmallows, whipped cream, crushed peppermint, chocolate chips, caramel sauce, cinnamon sticks, cookies, and a bowl of chopped dark chocolate that melts into the cup.

**How do I keep hot cocoa warm for a party?**

Heat the milk or water and hold it in a slow cooker on the warm setting or in an insulated carafe. Let guests stir their own mix into each mug so the cocoa doesn't scorch.

**Hot chocolate vs hot cocoa: what's the difference?**

Labels use the words loosely. Read the ingredients: cocoa powder only (Swiss Miss, Lake Champlain) or real chocolate (Ghirardelli Double Chocolate, Williams Sonoma), which tastes richer.
