# The Best Store-Bought Crab Dip for a Party Board

> **Quick Answer:** Tasting Table's winner is **Ocean Legacy Jalapeño Crab Dip**, made with 100% real crab ($6.98 for two 6.5 oz tubs at BJ's). For a Maryland crab-cake flavor, **Inland Market Maryland-Style**. For a hot dip from the freezer, **Phillips Chesapeake Crab Dip**. On a budget, **Inland Market Cajun Krab Dip** at Publix (10/10 at Sporked) uses imitation crab.

<h2 id="how-to-choose">How to serve crab dip on a board</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Check the label for real crab.</strong> Most supermarket crab dips use imitation crab (pollock). Real crab is listed as crabmeat or blue crab.</li><li><strong>Cold dips:</strong> serve straight from the fridge in a bowl set over ice, and put them back within 2 hours.</li><li><strong>Hot dips:</strong> heat until steaming all the way through (165°F), then move to a small oven-safe dish so it stays warm on the board.</li><li><strong>Serve it with</strong> butter crackers, pita chips, pretzel crisps, toasted baguette, celery and sweet pepper strips.</li><li><strong>How much:</strong> a 13 to 16 oz tub serves about 8 to 12 people as one of several dips.</li></ul>

## Crab dip questions

**What is the best store-bought crab dip?**

Ocean Legacy Jalapeño Crab Dip, made with 100% real crab, won Tasting Table's 2025 test of nine. Inland Market's Maryland-Style, also real crab, came second.

**Is store-bought crab dip made with real crab?**

Usually not. Seven of the nine dips in Tasting Table's test used imitation crab made from pollock or whiting. Look for crabmeat or blue crab on the ingredient list.

**Which crab dip should you skip?**

Kroger's Kickin' Krab Salad came last at Tasting Table, with little seafood flavor.

**Should crab dip be served hot or cold?**

Both work. Most refrigerated tubs are cold dips; frozen dips like Phillips are made to be heated to 165°F. Ocean Legacy is good either way.

**How long can crab dip sit out?**

No more than 2 hours. Set a cold dip over ice and keep a hot dip warm.

**What do you serve with crab dip?**

Butter crackers, pita chips, pretzel crisps, toasted baguette slices, celery and bell pepper strips.
