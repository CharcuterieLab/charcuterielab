<h2 id="what-to-look-for">What to look for</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Look for:</strong> A soft, giving feel through the wrapper and a white rind with faint reddish-brown flecks, not grey and not shrunken away from the paste.</li><li class="sk-x"><strong>Skip:</strong> Pre-cut brie wedges in plastic. They dry at the cut faces. Buy a small wheel instead.</li></ul>

<h2 id="faq">Brie questions</h2>
:::

**What is the best grocery store brie?**

Fromager d'Affinois ranked first in Tasting Table's test. For classic brie, Président is the reliable everyday choice.

**Is Fromager d'Affinois a brie?**

Not technically. It is made like a brie but from ultrafiltered milk, which makes it richer and quicker to produce.

**How long before serving should brie come out of the fridge?**

20 to 30 minutes.
