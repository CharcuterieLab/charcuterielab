# The Best Pepper Jack Cheese for a Charcuterie Board, Ranked

> **Quick Answer:** Our top pick for pepper jack on a charcuterie board is Cabot Pepper Jack (4.7 out of 5). Mildest heat: Tillamook Pepper Jack. Hottest: Sargento Carolina Reaper Jack. Organic: Trader Joe's Organic Pepper Jack. We ranked 6 widely sold options on how they work on a board, published taste tests and awards, ingredients, value and how easy they are to find.

Pepper jack is Monterey Jack studded with hot peppers: creamy, mild cheese with a kick. On a board it's the cheese that pairs with summer sausage, salsa and beer, and it's a favorite on game-day and Tex-Mex boards. These rankings combine The Takeout's and Tasting Table's 2026 cheese rankings and shopper ratings.

### 1. Cabot Pepper Jack

**★★★★½ 4.7 out of 5** · Best overall · Best for: The all-rounder · $ budget

[The Takeout](https://www.thetakeout.com/2271671/popular-sliced-cheese-brands-ranked-worst-best/) ranked it 2nd of 13 sliced cheeses, right behind Cabot's sharp cheddar: a great cheese if you like a little fire.

- **Why we like it:** Creamy with real heat, from a well-loved Vermont co-op.
- **Keep in mind:** The heat builds, so cut small cubes.
- **Where to buy:** Most grocery stores, in blocks and slices.

### 2. Tillamook Pepper Jack

**★★★★½ 4.5 out of 5** · Mildest heat · Best for: Guests new to spicy cheese · $ budget

[Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2169853/sliced-cheese-brands-ranked-worst-best/) ranked Tillamook third of nine cheese brands, calling its pepper jack a great entry point into peppery cheeses.

- **Why we like it:** Mild heat and a creamy texture.
- **Keep in mind:** Less fire than Cabot.
- **Where to buy:** Most grocery stores.

### 3. Lucerne Pepper Jack

**★★★★☆ 4.0 out of 5** · Best for: A budget block · $ budget

The cheese [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2169853/sliced-cheese-brands-ranked-worst-best/)'s taster liked best from Lucerne, the Albertsons store brand.

- **Why we like it:** Cheap and good.
- **Keep in mind:** Only at Albertsons-family stores.
- **Where to buy:** Safeway, Albertsons, Vons, Jewel-Osco and sister stores.

### 4. Sargento Carolina Reaper Jack

**★★★★☆ 3.9 out of 5** · Hottest · Best for: Heat lovers · $ budget

A Monterey Jack with Carolina Reaper peppers. [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2169853/sliced-cheese-brands-ranked-worst-best/)'s taster enjoyed it.

- **Why we like it:** Very hot, for the chili fans at the party.
- **Keep in mind:** Too hot for most guests. Label it.
- **Where to buy:** Most grocery stores.

### 5. Trader Joe's Organic Pepper Jack

**★★★★☆ 3.8 out of 5** · Organic · Best for: An organic pick · $ budget

[Eat This](https://www.eatthis.com/trader-joes-charcuterie-board-items) recommends it for a board: jalapeño heat that isn't overpowering.

- **Why we like it:** Organic, with balanced heat.
- **Keep in mind:** Harder to find than other Trader Joe's cheeses.
- **Where to buy:** Trader Joe's.

### 6. Sprouts Sliced Pepper Jack

**★★★½☆ 3.7 out of 5** · Best for: Slider and sandwich boards · $ budget

Sprouts' store-brand slices average 4.5 to 5 stars from shoppers ([Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/1521748/chain-grocery-store-cheese-ranked/)).

- **Why we like it:** Well-rated by shoppers, and cheap.
- **Keep in mind:** Slices, so best for sandwiches and sliders.
- **Where to buy:** Sprouts.

## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** Look for real peppers (jalapeño, habanero) listed after the cheese. Blocks are better than slices for a board: cut them into cubes or sticks.

**Skip:** Habanero pepper jack from pre-cut cheese trays: [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2169853/sliced-cheese-brands-ranked-worst-best/) found Primo Taglio's habanero version soapy and bitter.

**How much to buy:** about 1 oz per guest when it's one of three cheeses. Put it next to a mild cheese so guests have a choice.
**What it pairs with:** summer sausage, pepperoni, salsa, guacamole, tortilla chips, pickled jalapeños, mango or pineapple, and a lager or margarita. See our [Super Bowl board plans](/holidays/super-bowl/) for a full spread.

## Pepper jack questions

**What is the best pepper jack cheese?**

Cabot Pepper Jack, which The Takeout ranked 2nd of 13 sliced cheeses. Tillamook is the milder pick.

**Is pepper jack good on a charcuterie board?**

Yes. It's creamy with a little heat, and it's a natural fit for game-day and Tex-Mex boards with summer sausage and salsa.

**What peppers are in pepper jack?**

Usually jalapeños, sometimes habaneros or other chilies. Extra-hot versions use Carolina Reaper or ghost peppers.

**What is the mildest pepper jack?**

Tillamook, which Tasting Table called a great entry point into peppery cheeses.
