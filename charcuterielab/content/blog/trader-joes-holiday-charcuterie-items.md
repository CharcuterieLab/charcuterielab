## Trader Joe's holiday questions

**When do Trader Joe's holiday items come out?**

In two waves. Fall items like the pumpkin cranberry crisps and pumpkin butter were in stores by early September 2026. Holiday cheeses such as Cranberry Chèvre and the Holiday Cheer spread usually arrive around November.

**How long are Trader Joe's holiday items in stores?**

Until the season ends or they sell out. Trader Joe's says its holiday cheeses leave when the holidays are over, so buy them as soon as you see them.

**What's the best Trader Joe's cheese for a holiday board?**

For 2026, Creamy Toscano Dusted with Cinnamon, which is confirmed back. Pair it with a cranberry cheese: White Stilton with Cranberries, or Cranberry Chèvre when it arrives.

**How much Trader Joe's cheese do I need for 12 guests?**

Before a meal, about 2 oz of cheese and 2 oz of meat per guest: 1½ lb of each for 12. If the board is the party food, plan 3 oz of each, about 2¼ lb.

**Can you freeze Trader Joe's holiday cheeses?**

Some. Cranberry Chèvre can be frozen but turns crumbly after thawing. Brie en Croûte is sold frozen. For the others, buy close to the party instead.
