## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** Cranberries should be the first ingredient. Some cheaper sauces use high fructose corn syrup instead of sugar, and sugar content varies a lot by brand.

**Skip:** Pouring jellied sauce straight onto the board. Chill the can, open both ends, slide it out and slice, or cut it into leaves and stars with a small cookie cutter.

**Building the board:** cranberry sauce goes with baked brie, sharp white cheddar, goat cheese, sliced turkey and pecans. Serve whole berry sauce in a small bowl with a spoon. For the full board, see our [Thanksgiving charcuterie board](/blog/thanksgiving-charcuterie-board/).

## Cranberry sauce questions

**What is the best canned cranberry sauce?**

For a board, Ocean Spray Jellied, which Food & Wine named the best jellied sauce. For flavor, Tiptree Wild Cranberry was Food & Wine's overall winner.

**Is jellied or whole berry cranberry sauce better on a charcuterie board?**

Jellied slices into neat shapes. Whole berry spoons better onto brie and crackers. Many hosts put out both.

**What cheese goes with cranberry sauce?**

Brie, camembert, sharp white cheddar, goat cheese and Wensleydale.

**Can I make cranberry sauce shapes for a board?**

Yes. Chill the jellied sauce, slice it about half an inch thick, and cut it with small cookie cutters.
