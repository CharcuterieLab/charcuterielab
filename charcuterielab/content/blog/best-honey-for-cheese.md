<h2 id="faq">Honey questions</h2>
:::

**What honey is best for a cheese board?**

A raw wildflower honey like Nature Nate's for most cheeses, plus chestnut honey if you are serving blue cheese.

**What honey goes with blue cheese?**

Dark honey such as chestnut or buckwheat. Its bitterness balances the salt and sharpness.

**Is hot honey good on a cheese board?**

Yes, especially with salami, aged cheddar and fresh mozzarella. Tasting Table's top pick was Björn's Sweet and Spicy.
