> **Quick Answer:** Our top pick for honey on a charcuterie board is Nature Nate's Raw & Unfiltered Honey (4.6 out of 5). Best value: 365 by Whole Foods Market Organic Raw Wildflower Honey. ## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** 'pure honey' tells you little. Look for a single floral source and a named producer, and prefer raw.

**Skip:** Squeeze-bear honey on a nice board. It works, it just undoes the effort. Decant it into a small bowl.

## Honey questions

**What honey is best for a cheese board?**

A raw wildflower honey like Nature Nate's for most cheeses, plus chestnut honey if you are serving blue cheese.

**What honey goes with blue cheese?**

Dark honey such as chestnut or buckwheat. Its bitterness balances the salt and sharpness.

**Is hot honey good on a cheese board?**

Yes, especially with salami, aged cheddar and fresh mozzarella. Tasting Table's top pick was Björn's Sweet and Spicy.
