### 1. Kirkland Signature Basil Pesto

**★★★★½ 4.7 out of 5** · Best overall · Best for: Fresh-tasting pesto · $ budget

## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** Look for basil, extra virgin olive oil, cheese (Parmigiano-Reggiano, Grana Padano or Pecorino) and pine nuts near the top. Sunflower or soybean oil, potato and sugar are signs of a filler-heavy pesto. Check for nuts if you have allergy guests: many pestos use cashews.

**Skip:** Pesto as the only spread. It's oily and strong, so pair it with fresh mozzarella or burrata rather than with cheddar or blue.

**Serving it:** spoon pesto over burrata or a log of fresh goat cheese, or put it in a small bowl with crostini. Keep refrigerated pesto cold until shortly before serving.

## Pesto questions

**What is the best store-bought pesto?**

Kirkland Signature Basil Pesto from Costco. Sporked gave it 10 out of 10 and Daily Meal ranked it first of 12.

**Is refrigerated pesto better than jarred?**

Usually. The refrigerated tubs (Kirkland, Buitoni, Nuovo) won most tests. DeLallo is the best jarred pick.

**Is pesto nut-free?**

Not usually. Most contain pine nuts or cashews. Classico Traditional is made without nuts.

**What goes with pesto on a charcuterie board?**

Burrata, fresh mozzarella, cherry tomatoes, prosciutto and crostini.
