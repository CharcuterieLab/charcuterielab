# The Best Cream Cheese Brands for a Charcuterie Board, Ranked

> **Quick Answer:** Our top pick for cream cheese on a charcuterie board is Philadelphia Original Cream Cheese (4.7 out of 5). Value: Great Value Cream Cheese. We ranked 6 widely sold options on how they work on a board, published taste tests and awards, ingredients, value and how easy they are to find.

On a board, cream cheese has one famous job: a block topped with pepper jelly, surrounded by crackers. It also anchors bagel and smoked salmon boards. Three taste tests in the past year disagree on almost everything except one brand, which came first or second in all three.

### 1. Philadelphia Original Cream Cheese

**★★★★½ 4.7 out of 5** · Best overall · Best for: Every board · $ budget

In the top two of all three tests. [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2047284/store-bought-cream-cheese-ranked) ranked it first, calling it "the be-all and end-all of cream cheeses" with flawless texture. [The Takeout](https://www.thetakeout.com/2126567/store-bought-cream-cheese-ranked-worst-best/) and [Chowhound](https://www.chowhound.com/2133656/cream-cheese-brand-ranked-best) both ranked it second.

- **Why we like it:** Tangy, holds its shape as a block, and sold everywhere.
- **Keep in mind:** Saltier and sharper than the mild brands.
- **Where to buy:** Every grocery store.

### 2. Great Value Cream Cheese

**★★★★☆ 4.2 out of 5** · Best value · Best for: Budget boards · $ budget

[The Takeout](https://www.thetakeout.com/2126567/store-bought-cream-cheese-ranked-worst-best/) was surprised to rank it 3rd of 11: dense and rich. [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2047284/store-bought-cream-cheese-ranked) placed it 7th of nine as balanced but nondescript.

- **Why we like it:** Dense enough to hold pepper jelly, at a low price.
- **Keep in mind:** Plain flavor.
- **Where to buy:** Walmart.

### 3. Kroger Cream Cheese

**★★★★☆ 4.2 out of 5** · Best for: A tangy store brand · $ budget

4th in both [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2047284/store-bought-cream-cheese-ranked)'s and [The Takeout](https://www.thetakeout.com/2126567/store-bought-cream-cheese-ranked-worst-best/)'s tests: smooth, with a distinct tang balanced by a little sweetness.

- **Why we like it:** Consistent middle-of-the-pack scores at a low price.
- **Keep in mind:** Only at Kroger-family stores.
- **Where to buy:** Kroger, Ralphs, Fred Meyer and other Kroger stores.

### 4. Tillamook Cream Cheese

**★★★★☆ 3.9 out of 5** · Best for: Mild and spreadable · $ budget

The most divisive brand. [Chowhound](https://www.chowhound.com/2133656/cream-cheese-brand-ranked-best) ranked it first in a blind test for its light, almost whipped texture and mild sweetness. [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2047284/store-bought-cream-cheese-ranked) ranked it last for a chalky mouthfeel, and [The Takeout](https://www.thetakeout.com/2126567/store-bought-cream-cheese-ranked-worst-best/) placed it 7th of 11.

- **Why we like it:** More cream and no gum thickener.
- **Keep in mind:** Softer, so it slumps under pepper jelly.
- **Where to buy:** Most grocery stores.

### 5. Trader Joe's Cream Cheese

**★★★★☆ 3.8 out of 5** · Best for: Smooth and mild · $ budget

6th in both [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2047284/store-bought-cream-cheese-ranked) and [The Takeout](https://www.thetakeout.com/2126567/store-bought-cream-cheese-ranked-worst-best/): texturally flawless and buttery, but mild and a little sweet.

- **Why we like it:** Smooth and cheap.
- **Keep in mind:** Not much tang.
- **Where to buy:** Trader Joe's.

### 6. Organic Valley Cream Cheese

**★★★½☆ 3.6 out of 5** · Best for: Organic boards · $$ mid-range

[Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2047284/store-bought-cream-cheese-ranked) ranked it 2nd of nine as the closest match to Philadelphia. [The Takeout](https://www.thetakeout.com/2126567/store-bought-cream-cheese-ranked-worst-best/) disagreed, placing it 8th of 11 as bland and sticky.

- **Why we like it:** Organic, with a complex tang in one test.
- **Keep in mind:** Split reviews.
- **Where to buy:** Whole Foods and many grocery stores.

## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** For a pepper-jelly block, buy the 8 oz brick, not the whipped tub. Bricks hold a clean shape. Most brands use a gum (like carob bean gum) to stay firm, which is normal.

**Skip:** Whipped or flavored tubs for the board. They spread across the plate and don't hold toppings.

**Serving it:** set an 8 oz brick on a small plate, spoon pepper jelly or hot honey over it, and surround it with butter crackers. Cream cheese is a fresh cheese: take it out 15 to 20 minutes before serving and don't leave it out more than 2 hours.

## Cream cheese questions

**What is the best cream cheese brand?**

Philadelphia. It ranked first at Tasting Table and second at both The Takeout and Chowhound.

**Which cream cheese is best with pepper jelly?**

A firm brick like Philadelphia or Great Value. Softer brands like Tillamook slump under the jelly.

**Is store-brand cream cheese as good as Philadelphia?**

Some are close. Great Value ranked 3rd of 11 at The Takeout, and Kroger ranked 4th in two tests.

**How long can cream cheese sit out on a board?**

No more than 2 hours, or 1 hour above 90°F.
