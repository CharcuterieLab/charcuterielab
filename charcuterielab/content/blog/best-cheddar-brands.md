<h2 id="what-to-look-for">What to look for</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Look for:</strong> An age statement in months or years. The words sharp and extra sharp are not regulated. Cloth-bound means a drier, more complex cheese.</li><li class="sk-x"><strong>Skip:</strong> Pre-sliced or shredded cheddar for a board, and orange cheddar if you have a choice. The color is annatto and adds nothing.</li></ul>

<h2 id="faq">Cheddar questions</h2>
:::

**What is the best cheddar for a charcuterie board?**

Kerrygold Reserve for availability and flavor, Trader Joe's Unexpected Cheddar for value, and Cabot Clothbound as the splurge.

**What are the white crystals in aged cheddar?**

Small crystals that form as the cheese ages. They are a sign of a well-aged cheddar.

**How long before serving should cheddar come out of the fridge?**

Aged hard cheese needs 45 to 60 minutes.
