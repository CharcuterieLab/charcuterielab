<h2 id="how-to-choose">Spiral ham on a charcuterie board</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Serve it cold.</strong> Spiral ham is fully cooked, and food safety guidance says spiral-cut hams are best served cold; reheating dries out the thin slices.</li><li><strong>If you want it warm,</strong> cover it tightly in foil and heat at 325°F for about 10 minutes per pound, to 140°F inside.</li><li><strong>Build a ham slider bar:</strong> Hawaiian rolls or biscuits, Dijon and honey mustard, pepper jelly, sliced Gruyère or sharp cheddar, cornichons and pimento cheese.</li><li><strong>Next-day board:</strong> roll leftover slices around cheese sticks or asparagus, and add deviled eggs, pickles and crackers.</li><li><strong>How much:</strong> about half a pound of bone-in ham per person for a dinner, and 2 oz per person on an appetizer board. Leftovers keep about 5 days in the fridge.</li></ul>

## Spiral ham questions

**What is the best spiral ham to buy?**

At the store, Target's Market Pantry hickory smoked ham, which won Consumer Reports' 2024 test, with Aldi's Appleton Farms second. By mail, D'Artagnan's Berkshire half ham, Food Network's best overall.

**Is HoneyBaked ham worth it?**

Reviewers praise its juiciness and flavor, but Tasting Table ranked it 18th of 19 brands mostly on value, at about $107 for an 8 lb half ham.

**Do you have to heat a spiral ham?**

No. It's fully cooked and best served cold. To warm it, wrap it in foil and heat at 325°F for about 10 minutes per pound, to 140°F inside.

**How much spiral ham per person?**

About half a pound of bone-in ham per person for a main course, or about 2 oz per person on an appetizer board.

**How long does leftover ham last?**

Cooked leftover ham keeps about 5 days in the fridge. Reheat leftover slices to 165°F.

**What goes with ham on a charcuterie board?**

Dijon and honey mustard, pepper jelly, Gruyère or sharp cheddar, pimento cheese, cornichons, biscuits or Hawaiian rolls and deviled eggs.
