### 1. Boursin Dairy-Free Garlic & Herbs

**★★★★½ 4.4 out of 5** · Best overall · Best for: The spreadable cheese · $$ mid-range

[Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/best-vegan-cheese/) scored it 9.5 out of 10 and named it the best spreadable vegan cheese: it closely copies regular Boursin, and it's very garlicky and herby.

- **Why we like it:** Tastes like the original, and spreading hides any texture difference.
- **Keep in mind:** Made with coconut oil, so don't leave it out for long.
- **Where to buy:** Most grocery stores, Target and Whole Foods.

### 2. Follow Your Heart Smoked Gouda

**★★★★☆ 4.2 out of 5** · Best for: A smoky sliced cheese · $ budget

[Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/best-vegan-cheese/)'s best of the best, with 10 out of 10: smooth and creamy with a well-balanced smoky flavor, and good for snacking.

- **Why we like it:** A top score, and cheap.
- **Keep in mind:** Sold as slices, so fold or roll them.
- **Where to buy:** Whole Foods, Kroger and most grocery stores.

### 3. Violife Mature Cheddar

**★★★★☆ 4.2 out of 5** · Best value · Best for: A cheddar-style block · $ budget

[Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/best-vegan-cheese/) gave it 10 out of 10 for its mild cheddar flavor and lingering creaminess.

- **Why we like it:** Cheap, everywhere, and cuts into neat cubes.
- **Keep in mind:** Mild. Coconut oil base.
- **Where to buy:** Most grocery stores, Target and Walmart.

### 4. Treeline Cashew Cheese

**★★★★☆ 4.2 out of 5** · Best for: A cultured nut cheese · $$ mid-range

[Tasting Table](https://tastingtable.com/1823872/best-vegan-cheese-brand) named Treeline the best vegan cheese brand after tasting 16. Its cashew cheeses are made like artisan cheese. [Daily Meal](https://www.thedailymeal.com/1479432/non-dairy-cheeses-ranked/) found the goat-style log convincingly tangy.

- **Keep in mind:** Soft and spreadable rather than firm.
- **Where to buy:** Whole Foods and natural grocers.

### 5. Babybel Plant-Based

## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** Check the base. Cultured cashew or almond cheeses taste the most like real cheese. Coconut-oil cheeses are cheaper and slice neatly, but taste milder. For allergy guests, check for cashews and other tree nuts, and look for a certified vegan mark, since a few "almond" cheeses contain casein, a milk protein. (Source: [Daily Meal]({DM}).)

**Skip:** Vegan shreds and melting slices made for pizza. They're starchy and rubbery cold.

**Building the board:** two vegan cheeses are plenty. Pair one spreadable (Boursin or Treeline) with one you can slice or cube (Violife or Follow Your Heart). Keep them in the fridge until 15 to 20 minutes before serving. ## Vegan cheese questions

**What is the best vegan cheese for a charcuterie board?**

Boursin Dairy-Free Garlic & Herbs, which Sporked scored 9.5 out of 10 as the best spreadable vegan cheese. Follow Your Heart Smoked Gouda and Violife Mature Cheddar both scored 10 out of 10.

**What is the best vegan cheese brand?**

Tasting Table named Treeline the best vegan cheese brand after tasting 16. For easy-to-find options, Violife and Follow Your Heart both scored highly in Sporked's test.

**Is vegan cheese nut-free?**

Not always. Cashew and almond cheeses contain tree nuts. Coconut-oil cheeses like Violife are usually nut-free, but check the label.

**How many vegan cheeses do I need for a board?**

Two is plenty: one spreadable and one you can slice or cube.
