<h2 id="how-to-choose">Which nut goes with which cheese</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Candied or praline pecans</strong> with blue cheese, brie and fresh goat cheese. The sugar balances salt and tang.</li><li><strong>Smoked almonds</strong> with aged cheddar, Manchego, aged Gouda and salami.</li><li><strong>Maple or honey nuts</strong> with sharp cheddar and a fall or holiday board.</li><li><strong>Keep them crunchy:</strong> add sugared nuts last and keep them away from grapes, jam and other wet items.</li><li><strong>How much:</strong> about 1 oz of nuts per guest in total; one sweet and one savory bowl is plenty.</li></ul>

## Candied nut questions

**What are the best candied pecans to buy?**

Kirkland Signature Praline Pecans at Costco for a crowd. Trader Joe's Maple Spiced Nut Mix if you want sweet and savory together.

**Are praline pecans the same as candied pecans?**

Close. Praline pecans have a buttery brown-sugar coating; candied pecans can be any sugar glaze, sometimes with cinnamon or spice.

**What cheese goes with candied pecans?**

Blue cheese, brie and goat cheese. The sweetness balances salty, tangy cheese.

**How do I keep candied nuts crunchy on a board?**

Put them out last, in their own small bowl, away from fruit and jam. Store opened bags airtight.

**How many nuts per person?**

About 1 oz per guest in total, split between a sweet nut and a savory one.
