# The Best Soppressata Brands for a Charcuterie Board, Ranked

> **Quick Answer:** Our top pick for soppressata on a charcuterie board is Brooklyn Cured Sweet Soppressata (4.2 out of 5). Best value: Volpi Soppressata. Splurge: Fra' Mani Soppressata. We ranked 5 widely sold options on how they work on a board, published taste tests and awards, ingredients, value and how easy they are to find.

Soppressata is the coarse-ground, often spicy cousin of Genoa salami. The best ones have visible fat, a firm chew and real heat or sweetness. Published taste tests are limited, so ingredients and reputation carry more weight here.

*How we ranked these: these are researched picks, not a side-by-side tasting. We scored each product on published taste tests and awards, its ingredient list, price and where you can buy it, plus how that style of product behaves on a board. Sources are listed at the end, and the full method is on our [buying guides hub](/blog/best-charcuterie-brands/#how-we-rank). Prices and stock last checked September 2026.*

### 1. Brooklyn Cured Sweet Soppressata

**★★★★☆ 4.2 out of 5** · Best overall · Best for: Sweet, buttery soppressata · $$ mid-range

[Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/1880891/salami-brands-ranked-worst-best/)'s number one salami across nine brands, praised for a buttery texture and a hint of sweetness balancing savory notes.

- **Why we like it:** Top-ranked. Very versatile.
- **Keep in mind:** Not in every store.
- **Where to buy:** Grocery, specialty stores.

### 2. Fra' Mani Soppressata

**★★★★☆ 3.8 out of 5** · Best splurge · Best for: Award-winning maker · $$$ premium

From the heavily awarded Berkeley salumeria founded by chef Paul Bertolli. (Source: [Fra' Mani](https://framani.com/products/soppressata), [The National Provisioner](https://www.provisioneronline.com/articles/118703-fra-mani-champions-traditional-culinary-methods).)

### 3. Volpi Soppressata

**★★★★☆ 3.8 out of 5** · Best value · Best for: Easy to find · $$ mid-range

From the St. Louis family salumi maker founded in 1902. Widely available and reliable. (Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volpi_Foods).)

- **Keep in mind:** Few published reviews.
- **Where to buy:** Grocery, Whole Foods.

### 4. Olympia Provisions Sopressata

**★★★½☆ 3.6 out of 5** · Best for: Craft pick · $$$ premium

A Portland salami with clove, oregano, garlic and chili, made with no added nitrates or nitrites and fermented with natural molds. (Source: [Olympia Provisions](https://www.olympiaprovisions.com/products/sopressata).)

Cut to order at the deli, so the edges stay fresh. A fine everyday option.

- **Where to buy:** Deli counters.

## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** Pork, salt, spices and wine or garlic. 'Hot' or 'piccante' means chili; 'sweet' or 'dolce' means no heat.

**Skip:** Pre-sliced soppressata in a tray that has curled at the edges. Buy a whole stick when you can.

## Soppressata questions

**What is the best soppressata for a charcuterie board?**

Brooklyn Cured Sweet Soppressata, which topped Tasting Table's salami test.

**Is soppressata spicy?**

Hot soppressata is; sweet (dolce) soppressata is not. The label will say.

**What goes with soppressata?**

Provolone, fresh mozzarella, figs, honey and a red like Barbera or Lambrusco.
