## How to build any of these boards

1. **Pick the board for your crowd and your clock.** Use the guest, budget and time filters above. A board for 8 to 10 needs a 16 to 18 inch board; see [charcuterie board sizes](/blog/charcuterie-board-sizes/).
2. **Buy by weight, not by package.** Plan **2 oz of meat and 2 oz of cheese per person** before a meal, **3 oz each** if the board is the party food and **4 oz each** for dinner. The [Party Planner](/party-planner/) does the math for any guest count, and [how much charcuterie per person](/blog/how-much-charcuterie-per-person/) explains it.
3. **Take the cheese out 30 to 60 minutes early.** Cold cheese tastes muted. Most of the board can be prepped [the night before](/blog/make-charcuterie-board-night-before/).
4. **Build in order:** cheese spread out, meat folded between, fruit and spreads in the gaps, crackers last. The full method is in [how to make a charcuterie board](/blog/how-to-make-charcuterie-board/), and [what goes on a charcuterie board](/blog/what-goes-on-charcuterie-board/) lists every category.
5. **Keep it to two hours out.** That's the USDA limit at room temperature (one hour above 90°F). See [how long a charcuterie board can sit out](/blog/how-long-charcuterie-board-last/).
