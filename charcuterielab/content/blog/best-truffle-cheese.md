<h2 id="how-to-choose">How to serve truffle cheese</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>One is enough:</strong> put a single truffle cheese next to milder ones so it stands out.</li><li><strong>Pair it with</strong> prosciutto, plain crackers, Marcona almonds and Champagne or Pinot Noir.</li><li><strong>Skip sweet pairings:</strong> honey and jam fight the truffle.</li><li><strong>Read the label:</strong> look for real truffle ("tartufo") pieces, not just truffle aroma or oil.</li><li><strong>How much:</strong> about 1 oz per person as one of three or four cheeses.</li></ul>

## Truffle cheese questions

**What is the best truffle cheese?**

Sottocenere is the best all-rounder. Truffle Tremor is the creamy splurge, and Trader Joe's Italian Truffle Cheese is the budget pick.

**Does truffle cheese have real truffles?**

The good ones do: look for black or white truffle (tartufo) in the ingredients. Cheaper versions may use only truffle aroma.

**What goes with truffle cheese?**

Prosciutto, plain crackers, Marcona almonds, mushrooms and dry sparkling wine. Skip honey and jam.

**Can you eat the rind on Sottocenere?**

Yes. It's coated in spiced ash, which some people trim off.

**How much truffle cheese per person?**

About 1 oz per person when it's one of several cheeses.
