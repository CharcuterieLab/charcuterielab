<h2 id="how-to-choose">How to use tapenade on a board</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>On crostini:</strong> a thin layer of tapenade with a crumble of goat cheese or a slice of fresh mozzarella is the easiest bite on any board.</li><li><strong>With cheese:</strong> it's salty and briny, so it suits mild, creamy cheeses (chèvre, burrata, ricotta, brie) better than salty aged ones.</li><li><strong>Black vs green:</strong> black (Kalamata or Niçoise) is rich and earthy; green is brighter and works with fish and chicken.</li><li><strong>Read the label for capers and anchovies.</strong> Classic Provençal tapenade has both. Many jars skip the anchovy, so check if you're serving vegetarians.</li></ul>

## Tapenade questions

**What is the best store-bought olive tapenade?**

DeLallo Black Olive Tapenade for pure olive flavor, or Divina Organic Kalamata Olive Spread for the classic style with capers and garlic.

**What is tapenade made of?**

Finely chopped or puréed olives with olive oil. The Provençal original also has capers and usually anchovies and garlic.

**What goes with tapenade on a charcuterie board?**

Crostini, goat cheese, burrata, fresh mozzarella, brie, roasted red peppers and Italian salami.

**Is olive tapenade vegan?**

Most jarred tapenades are, but traditional recipes use anchovies. Check the label.

**How long does tapenade last once opened?**

Refrigerate it and follow the jar's guidance; pressing a thin layer of olive oil on top helps keep it fresh.
