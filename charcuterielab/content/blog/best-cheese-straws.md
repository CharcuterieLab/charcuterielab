<h2 id="how-to-choose">Cheese straws on a board</h2>

## Cheese straw questions

**What are the best store-bought cheese straws?**

Mama Geraldine's Aged Cheddar for something you can find at the grocery store, and Kim's Pecan Cheddar, a Flavor of Georgia winner, to order online.

**What are cheese straws?**

A Southern snack of sharp cheddar, butter, flour and a little cayenne, piped or cut into sticks and baked until crisp.

**Are there gluten-free cheese straws?**

Yes. Mama Geraldine's makes gluten-free Aged Cheddar and Parmesan Herb versions.

**What do you serve with cheese straws?**

Pimento cheese, pepper jelly, country ham, pickled okra and olives, with bourbon, sparkling wine or iced tea.

**How do you keep cheese straws crisp?**

Store them airtight and set them out shortly before serving, especially on a humid day.
