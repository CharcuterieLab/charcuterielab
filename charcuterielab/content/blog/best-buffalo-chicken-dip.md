<h2 id="how-to-choose">How to serve it on a game day board</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Hot or cold:</strong> most tubs can be eaten cold or heated. To serve hot, bake in an oven-safe dish at 350°F until it reaches 165°F in the center, or microwave in short bursts and stir.</li><li><strong>Dippers:</strong> celery and carrot sticks (the classic), tortilla chips, pita chips, Fritos Scoops, sturdy crackers and toasted baguette.</li><li><strong>Top it</strong> with crumbled blue cheese, sliced green onion and a drizzle of extra hot sauce or ranch.</li><li><strong>Keep it safe:</strong> hot dip should stay hot (in a slow cooker on warm) and cold dip cold. Either way, no more than 2 hours at room temperature.</li></ul>

## Buffalo dip questions

**What is the best store-bought buffalo chicken dip?**

Marketside Buffalo Style Chicken Dip from Walmart scored 10/10 at Sporked. Good & Gather's from Target won PureWow's and The Takeout's tests.

**Can you heat store-bought buffalo chicken dip?**

Most refrigerated tubs can be eaten cold or heated; check the label. Heat it to 165°F in the center, in the oven at 350°F or the microwave in short bursts.

**What do you dip in buffalo chicken dip?**

Celery, carrots, tortilla chips, pita chips, Fritos Scoops, crackers or toasted baguette.

**How long can buffalo chicken dip sit out?**

No more than 2 hours at room temperature, or 1 hour above 90°F. Keep hot dip in a slow cooker on warm.

**How much buffalo chicken dip for a party?**

About 2 to 3 tablespoons per person per dip if you're serving several. A 10 oz tub serves 5 to 8 people alongside other food.
