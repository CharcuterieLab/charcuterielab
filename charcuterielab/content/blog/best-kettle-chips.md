### 1. Cape Cod Original Sea Salt Kettle Cooked Chips

**★★★★½ 4.7 out of 5** · Best overall · Best for: Every board · $ budget

[Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2162118/best-kettle-cooked-chip-brand-cape-cod/) found Cape Cod's original sea salt beat every other kettle brand it tried, for its crunch and balanced salt. [Eat This, Not That](https://www.eatthis.com/kettle-cooked-potato-chips-taste-test) praised its curly, crunchy slices, and [Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/best-kettle-cooked-potato-chips/) gave the reduced-fat version 9 out of 10.

- **Why we like it:** Balanced salt that doesn't fight the cheese.
- **Keep in mind:** Lots of small and broken pieces at the bottom of the bag.
- **Where to buy:** Nearly every grocery store.

### 2. Miss Vickie's Original Kettle Cooked Chips

**★★★★½ 4.5 out of 5** · Crunchiest · Best for: Thick dips · $ budget

[Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/best-kettle-cooked-potato-chips/)'s best of the best with 10 out of 10, and its biggest surprise: very crunchy and robust, with a neutral, pleasant flavor.

- **Why we like it:** Robust enough for the thickest dip.
- **Keep in mind:** Not in every store.
- **Where to buy:** Grocery stores and Walmart.

### 3. Lay's Kettle Cooked Original

**★★★★☆ 4.2 out of 5** · Best value · Best for: Familiar flavor · $ budget

[Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/best-kettle-cooked-potato-chips/) scored it 9.5 out of 10 as the best classic flavor: thinner than most kettle chips but thicker than regular Lay's, and it doesn't crumble easily.

- **Why we like it:** Cheap and everywhere.
- **Keep in mind:** [Eat This, Not That](https://www.eatthis.com/kettle-cooked-potato-chips-taste-test) found it oily.
- **Where to buy:** Every grocery store.

### 4. Kettle Brand Sea Salt

## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** Potatoes, oil and salt. That's it. Check the oil if guests have allergies: [Eat This, Not That](https://www.eatthis.com/kettle-cooked-potato-chips-taste-test) notes Utz kettle chips are cooked in peanut oil.

**Skip:** Thin regular chips for dip. They snap in thick dips. Keep flavored chips like salt and vinegar or jalapeño for the snack bowl, not the cheese board.

**Serving it:** pour chips into a bowl or a corner of the board right before guests arrive, and refill from the bag so they stay crisp. Pair them with French onion dip, pimento cheese, cheddar and summer sausage.

## Kettle chip questions

**What are the best kettle chips?**

Cape Cod Original Sea Salt won Tasting Table's kettle chip test. Miss Vickie's was Sporked's best of the best.

**Are kettle chips better for dip?**

Yes. They're thicker and crunchier than regular chips, so they don't break in thick dips.

**Do potato chips belong on a charcuterie board?**

On casual and game-day boards, yes. Put them next to the dips so they don't get greasy against the cheese.

**Which kettle chips are cooked in peanut oil?**

Utz Kettle Classics, according to Eat This, Not That. Always check the label for allergy guests.
