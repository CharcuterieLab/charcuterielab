<h2 id="how-to-choose">How to use breadsticks on a board</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Stand them in a glass or jar</strong> at the back of the board for height.</li><li><strong>Wrap the top third</strong> of a thin grissino with a slice of prosciutto, bresaola or speck.</li><li><strong>Plan 2 to 3 sticks per guest</strong> next to a regular cracker.</li><li><strong>Keep them dry:</strong> put them out last; they soften next to wet cheese or olives.</li><li><strong>Dip them:</strong> they're good in olive oil, whipped ricotta or tapenade.</li></ul>

## Breadstick questions

**What are the best breadsticks for a charcuterie board?**

Alessi Sesame Breadsticks scored 9.5/10 at Sporked. For wrapping prosciutto, use thin Alessi Grissini Torinesi.

**What's the difference between grissini and breadsticks?**

Grissini is the Italian word for thin, dry, crunchy breadsticks. In the US, breadsticks can also mean soft, garlicky ones you bake from frozen.

**How do you put breadsticks on a charcuterie board?**

Stand them in a glass for height, and wrap the tops of a few with prosciutto.

**How many breadsticks per person?**

About 2 to 3 per guest alongside crackers.

**Where can I buy grissini?**

Most supermarkets carry Alessi or DeLallo in the Italian or cracker aisle.
