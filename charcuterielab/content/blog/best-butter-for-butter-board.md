# The Best Butter for a Butter Board, Ranked

> **Quick Answer:** Our top pick for butter on a charcuterie board is Kerrygold Pure Irish Butter (Salted) (4.7 out of 5). Value: Land O Lakes Salted Butter. Splurge: Les Prés Salés Camargue Sea Salt Butter. We ranked 7 widely sold options on how they work on a board, published taste tests and awards, ingredients, value and how easy they are to find.

A butter board is only as good as the butter. When it's spread across a board and eaten on bread, you taste every difference in cream, salt and tang. Three taste tests ranked grocery butters, and one brand came out on top or close to it in all three.

### 1. Kerrygold Pure Irish Butter (Salted)

**★★★★½ 4.7 out of 5** · Best overall · Best for: Every butter board · $$ mid-range

First at [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/1355867/grocery-store-butter-brands-ranked/) ("worth every penny") and [Food Republic](https://www.foodrepublic.com/1561286/store-bought-butter-brands-ranked-worst-best) ("as smooth as ... silk"), and 2nd at [Daily Meal](https://www.thedailymeal.com/2255261/store-bought-butters-ranked/), which called it the creamiest of 12.

- **Keep in mind:** Costs more than American butter.
- **Where to buy:** Nearly every grocery store and Costco.

## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** Look for cream (and salt) as the only ingredients. "European style" or a butterfat number of 82% or more means a richer, softer spread. Salted butter tastes better on a board. If you buy unsalted, finish it with flaky salt.

**Skip:** Spreads, margarine and plant butters labeled "spread." [Food Republic](https://www.foodrepublic.com/1561286/store-bought-butter-brands-ranked-worst-best) ranked them at the bottom, and they turn greasy or gelatinous at room temperature.

**Serving it:** soften the butter at room temperature for about 1 hour so it spreads without tearing, then swoop it across the board and top it with flaky salt, honey, herbs or jam. Once it's topped with herbs, garlic, cheese or fruit, leave it out no more than 2 hours, or 1 hour above 90°F. See our [butter board guide](/blog/butter-board/) for toppings and bread.

## Butter questions

**What is the best butter for a butter board?**

Kerrygold salted. It ranked first at Tasting Table and Food Republic and 2nd at Daily Meal for its rich flavor and creamy texture.

**Salted or unsalted butter for a butter board?**

Salted tastes better on its own. If you use unsalted, add flaky salt on top.

**How much butter do I need for a butter board?**

About 1 to 1½ tablespoons per guest: half a stick to ¾ stick for 4 people, 1 to 1½ sticks for 8.

**How long can a butter board sit out?**

Once it's topped, no more than 2 hours, or 1 hour above 90°F.
