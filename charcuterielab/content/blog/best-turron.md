Turrón is Spain's Christmas candy: toasted almonds bound with honey, either hard and crunchy (Alicante) or soft and creamy (Jijona). A few slices on a holiday board with Manchego and Marcona almonds look like a trip to Madrid. The best bars are mostly almonds, and the labels tell you how much.

<h2 id="how-to-choose">How to serve turrón on a board</h2>

## Turrón questions

**What is the best turrón brand?**

1880 is widely called the gold standard. In Spanish consumer group OCU's 2021 test of 25 soft turróns, Antiu Xixona Calidad Suprema scored best.

**What's the difference between turrón de Alicante and turrón de Jijona?**

Alicante is hard and crunchy, with whole toasted almonds in honey. Jijona is soft and creamy, with the almonds ground into a paste. Both are protected names (PGI) from Alicante province.

**What does Suprema mean on turrón?**

It's the top grade, with at least 60% almonds. The best bars have 65 to 72%.

**How do you cut hard turrón?**

Press a heavy knife straight down, or wrap the bar in a towel and tap it with a mallet or rolling pin.

**Does turrón need to be refrigerated?**

No. Keep it in a cool, dry place and wrap opened bars tightly.
