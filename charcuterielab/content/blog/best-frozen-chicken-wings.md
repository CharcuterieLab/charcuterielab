<h2 id="how-to-choose">How to serve frozen wings at a party</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>How many:</strong> 6 to 8 wings per guest if there's other food, 10 to 12 if wings are the main event.</li><li><strong>Air fry in batches</strong> for the crispiest skin; keep finished ones warm on a rack in a 200°F oven.</li><li><strong>Sauce at the last minute,</strong> in a big bowl, and serve right away.</li><li><strong>Offer three heat levels:</strong> mild, hot and a dry rub, plus blue cheese and ranch.</li><li><strong>Food safety:</strong> cook to 165°F, and don't leave wings out of the heat for more than 2 hours.</li></ul>

## Frozen chicken wing questions

**What are the best frozen chicken wings?**

Foster Farms Take Out Crispy Classic Buffalo Wings scored 10/10 at Sporked, and Foster Farms Take Out also won Daily Meal's test.

**How many wings per person for a Super Bowl party?**

About 6 to 8 per guest with other food, 10 to 12 if wings are the main dish.

**Can you cook frozen wings in an air fryer?**

Yes; they come out crispier than in the oven. Cook in a single layer and check they reach 165°F.

**What are the best mild frozen wings?**

Aunt Bessie's Buffalo Style, 9/10 at Sporked, with a mild, buttery sauce.

**Are Trader Joe's wings good?**

BuzzFeed's air-fryer test named Trader Joe's Hot & Spicy Chicken Wings best in show.
