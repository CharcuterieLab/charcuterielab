## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** The date. Burrata is best within a few days of being made. It should feel soft and full, not deflated.

**Skip:** Burrata past its prime. If the outside is slimy or it smells sour, it's done.

## Burrata questions

**What is the best store-bought burrata?**

BelGioioso, which scored 10 out of 10 at Sporked. Aldi's Specially Selected is the best value.

**How do I serve burrata on a board?**

Take it out about 15 minutes before serving, set it in a shallow bowl, drizzle with olive oil and flaky salt, and cut it open at the table.

**What goes with burrata?**

Prosciutto, tomatoes, peaches, basil, crusty bread and a drizzle of balsamic.
