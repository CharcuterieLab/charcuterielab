# Chorizo and Manchego: Why This Pairing Works

> **Quick Answer:** **The paprika in chorizo dissolves into the fat in Manchego**, so each makes the other taste bigger. Sheep's milk is especially good at carrying that flavor. It's Spain's classic pair for a reason.

## Why it works

Spanish chorizo gets its color and flavor from **pimentón**, Spanish smoked paprika. Pimentón's flavor dissolves in fat, not water. In the sausage, the pork fat carries it.

When you eat chorizo with Manchego, the cheese's fat picks up that paprika flavor too. Manchego is made from **sheep's milk**, and sheep's milk fat melts easily in your mouth and releases flavor quickly. So the smoky paprika tastes bigger, and it blends with the cheese's nutty, grassy flavor into one taste instead of two.

## Spicy chorizo and the heat

With **picante (hot) chorizo**, there's a second benefit. The burn from chili dissolves in fat, which is why milk and cheese cool spicy food. Manchego's fat soaks up some of the chili, so:

- The paprika flavor tastes **stronger**.
- The heat feels **gentler**.

You get more spice flavor with less burn.

## Why Spain got it right

Manchego comes from La Mancha in central Spain, and much of Spain's pimentón comes from Extremadura. Sheep farming and pork curing grew up side by side there for centuries. The pairing lasted because it works every time.

## Build the Spanish cluster

1. **Chorizo and Manchego.** Slice the chorizo 3 to 4 mm thick on an angle and set it right next to Manchego wedges or chunks. Serve both at room temperature.
2. **Add membrillo (quince paste).** Its sweetness softens the cheese's sharp edge, and its tartness cuts the fat.
3. **Add Marcona almonds.** Their mild, roasted flavor matches both the cheese and the smoky paprika.
4. **Add Castelvetrano olives.** They're mild and buttery, and they reset your palate between bites.

Chorizo, Manchego, membrillo and Marcona almonds is the classic Spanish board in four items.

## FAQ

**Why do chorizo and Manchego go together?**

Chorizo's smoked paprika dissolves in fat, and Manchego's sheep's milk fat releases that flavor easily. Together the paprika tastes bigger and blends with the nutty cheese.

**What type of chorizo is best?**

Spanish cured chorizo, either picante (hot) or dulce (sweet). Manchego softens the heat of picante. Dulce suits a mixed crowd. Fresh Mexican-style chorizo must be cooked, so it doesn't go on a board.

**Is this a traditional Spanish pairing?**

Yes. It's a classic tapa, sandwich and board pair from the same regions that make both foods.

**What else goes with chorizo and Manchego?**

Quince paste (membrillo), Marcona almonds and Castelvetrano olives.

**How do I serve them?**

Slice chorizo 3 to 4 mm thick on an angle and serve at room temperature: cold chorizo fat tastes waxy. Take Manchego out 30 to 45 minutes early and put the two side by side.
