# The Best Store-Bought Hummus for a Charcuterie Board, Ranked

> **Quick Answer:** Our top pick for hummus on a charcuterie board is Cedar's Classic Original Hummus (4.6 out of 5). Splurge: Little Sesame Classic Hummus. We ranked 7 widely sold options on how they work on a board, published taste tests and awards, ingredients, value and how easy they are to find.

Hummus turns a board into a mezze spread, and it's the easiest dip for vegetarian and vegan guests. The tests disagree more on hummus than on almost anything: one outlet's favorite is another's bottom pick. So these rankings lean on three published taste tests together, then ingredients, price and how easy each tub is to find.

*How we ranked these: these are researched picks, not a side-by-side tasting. ### 1. Cedar's Classic Original Hummus

**★★★★½ 4.6 out of 5** · Best overall · Best for: Every board · $ budget

The most consistent performer. [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/1953984/store-bought-hummus-ranked/) ranked it 2nd of 13 for a rich, creamy, earthy flavor, and [Today](https://www.today.com/today/amp/rcna41139) put it 4th of 10 as silky smooth with almost no aftertaste.

- **Why we like it:** Ranked high in two tests, with a clean ingredient list and a low price.
- **Keep in mind:** [Tasting Table]({TT}) wanted more lemon. Add a squeeze yourself.
- **Where to buy:** Most grocery stores.

### 2. Tribe Classic Hummus

**★★★★½ 4.5 out of 5** · Best for: Smooth, balanced dip · $ budget

[Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/1953984/store-bought-hummus-ranked/)'s top hummus, with subtle citrus and salt that complement the chickpeas and tahini. [Today](https://www.today.com/today/amp/rcna41139) was less impressed, ranking it 7th of 10 as bland.

- **Why we like it:** Top of the most recent big test, and easy to find.
- **Keep in mind:** Opinions split, so taste it before a big party.
- **Where to buy:** Nearly every grocery store.

### 3. Boar's Head Traditional Hummus

**★★★★½ 4.3 out of 5** · Best for: Chickpea-forward flavor · $ budget

Ranked 3rd in two tests: [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/1953984/store-bought-hummus-ranked/) called it smooth and well balanced, and [Today](https://www.today.com/today/amp/rcna41139) liked that it puts the chickpeas first.

- **Why we like it:** Consistently near the top.
- **Keep in mind:** [Tasting Table]({TT}) found it a little salty.
- **Where to buy:** Deli counters and grocery stores that carry Boar's Head.

### 4. Little Sesame Classic Hummus

**★★★★☆ 4.2 out of 5** · Best splurge · Best for: Lemon lovers · $$ mid-range

[Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/best-hummus/) gave it 9.5 out of 10 as the best lemony hummus, very creamy with a preserved-lemon note. [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/1953984/store-bought-hummus-ranked/) placed it 6th of 13 as smooth but basic.

- **Why we like it:** Bright and creamy, with a short ingredient list.
- **Keep in mind:** Pricier and harder to find.
- **Where to buy:** Amazon and some grocery stores.

### 5. Cava Traditional Hummus

**★★★★☆ 4.2 out of 5** · Best for: Mezze boards · $ budget

[Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/best-hummus/) gave it 9 out of 10 as the best traditional hummus: creamy and balanced, with tahini, lemon and a hint of garlic. [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/1953984/store-bought-hummus-ranked/) disagreed, ranking it 10th of 13 as thick and bland.

- **Why we like it:** Restaurant-style flavor at grocery prices.
- **Keep in mind:** The tests are split on it.
- **Where to buy:** Whole Foods and many grocery stores.

### 6. Holy Hummus Traditional Classic

**★★★★☆ 4.0 out of 5** · Best for: Tahini lovers · $$ mid-range

[Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/best-hummus/)'s best of the best with 10 out of 10: creamy and smooth, with a strong tahini flavor.

- **Why we like it:** The top score in Sporked's test.
- **Keep in mind:** Hard to find outside some regions. Check Instacart.
- **Where to buy:** Instacart and select grocery stores.

### 7. Sabra Classic Hummus

**★★★★☆ 3.9 out of 5** · Best for: The safe, everywhere option · $ budget

Loved by some and panned by others. [Today](https://www.today.com/today/amp/rcna41139) ranked it 1st of 10 as thick and dense, but [Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/best-hummus/) gave it 5 out of 10 and [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/1953984/store-bought-hummus-ranked/) ranked it 12th of 13 as dry and bland, noting citric acid in place of lemon juice.

- **Why we like it:** The easiest hummus to find, in every size.
- **Keep in mind:** Thick and mild. Loosen it with olive oil and lemon.
- **Where to buy:** Every grocery store.

## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** Chickpeas, tahini, lemon juice, garlic, olive oil and salt, near the top of the list. Hummus made with real lemon juice tastes brighter than one made with citric acid.

**Skip:** Dessert hummus and heavily flavored tubs on a savory board. Classic or roasted red pepper is the safe choice.

**Serving it:** scoop it into a shallow bowl, swirl the top with the back of a spoon, then add olive oil, paprika or za'atar. Serve it with pita chips, cucumbers and carrots, and keep it near the feta and olives.

## Hummus questions

**What is the best store-bought hummus?**

Cedar's Classic Original placed near the top of two taste tests. Tribe won Tasting Table's 13-brand test, and Holy Hummus scored 10 out of 10 at Sporked.

**Is Sabra good hummus?**

It depends who you ask. Today ranked Sabra Classic 1st, but Sporked and Tasting Table ranked it near the bottom.

**How do you make store-bought hummus taste better?**

Stir in a squeeze of lemon and a spoon of olive oil, then top it with paprika, za'atar or chopped parsley.

**How long can hummus sit out on a board?**

No more than 2 hours, like other perishable food.
