## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** Cheddar should be the first ingredient, ahead of mayonnaise. Spreads built on "cheese product" or American cheese are milder and smoother.

**Skip:** Squeeze-bottle or shelf-stable cheese spreads. They taste processed next to real cheese on a board.

**Serving it:** pimento cheese is mayo-based, so keep it cold until 15 to 20 minutes before guests arrive and don't leave it out more than 2 hours. Serve it in a bowl with Ritz or butter crackers, celery and pickled okra, and top it with a spoonful of pepper jelly.

## Pimento cheese questions

**What is the best store-bought pimento cheese?**

MyThreeSons Emmy's Original. It won Sporked's and Southern Living's tests and came second in StyleBlueprint's.

**Is it pimento or pimiento cheese?**

Both. Pimiento is the Spanish spelling and the name of the pepper. Most brands use pimento.

**What goes with pimento cheese on a board?**

Butter crackers, celery, pickles, pepper jelly, ham and smoked sausage.

**How long can pimento cheese sit out?**

No more than 2 hours, or 1 hour above 90°F, because it's made with mayonnaise and cheese.
