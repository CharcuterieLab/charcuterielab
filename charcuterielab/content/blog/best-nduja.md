## 'Nduja questions

**What is the best 'nduja brand?**

Tempesta, made in Chicago from a Calabrian family recipe. For a milder spread, La Quercia; for a pantry jar, Tutto Calabria.

**Where can I buy 'nduja?**

Murray's, Eataly, Whole Foods, some Hy-Vee stores, Italian delis and Amazon.

**Does 'nduja need to be cooked?**

No. It's cured, so you can spread it straight onto bread, though warming it brings out the flavor.

**Is 'nduja very spicy?**

Calabrian 'nduja is hot. La Quercia's American version is milder.

**How long does 'nduja last once opened?**

Several weeks in the fridge if you keep it wrapped or covered with oil and use a clean spoon.
