# The Best Meat Sticks for a Charcuterie Board, Ranked

> **Quick Answer:** Our top pick for meat sticks on a charcuterie board is Chomps Original Beef Stick (4.8 out of 5). Spicy: Chomps Jalapeño Beef Stick. Smoked: Tillamook Country Smoker Teriyaki Meat Sticks. We ranked 6 widely sold options on how they work on a board, published taste tests and awards, ingredients, value and how easy they are to find.

Meat sticks are the easiest cured meat for a game-day, kids' or hiking board: no slicing, no folding, and they stand up in a cup. Grocery shelves are full of them, and quality ranges from grass-fed beef to mechanically separated chicken. These rankings lean on Sporked's 25-stick test and The Takeout's 12-brand ranking.

*How we ranked these: these are researched picks, not a side-by-side tasting. We scored each product on published taste tests and awards, its ingredient list, price and where you can buy it, plus how that style of product behaves on a board. **★★★★★ 4.8 out of 5** · Best overall · Best for: Every board · $$ mid-range

Top of both tests. [Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/best-beef-sticks/) named it the best of the best with 9.5 out of 10, tasting of beef fat "like a ribeye." ## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** The first ingredient should be beef (or pork). Skip sticks made with mechanically separated chicken: Sporked disqualified them, and The Takeout ranked Slim Jim last for it.

**Skip:** Using meat sticks as your only meat. Pair them with one sliced cured meat, like salami or summer sausage, so the board doesn't read as a snack bag.

**Serving it:** cut sticks in half and stand them upright in a small cup or jar, or fan them across the board next to cheese cubes. One or two sticks per guest is plenty alongside other meats.

## Meat stick questions

**What is the best meat stick brand?**

Chomps. It won both Sporked's and The Takeout's tests for its beefy flavor, firm texture and short ingredient list.

**Are meat sticks good on a charcuterie board?**

Yes, for casual boards like game day, kids' boards and hiking. Pair them with at least one sliced cured meat.

**Do meat sticks need to be refrigerated?**

Most are shelf-stable until opened. Check the package.

**What should I avoid in a meat stick?**

Mechanically separated chicken and long lists of fillers. Beef should be the first ingredient.
