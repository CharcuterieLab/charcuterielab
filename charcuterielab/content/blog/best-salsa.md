# The Best Store-Bought Salsa for a Game Day Board, Ranked

> **Quick Answer:** Our top pick for salsa on a charcuterie board is Siete Mild Salsa (4.4 out of 5). Value: On The Border Medium Chunky Salsa. Refrigerated: Green Mountain Gringo Medium Salsa. We ranked 8 widely sold options on how they work on a board, published taste tests and awards, ingredients, value and how easy they are to find.

Salsa taste tests disagree more than almost any other category: a jar that wins one test lands near the bottom of the next. So these rankings favor salsas that did well in more than one test. We combined Sporked's 2026 roundup, The Takeout's and Chowhound's 15-jar rankings, and Eat This, Not That's taste test.

*How we ranked these: these are researched picks, not a side-by-side tasting. We scored each product on published taste tests and awards, its ingredient list, price and where you can buy it, plus how that style of product behaves on a board. Sources are listed at the end, and the full method is on our [buying guides hub](/blog/best-charcuterie-brands/#how-we-rank). Prices and stock last checked October 2026.*

### 1. Siete Mild Salsa

**★★★★½ 4.4 out of 5** · Best overall · Best for: Fresh flavor with little heat · $$ mid-range

[The Takeout](https://www.thetakeout.com/1914575/salsa-store-bought-ranked-worst-best/)'s #1 of 15 for fresh tomato, plenty of cilantro and gentle jalapeño heat. [Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/best-store-bought-salsa/) gave it 8.5 out of 10, and [Eat This, Not That](https://www.eatthis.com/store-bought-salsas-taste-test/) praised its roasted tomato flavor and short ingredient list.

- **Why we like it:** Tastes like fresh salsa, and it's mild enough for everyone.
- **Keep in mind:** Thin and salty, and pricier than most jars.
- **Where to buy:** Target, Walmart, Whole Foods and Kroger.

### 2. On The Border Medium Chunky Salsa

**★★★★½ 4.3 out of 5** · Best value · Best for: Chunky salsa on a budget · $ budget

[Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/best-store-bought-salsa/)'s best chunky salsa at 8 out of 10, and The Takeout named On The Border's red salsa the [best jarred red salsa](https://www.thetakeout.com/2006238/best-tortilla-chips-on-the-border-cafe-style/) after a 13-jar test. [Chowhound](https://www.chowhound.com/1903670/popular-jarred-store-bought-salsa-ranked-worst-best) ranked its mild version 6th of 15.

- **Why we like it:** Thick enough to stay on the chip, and cheap.
- **Keep in mind:** Not as fresh-tasting as refrigerated salsa.
- **Where to buy:** Nearly every grocery store.

### 3. Green Mountain Gringo Medium Salsa

**★★★★☆ 4.0 out of 5** · Best refrigerated · Best for: Chunky, homemade-style · $$ mid-range

Top half in three tests: 4th at [Chowhound](https://www.chowhound.com/1903670/popular-jarred-store-bought-salsa-ranked-worst-best), 6th at [The Takeout](https://www.thetakeout.com/1914575/salsa-store-bought-ranked-worst-best/), and a fresh, homemade-tasting favorite at [Eat This, Not That](https://www.eatthis.com/store-bought-salsas-taste-test/).

- **Why we like it:** Short, clean ingredient list and big chunks.
- **Keep in mind:** Sold refrigerated, and the flavor can lean acidic.
- **Where to buy:** Refrigerated section at most grocery stores.

## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** Tomatoes first, then peppers, onions and cilantro. Refrigerated salsas taste fresher; jarred ones last longer and are thicker.

**Skip:** Choosing by heat level alone. Medium from one brand can be hotter than hot from another. For a mixed crowd, put out a mild salsa and a hot one.

**How much to buy:** about 2 to 3 tablespoons of salsa per guest, so a 16 oz jar covers 10 to 12 people. Once a jar is open, keep it in the fridge and follow the use-by time on the label. Fresh refrigerated salsa goes off faster than jarred.

## Salsa questions

**What is the best store-bought salsa?**

Siete Mild Salsa did best across tests: #1 of 15 at The Takeout and 8.5 out of 10 at Sporked. For a cheaper chunky jar, On The Border Medium.

**What is the best jarred salsa?**

Siete Mild for flavor and On The Border Medium Chunky for value. Pace Restaurant Style scored 9.5 at Sporked.

**What is the best hot salsa?**

Casa Sanchez Hot Salsa Roja if you can find it. Mateo's Hot is the easier-to-find option.

**How much salsa do I need for a party?**

About 2 to 3 tablespoons per guest, or a 16 oz jar for every 10 to 12 people.
