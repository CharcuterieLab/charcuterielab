## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** Look for a short list: flour, oil and salt. Baked chips with seasoning baked in (like Stacy's Rosemary) hold their flavor better than chips dusted with powder.

**Skip:** Pita "thins" and crackers for heavy dips. They're thinner and snap in thick hummus or whipped feta.

**Serving it:** plan on 10 to 12 chips per guest when the board is the party food. Put them next to the dips, not under the cheese, and refill from the bag instead of piling them high so they stay crisp.
