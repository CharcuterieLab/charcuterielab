<h2 id="what-to-look-for">What to look for</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Look for:</strong> Look for a short list: flour, oil and salt. Baked chips with seasoning baked in (like Stacy&#x27;s Rosemary) hold their flavor better than chips dusted with powder.</li><li class="sk-x"><strong>Skip:</strong> Pita &quot;thins&quot; and crackers for heavy dips. They&#x27;re thinner and snap in thick hummus or whipped feta.</li></ul>
:::

**Serving it:** plan on 10 to 12 chips per guest when the board is the party food. Put them next to the dips, not under the cheese, and refill from the bag instead of piling them high so they stay crisp.

<h2 id="faq">Pita chip questions</h2>
:::

**What are the best pita chips?**

Stacy's Simply Naked. Sporked gave it 10 out of 10, and Eat This, Not That ranked it first of seven Stacy's flavors.

**Are there cheaper pita chips as good as Stacy's?**

Aldi's Specially Selected Sea Salt Pita Chips scored 8.5 out of 10 in Sporked's test, and shoppers often compare them to Stacy's.

**Are pita chips good on a charcuterie board?**

Yes. They're sturdier than most crackers, so they work for hummus, whipped feta and soft cheese.

**How many pita chips per person?**

About 10 to 12 when the board is the party food, or 6 to 8 before a meal.
