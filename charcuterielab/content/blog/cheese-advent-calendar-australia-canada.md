## Make your own (the most reliable option)

If nothing near you is in stock, a homemade calendar is easy and usually better cheese for the money.

1. **Pick 6 cheeses for 12 days, or 12 for 24 days.** Mix textures: one soft, one blue, one aged hard cheese, one washed-rind and a couple of crowd-pleasers.
2. **Cut each cheese into two pieces of about 30 to 40 g.** That's 400 to 500 g of cheese for a 12-day calendar.
3. **Wrap each piece in baking paper**, number it and keep the lot in a container in the fridge. Add a small jar of honey, quince paste or crackers on a few days.

**Australian picks:** a local brie or triple cream, a vintage cheddar, a blue, [Jarlsberg](/ingredients/jarlsberg/), a goat's cheese and an aged gouda.

**Canadian picks:** Oka, a clothbound cheddar, a Quebec brie, a Bleu Bénédictin-style blue, a washed-rind from Quebec and an aged gouda from a Canadian dairy.

## FAQ

**Is there a cheese advent calendar in Australia in 2026?**

Yes. Hunter Valley Cheese Company sells a 12 Day Artisan Cheese Advent Calendar for A$150, dispatched from December 7. Woolworths has sold the Thomas Dux 12 Days of Cheese calendar in past years, but we couldn't confirm it for 2026 yet.

**Does Aldi Australia sell a cheese advent calendar in 2026?**

Not that we could find. Aldi Australia's advent calendars go on sale Wednesday, November 4, and none of the eight listed is a cheese calendar.

**Is there a cheese advent calendar in Canada in 2026?**

Metro lists the Ilchester Cheese Advent Calendar online, and Laliberté Cheese in BC's Comox Valley sells a 24-day artisan calendar from C$135. Aldi doesn't operate in Canada.

**Can I order a US or UK cheese advent calendar to Australia or Canada?**

Usually not. The US cheesemaker calendars we checked list US shipping only, because the cheese is perishable and ships chilled.

**How do I make my own cheese advent calendar?**

Buy six cheeses, cut each into two pieces of about 30 to 40 g, then wrap and number the 12 parcels. Use 12 cheeses for a 24-day calendar, and keep it in the fridge.
