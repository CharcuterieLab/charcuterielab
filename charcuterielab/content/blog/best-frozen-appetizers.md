<h2 id="how-to-choose">How to serve frozen appetizers at a party</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>How many:</strong> plan 4 to 6 pieces per guest before a dinner, 8 to 10 for a drop-in party, and 12 to 15 when appetizers are the meal.</li><li><strong>Mix three kinds:</strong> something crisp (wontons, spring rolls), something rich (quiche, pastry bites) and one vegetarian option.</li><li><strong>Bake in waves.</strong> Put out one tray, then the next 20 minutes later, so nothing sits out cold.</li><li><strong>Use the air fryer for small batches;</strong> pastry and tempura come out crisper than in a crowded oven.</li><li><strong>Set them next to a board.</strong> A cold charcuterie board fills the table while the hot trays cycle through.</li><li><strong>Food safety:</strong> hot appetizers shouldn't sit out more than 2 hours.</li></ul>

## Frozen appetizer questions

**What are the best frozen appetizers?**

Trader Joe's Pastry Bites with Feta & Caramelized Onions and Mini Quiche Duo both scored 10/10 at Sporked. At Costco, Cuisine Adventures Spanakopita was Consumer Reports' top heat-and-eat appetizer.

**How many frozen appetizers do I need per person?**

About 4 to 6 pieces per guest before a dinner, 8 to 10 for a drop-in party, and 12 to 15 if appetizers are the meal.

**What's the best Costco frozen appetizer for a party?**

Cuisine Adventures Spanakopita for a crowd-pleasing vegetarian bite, and Kirkland Signature Tempura Shrimp when you need a lot: a box holds about 40 to 50 shrimp.

**Can I cook frozen appetizers in an air fryer?**

Yes, and pastry and tempura usually come out crisper. Cook in single layers and start checking a few minutes before the box's oven time.

**What's a good vegetarian frozen appetizer?**

Trader Joe's Mini Vegetable Samosas, the feta and caramelized onion pastry bites, and Costco's spanakopita.
