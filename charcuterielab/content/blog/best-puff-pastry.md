# The Best Store-Bought Puff Pastry for Baked Brie and Holiday Appetizers

> **Quick Answer:** **Dufour Classic Puff Pastry** won all three published taste tests (America's Test Kitchen, Tasting Table and Allrecipes), at about $10 to $13 for one sheet. For all-butter pastry on a budget, **Trader Joe's All Butter Puff Pastry**, ATK's co-winner, about $5 for two sheets, in stores only in late fall and winter.

Puff pastry is the shortcut behind half the holiday appetizers on a board: baked brie, pigs in a blanket, cheese straws and pastry Christmas trees. The difference between brands comes down to fat. All-butter sheets taste richer and rise higher, while shortening-based sheets are cheaper and easier to find. Three published taste tests agree on the winner.

<h2 id="how-to-choose">Puff pastry tips for baked brie and party bites</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Thaw it in the fridge overnight</strong> so it stays cold. Warm, sticky pastry won&#x27;t puff.</li><li><strong>One sheet wraps one 8 oz brie wheel.</strong> Bake at 400°F for 20 to 25 minutes, until deep golden. Our <a href="/blog/baked-brie-recipe/">baked brie recipe</a> has the full method and 12 toppings.</li><li><strong>Chill before baking.</strong> 15 minutes in the fridge after shaping makes the layers rise higher.</li><li><strong>Brush with egg wash</strong> for a glossy, deep brown top, and keep it off the cut edges so they can rise.</li><li><strong>All-butter pastry needs a little longer</strong> in the oven than shortening-based sheets, or the bottom stays doughy.</li></ul>

## Puff pastry questions

**What is the best store-bought puff pastry?**

Dufour Classic Puff Pastry. It won at America's Test Kitchen (tied with Trader Joe's), Tasting Table and Allrecipes.

**Is Pepperidge Farm puff pastry made with butter?**

No. It's made with palm, soybean and cottonseed oils. That's why it's cheaper and milder than all-butter brands like Dufour and Trader Joe's.

**When does Trader Joe's sell puff pastry?**

Its All Butter Puff Pastry is seasonal, in stores in late fall and early winter. It keeps for months in the freezer.

**What puff pastry is best for baked brie?**

An all-butter sheet: Dufour for the best flavor, or Trader Joe's to save money. One sheet wraps an 8 oz wheel; bake at 400°F for 20 to 25 minutes.

**How do you thaw frozen puff pastry?**

Overnight in the fridge is best, so it stays cold and puffs well. Keep it chilled until right before you shape it.

**Is there gluten-free puff pastry?**

Yes. Schär's was Allrecipes' best gluten-free puff pastry, sold in 17.6 oz packs of two sheets.
