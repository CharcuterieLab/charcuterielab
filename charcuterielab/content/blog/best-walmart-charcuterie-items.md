## Building a board from these

For 6 people:

- **Cheese:** the Parmigiano, the triple crème brie and Boursin
- **Meat:** the prosciutto and peppercorn salami
- **Crackers:** Good Thins
- **Extras:** olives, plus grapes and raspberries from produce

[Ain't Too Proud To Meg](https://ainttooproudtomeg.com/how-to-create-a-walmart-charcuterie-cheese-board/)'s similar Walmart boards came to about $32 to $35 before tax.

Plan on about 3 oz of cheese and 3 oz of meat per guest when the board is the party food, or 2 oz each before a meal. For more cheap boards, see our [budget charcuterie board](/blog/budget-charcuterie-board/) and the [$20 charcuterie board challenge](/blog/20-dollar-charcuterie-board-challenge/).

## Shopping tips

- **Find the specialty case.** In many stores the specialty cheeses are in a case near produce or the deli, separate from the dairy aisle.
- **Skip the cubed trays for a nice board.** The Marketside cubed meat and cheese tray is a top-rated quick snack on [Walmart](https://www.walmart.com/browse/food/specialty-cheese-charcuterie/marketside/976759_976789_4740967_6993020/YnJhbmQ6TWFya2V0c2lkZQieie), but wedges cut by hand look and taste better.
- **Know the trade-off.** [The Takeout](https://www.thetakeout.com/2249755/every-major-grocery-store-ranked-cheese-selection/) ranked Walmart last of 13 chains for cheese selection, because it carries few artisan cheeses. For value and a quick, good board, the picks above are enough.
