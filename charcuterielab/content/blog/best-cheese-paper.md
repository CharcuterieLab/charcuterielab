## Cheese paper questions

**Is cheese paper worth it?**

For convenience, yes. In America's Test Kitchen's test, parchment plus loose foil worked as well; cheese paper added only a few days.

**Can you reuse cheese paper?**

Yes, for the same type of cheese. Wipe the inside with a damp paper towel and replace it once butterfat stains show.

**Why not plastic wrap?**

It traps moisture against the cheese. In America's Test Kitchen's test, plastic-wrapped cheese molded first.

**Where in the fridge should cheese go?**

The vegetable drawer, where it's colder and more humid than the door.

**What's a good gift for a cheese lover?**

A box of cheese storage bags is a good stocking stuffer; a Cheese Grotto is the splurge.
