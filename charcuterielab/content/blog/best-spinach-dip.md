<h2 id="what-to-look-for">What to look for</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Look for:</strong> Look for cream cheese, sour cream or real cheese near the top and visible spinach and artichoke pieces. Dips built mostly on mayonnaise or yogurt taste tangier and thinner.</li><li class="sk-x"><strong>Skip:</strong> Very thick, cream-cheese-style dips for chips. <a href="https://www.eatthis.com/best-tasting-spinach-artichoke-dip/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Eat This, Not That</a> found Philadelphia&#x27;s spinach dip so thick it broke sturdy chips. Use those as a spread instead.</li></ul>
:::

**Serving it:** cold dips should stay in the fridge until guests arrive and come off the table after 2 hours. Serve warm dips in a small oven-safe dish or mini slow cooker. Put out sturdy dippers: pita chips, tortilla chips, baguette slices, carrots and bell pepper strips.

<h2 id="faq">Spinach dip questions</h2>
:::

**What is the best store-bought spinach dip?**

Marketside Spinach & Artichoke Dip from Walmart, which Sporked scored 10 out of 10. La Terra Fina, at Costco and many grocers, ranked first at Eat This, Not That.

**Can you heat store-bought spinach artichoke dip?**

Many can be. Check the tub. La Terra Fina is made to be served cold or warm, and Trader Joe's frozen dip must be heated.

**What do you dip in spinach dip?**

Pita chips, tortilla chips, baguette slices, crackers, carrots and bell pepper strips.

**How long can spinach dip sit out?**

No more than 2 hours, or 1 hour above 90°F.
