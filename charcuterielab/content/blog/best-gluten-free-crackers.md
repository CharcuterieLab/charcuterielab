Rice and seed crackers with a big crunch. The Roasted Garlic version scored 10 out of 10 at [Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/best-gluten-free-crackers/), and [Daily Meal](https://www.thedailymeal.com/1568492/gluten-free-cracker-brands-ranked/) praised how the plain version lets toppings shine.

- **Where to buy:** Most grocery stores, Costco club size.

## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** Look for a certified gluten-free mark, not just 'wheat-free'. Rice crackers glazed with regular soy sauce are not gluten-free, and several grain-free crackers are made with almond flour, which matters for nut allergies.

**Skip:** Soft, saltine-style gluten-free crackers for a board. In Daily Meal's test they were extremely fragile and crumbled under toppings.

## Gluten-free crackers questions

**What is the best gluten-free cracker for a charcuterie board?**

Crunchmaster Multi-Seed. It is crunchy, inexpensive, easy to find and scored highly in published taste tests.

**Are rice crackers gluten-free?**

Not always. Crackers glazed with regular soy sauce contain wheat. Look for a certified gluten-free label.

**How do I keep gluten-free crackers safe on a shared board?**

Give them their own section or a separate small board, with their own knives, so crumbs from wheat crackers don't cross over.
