<h2 id="what-to-look-for">What to look for</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Look for:</strong> Look for a certified gluten-free mark, not just &#x27;wheat-free&#x27;. Rice crackers glazed with regular soy sauce are not gluten-free, and several grain-free crackers are made with almond flour, which matters for nut allergies.</li><li class="sk-x"><strong>Skip:</strong> Soft, saltine-style gluten-free crackers for a board. In Daily Meal&#x27;s test they were extremely fragile and crumbled under toppings.</li></ul>

<h2 id="faq">Gluten-free crackers questions</h2>
:::

**What is the best gluten-free cracker for a charcuterie board?**

Crunchmaster Multi-Seed. It is crunchy, inexpensive, easy to find and scored highly in published taste tests.

**Are rice crackers gluten-free?**

Not always. Crackers glazed with regular soy sauce contain wheat. Look for a certified gluten-free label.

**How do I keep gluten-free crackers safe on a shared board?**

Give them their own section or a separate small board, with their own knives, so crumbs from wheat crackers don't cross over.
