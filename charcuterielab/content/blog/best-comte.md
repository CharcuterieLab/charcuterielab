# The Best Comté Cheese to Buy for a Cheese Board

> **Quick Answer:** Best overall: **Marcel Petite Comté** (sold as **Essex Comté**), aged 12 to 18 months. Best value: **Trader Joe's Comté**. For a showpiece: a **24-month** Comté. For melting: a young **8 to 10-month** Comté. Plan 1 to 1.5 oz per guest.

<h2 id="how-to-choose">How to pick and serve Comté</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Read the age:</strong> 8 to 12 months is mild and milky, 12 to 18 months is nutty and fruity, 24 months and up is savory and crunchy.</li><li><strong>For one cheese board,</strong> 12 to 18 months is the sweet spot: complex but friendly.</li><li><strong>Slice it thin</strong> into triangles from the wedge, or break old Comté into chunks.</li><li><strong>Pair it with</strong> walnuts, apples, Chardonnay or Riesling; see <a href="/pairings/food/comte/">what goes with Comté</a>.</li><li><strong>Comté or Gruyère?</strong> Comté is fruitier and changes with the seasons; Gruyère is saltier. ## Comté questions

**What is the best Comté to buy?**

Comté aged by Marcel Petite (often sold as Essex Comté) is the cheesemonger favorite. Trader Joe's Comté is the best value.

**What age Comté is best?**

12 to 18 months for a cheese board, 24 months for a showpiece, and 8 to 10 months for melting.

**Is Trader Joe's Comté real Comté?**

Yes. The Takeout called it totally legit, and Trader Joe's has stocked it for years.

**Is Comté pasteurized?**

No. AOP Comté is made from raw milk, so most pregnant guests should skip it.

**How much Comté per person?**

About 1 to 1.5 oz per guest when it's one of three cheeses.
