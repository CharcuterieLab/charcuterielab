## Board set questions

**Is the SMIRLY charcuterie board worth it?**

As a gift, yes: it includes knives, bowls, a fruit tray and labels, and buyers rate it highly. If you already have knives, a plain board like Royal Craft Wood costs about a third as much.

**Bamboo or acacia?**

Bamboo is lighter, cheaper and harder on knives over time; acacia has richer grain and looks more upscale. Both are fine for serving.

**Are charcuterie boards dishwasher safe?**

No. Wood boards warp and crack in a dishwasher. Hand-wash, dry right away, and oil them now and then.

**What size board for 10 people?**

About 18 inches, or 24 inches if the board is the main food.

**Did you test these boards?**

No. This review is built from published testers (Reviewed, NBC Select), Tasting Table's review roundup and buyer ratings, and we say where those sources disagree.
