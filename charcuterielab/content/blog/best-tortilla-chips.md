### 1. On The Border Café Style

**★★★★½ 4.7 out of 5** · Best overall · Best for: Dips and nachos · $ budget

The winner at [The Takeout](https://www.thetakeout.com/2006238/best-tortilla-chips-on-the-border-cafe-style/), which praised its corn flavor and airy, bubbly crunch, and at [Eat This, Not That](https://www.eatthis.com/tortilla-chips-taste-test/), which said it's the closest thing to restaurant chips. [Food Network](https://www.foodnetwork.com/how-to/packages/shopping/product-reviews/best-tortilla-chips) named it best for nachos because it's sturdy under toppings.

- **Why we like it:** Light but sturdy, so it scoops thick dips without breaking.
- **Keep in mind:** Salty, and the big bags go stale fast once opened.
- **Where to buy:** Nearly every grocery store, Target and Walmart.

### 2. Late July Organic Sea Salt

**★★★★½ 4.3 out of 5** · Best organic · Best for: Organic and lighter · $$ mid-range

[Eat This, Not That](https://www.eatthis.com/tortilla-chips-taste-test/) ranked it second of seven: thin, crisp, airy and not greasy.

- **Why we like it:** Organic corn and a short ingredient list.
- **Keep in mind:** Thin, so it can crack in very thick dips.
- **Where to buy:** Target, Whole Foods, Kroger and most grocery stores.

### 3. El Nacho Original Sea Salt "OG"

**★★★★☆ 4.0 out of 5** · Best for: A restaurant-style splurge · $$ mid-range

[Food Network](https://www.foodnetwork.com/how-to/packages/shopping/product-reviews/best-tortilla-chips)'s best overall in its 2026 test. One tester called it "your restaurant chip," and it held up under toppings.

- **Why we like it:** Balanced salt and a real restaurant crunch.
- **Keep in mind:** Newer brand, so it's harder to find and costs more.
- **Where to buy:** Select grocery stores and online.

### 4. Xochitl White Corn Chips

**★★★★☆ 4.0 out of 5** · Best for: Snacking with salsa · $$ mid-range

[Food Network](https://www.foodnetwork.com/how-to/packages/shopping/product-reviews/best-tortilla-chips)'s best for snacking, with a toasted flavor and crunch. [Eat This, Not That](https://www.eatthis.com/tortilla-chips-taste-test/) found them extra salty and crispy and "could be addictive."

- **Why we like it:** Great plain or with thin salsa.
- **Keep in mind:** Too thin for loaded nachos.
- **Where to buy:** Grocery stores, Costco and online.

### 5. Tostitos Original Restaurant Style

**★★★★☆ 3.9 out of 5** · Best value · Best for: Budget boards · $ budget

The default grocery-store chip. It didn't win a test, but it's light, crisp and evenly salted, and it's on sale somewhere every week.

- **Why we like it:** Cheap and available everywhere.
- **Keep in mind:** Less corn flavor than the winners.
- **Where to buy:** Every grocery store.

### 6. Siete Sea Salt Tortilla Chips (Maíz)

**★★★½☆ 3.6 out of 5** · Best for: Avocado-oil chips · $$$ premium

[Food Network](https://www.foodnetwork.com/how-to/packages/shopping/product-reviews/best-tortilla-chips)'s best with avocado oil, made from nixtamalized corn and still crunchy and scoopable. [Eat This, Not That](https://www.eatthis.com/tortilla-chips-taste-test/) found the flavor a little packaged for the price.

- **Why we like it:** Fried in avocado oil, with a short ingredient list.
- **Keep in mind:** One of the priciest chips per ounce.
- **Where to buy:** Target, Whole Foods, Kroger and Costco (varies).

## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** Corn, oil and salt, and not much else. Look for "restaurant style" or "café style" if you want thicker chips for dipping.

**Skip:** Flavored chips (lime, nacho cheese) as your only chip. They fight the salsa and guacamole. Add one flavored bag next to a plain one.

**How much to buy:** plan on about 1 to 1.5 oz of chips per guest when there are dips, or a 13 oz bag for every 8 to 10 people. Refill a small bowl rather than piling the whole bag on the board, so the chips stay crisp.

## Tortilla chip questions

**What are the best store-bought tortilla chips?**

On The Border Café Style. It won taste tests at The Takeout and Eat This, Not That, and Food Network named it best for nachos.

**What are the best tortilla chips for nachos?**

Thicker restaurant-style chips like On The Border Café Style, which stay crisp under toppings. Thin chips like Xochitl get soggy.

**What are the best organic tortilla chips?**

Late July Organic Sea Salt.

**How do you keep tortilla chips crisp on a board?**

Serve a small bowl and refill it, keep the bag sealed between refills, and keep wet dips in bowls rather than spooned onto the board.
