<h2 id="what-to-look-for">What to look for</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Look for:</strong> Corn, oil and salt, and not much else. Look for &quot;restaurant style&quot; or &quot;café style&quot; if you want thicker chips for dipping.</li><li class="sk-x"><strong>Skip:</strong> Flavored chips (lime, nacho cheese) as your only chip. They fight the salsa and guacamole. Add one flavored bag next to a plain one.</li></ul>
:::

**How much to buy:** plan on about 1 to 1.5 oz of chips per guest when there are dips, or a 13 oz bag for every 8 to 10 people. Refill a small bowl rather than piling the whole bag on the board, so the chips stay crisp.

<h2 id="faq">Tortilla chip questions</h2>
:::

**What are the best store-bought tortilla chips?**

On The Border Café Style. It won taste tests at The Takeout and Eat This, Not That, and Food Network named it best for nachos.

**What are the best tortilla chips for nachos?**

Thicker restaurant-style chips like On The Border Café Style, which stay crisp under toppings. Thin chips like Xochitl get soggy.

**What are the best organic tortilla chips?**

Late July Organic Sea Salt.

**How do you keep tortilla chips crisp on a board?**

Serve a small bowl and refill it, keep the bag sealed between refills, and keep wet dips in bowls rather than spooned onto the board.
