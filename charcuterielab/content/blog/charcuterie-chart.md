# The Charcuterie Chart: Amounts, Timing and Pairings on One Page

> **Quick Answer:** Meat and cheese, each: **2 oz per guest before a meal, 3 oz as party food, 4 oz as dinner**, plus 10% over 20 guests. Hard cheese out 45–60 minutes early, soft cheese 20–30. Board out 2 hours max.

<h2 id="per-guest">1. How much per guest</h2>
:::

| The board is... | Meat per guest | Cheese per guest | Crackers per guest |
|---|---|---|---|
| An appetizer (a meal follows) | 2 oz | 2 oz | 6 to 8 |
| The party food (no meal) | 3 oz | 3 oz | 10 to 12 |
| Dinner | 4 oz | 4 oz | 12 to 15 |

From **20 guests up, add 10%** to cover second helpings and late arrivals.

<h2 id="totals">2. Quick totals for small groups</h2>
:::

Meat and cheese **each**, before any buffer:

| Guests | Appetizer | Party food | Dinner |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 8 oz | 12 oz | 16 oz (1 lb) |
| 8 | 16 oz (1 lb) | 24 oz (1.5 lb) | 32 oz (2 lb) |
| 12 | 24 oz (1.5 lb) | 36 oz (2.25 lb) | 48 oz (3 lb) |

For 20, 50 or 100 guests, the [Party Planner](/party-planner/) builds the list with the buffer included and rounds cheese up to the half pound.

:::html
<h2 id="fridge">3. When to take things out of the fridge</h2>
:::

| Food | Time before serving |
|---|---|
| Aged hard cheese (Parmigiano, aged cheddar) | 45 to 60 min |
| Semi-firm cheese (Manchego, Gouda) | 30 to 45 min |
| Soft cheese (brie, camembert, triple-crème, Taleggio) | 20 to 30 min |
| Fresh cheese (mozzarella, goat cheese) | 15 to 20 min |
| Cured meats | 20 to 30 min |

![Chart of when to take cheese out of the fridge before serving](/images/charcuterie-chart-fridge-times.webp)

<h2 id="pairings">4. Pairings that always work</h2>
:::

| Cheese | Meat | Sweet | Drink |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brie | Prosciutto | Fig jam | Sparkling wine |
| Aged cheddar | Genoa salami | Apple | Pinot Noir or amber ale |
| Manchego | Chorizo | Quince paste | Tempranillo |

<h2 id="build">5. The build order</h2>
:::

1. Set down small bowls for anything wet (honey, jam, olives, pickles).
2. Place the cheeses, spread apart.
3. Fold or roll the meats into a river between the cheeses.
4. Fill the gaps with fruit.
5. Add nuts and small fillers.
6. Add the crackers and bread within 30 minutes of serving, so they stay crisp.
7. Finish with herbs or honeycomb.

Why this order: see [the right order to build a charcuterie board](/blog/build-sequence/).

:::html
<h2 id="safety">6. Food safety</h2>
:::

Keep perishable food out **no more than 2 hours**, or **1 hour above 90°F**, per USDA guidance. For pregnant guests, the CDC advises reheating deli meats and dry sausages until steaming hot and choosing pasteurized cheeses.
