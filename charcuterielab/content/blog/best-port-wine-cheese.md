<h2 id="how-to-choose">How to serve port wine cheese on a board</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Take it out 15 to 20 minutes early</strong> so it spreads without tearing the crackers. Don&#x27;t leave it out more than 2 hours.</li><li><strong>Serve it in its own small bowl or crock</strong> with a spreader, or pipe it onto crackers for a party tray.</li><li><strong>Pair it with</strong> butter crackers, pretzel crisps, toasted pecans, apple and pear slices, red grapes and summer sausage.</li><li><strong>Pour a glass of tawny port</strong> or a fruity red alongside. The fruit in the cheese and the wine meet in the middle.</li><li><strong>How much:</strong> about 1 to 2 oz per guest when it&#x27;s one of several cheeses, so a 12.9 oz tub covers 6 to 12 people.</li></ul>

## Port wine cheese questions

**What is the best port wine cheese spread?**

For value, Merkt's (about $5.48 for 12.9 oz at Walmart). For a sweeter spread, Kaukauna, which Sporked named its best dessert cheese. Pine River is the pick for vegetarian guests because it's colored without carmine.

**What is port wine cheese made of?**

Cheddar that's ground and blended cold with port wine, cream and whey, then colored red or purple. It's called cold pack, club or crock cheese because it's never heated.

**Is port wine cheese vegetarian?**

Often not. Merkt's and Kaukauna's port wine ball use carmine, a red color made from insects. Pine River uses annatto and Red 40 instead.

**Does port wine cheese have alcohol in it?**

It's made with port wine and isn't cooked, so a small amount can remain. Check the label for anyone who avoids alcohol entirely.

**What goes with port wine cheese?**

Butter crackers, pretzel crisps, pecans, apple and pear slices, grapes and summer sausage, with a glass of tawny port.

**How long can port wine cheese sit out?**

Up to 2 hours at room temperature, then back in the fridge.
