# The Best Store-Bought Cocktail Sauce for Shrimp Cocktail

> **Quick Answer:** Three taste tests picked three different winners. **Whole Foods 365** won Tasting Table's test for the richest flavor (about $3.29). **Louisiana Fish Fry**, made with anchovy, won Chowhound's. **Waterfront Bistro** at Safeway and Albertsons won The Daily Meal's and was the cheapest. Avoid budget sauces sweetened mostly with corn syrup.

<h2 id="how-to-choose">How to serve shrimp cocktail on a board</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Buy cooked, peeled shrimp with the tails on</strong> so guests have a handle. Thaw frozen shrimp overnight in the fridge and pat them dry.</li><li><strong>Keep it cold.</strong> Set the bowl of shrimp over a larger bowl of ice, and don&#x27;t leave it out more than 2 hours.</li><li><strong>Taste the sauce first.</strong> Most jars are mild on horseradish. Stir in a spoonful of prepared horseradish and a squeeze of lemon to wake it up.</li><li><strong>Round out the board</strong> with lemon wedges, smoked salmon, smoked oysters, crab dip, crackers, celery and a dry sparkling wine.</li><li><strong>How much:</strong> about 4 to 6 shrimp per person as one of several appetizers, and about 2 tablespoons of sauce per person.</li></ul>

## Cocktail sauce questions

**What is the best store-bought cocktail sauce?**

Whole Foods' 365 won Tasting Table's test, Louisiana Fish Fry won Chowhound's and Waterfront Bistro won The Daily Meal's. For one jar that pleased the most testers, start with 365 or Louisiana Fish Fry.

**Is Heinz cocktail sauce good?**

It landed in the middle of all three tests: thick and easy to find, but mostly ketchup and sweetness, with horseradish that fades quickly.

**How do you make store-bought cocktail sauce better?**

Stir in a spoonful of prepared horseradish and a squeeze of lemon. A dash of hot sauce or Worcestershire adds depth.

**What is cocktail sauce made of?**

Ketchup or tomato paste, horseradish and lemon or vinegar, often with hot sauce and Worcestershire.

**How much shrimp cocktail per person?**

About 4 to 6 shrimp per person as one of several appetizers, with about 2 tablespoons of sauce each.

**How long can shrimp cocktail sit out?**

No more than 2 hours. Keep the shrimp on ice while it's out.
