<h2 id="how-to-choose">How to choose</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Check the weight.</strong> Balls run from 6 to 10 oz. A 6 oz ball serves about 6 guests as one of several cheeses, or 3 if it's the only cheese.</li><li><strong>Nut allergy at the party?</strong> Almonds or pecans coat nearly every store-bought ball. Read the label.</li><li><strong>Vegetarian guests?</strong> Port-wine balls are often colored with carmine, which is made from insects.</li><li><strong>Take it out 15 to 20 minutes early</strong> so it spreads, and put it out with a spreader and sturdy crackers.</li></ul><p class="sk-note">Want to make your own? Our <a href="/blog/cheese-ball-recipe/">cheese ball recipe</a> takes 15 minutes plus chilling and can be made up to 3 days ahead.</p>

## Cheese ball questions

**How many people does a cheese ball serve?**

A 6 oz ball serves about 6 guests when it's one of several cheeses, or about 3 if it's the only cheese. A 9 to 10 oz ball serves about 9 to 10 as part of a spread.

**Can you freeze a store-bought cheese ball?**

The packages we checked don't give freezing directions, so keep store-bought balls in the fridge and go by the best-by date. Homemade cheese balls freeze well.

**How far ahead can I buy a cheese ball?**

Check the best-by date and keep it refrigerated until the party. Mail-order balls from Hickory Farms come with at least 45 days before the best-by date.

**What crackers go with a cheese ball?**

Sturdy ones that won't snap: water crackers, pita crackers, pretzel crisps or crostini. Add celery and bell pepper strips, and graham crackers for a sweet cranberry ball.

**Should a cheese ball sit out before serving?**

Yes. Take it out about 15 to 20 minutes before serving so it softens enough to spread, and don't leave it out more than 2 hours.
