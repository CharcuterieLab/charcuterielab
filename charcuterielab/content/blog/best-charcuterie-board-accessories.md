## Charcuterie accessory questions

**What accessories do you need for a charcuterie board?**

Small bowls or ramekins, cheese knives, cocktail picks, cheese markers, small spoons and a honey dipper.

**What size ramekins are best for a charcuterie board?**

2 to 4 oz. They hold enough olives or jam without taking over the board.

**How do you label cheese on a board?**

Slate or porcelain cheese markers, or small paper flags on picks. Add the milk type for guests with allergies.

**What's a good charcuterie gift under $25?**

Slate cheese markers, a pack of honey dippers with a jar of local honey, or a ramekin set.

**Do I need a cheese dome?**

It's nice for hosting: cheese can warm to room temperature under it without drying out.
