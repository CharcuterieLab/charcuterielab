<h2 id="how-to-choose">Dolce vs piccante, and what goes with Gorgonzola</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Dolce</strong> is young, soft and sweet: the board choice for most guests.</li><li><strong>Piccante</strong> is aged longer, firmer and spicier: for blue lovers.</li><li><strong>Pair it with</strong> honey, pears, figs, walnuts and prosciutto.</li><li><strong>Drink:</strong> ruby port, Moscato d'Asti or a big red like Barolo or Zinfandel.</li><li><strong>Serve it</strong> at room temperature, out of the fridge 45 minutes ahead. About 1 oz per person.</li></ul>

## Gorgonzola questions

**What is the best Gorgonzola to buy?**

Gorgonzola Dolce DOP for a board. At the supermarket, Litehouse (Sporked's best Gorgonzola) or BelGioioso crumbles.

**What's the difference between Gorgonzola dolce and piccante?**

Dolce is young, creamy and mild; piccante is aged longer and is firmer, crumblier and spicier.

**Is Gorgonzola milder than blue cheese?**

Gorgonzola dolce is milder and creamier than most blues, which is why it's a good first blue.

**What goes with Gorgonzola on a charcuterie board?**

Honey, pears, figs, walnuts and prosciutto, with port or Moscato d'Asti.

**How much Gorgonzola per person?**

About 1 oz per person as one of several cheeses.
