<h2 id="what-to-look-for">What to look for</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Look for:</strong> The DOP seal and the words &#x27;queso manchego&#x27;. The age matters most: semicurado is about 3 months, curado about 6, and viejo a year or more. Look for the zigzag basket-weave rind.</li><li class="sk-x"><strong>Skip:</strong> &#x27;Manchego-style&#x27; cheese that isn&#x27;t DOP. It is usually a mixed-milk cheese without the sheep&#x27;s milk richness.</li></ul>
:::

<h2 id="faq">Manchego questions</h2>
:::

**What is the best Manchego for a charcuterie board?**

A 6-month DOP Manchego. Kirkland's at Costco is the best value; for more flavor, choose one aged 12 months.

**How can I tell real Manchego?**

Look for the DOP seal and the basket-weave rind. Real Manchego is made only from Manchega sheep's milk in La Mancha.

**What goes with Manchego?**

Quince paste (membrillo), Marcona almonds, chorizo and a Spanish red like Tempranillo. Our [quince paste buyer's guide](/blog/best-quince-paste/) names the ones to buy.
