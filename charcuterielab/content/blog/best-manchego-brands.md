## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** The DOP seal and the words 'queso manchego'. The age matters most: semicurado is about 3 months, curado about 6, and viejo a year or more. Look for the zigzag basket-weave rind.

**Skip:** 'Manchego-style' cheese that isn't DOP. It is usually a mixed-milk cheese without the sheep's milk richness.

## Manchego questions

**What is the best Manchego for a charcuterie board?**

A 6-month DOP Manchego. Kirkland's at Costco is the best value; for more flavor, choose one aged 12 months.

**How can I tell real Manchego?**

Look for the DOP seal and the basket-weave rind. Real Manchego is made only from Manchega sheep's milk in La Mancha.

**What goes with Manchego?**

Quince paste (membrillo), Marcona almonds, chorizo and a Spanish red like Tempranillo.
