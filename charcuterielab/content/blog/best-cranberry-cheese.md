## Cranberry cheese questions

**What is cranberry cheese called?**

The classic is Wensleydale with cranberries, a crumbly English cheese. Cranberry goat cheese and cranberry white cheddar are popular too.

**Where can I buy Wensleydale with cranberries?**

Costco carries it seasonally (about November to January), and Somerdale's wedge is sold at many supermarkets and Murray's.

**What goes with cranberry cheese?**

Sharp cheddar, blue cheese, prosciutto, plain crackers, pecans and pears.

**Is Wensleydale with cranberries sweet?**

Yes, mildly. The cheese is tangy and crumbly, and the dried cranberries add sweetness.

**How much cranberry cheese per person?**

About 1 oz per person when it's one of several cheeses.
