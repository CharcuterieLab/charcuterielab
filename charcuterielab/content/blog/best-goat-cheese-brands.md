### 1. Laura Chenel Original Fresh Goat Cheese

**★★★★½ 4.7 out of 5** · Best overall · Best for: Spreadable classic · $$ mid-range

[America's Test Kitchen](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/taste_tests/2324-all-about-goat-cheese)'s top pick, described as delicate, bright and super spreadable. [Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/best-goat-cheese/) scored it 8.5 out of 10.

- **Why we like it:** Everywhere. Very spreadable.
- **Keep in mind:** Milder than Vermont Creamery.
- **Where to buy:** Most grocery stores.

### 2. Vermont Creamery Fresh Goat Cheese

**★★★★½ 4.5 out of 5** · Best for: Tangy fresh chèvre · $$ mid-range

[Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/best-goat-cheese/)'s top goat cheese, scoring 10 out of 10 for being classically tangy, grassy and still creamy.

- **Why we like it:** Top-ranked. Clean, tangy flavor.
- **Keep in mind:** Pricier than store brands.
- **Where to buy:** Whole Foods, most grocery.

### 3. Cypress Grove Humboldt Fog

**★★★★☆ 4.0 out of 5** · Best splurge · Best for: The showpiece · $$$ premium

An aged goat cheese with a line of ash through the middle and a creamy edge around a crumbly center. [America's Test Kitchen](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/taste_tests/2324-all-about-goat-cheese) recommends it, and it's a natural centerpiece for a board. (Source: [Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/best-goat-cheese/).)

- **Why we like it:** Striking look. Complex flavor.
- **Keep in mind:** Expensive.
- **Where to buy:** Whole Foods, cheese counters.

### 4. Mitica The Drunken Goat

**★★★★☆ 4.0 out of 5** · Best for: A firm goat cheese · $$ mid-range

A Spanish goat cheese soaked in red wine, which turns the rind purple. [America's Test Kitchen](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/taste_tests/2324-all-about-goat-cheese) called it approachable, fruity and nutty. (Source: [Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/best-goat-cheese/).)

- **Why we like it:** Firm, easy to slice. Pretty purple rind.
- **Keep in mind:** Mild for goat cheese.
- **Where to buy:** Whole Foods, most grocery.

### 5. Trader Joe's Chèvre

**★★★★☆ 3.9 out of 5** · Best value · Best for: Budget chèvre · $ budget

Very creamy with a grassy sweetness; [Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/best-goat-cheese/) noted it pairs especially well with honey.

- **Why we like it:** Cheap.
- **Keep in mind:** Only at Trader Joe's.
- **Where to buy:** Trader Joe's.

## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** Fresh chèvre should smell clean and tangy, not sour. Check the date: fresh goat cheese is best within a couple of weeks.

**Skip:** Pre-crumbled goat cheese for a board. It dries out. Buy a log and roll it in herbs or honey yourself.

## Goat cheese questions

**What is the best goat cheese for a charcuterie board?**

Vermont Creamery or Laura Chenel for a fresh log; Humboldt Fog if you want an aged showpiece.

**How do I make goat cheese look nice on a board?**

Roll a log in chopped herbs, crushed pistachios or cracked pepper, or drizzle it with honey.

**How long before serving should goat cheese come out?**

Fresh goat cheese needs 15 to 20 minutes out of the fridge.
