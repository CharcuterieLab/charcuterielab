# The Best Wegmans Charcuterie Board Items, Ranked

> **Quick Answer:** The best Wegmans cheese for a charcuterie board is the 18-Month Aged Medium Gouda (4.5 out of 5). For a showpiece, add one of the award-winning cheeses from Wegmans' own cheese caves: Professor's Brie or Truffle Shuffle aged goat cheese. Best extra: Castelvetrano olives from the olive bar.

Wegmans has some of the best grocery-store cheese in the country: The Takeout ranked it first of 13 chains for cheese in September 2026, and it ages its own cheeses in caves in Rochester, New York. These are the cheeses and extras that make the best board, ranked, with the meat and crackers to add at the end.

*How we ranked these: these are researched picks, not a side-by-side tasting. We scored each product on published taste tests, awards and shopper ratings, its ingredients, price and how easy it is to find, plus how it behaves on a board. Store stock changes, especially for seasonal items, so check your local store. Sources are listed at the end, and the full method is on our [buying guides hub](/blog/best-charcuterie-brands/#how-we-rank). Prices and stock last checked October 2026.*

### 1. Wegmans 18-Month Aged Medium Gouda

**★★★★½ 4.5 out of 5** · Best cheese · Best for: Everyone at the table · $ budget

Aged 18 months, with butterscotch notes and crunchy crystals. A Wegmans regular calls it always the first cheese to disappear from the board ([ShopFood](https://www.shopfood.com/4103400/best-things-to-buy-at-wegmans/)), and [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/1521748/chain-grocery-store-cheese-ranked/) singled out the Beemster 18-month Gouda in its chain cheese ranking.

- **Why we like it:** A crowd-pleasing aged cheese at a fair price.
- **Keep in mind:** Hard, so cut it into shards rather than slices.
- **Where to find it:** Wegmans cheese shop.

### 2. Professor's Brie

**★★★★☆ 4.2 out of 5** · Best soft cheese · Best for: The soft cheese · $$ mid-range

A brie made with Old Chatham Creamery that, [The Takeout](https://www.thetakeout.com/2249755/every-major-grocery-store-ranked-cheese-selection/) reports, took top honors at the 2026 American Cheese Society competition.

- **Why we like it:** An award-winning brie you can only buy here.
- **Keep in mind:** Costs more than a supermarket brie.
- **Where to find it:** Wegmans cheese shop.

### 3. Truffle Shuffle Aged Goat Cheese

**★★★★☆ 4.0 out of 5** · Award winner · Best for: A cheese with a story · $$ mid-range

An aged goat cheese ripened in Wegmans' cheese caves that won a gold medal at the American Cheese Society ([Wegmans](https://www.wegmans.com/news-media/articles/celebrating-a-decade-of-delicious-wegmans-cheese-caves-turn-ten)). [The Takeout](https://www.thetakeout.com/2249755/every-major-grocery-store-ranked-cheese-selection/) says it has also won at the World Cheese Awards.

- **Why we like it:** Award-winning, and truffle makes it feel special.
- **Keep in mind:** Goat and truffle are strong flavors, so serve one small piece.
- **Where to find it:** Wegmans cheese shop.

### 4. Castelvetrano Olives from the Olive Bar

**★★★★☆ 4.0 out of 5** · Best extra · Best for: The olives · $ budget

Mild, buttery green olives from the Mediterranean bar, about $11.99 a pound, that suit a board with rich cheeses and smoked meats ([Eat This](https://www.eatthis.com/best-wegmans-grocery-buys/)).

- **Why we like it:** Buy exactly as many as you need.
- **Keep in mind:** Priced by the pound, so it adds up for a crowd.
- **Where to find it:** Wegmans Mediterranean bar.

### 5. Bon Vivant

**★★★★☆ 3.9 out of 5** · Best for: A buttery washed-rind cheese · $$ mid-range

A brie-like, buttery soft cheese with a washed rind, nuttier than a mild brie ([Eat This](https://www.eatthis.com/best-wegmans-grocery-buys/)).

- **Why we like it:** Rich and spreadable, a little more interesting than brie.
- **Keep in mind:** Washed rinds smell stronger than they taste.
- **Where to find it:** Wegmans cheese shop.

### 6. Wegmans Organic Rosemary Bread

**★★★★☆ 3.9 out of 5** · Best for: Toasts for the board · $ budget

A Wegmans fan's favorite bread and one of the best Wegmans products ([ShopFood](https://www.shopfood.com/4103400/best-things-to-buy-at-wegmans/)). Slice and toast it into crostini.

- **Why we like it:** Rosemary goes with almost every cheese.
- **Keep in mind:** Buy it the day of the party.
- **Where to find it:** Wegmans bakery.

### 7. Wegmans Cilantro Jalapeño Hummus

**★★★★☆ 3.8 out of 5** · Best for: A dip for a grazing board · $ budget

Cilantro balances the mild heat ([Eat This](https://www.eatthis.com/best-wegmans-grocery-buys/)).

- **Why we like it:** A vegetarian option with a little kick.
- **Keep in mind:** Better on a mezze or snack board than a classic one.
- **Where to find it:** Wegmans refrigerated dips.

### 8. Wegmans French Macarons

**★★★½☆ 3.6 out of 5** · Best for: A sweet finish · $$ mid-range

Crisp outside, airy inside and naturally gluten-free, in flavors like chocolate, raspberry and lemon ([Eat This](https://www.eatthis.com/best-wegmans-grocery-buys/)).

- **Why we like it:** Colorful, and nothing to make.
- **Keep in mind:** Delicate, so add them right before serving.
- **Where to find it:** Wegmans bakery.

### 9. Sake 2 Me

**★★★½☆ 3.5 out of 5** · Best for: A bold soft cheese · $$ mid-range

A sake-rinsed brie from Wegmans' caves, noted for its pungency ([The Takeout](https://www.thetakeout.com/2249755/every-major-grocery-store-ranked-cheese-selection/)).

- **Why we like it:** Unusual and memorable.
- **Keep in mind:** Pungent. Pair it with a mild cheese.
- **Where to find it:** Wegmans cheese shop.

## Building a board from these

For 8 to 10 guests, pick:

- **Cheeses:** the 18-Month Gouda, Professor's Brie and one of Truffle Shuffle or Bon Vivant.
- **Meats:** ask the deli counter to slice salami and prosciutto thin. Our [salami](/blog/best-salami-brands/) and [prosciutto](/blog/best-prosciutto-brands/) rankings name brands to look for.
- **Extras:** Castelvetrano olives, toasted rosemary bread or crackers, fruit and the macarons.

Plan on about 3 oz of cheese and 3 oz of meat per guest when the board is the party food, or 2 oz each before a meal.

Wegmans' cheese is a strength, so spend there and save on basics.
