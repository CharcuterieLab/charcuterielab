## Building a board from these

For 8 to 10 guests, pick:

- **Cheeses:** the 18-Month Gouda, Professor's Brie and one of Truffle Shuffle or Bon Vivant.
- **Meats:** ask the deli counter to slice salami and prosciutto thin. Our [salami](/blog/best-salami-brands/) and [prosciutto](/blog/best-prosciutto-brands/) rankings name brands to look for.
- **Extras:** Castelvetrano olives, toasted rosemary bread or crackers, fruit and the macarons.

Plan on about 3 oz of cheese and 3 oz of meat per guest when the board is the party food, or 2 oz each before a meal.

## What to skip

Wegmans' cheese is a strength, so spend there and save on basics. Its cheese caves also send out marinated sheep and goat cheeses and extra-aged Goudas that shoppers love ([Mashed](https://www.mashed.com/1836150/best-worst-grocery-store-buy-cheese/)), but stock rotates, so ask the cheese shop what's at its best that week.

For other stores, see our guides to [Costco](/blog/best-costco-charcuterie-items/), [Trader Joe's](/blog/best-trader-joes-cheeses/) and every store on our [charcuterie buying guides](/blog/best-charcuterie-brands/) page.
