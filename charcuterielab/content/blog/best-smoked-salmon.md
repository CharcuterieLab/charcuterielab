# The Best Smoked Salmon for a Charcuterie Board, Ranked

Cold-smoked salmon is the silky, thin-sliced kind that drapes over a cracker or a bagel, and it's the one you want for a board. Packages range from buttery and mild to strongly smoked and salty, and the cheapest ones aren't always the worst. ### 1. Kirkland Signature Smoked Salmon

**★★★★½ 4.7 out of 5** · Best overall · Best for: Big boards and brunch · $ budget

Costco's Norwegian smoked salmon took 1st of 11 in [Tasting Table's test](https://www.tastingtable.com/2130680/smoked-salmon-brands-ranked/): incredibly tender and buttery, mildly salty, with some of the largest slices in the lineup. A [follow-up piece](https://www.tastingtable.com/2138263/best-smoked-salmon-brand-costco-kirkland) puts it at about $1 an ounce, sold as two 12-ounce packs.

- **Why we like it:** Large, tender slices at a low price per ounce.
- **Keep in mind:** You need a Costco membership, and 24 ounces is a lot for a small board. It freezes well.
- **Where to buy:** Costco.

### 2. Latitude 45 Atlantic Salmon

**★★★★½ 4.5 out of 5** · Best value · Best for: Small boards on a budget · $ budget

Smoked salmon from Authentic Seafood of Patagonia, sold at Walmart. [Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/best-smoked-salmon) named it the best of the best with 10 out of 10: very silky and rich, balanced in salt and fishiness, and it slices cleanly. Their reviewer paid just over $5 for 4 ounces.

- **Why we like it:** Top-scoring salmon in a small, cheap pack.
- **Keep in mind:** Mostly a Walmart find, and stock varies by store.
- **Where to buy:** Walmart.

### 3. Santa Barbara Smokehouse Smoked Atlantic Salmon

**★★★★☆ 4.2 out of 5** · Best splurge · Best for: A brunch board that impresses · $$$ premium

Cured with sea salt and brown sugar and smoked over an open fire. It was [Eat This, Not That!'s](https://www.eatthis.com/smoked-salmon-taste-test/) top pick, and [Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/best-smoked-salmon) ranked it runner-up with 9.5 out of 10 for thin, silky slices with a real smokehouse flavor.

- **Why we like it:** The most traditional flavor here, and two outlets put it at or near the top.
- **Keep in mind:** It costs more than the grocery brands, and the slices can look ragged.
- **Where to buy:** Whole Foods and some specialty grocers.

### 4. Private Selection Cold Smoked Norwegian Atlantic Salmon

**★★★★☆ 4.2 out of 5** · Best for: Smoke lovers · $$ mid-range

Kroger's store brand placed 3rd of 11 at [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2130680/smoked-salmon-brands-ranked/) for tender, generously sized slices with a very strong, campfire-like smoke. Buy the Norwegian Atlantic one: [Eat This, Not That!](https://www.eatthis.com/smoked-salmon-taste-test/) disliked the brand's wild sockeye.

- **Why we like it:** Strong smoke and big slices at a store-brand price.
- **Keep in mind:** The smoke can dominate soft cheeses.
- **Where to buy:** Kroger and its sister stores (Ralphs, Fred Meyer, King Soopers).

### 5. Acme Smoked Nova Salmon

**★★★★☆ 4.1 out of 5** · Best for: Classic New York lox style · $$ mid-range

From the Brooklyn smokehouse that supplies many delis. [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2130680/smoked-salmon-brands-ranked/) ranked it 2nd of 11: thin and impeccably tender, strongly smoky and salty without tasting fishy.

- **Why we like it:** Thin, deli-style slices that drape well.
- **Keep in mind:** Saltier and smokier than Kirkland, so a little goes a long way.
- **Where to buy:** Many grocery stores on the East Coast and nationwide.

### 6. Trader Joe's Norwegian Smoked Salmon

**★★★★☆ 4.0 out of 5** · Best for: A cheap, mild everyday pack · $ budget

[Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/best-smoked-salmon) gave it 9.5 out of 10 for a pale, silky, firm texture and a smoky, buttery flavor that holds together when sliced. [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2130680/smoked-salmon-brands-ranked/) was cooler on it, ranking it 7th of 11 for thick slices with chewy spots.

- **Why we like it:** One of the cheapest packs, and a crowd-pleasing flavor.
- **Keep in mind:** Slices can be thick. Fold them rather than draping.
- **Where to buy:** Trader Joe's.

### 7. Spence & Co. Traditional Scottish-Style Smoked Salmon

**★★★½☆ 3.7 out of 5** · Best for: Neat, interleaved slices · $$$ premium

Each slice comes separated by paper, which makes it easy to lay out. [Eat This, Not That!](https://www.eatthis.com/smoked-salmon-taste-test/) ranked it 3rd for mild flavor, nice smoke and a firm texture, and [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2130680/smoked-salmon-brands-ranked/) put it 4th of 11 but found it less tender and buttery than the leaders.

- **Why we like it:** Tidy slices that are easy to arrange.
- **Keep in mind:** Pricey for a texture that's firmer than the top picks.
- **Where to buy:** Whole Foods and specialty grocers.

## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** Cold-smoked salmon (sometimes called nova or lox style), with a short list: salmon, salt, sometimes sugar, and smoke. Check the use-by date and buy the freshest pack. Hot-smoked salmon is flaky and cooked, so save it for a dip.

**Skip:** Packs labeled "pieces" or "trimmings" for the board itself. They're fine for a dip or scrambled eggs but won't drape. Also skip anything with liquid smoke on the label.

**Serving it:** keep smoked salmon cold until the last minute and set it out for no more than 2 hours. Drape it in loose ribbons or roll it into rosettes, then put capers, red onion, lemon and dill nearby. Plan on about 1 to 2 ounces per person when it's one of several proteins.
**Pregnant guests:** the CDC advises against refrigerated smoked seafood like lox during pregnancy unless it's cooked into a dish, because of the listeria risk. Offer cooked or canned salmon instead.

## Smoked salmon questions

**What is the best smoked salmon brand at the grocery store?**

Kirkland Signature Smoked Salmon from Costco, which won Tasting Table's 11-brand test. Latitude 45 from Walmart scored 10 out of 10 at Sporked and is the best value in a small pack.

**Is smoked salmon the same as lox?**

Not exactly. True lox is salt-cured and not smoked. Most "lox" sold today is cold-smoked nova salmon, which is what these rankings cover.

**How much smoked salmon do I need for a board?**

About 1 to 2 ounces per person when it's one of several proteins, or 3 ounces per person on a brunch board built around it.

**Can you freeze smoked salmon?**

Yes. Freeze it in the unopened pack, then thaw it overnight in the fridge. The texture stays close to fresh.
