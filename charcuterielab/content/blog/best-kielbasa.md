<h2 id="how-to-choose">How to serve kielbasa on a game-day board</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Slice it</strong> into &frac12;-inch coins on a slight angle and sear in a hot skillet 2 to 3 minutes per side.</li><li><strong>Serve it warm</strong> with toothpicks, grainy mustard, sauerkraut or pickled onions.</li><li><strong>Round out the board</strong> with cheese curds, sharp cheddar, soft pretzels, beer cheese, dill pickles and jerky.</li><li><strong>Snack board shortcut:</strong> smoked kielbasa is fully cooked, so you can also serve it sliced cold.</li><li><strong>How much:</strong> one 13 to 14 oz rope serves about 6 people as an appetizer.</li></ul>

## Kielbasa questions

**What is the best kielbasa brand?**

Slotkowski was every taster's favorite at Allrecipes; Wellshire Farms won America's Test Kitchen's test; Kroger Polska was Sporked's best.

**Is Hillshire Farm kielbasa good?**

It's the easiest to find, but it placed 6th of 7 at Allrecipes and Sporked preferred Kroger's. Its turkey kielbasa did win Sporked's turkey sausage test.

**Is kielbasa the same as Polish sausage?**

In the US the names are used for the same smoked pork (or pork and beef) sausage.

**Do you have to cook kielbasa?**

Smoked kielbasa is fully cooked, so you can eat it cold, but searing it brings out the flavor and snap.

**How much kielbasa per person for a party?**

About 2 oz per person as an appetizer; a 14 oz rope serves about 6.
