# Bagel Board: How to Build a Brunch Bagel Bar (Serves 6 to 20)

> **Quick Answer:** Plan per guest: **1 to 1½ bagels, 2 oz cream cheese and 1½ to 2 oz lox**. Add capers, red onion, tomato, cucumber, lemon and dill. Slice everything the night before and build it 30 minutes before guests come.

It's the easiest brunch to host: no cooking, and everyone builds their own.

## Shopping list for 6, 12 or 20 guests

| Item | 6 guests | 12 guests | 20 guests |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bagels (regular, or 2–3 minis each) | 8 | 15 | 26 |
| Cream cheese (2–3 flavors) | 12 oz | 1½ lb | 2¾ lb |
| Smoked salmon (lox or Nova) | 10 oz | 1½ lb | 2½ lb |
| Capers | 1 small jar | 1 jar | 2 jars |
| Red onion | 1 small | 1 | 2 |
| Tomatoes and cucumbers | 2 each | 3 each | 5 each |
| Lemons | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| Fresh dill | 1 small bunch | 1 bunch | 2 bunches |
| Hard-boiled eggs (optional) | 6 | 12 | 20 |
| Fruit (berries, grapes, melon) | 1½ lb | 2½ lb | 4 lb |
| Jam and butter | 1 each | 2 each | 3 each |

From 20 guests the amounts include about 10% extra. If the board sits next to other brunch food like a quiche or a frittata, use the lower end.

## What to put on it

| Section | Pick from |
|---|---|
| Bagels | Plain, everything, sesame, poppy, onion, pumpernickel, cinnamon raisin, mini bagels for kids |
| Cream cheese | Plain, scallion, vegetable, honey walnut, strawberry, whipped; a dairy-free spread for guests who need one |
| Fish | Cold-smoked salmon (lox or Nova), [gravlax](/ingredients/gravlax/), hot-smoked salmon, [smoked trout](/ingredients/smoked-trout/) |
| Briny and fresh | [Capers](/ingredients/capers/), thin red onion, tomato slices, cucumber, lemon wedges, fresh dill, [pickled red onions](/ingredients/pickled-red-onions/) |
| Filling extras | Hard-boiled or jammy eggs, crisp bacon, avocado, sliced cheddar or [Havarti](/ingredients/havarti/) |
| Sweet corner | Butter, [strawberry jam](/blog/best-strawberry-jam/), honey, berries, grapes, melon |

**Lox, Nova or gravlax?** All three are thin, silky cured salmon that drapes on a bagel. Nova is cold-smoked, gravlax is cured with dill and not smoked, and the word "lox" is often used for either. Hot-smoked salmon is flaky and cooked-tasting, so serve it in chunks. Our [best smoked salmon](/blog/best-smoked-salmon/) guide ranks the grocery-store options, and [why smoked salmon and cream cheese work](/blog/smoked-salmon-cream-cheese-pairing/) explains the classic pairing.

## How to lay out a bagel board

1. **Use a big board or a sheet pan.** A 20-inch board or a large tray holds a bagel bar for 12. For 20, use two boards or a long table.
2. **Bowls first.** Spoon each cream cheese into its own bowl with its own spreader, and put capers in a small dish. Spread the bowls out across the board.
3. **Bagels along one edge.** Slice them and stack them in a row or in a basket at the start of the line, so guests take a bagel first.
4. **Salmon in the middle.** Drape it in loose folds on a chilled plate or next to an ice pack under the board, and add it last.
5. **Fresh toppings around the salmon.** Fan the tomato and cucumber slices, separate the onion rings, and tuck in lemon wedges and dill.
6. **Sweet corner at the far end,** with jam, butter and fruit, so it doesn't mix with the fish.
7. **Toaster nearby.** If you can, put a toaster at the end of the line for guests who want their bagel warm.

## Make-ahead timing

- **The night before:** slice the bagels and store them in a sealed bag. Slice the tomatoes, cucumbers and onion, hard-boil the eggs, wash and dry the fruit, and spoon the cream cheese into serving bowls. Cover everything and refrigerate.
- **30 minutes before:** take out the cream cheese so it spreads easily, and build the board.
- **Right before guests arrive:** add the salmon.
- **Once it's out:** serve within 2 hours, or 1 hour above 90°F. Keep the salmon on ice or refill it from the fridge.

## Food-safety notes

Smoked salmon is perishable, so it's the last thing on the board and the first back in the fridge. **For pregnant guests,** US and UK guidance advises avoiding refrigerated smoked fish unless it's cooked until steaming hot, so offer a cooked option like baked salmon, plus eggs and plenty of other toppings. ## FAQ

**How many bagels do I need per person?**
Plan 1 to 1½ regular bagels per guest, or 2 to 3 mini bagels. Slice them before serving so guests can take a half, which stretches the count further.

**How much cream cheese for a bagel board?**
About 2 tablespoons (1 oz) per bagel half that gets a schmear, so roughly 2 oz per guest. For 12 guests, buy about 1½ lb across two or three flavors.

**How much lox or smoked salmon per person?**
About 1½ to 2 oz of smoked salmon per guest when it's one of several toppings. For 12 guests, that is about 1½ lb.

**What goes on a bagel board?**
- Sliced bagels and two or three cream cheeses
- Smoked salmon and capers
- Thinly sliced red onion, tomatoes and cucumbers
- Lemon wedges and fresh dill
- A sweet side: jam, butter and fruit
- To make it more filling: hard-boiled eggs and bacon

**Is a bagel board with lox safe for pregnant guests?**
US and UK guidance advises pregnant people to avoid refrigerated smoked fish unless it is cooked until steaming hot. Offer a cooked option such as baked salmon or hot-smoked salmon heated through, plus eggs and plenty of other toppings.
