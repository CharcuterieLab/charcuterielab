<h2 id="how-to-choose">How to serve shrimp cocktail at a party</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>How much:</strong> 4 to 6 shrimp per guest when it's one of several appetizers, 8 to 10 when it's the star.</li><li><strong>Thaw in the fridge overnight</strong>, then pat dry. Never thaw cooked shrimp on the counter.</li><li><strong>Keep it cold:</strong> set the platter over a tray of crushed ice, and swap in a fresh tray instead of leaving one out longer than 2 hours.</li><li><strong>Upgrade the sauce:</strong> stir extra horseradish and a squeeze of lemon into any store-bought sauce.</li><li><strong>Allergies:</strong> keep shellfish on its own platter, with its own serving fork, and label it.</li></ul>

## Shrimp cocktail questions

**What is the best store-bought shrimp cocktail?**

Kirkland Signature Shrimp Cocktail from Costco scored 10/10 in Sporked's test: over a pound of plump shrimp with sauce and lemon for about $15.

**What's the best shrimp cocktail without a Costco membership?**

Sam's Choice Cocktail Shrimp with Sauce from Walmart, Sporked's runner-up at 8/10, for under $10.

**How many shrimp per person for shrimp cocktail?**

About 4 to 6 per guest as one of several appetizers, or 8 to 10 if it's the main event.

**How long can shrimp cocktail sit out?**

No more than 2 hours at room temperature, or 1 hour above 90°F. Serve it over ice and refill from the fridge.

**How do you thaw a frozen shrimp ring?**

In the fridge overnight, still sealed. Pat the shrimp dry before serving.
