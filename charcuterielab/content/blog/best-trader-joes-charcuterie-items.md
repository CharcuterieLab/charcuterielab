# The Best Trader Joe's Charcuterie Board Items, Ranked (Meats, Crackers and Extras)

> **Quick Answer:** The best Trader Joe's meat for a charcuterie board is Salame di Parma Mild Salami (4.5 out of 5). Best crackers: Raisin Rosemary Crisps. Best extras: Fig Butter and Roasted & Salted Rosemary Marcona Almonds. Splurge: the Citterio Prosciutto di Parma. For the cheeses, see our separate ranking of the best Trader Joe's cheeses.

### 1. Trader Joe's Salame di Parma Mild Salami

**★★★★½ 4.5 out of 5** · Best meat · Best for: The main meat · $ budget

Mild, thinly sliced salami imported from Parma, with no added nitrates or nitrites ([Eat This](https://www.eatthis.com/trader-joes-charcuterie-board-items)). [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2203603/best-trader-joes-meats-charcuterie-board/) put it on its July 2026 list of the best Trader Joe's meats for a board: you can't go wrong with it.

- **Why we like it:** Mild enough for everyone, and already sliced.
- **Keep in mind:** Mild, so add a spicier meat if your crowd likes heat.
- **Where to find it:** Trader Joe's deli case.

### 2. Trader Joe's Fig Butter

**★★★★½ 4.5 out of 5** · Best extra · Best for: The sweet spread · $ budget

A smooth fig spread that works with sweet or savory things. [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/1944136/trader-joes-items-grazing-board-not-cheese)'s taster was thoroughly pleased with it.

- **Why we like it:** Fig and cheese is a classic, and the jar is cheap.
- **Keep in mind:** Smooth rather than chunky, so it's easiest served in a small bowl with a spoon.
- **Where to find it:** Trader Joe's, near the jams and nut butters.

### 3. Trader Joe's Spanish Inspired Charcuteria

**★★★★½ 4.4 out of 5** · Best for: Three meats in one pack · $ budget

Serrano ham, salchichón and chorizo in one 6 oz pack. Both [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2203603/best-trader-joes-meats-charcuterie-board/) lists include it: each meat has its own flavor, the slices are thin, and the chorizo is the standout ([Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/1944136/trader-joes-items-grazing-board-not-cheese)).

- **Why we like it:** Variety in one pack, and a natural fit for a Spanish board.
- **Keep in mind:** Three meats in small amounts, so buy two packs for 8 or more guests.
- **Where to find it:** Trader Joe's deli case.

### 4. Trader Joe's Raisin Rosemary Crisps

**★★★★½ 4.4 out of 5** · Best crackers · Best for: Sweet-savory crackers · $ budget

Crisp, slightly sweet crackers with rosemary and raisins. [Chowhound](https://www.chowhound.com/1964234/trader-joes-charcuterie-board-crackers) recommends them for a board, mildly sweet and herb-forward, and [Eat This](https://www.eatthis.com/trader-joes-charcuterie-board-items) picked them too.

- **Why we like it:** Taste like the pricey artisan crisps for much less.
- **Keep in mind:** Sweet, so pair them with a plain cracker for the meats.
- **Where to find it:** Trader Joe's cracker aisle.

### 5. Trader Joe's Roasted & Salted Rosemary Marcona Almonds

**★★★★½ 4.4 out of 5** · Best for: The nut · $ budget

Spanish Marcona almonds roasted with rosemary. [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/1944136/trader-joes-items-grazing-board-not-cheese) loves the herbal note with rich cheese and cured meat, and [Eat This](https://www.eatthis.com/trader-joes-charcuterie-board-items) says shoppers rave about them.

- **Why we like it:** The rosemary makes them taste special.
- **Keep in mind:** Often sells out, so buy them when you see them.
- **Where to find it:** Trader Joe's nut aisle.

### 6. Citterio Prosciutto di Parma at Trader Joe's

**★★★★☆ 4.2 out of 5** · Splurge · Best for: The splurge meat · $$ mid-range

Real Prosciutto di Parma, sliced fresh (Citterio's Tagliofresco). [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/1944136/trader-joes-items-grazing-board-not-cheese) calls it a truly excellent slice of meat, soft and buttery.

- **Why we like it:** Proper Parma ham, soft and silky.
- **Keep in mind:** Costs more than Trader Joe's own sliced prosciutto.
- **Where to find it:** Trader Joe's deli case.

### 7. Trader Joe's Sliced Prosciutto

**★★★★☆ 4.0 out of 5** · Best for: Budget prosciutto · $ budget

Trader Joe's own sliced prosciutto, aged up to 12 months, salty with a hint of sweetness ([Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2203603/best-trader-joes-meats-charcuterie-board/)). [Eat This](https://www.eatthis.com/trader-joes-charcuterie-board-items) calls it a better deal than other stores' versions. It's also the best value in our [prosciutto ranking](/blog/best-prosciutto-brands/).

- **Why we like it:** Cheap and easy.
- **Keep in mind:** Saltier and less silky than the Citterio.
- **Where to find it:** Trader Joe's deli case.

### 8. Trader Joe's Sliced Bresaola

**★★★★☆ 4.0 out of 5** · Best for: A lean, different meat · $ budget

Air-dried beef, sliced thin. [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2203603/best-trader-joes-meats-charcuterie-board/) calls it one of the most interesting and tasty meats you can get at Trader Joe's.

- **Why we like it:** A beef option for guests who skip pork.
- **Keep in mind:** Lean and delicate, so drizzle it with olive oil and lemon, or roll it around arugula.
- **Where to find it:** Trader Joe's deli case.

### 9. Trader Joe's Organic Naan Crackers

**★★★★☆ 4.0 out of 5** · Best for: An everyday cracker · $ budget

[Chowhound](https://www.chowhound.com/1964234/trader-joes-charcuterie-board-crackers) ranked them first in its roundup of Trader Joe's crackers for a board: a basic but rich pick. The garlic version is a more savory option.

- **Why we like it:** Sturdy and rich, and they go with everything.
- **Keep in mind:** Thicker than a water cracker.
- **Where to find it:** Trader Joe's cracker aisle.

### 10. Trader Joe's Green Olive Flats

**★★★★☆ 4.0 out of 5** · Best for: A thin, crunchy cracker · $ budget

Thin Italian lingue crackers with green olive. [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/1944136/trader-joes-items-grazing-board-not-cheese) calls them particularly spectacular on a grazing board, and [Chowhound](https://www.chowhound.com/1964234/trader-joes-charcuterie-board-crackers) likes the herbal note.

- **Why we like it:** Snappy, thin and good-looking on a board.
- **Keep in mind:** Many arrive broken in the thin packaging.
- **Where to find it:** Trader Joe's cracker aisle.

### 11. Trader Joe's Organic Conversation Olive Trio

**★★★★☆ 3.9 out of 5** · Best for: The olives · $ budget

Three olive varieties in one tub, with meaty texture ([Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/1944136/trader-joes-items-grazing-board-not-cheese)). It's also the best value in our [olive ranking](/blog/best-olives-for-charcuterie/).

- **Why we like it:** Three olives for the price of one.
- **Keep in mind:** Not pitted, so put out a small bowl for pits.
- **Where to find it:** Trader Joe's refrigerated case.

### 12. Trader Joe's Organic Apricot Preserves

**★★★★☆ 3.9 out of 5** · Best for: A second sweet spread · $ budget

Chunkier than jam, with whole pieces of fruit ([Eat This](https://www.eatthis.com/trader-joes-charcuterie-board-items)).

- **Why we like it:** Good with blue cheese and brie.
- **Keep in mind:** Only one roundup mentioned it.
- **Where to find it:** Trader Joe's, near the jams.

## Building a board from these

For 8 to 10 guests, pick:

- **Meats:** Salame di Parma plus the Spanish Charcuteria, or the Citterio prosciutto for a splurge.
- **Crackers:** Raisin Rosemary Crisps plus Organic Naan Crackers.
- **Extras:** Fig Butter, Rosemary Marcona Almonds and the Olive Trio.
- **Cheeses:** three from our [Trader Joe's cheese ranking](/blog/best-trader-joes-cheeses/).

Plan on about 3 oz of meat and 3 oz of cheese per guest in total when the board is the party food, or 2 oz each before a meal. Our [Trader Joe's charcuterie board shopping list](/blog/trader-joes-charcuterie-board/) has the full cart for 10 with prices.

## What to skip

The Spicy Uncured Charcuterie Collection (coppa, sopressa and cooked capocolla) is good, but [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2203603/best-trader-joes-meats-charcuterie-board/) notes some people find it too spicy, so pair it with a mild meat. And check the meat case date: sliced meats go faster once opened, so buy them within a day or two of the party.

**Short on time?** Trader Joe's Antipasti Platter ($10.99 in early 2026) has prosciutto, salami, olives and provolone on one tray. [Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/trader-joes-charcuterie-board-news/) called it the cheapest premade platter it has seen, but it serves only 3 to 5 people and has one cheese and no crackers, so add a box of crisps and an Unexpected Cheddar.

## Trader Joe's charcuterie questions

**What should I buy at Trader Joe's for a charcuterie board?**

Salame di Parma and the Spanish Inspired Charcuteria for meat, Raisin Rosemary Crisps and Naan Crackers, Fig Butter, Rosemary Marcona Almonds, the Olive Trio, and three cheeses such as Unexpected Cheddar, Triple Crème Brie and 1000 Day Gouda.

**What is the best Trader Joe's salami?**

Salame di Parma Mild Salami: mild, thinly sliced and imported from Parma. For more variety, the Spanish Inspired Charcuteria has serrano ham, salchichón and chorizo in one pack.

**What are the best Trader Joe's crackers for charcuterie?**

Raisin Rosemary Crisps for a sweet-savory cracker, and Organic Naan Crackers for an everyday one. Green Olive Flats are the thin, crunchy pick.

**Does Trader Joe's sell a ready-made charcuterie board?**

Yes. In early 2026 it added an Antipasti Platter for $10.99, with prosciutto, two salamis, salami nuggets, Castelvetrano olives and provolone. Sporked called it a great deal but small, serving 3 to 5 people, with only one cheese and no crackers.
