## Building a board from these

For 8 to 10 guests, pick:

- **Meats:** Salame di Parma plus the Spanish Charcuteria, or the Citterio prosciutto for a splurge.
- **Crackers:** Raisin Rosemary Crisps plus Organic Naan Crackers.
- **Extras:** Fig Butter, Rosemary Marcona Almonds and the Olive Trio.
- **Cheeses:** three from our [Trader Joe's cheese ranking](/blog/best-trader-joes-cheeses/).

Plan on about 3 oz of meat and 3 oz of cheese per guest in total when the board is the party food, or 2 oz each before a meal. Our [Trader Joe's charcuterie board shopping list](/blog/trader-joes-charcuterie-board/) has the full cart for 10 with prices.

## What to skip

The Spicy Uncured Charcuterie Collection (coppa, sopressa and cooked capocolla) is good, but [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2203603/best-trader-joes-meats-charcuterie-board/) notes some people find it too spicy, so pair it with a mild meat. And check the meat case date: sliced meats go faster once opened, so buy them within a day or two of the party.

**Short on time?** Trader Joe's Antipasti Platter ($10.99 in early 2026) has prosciutto, salami, olives and provolone on one tray. [Sporked](https://sporked.com/article/trader-joes-charcuterie-board-news/) called it the cheapest premade platter it has seen, but it serves only 3 to 5 people and has one cheese and no crackers, so add a box of crisps and an Unexpected Cheddar.

**What should I buy at Trader Joe's for a charcuterie board?**

- **Meat:** Salame di Parma and the Spanish Inspired Charcuteria
- **Crackers:** Raisin Rosemary Crisps and Naan Crackers
- **Extras:** Fig Butter, Rosemary Marcona Almonds and the Olive Trio
- **Cheese:** three, such as Unexpected Cheddar, Triple Crème Brie and 1000 Day Gouda

**What is the best Trader Joe's salami?**

Salame di Parma Mild Salami: mild, thinly sliced and imported from Parma. For more variety, the Spanish Inspired Charcuteria has serrano ham, salchichón and chorizo in one pack.

**What are the best Trader Joe's crackers for charcuterie?**

Raisin Rosemary Crisps for a sweet-savory cracker, and Organic Naan Crackers for an everyday one. Green Olive Flats are the thin, crunchy pick.
