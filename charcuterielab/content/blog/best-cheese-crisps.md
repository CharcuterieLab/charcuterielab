<h2 id="how-to-choose">How to use cheese crisps on a board</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>As crackers:</strong> the flat, sturdy ones (Whisps, ParmCrisps, Sonoma Creamery) hold firm cheese, salami and a little jam.</li><li><strong>For strict keto,</strong> look for cheese as the only ingredient and 0 to 2 g carbs per serving; skip seasoned flavors with sugar.</li><li><strong>For celiac guests,</strong> choose a bag with a certification mark (GFCO or Beyond Celiac), not just a gluten-free claim.</li><li><strong>Open the bag last.</strong> They soften in humid air, so put them out just before guests arrive.</li></ul>

## Cheese crisp questions

**Are cheese crisps keto?**

Most single-cheese crisps are: Whisps Parmesan has 1 g carbs per ounce and ParmCrisps Original 0 g. Blends with grains, like Sonoma Creamery, have more, and some seasoned flavors add sugar.

**Are cheese crisps gluten-free?**

Pure cheese crisps have no gluten ingredients, and ParmCrisps, Sonoma Creamery and Moon Cheese carry gluten-free certification. For celiac guests, choose a certified bag.

**Can you use cheese crisps as crackers on a board?**

Yes. Whisps Parmesan and ParmCrisps are sturdy enough for firm cheese and salami, and Sonoma Creamery's thick squares work best of all as crackers.

**How do you make cheese crisps at home?**

Drop tablespoons of shredded sharp cheddar 2 inches apart on a parchment-lined sheet and bake at 375°F for 9 to 14 minutes until golden. They crisp as they cool.

**How long do cheese crisps stay crisp once opened?**

Reseal the bag right away; they soften in humid air. Homemade crisps are best the day they're baked.
