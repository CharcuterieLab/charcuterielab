So the best H-E-B board mixes national-brand cheeses from the deli case with H-E-B's own deli meats, spreads and dips. Few food sites have rated H-E-B's deli cheeses, so these rankings lean on ingredients, value and how each one works on a board.

## Building a board from these

For 12 people with the board as the party food, plan about 2½ pounds of cheese and 2¼ pounds of meat. A strong H-E-B board is two blocks of Kerrygold cheddar, the triple crème brie and SarVecchio in chunks, with deli-sliced Italian dry salami. Add the smoked pimento cheese with crackers, grapes and pecans. For a Texas board, add the guacamole and tortilla chips at one end.

Use our [Party Planner](/party-planner/) for exact amounts for your guest count.

## What to skip

- **H-E-B-brand cheese blocks as the star.** [The Takeout](https://www.thetakeout.com/2249755/every-major-grocery-store-ranked-cheese-selection/) found H-E-B's cheese is where its store brand is weakest. Use national brands from the deli case for the main cheeses, and H-E-B's deli for meat, spreads and dips.
- **Cubed snack trays for a nice board.** They're fine for kids, but wedges cut by hand look and taste better.

H-E-B also sells [Post Oak smoked cheddar cubes](https://www.heb.com/product-detail/h-e-b-deli-post-oak-smoked-cheddar-cheese-cubes/4451059) if you want a smoky cheese without the spread. See every ranked guide on our [charcuterie buying guides](/blog/best-charcuterie-brands/) page.

**What is the best H-E-B cheese for a charcuterie board?**

Kerrygold Aged Irish Cheddar from the deli cheese case, about $5.74 for 7 oz. For an H-E-B-brand cheese, the Deli Artisan Triple Crème Brie.

**Does H-E-B sell charcuterie boards?**

Yes. The H-E-B Deli sells small ready-made cheese boards, such as Everyone's Favorite for about $9.98, which serves two.

**What is H-E-B Post Oak smoked pimento cheese?**

A pimento cheese spread smoked over post oak, sold at the H-E-B Deli. Southern Living lists it among the items Texans always buy at H-E-B.

**How much should I buy at H-E-B for a board?**

About 3 oz of cheese and 3 oz of meat per guest when the board is the party food, or 2 oz each before a meal, plus 10 to 12 crackers per guest.
