<h2 id="how-to-choose">Deli meat on a board: what to buy and how to use it</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Cured meats</strong> (salami, prosciutto, soppressata) are the main event; <strong>cooked deli meats</strong> (turkey, ham, roast beef) are for roll-ups and kid boards.</li><li><strong>How much:</strong> about 2 oz of meat per person as an appetizer, more if the board is dinner.</li><li><strong>Read the label:</strong> short ingredient lists taste better. Tasting Table's last-place brand, Land O'Frost, was watery and bulked out with fillers.</li><li><strong>At the counter,</strong> ask for thin slices laid flat with paper between layers.</li><li><strong>Roll-ups:</strong> wrap turkey or ham around a cheese stick, pickle spear or asparagus, seam side down.</li></ul>

## Deli meat questions

**What is the best deli meat brand?**

Boar's Head, Tasting Table's #1 of 12 brands. For cured meats, Creminelli; for salami, Gusto.

**Is Boar's Head still the best?**

It still wins taste tests. After its 2024 recall some shoppers switched; Creminelli and Gusto are good cured-meat alternatives.

**What deli meat is best for a charcuterie board?**

Genoa salami, soppressata, prosciutto and pepperoni. Add turkey or ham roll-ups for kids.

**What's the worst packaged deli meat brand?**

Land O'Frost came last in Tasting Table's ranking: watery and short on flavor.

**How much deli meat per person for a board?**

About 2 oz per person as an appetizer.
