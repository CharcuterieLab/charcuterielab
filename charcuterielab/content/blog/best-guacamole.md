# The Best Store-Bought Guacamole for a Game Day Board, Ranked

Store-bought guacamole ranges from close to homemade to avocado-flavored paste. The good ones are sold refrigerated, made with high-pressure processing instead of preservatives. ## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** Avocados first, then lime, onion, cilantro and salt. Refrigerated tubs labeled high-pressure processed (HPP) taste fresher than shelf-stable dips.

**Skip:** Opening it hours early. Guacamole browns on the surface once it meets air. Open it right before guests arrive, and squeeze a little lime over the top.

**Serving it:** set the bowl in a larger bowl of ice if it'll be out a while, and press plastic wrap onto the surface of leftovers to slow browning. Like any dip, it shouldn't sit out more than 2 hours, or 1 hour above 90°F. Plan on about 2 tablespoons per guest.

## Guacamole questions

**What is the best store-bought guacamole?**

Sabra Classic Guacamole, the #1 pick at Cheapism and Consumer Reports. For a chunkier, cleaner-ingredient tub, Good Foods.

**What is the best guacamole at Costco?**

Good Foods Chunky Guacamole, Tasting Table's runner-up of 11.

**How do you keep store-bought guacamole from browning?**

Open it right before serving, squeeze lime over the top, and press plastic wrap directly onto the surface of leftovers.

**How long can guacamole sit out?**

No more than 2 hours, or 1 hour above 90°F. Set the bowl over ice to keep it cold.
