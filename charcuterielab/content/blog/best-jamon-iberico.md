<h2 id="how-to-choose">How much to buy, and how to serve it</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Per person:</strong> 1 to 2 ounces; it's rich and meant to be savored.</li><li><strong>Read the tag:</strong> black is 100% ibérico de bellota, red is crossbred bellota, green and white are grain-fed.</li><li><strong>Paleta vs. jamón:</strong> shoulder (paleta) is smaller and cheaper than the hind leg (jamón).</li><li><strong>Serve warm:</strong> out of the fridge 30 to 60 minutes ahead so the fat turns glossy.</li><li><strong>Lay it flat</strong> on its own plate, and serve it before anything strong.</li></ul>

## Jamón ibérico questions

**Where can I buy jamón ibérico in the US?**

Spanish importers like La Tienda and Little Spain carry the widest range. Some mainstream retailers and Amazon carry sliced packs.

**What is the best brand of jamón ibérico?**

Cinco Jotas, Joselito, Fermín and Montaraz are well-known names. The grade on the label matters more than the brand.

**How much does jamón ibérico cost?**

Sliced bellota typically runs about $30 to $55 for a 2 to 3 ounce pack; a whole bone-in leg can top $1,500.

**Is shoulder (paleta) as good as ham (jamón)?**

It's excellent and cheaper, but smaller and a little less complex than the hind leg.

**How much jamón ibérico per person?**

One to two ounces as part of a board.
