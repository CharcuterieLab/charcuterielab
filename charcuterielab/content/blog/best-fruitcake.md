# The Best Fruitcake to Buy (Yes, Really) for a Christmas Board

> **Quick Answer:** For the famous gift tin, **Collin Street Bakery DeLuxe** ($37.95, alcohol-free, packed with pecans). For a boozy, aged cake, **Assumption Abbey**, baked by Trappist monks and a St. Louis Magazine favorite. For a nut-heavy loaf under $20, **Southern Supreme**, which won a North Carolina taste test. On a budget, **Claxton** for about $4.

<h2 id="how-to-choose">Fruitcake on a Christmas cheese board</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Slice it thin,</strong> about &frac14; inch, with a sharp knife wiped between cuts. Cold fruitcake slices cleanest.</li><li><strong>Cut slices into fingers or small squares</strong> so they sit like crackers on a board.</li><li><strong>Pair it with</strong> sharp cheddar, Stilton or another blue, aged Gouda and a wedge of brie. The British serve fruitcake with cheese for a reason.</li><li><strong>Add</strong> toasted pecans, dried apricots, apple slices and a glass of tawny port or sherry.</li><li><strong>How much:</strong> a 1 lb loaf gives about 16 to 20 thin slices, enough for a board of 10 to 12.</li></ul>

## Fruitcake questions

**What is the best fruitcake to buy?**

Collin Street Bakery's DeLuxe for a gift tin, Assumption Abbey for a rum-aged monastery cake, and Claxton for about $4.

**Which fruitcakes are alcohol-free?**

Collin Street Bakery's DeLuxe is alcohol-free. Monastery cakes like Assumption Abbey (rum) and Gethsemani (bourbon) are not.

**What cheese goes with fruitcake?**

Sharp cheddar, Stilton and aged Gouda. Salty, crumbly cheese balances the sweet fruit.

**How long does fruitcake keep?**

St. Louis Magazine says Assumption Abbey's lasts about two years unopened and a year in the fridge after opening. Check the label for others.

**Which fruitcake should you skip?**

The Fayetteville Observer ranked Walmart's 13 oz fruitcake last, calling it bland with little fruit or nuts.
