<h2 id="how-to-choose">How many dips to buy for a party</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>How many kinds:</strong> 2 dips for up to 8 guests, 3 for 12, and 4 or 5 for 20 or more.</li><li><strong>How much:</strong> about 2 to 3 tablespoons of each dip per guest, so a 16-ounce tub covers about 10 people.</li><li><strong>Mix textures:</strong> one creamy (spinach or French onion), one warm (queso or buffalo chicken) and one fresh (salsa, guacamole or hummus).</li><li><strong>Match the dippers:</strong> kettle chips for onion dip, tortilla chips for queso and salsa, pita and vegetables for hummus.</li><li><strong>Food safety:</strong> cold dairy and seafood dips shouldn't sit out more than 2 hours. Put out half and refill from the fridge.</li></ul>

## Store-bought dip questions

**What is the best store-bought dip?**

Marketside Spinach & Artichoke Dip, our highest-scoring dip at 4.7/5, tied with Heluva Good! French Onion Dip.

**How many dips should I have for a party?**

Two for up to 8 guests, three for 12, and four or five for 20 or more: one creamy, one warm and one fresh.

**How much dip per person?**

About 2 to 3 tablespoons of each dip per guest. A 16-ounce tub serves about 10 people.

**What's the best warm dip to buy?**

Marketside Queso Blanco for chips, or Marketside Buffalo Style Chicken Dip for game day. Bake or warm them right before serving.

**How long can dip sit out at a party?**

No more than 2 hours, or 1 hour above 90°F, for dairy and seafood dips.
