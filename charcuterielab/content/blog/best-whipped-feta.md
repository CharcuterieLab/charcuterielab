## Whipped feta questions

**What is the best store-bought whipped feta?**

Trader Joe's Whipped Feta Spread is the fan favorite at $3.99 for 8 oz. Athenos is the easiest to find, and Costco has two 16-oz options.

**What is whipped feta made of?**

Feta blended until smooth with Greek yogurt, cream cheese or ricotta and olive oil, so it's creamy and spreadable but still salty.

**What goes with whipped feta?**

Hot honey, olive oil, black pepper, pistachios, za'atar, warm pita, cucumbers, cherry tomatoes and grapes.

**Can I make whipped feta at home?**

Yes: blend 8 oz block feta with 4 oz cream cheese or Greek yogurt and a splash of olive oil for about a minute. Keep it covered in the fridge and use it within a few days.

**How long can whipped feta sit out?**

No more than 2 hours at room temperature, or 1 hour above 90°F.
