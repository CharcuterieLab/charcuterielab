## Aldi holiday questions

**When do Aldi holiday items come out?**

Aldi Finds arrive on Wednesdays from early November. In 2025, the advent calendars arrived on November 5, the first Wednesday of the month. Aldi hasn't confirmed its 2026 dates.

**What are Aldi Finds?**

Limited-time items, separate from Aldi's everyday groceries. They're listed in the weekly ad, and stores usually get one shipment, so they can sell out in days.

**What's the best Aldi cheese for a holiday board?**

The Emporium Selection Brie for baking, and the seasonal Cranberry White Cheddar for color. The cranberry cinnamon goat cheese log is a good third cheese for under $4.

**Does Aldi have a charcuterie tray?**

Yes. The Specially Selected Panino Tray, mozzarella wrapped in salami, prosciutto and pepperoni, is listed at $10.99 for 18 oz.

**How much does an Aldi holiday board cost?**

Our Aldi guide builds a full board for 6 to 8 for well under $40. Adding a seasonal cheese and a spread set adds about $12.
