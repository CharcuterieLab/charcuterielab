<h2 id="how-to-choose">How to make party meatballs from frozen</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>How many:</strong> 4 to 5 meatballs per guest as an appetizer, 8 to 10 if they're the main dish.</li><li><strong>Classic slow-cooker sauce:</strong> grape jelly and chili sauce, or barbecue sauce, 3 to 4 hours on low.</li><li><strong>Keep them hot:</strong> leave the slow cooker on warm and put out toothpicks.</li><li><strong>Food safety:</strong> heat frozen meatballs to 165°F, and don't leave them out of the heat for more than 2 hours.</li><li><strong>Put a board next to it:</strong> a cold board fills the table while the meatballs heat.</li></ul>

## Frozen meatball questions

**What are the best frozen meatballs?**

Kirkland Signature from Costco scored 9/10 at Sporked. Johnsonville Italian scored 8.5/10, and Trader Joe's Italian Style topped Daily Meal's test.

**How many frozen meatballs per person for a party?**

About 4 to 5 per guest as an appetizer, 8 to 10 if they're the main dish.

**How long do frozen meatballs take in a slow cooker?**

About 3 to 4 hours on low, or until they reach 165°F.

**What sauce goes with party meatballs?**

Grape jelly and chili sauce is the classic; barbecue sauce or marinara also work.

**Are there good vegan frozen meatballs?**

Beyond Italian Style scored 8.5/10 at Sporked.
