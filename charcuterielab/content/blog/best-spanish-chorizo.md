<h2 id="faq">Spanish chorizo questions</h2>
:::

**What is the best Spanish chorizo for a charcuterie board?**

Palacios, which is imported from Spain, dry-cured and sold in most grocery stores.

**What's the difference between Spanish and Mexican chorizo?**

Spanish chorizo is dry-cured and ready to eat; Mexican chorizo is raw and must be cooked.

**What goes with chorizo?**

Manchego, Marcona almonds, olives, quince paste and a glass of Rioja.
