### 1. Palacios Chorizo (mild or hot)

**★★★★½ 4.3 out of 5** · Best overall · Best for: Everyday Spanish chorizo · $$ mid-range

Imported from La Rioja, dry-cured with smoked paprika and no added nitrates or nitrites. One of the most widely stocked Spanish chorizos in US grocery stores. (Source: [Palacios Chorizo](http://www.palacioschorizo.com/).)

- **Why we like it:** Everywhere. Mild and hot versions.
- **Keep in mind:** Few published taste tests.
- **Where to buy:** Most grocery stores, Whole Foods.

### 2. Fermín Chorizo Ibérico

**★★★★☆ 3.8 out of 5** · Best splurge · Best for: Ibérico richness · $$$ premium

Made from Iberian pork, which is richer and more marbled than regular pork. Sold sliced and whole. (Source: [José Andrés Foods](https://www.joseandresfoods.com/fermin-usa-sliced-iberico-chorizo-c8180).)

- **Why we like it:** Ibérico pork. Rich flavor.
- **Keep in mind:** Expensive.
- **Where to buy:** Specialty grocers, online.

### 3. Columbus Spanish Style Chorizo Salame

**★★★½☆ 3.5 out of 5** · Best value · Best for: American-made option · $$ mid-range

A domestic dry salami seasoned in the Spanish style. Easy to find and easy to slice. (Source: [Columbus](https://www.columbuscraftmeats.com/products/salami/spanish-style-chorizo-salame/).)

- **Why we like it:** Available. Consistent.
- **Keep in mind:** Not imported Spanish chorizo.
- **Where to buy:** Most grocery stores.

### 4. MeatCrafters Chorizo de Pamplona

**★★★☆☆ 3.2 out of 5** · Best for: Clean ingredients · $$ mid-range

An American-made, Pamplona-style cured chorizo with no added nitrites or nitrates, made from antibiotic-free pork. (Source: [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/clp/B08L8DK9VZ).)

- **Why we like it:** Clean label.
- **Keep in mind:** Mostly online.
- **Where to buy:** Amazon, specialty grocers.

## What to look for on the label

**Label check:** 'dry cured' and 'Spanish chorizo'. Paprika should be the main seasoning. 'Ibérico' means it's made from Iberian pork.

**Skip:** Mexican chorizo from the refrigerated case. It's raw and must be cooked.

## Spanish chorizo questions

**What is the best Spanish chorizo for a charcuterie board?**

Palacios, which is imported from Spain, dry-cured and sold in most grocery stores.

**What's the difference between Spanish and Mexican chorizo?**

Spanish chorizo is dry-cured and ready to eat; Mexican chorizo is raw and must be cooked.

**What goes with chorizo?**

Manchego, Marcona almonds, olives, quince paste and a glass of Rioja.
