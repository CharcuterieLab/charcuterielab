<h2 id="how-to-choose">How to make a store-bought tray look homemade</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Move it to a real board.</strong> Lift each section out in one piece and spread it out.</li><li><strong>Add three fresh things:</strong> grapes, sliced apples or pears, and a sprig of rosemary.</li><li><strong>Swap the crackers</strong> for a better one, and add a bowl of nuts.</li><li><strong>Fold the salami</strong> into a river or a <a href="/blog/how-to-make-a-salami-rose/">salami rose</a>.</li><li><strong>How many trays:</strong> one 12.5 oz tray per 3 to 4 guests as an appetizer, or use the <a href="/party-planner/">Party Planner</a>.</li></ul>

## Charcuterie tray questions

**What is the best store-bought charcuterie tray?**

The Columbus Charcuterie Tasting Board: $14.97 for 12.5 oz at Walmart, with 4.4 stars from 1,383 reviews.

**How many people does a store-bought charcuterie tray feed?**

A 12.5 oz tray feeds about 3 to 4 people as an appetizer; the 16.3 oz Sam's Club tray about 5 to 6.

**Is Aldi's charcuterie tray good?**

It's the cheapest at $9.89, but a Business Insider reviewer found the portions small and the salami bland.

**Is a pre-made tray cheaper than building a board?**

Not usually. Our Price Index built a board for 10 at Aldi for $33.90, about $3.39 a guest.

**How far ahead can I buy a charcuterie tray?**

Check the use-by date and keep it sealed in the fridge until an hour before serving.
