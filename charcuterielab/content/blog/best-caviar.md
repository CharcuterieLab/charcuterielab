## Caviar questions

**What's the best cheap caviar?**

Whole Foods whitefish caviar scored 9.5/10 in Sporked's under-$25 tasting. For real sturgeon, Noir hackleback is about $32 for 30 g.

**Is salmon roe real caviar?**

Strictly, caviar is sturgeon roe. Salmon roe (ikura) and whitefish roe are sold as caviar, and they're good and much cheaper.

**What caviar is best for beginners?**

A mild, buttery Ossetra if you're splurging; whitefish or salmon roe if you're testing the waters.

**How much caviar per person?**

About ½ oz as a taste, or 1 oz per person if it's the main event.

**How long does opened caviar last?**

Eat it within a day or two of opening, kept very cold.
