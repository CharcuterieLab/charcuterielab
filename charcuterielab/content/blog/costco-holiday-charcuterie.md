## Costco holiday questions

**Does Costco sell charcuterie trays?**

Yes. In warehouses there's the Fratelli Beretta Charcuterie Kit, a pre-plated tray. Costco.com sells shipped boards such as Luxe Bites, and the deli has sold a Fruit, Meat and Cheese Party Platter since 2023.

**How much is the Costco charcuterie board?**

The Beretta Charcuterie Kit was tracked at about $25 in October 2026. The deli party platter was $39.99 when last reported in 2024. Shipped boards run higher; the Wisconsin Select bundle is $179.99.

**How many people does a Costco tray serve?**

The deli party platter is advertised for 18 to 24 people, and the shipped Luxe Bites board for 5 to 6. Costco doesn't give a serving count for the Beretta kit; plan on it as one board for a small group.

**When do Costco holiday items arrive?**

From late October. Baked brie, Raincoast Crisps and the Beretta kit all first appeared in the last week of October in recent years, and they sell out, so buy a week or two before the party.

**Is Costco open on Thanksgiving and Christmas?**

No. US warehouses are closed on Thanksgiving (Thursday, November 26, 2026) and Christmas Day (Friday, December 25). Hours on Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve can differ, so check your warehouse.
