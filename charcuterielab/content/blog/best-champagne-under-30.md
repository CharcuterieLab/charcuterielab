<h2 id="how-to-choose">How to pick bubbly for a board</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Champagne or not:</strong> only wine from France's Champagne region can use the name. Crémant, Gruet and cava are made the same way, with the second fermentation in the bottle, for much less.</li><li><strong>Read the sweetness word:</strong> Brut is dry. Extra Dry is actually a little sweeter than Brut.</li><li><strong>Pair it with salt and fat:</strong> brie, aged Gouda, Parmesan, prosciutto, smoked salmon and even potato chips. Bubbles and acidity cut the richness.</li><li><strong>How much:</strong> a 750 ml bottle pours about five glasses. For a midnight toast, one bottle covers about eight small pours.</li><li><strong>Skip:</strong> Charles de Marques Champagne at Trader Joe's ($20.99). The Chronicle's tasters found it oxidized and metallic.</li></ul>

## Champagne questions

**What's the best cheap Champagne?**

Kirkland Signature Champagne Brut at Costco, about $20, is real grand cru Champagne. Outside Costco, true Champagne usually starts around $35.

**What's the difference between Champagne and crémant?**

Both are made with a second fermentation in the bottle. Champagne must come from the Champagne region; crémant comes from other French regions such as Burgundy and Alsace, and costs far less.

**Is Brut or Extra Dry sweeter?**

Extra Dry is sweeter. Brut is the dry choice for a cheese board.

**What cheese goes with Champagne?**

Brie and other bloomy cheeses, aged Gouda, Parmesan and Comté. Salty cured meats like prosciutto work too.

**How many bottles for a New Year's Eve party?**

A bottle pours about five glasses. Plan on half a bottle per guest for the night, or one bottle per eight guests for just the midnight toast.
