<h2 id="how-to-choose">How to serve eggnog at a holiday party</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Serve it very cold</strong> in small glasses or punch cups; eggnog is rich, so 4 oz is a serving.</li><li><strong>Grate fresh nutmeg</strong> over the top. It fixes the flat, sweet nogs instantly.</li><li><strong>Spiked:</strong> about 1 part bourbon, dark rum or brandy to 4 parts nog. Label it so kids and non-drinkers can tell.</li><li><strong>Next to it,</strong> set out a dessert board: shortbread, gingerbread, panettone, candied pecans, dark chocolate and clementines.</li><li><strong>For a crowd</strong> of 12, plan on 2 quarts, or a half gallon plus a dairy-free quart.</li><li><strong>Keep it cold:</strong> set the bowl in a larger bowl of ice, and don't leave dairy out more than 2 hours.</li></ul>

## Eggnog questions

**What is the best store-bought eggnog?**

Trader Joe's Egg Nog topped Daily Meal's 13-brand test. Lactaid was the most consistent, placing high in three tests.

**What's the best eggnog for spiking?**

Southern Comfort Vanilla Spice: thick and sweet enough to balance bourbon, rum or brandy.

**What's the best dairy-free eggnog?**

Chobani Oat Nog. It's also nut-free, unlike almond and cashew nogs.

**How much eggnog do I need for a party?**

About 4 oz per person; 2 quarts covers about 12 to 16 guests.

**What goes with eggnog?**

Shortbread, gingerbread, panettone, candied pecans and clementines.
