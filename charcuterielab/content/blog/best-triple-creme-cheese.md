# The Best Triple Crème Cheese for a Charcuterie Board

> **Quick Answer:** Best overall: **Trader Joe's Triple Creme Brie** (9.5/10 at Sporked). Creamiest: **Saint Angel Original Triple Cream** (#2 of 9 at Tasting Table). For a cheese shop: **Délice de Bourgogne** or **Brillat-Savarin**. Budget: **Marketside Triple Creme Brie**, $3.24 at Walmart. Plan about 1 oz per guest.

<h2 id="how-to-choose">How to serve triple crème on a board</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Take it out an hour early.</strong> Cold triple crème tastes waxy; at room temperature it turns spoonable.</li><li><strong>About 1 oz per guest</strong> when it's one of three cheeses; it's rich, so a little goes far.</li><li><strong>Give it a spreader,</strong> not a knife, and put it on the edge of the board so it can soften without running into the crackers.</li><li><strong>Pair it with something sharp or sweet:</strong> tart apples, grapes, fig jam, honey, or crisp sparkling wine.</li><li><strong>Need the closest thing?</strong> Fromager d'Affinois is a double crème, but it won America's Test Kitchen's supermarket brie test and Tasting Table's.</li></ul>

## Triple crème questions

**What is the best triple crème cheese?**

Trader Joe's Triple Creme Brie scored 9.5/10 at Sporked. For a cheese shop, Délice de Bourgogne and Brillat-Savarin are the classics.

**What does triple crème mean?**

Cream is added to the milk, so the cheese is very high in butterfat for its dry weight. It makes the paste soft, rich and buttery.

**Is Saint André a triple crème?**

Yes. It's the easiest triple crème to find, though Tasting Table found it less creamy than other bries.

**Is triple crème the same as brie?**

Not exactly. Many triple crèmes are brie-style (white bloomy rind), but triple crème means the extra cream, not the style.

**How much triple crème do I need per person?**

About 1 oz per guest when it's one of three cheeses on the board.
