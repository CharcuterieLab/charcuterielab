## Building a board from these

Three cheeses cover a board for 6 to 10 people. Pick one from each line:

- **Firm and aged:** Unexpected Cheddar or 1000 Day Gouda.
- **Soft:** Triple Crème Brie or Chèvre.
- **With a twist:** Toscano Soaked in Syrah, Cave Aged Blue or White Stilton with Apricots.

Plan on about 3 oz of cheese per guest in total when the board is the party food, or 2 oz before a meal. Take the aged cheeses out of the fridge 45 to 60 minutes before serving and the brie 20 to 30 minutes before.

For the full cart, including meats, crackers and extras with prices, see our [Trader Joe's charcuterie board shopping list](/blog/trader-joes-charcuterie-board/).

## What to skip

In [Cheapism](https://www.cheapism.com/trader-joes-most-popular-cheeses/)'s test, the Toscano with Black Pepper had little pepper flavor, the burrata was bland and the Laura Chenel goat brie was underripe. The Toscano Soaked in Syrah and fresh mozzarella are better buys.

**What is the best Trader Joe's cheese for a charcuterie board?**

Unexpected Cheddar. It scored 9 out of 10 in Sporked's test, it suits almost everyone and it costs about $4. 1000 Day Gouda and Toscano Soaked in Syrah are close behind.

**How many Trader Joe's cheeses do I need for a board?**

Three is plenty for 6 to 10 people: one aged cheese, one soft cheese and one with a twist. Plan on about 3 oz of cheese per guest in total when the board is the party food.

**Are Trader Joe's cheeses available all year?**

Most of the cheeses here are regulars, but Trader Joe's rotates its cheese case and flavored cheeses come and go. If one is missing, our Gouda, cheddar and brie guides list alternatives.
