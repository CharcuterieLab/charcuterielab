> **Quick Answer:** If you buy one bottle, buy a dry sparkling: **Segura Viudas Brut Reserva Cava** (about $11) cuts through salami, prosciutto and brie. Add a light red: **Mark West Pinot Noir** (about $11) or the dry, fizzy **Cleto Chiarli Lambrusco** (about $20). For blue cheese, **Graham's Six Grapes Port**.

<h2 id="how-to-choose">How to pick wine for a board</h2>
<ul class="sk-checks"><li><strong>Two bottles cover most boards:</strong> one sparkling or crisp white, and one light red like Pinot Noir or Lambrusco.</li><li><strong>Match the strongest thing on the board:</strong> Port for blue cheese, Garnacha for spicy salami, Riesling for ham.</li><li><strong>How much:</strong> a 750 ml bottle pours five 5 oz glasses. A common rule is one bottle for every two guests at a 2 to 3 hour party.</li><li><strong>Chill</strong> the sparkling, white and rosé, and give light reds 15 minutes in the fridge.</li></ul>

## Wine questions

**What wine goes with a charcuterie board?**

High-acid wines with soft tannins: dry sparkling wine, Sauvignon Blanc, Pinot Noir, dry Lambrusco or Riesling. In one tasting, dry sherry won overall, then Lambrusco and Riesling.

**Red or white with charcuterie?**

Either. A crisp white or sparkling for prosciutto and soft cheese, and a light red for salami and aged cheese. One of each covers most boards.

**What wine goes with salami?**

A juicy, spicy red such as Garnacha, or a dry rosé. For spicy chorizo, go bigger with Tempranillo or Malbec.

**What wine goes with prosciutto?**

Crisp whites like Riesling, or sparkling wine. If you prefer red, a light Sangiovese or Grenache.

**How many bottles of wine for 12 guests?**

A 750 ml bottle pours five 5 oz glasses. A common party rule is one bottle for every two guests over two to three hours, so about 6 bottles for 12.
